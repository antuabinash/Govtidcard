✍️ Activity 3

Instructions (English): Complete the sentences using the phrasal verbs and the objects in the appropriate places. Put the verbs given in brackets into the correct tenses.

Instructions (Odia): phrasal verb ଏବଂ object ଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ଉପଯୁକ୍ତ ସ୍ଥାନରେ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ କରନ୍ତୁ। ବନ୍ଧନୀରେ ଥିବା କ୍ରିୟାଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ସଠିକ୍ କାଳ (tense) ରେ ରଖନ୍ତୁ।

  1. I ___________ on my way to school. (come across / a strange animal)

    came across a strange animal

  2. It is better to ___________ . (throw away / broken chairs)

    throw away broken chairs (or throw broken chairs away)

  3. It was a mistake. My friend ___________ . (point out / it)

    pointed it out

  4. Who is ___________ nowadays? (looking after / this building)

    looking after this building

  5. His son died. He has never ___________ . (get over / his sorrows)

    gotten over his sorrows (or got over his sorrows)

  6. The machine is making a lot of noise. Can you ___________ ? (switch off / it)

    switch it off

  7. She offered to help me, but I ___________ . (turn down / it)

    turned it down

  8. I met Ramesh today. He ___________ . (asking after / you)

    was asking after you

  9. This school is running well. We will ___________ . (close down / it)

    close it down

  10. Your brother is in trouble. You must ___________ during his distress. (stand by / him)

    stand by him

✍️ Activity 6

Instructions (English): Replace the underlined expressions with phrasal verbs using the verbs given in brackets.

Instructions (Odia): ରେଖାଙ୍କିତ ଅଂଶଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ବନ୍ଧନୀରେ ଥିବା କ୍ରିୟା ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ଉପଯୁକ୍ତ phrasal verb ଦ୍ୱାରା ବଦଳାନ୍ତୁ।

(i) I don't like the way Swami is being  educated. (bring)

Answer: I don't like the way Swami is being brought up.

(ii) The government  rejected  the employees' request for pay revision. (turn)

Answer: The government turned down the employees' request for pay revision.

(iii) The strike was  cancelled. (call)

Answer: The strike was called off.

(iv) We  reduced  our expenses after our father's retirement. (cut)

Answer: We cut down our expenses after our father's retirement.

(v) I  disappointed  my parents by my poor results. (let)

Answer: I let my parents down by my poor results.

(vi) I can hardly  understanwhat you say. (make)

Answer: I can hardly make out what you say.

(vii) Most children  resemble  their parents. (take)

Answer: Most children take after their parents.

(viii) We  called  a doctor when my mother was ill. (send)

Answer: We sent for a doctor when my mother was ill.

(ix) We  continued working  long after the evening. (keep)

Answer: We kept on working long after the evening.

(x) Have you  read  the book I gave you? (go)

Answer: Have you gone through the book I gave you?

9. put offNever put off today's work for tomorrow.ଆଜିର କାମ କାଲିକୁ କେବେବି ଗଡ଼ାନ୍ତୁ ନାହିଁ।
10. take afterShe takes after her mother.ସେ ତାଙ୍କ ମା'ଙ୍କ ପରି ଦେଖାଯାଆନ୍ତି।
