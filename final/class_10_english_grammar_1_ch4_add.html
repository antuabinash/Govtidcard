Direct and Indirect Speech – Additional Questions Class 10 English Grammar

Direct and Indirect Speech (Additional Questions)

✍️ Activity A: Transformation (Statements: Direct to Indirect)

Instructions (English): Change the following sentences from Direct Speech to Indirect Speech. Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ପ୍ରତ୍ୟକ୍ଷ ଉକ୍ତି (Direct Speech) ରୁ ପରୋକ୍ଷ ଉକ୍ତି (Indirect Speech) କୁ ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ କରନ୍ତୁ।

  1. Direct: He said, "I am busy now."

    Indirect: He said that he was busy then. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ କହିଲେ ଯେ ସେ ସେତେବେଳେ ବ୍ୟସ୍ତ ଥିଲେ।)

  2. Direct: She said, "My mother is cooking food."

    Indirect: She said that her mother was cooking food. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ କହିଲା ଯେ ତା’ ମା' ଖାଦ୍ୟ ରାନ୍ଧୁଥିଲେ।)

  3. Direct: They said, "We have finished our work."

    Indirect: They said that they had finished their work. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେମାନେ କହିଲେ ଯେ ସେମାନେ ସେମାନଙ୍କ କାମ ସାରିଦେଇଥିଲେ।)

  4. Direct: The boy said, "I bought a pen yesterday."

    Indirect: The boy said that he had bought a pen the previous day. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ପିଲାଟି କହିଲା ଯେ ସେ ପୂର୍ବଦିନ ଏକ କଲମ କିଣିଥିଲା।)

  5. Direct: He said, "I was doing my homework."

    Indirect: He said that he had been doing his homework. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ କହିଲା ଯେ ସେ ତା’ର ଗୃହକାର୍ଯ୍ୟ କରୁଥିଲା।)

  6. Direct: She said to me, "You will go to the market."

    Indirect: She told me that I would go to the market. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ମୋତେ କହିଲା ଯେ ମୁଁ ବଜାର ଯିବି।)

  7. Direct: I said to him, "I can help you."

    Indirect: I told him that I could help him. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ମୁଁ ତାଙ୍କୁ କହିଲି ଯେ ମୁଁ ତାଙ୍କୁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରିପାରିବି।)

  8. Direct: He said, "I shall come tomorrow."

    Indirect: He said that he would come the next day. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ କହିଲେ ଯେ ସେ ପରଦିନ ଆସିବେ।)

  9. Direct: The teacher said, "The Earth moves round the Sun."

    Indirect: The teacher said that the Earth moves round the Sun. (Tense does not change) (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଶିକ୍ଷକ କହିଲେ ଯେ ପୃଥିବୀ ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟ ଚାରିପଟେ ଘୂରେ।)

  10. Direct: He says, "I am ill."

    Indirect: He says that he is ill. (Reporting verb in present - no tense change) (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ କହନ୍ତି ଯେ ସେ ଅସୁସ୍ଥ ଅଛନ୍ତି।)

  11. Direct: She will say, "The student was lazy."

    Indirect: She will say that the student was lazy. (Reporting verb in future - no tense change) (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ କହିବେ ଯେ ଛାତ୍ରଟି ଅଳସୁଆ ଥିଲା।)

  12. Direct: He said, "I am glad to be here this evening."

    Indirect: He said that he was glad to be there that evening. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ କହିଲେ ଯେ ସେ ସେହି ସନ୍ଧ୍ୟାରେ ସେଠାରେ ଥିବାରୁ ଖୁସି ଥିଲେ।)

  13. Direct: She said, "I must go now."

    Indirect: She said that she had to go then. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ କହିଲେ ଯେ ତାଙ୍କୁ ସେତେବେଳେ ଯିବାକୁ ଥିଲା।)

  14. Direct: He said, "I have passed the examination."

    Indirect: He said that he had passed the examination. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ କହିଲେ ଯେ ସେ ପରୀକ୍ଷାରେ ପାସ୍ କରିଛନ୍ତି।)

  15. Direct: The farmer said, "I have been working since morning."

    Indirect: The farmer said that he had been working since morning. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: କୃଷକ ଜଣକ କହିଲେ ଯେ ସେ ସକାଳୁ କାମ କରୁଛନ୍ତି।)

  16. Direct: My friend said, "You are a good boy."

    Indirect: My friend told me that I was a good boy. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ମୋ ସାଙ୍ଗ ମୋତେ କହିଲା ଯେ ମୁଁ ଜଣେ ଭଲ ପିଲା ଥିଲି।)

  17. Direct: He said, "I did this two days ago."

    Indirect: He said that he had done that two days before. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ କହିଲେ ଯେ ସେ ତାହା ଦୁଇ ଦିନ ପୂର୍ବରୁ କରିଥିଲେ।)

  18. Direct: She said to us, "We are going to Puri next week."

    Indirect: She told us that they were going to Puri the following week. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ଆମକୁ କହିଲେ ଯେ ସେମାନେ ଆସନ୍ତା ସପ୍ତାହରେ ପୁରୀ ଯାଉଛନ୍ତି।)

  19. Direct: He said, "Honesty is the best policy."

    Indirect: He said that honesty is the best policy. (Tense does not change) (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ କହିଲେ ଯେ ସଚ୍ଚୋଟତା ହେଉଛି ସର୍ବୋତ୍ତମ ନୀତି।)

  20. Direct: The teacher said to me, "You can go."

    Indirect: The teacher told me that I could go. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଶିକ୍ଷକ ମୋତେ କହିଲେ ଯେ ମୁଁ ଯାଇପାରେ।)


✍️ Activity B: Transformation (Questions: Direct to Indirect)

Instructions (English): Change the following questions from Direct Speech to Indirect Speech. Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ପ୍ରତ୍ୟକ୍ଷ ଉକ୍ତିରୁ ପରୋକ୍ଷ ଉକ୍ତିକୁ ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ କରନ୍ତୁ।

  1. Direct: He said to me, "Are you ill?"

    Indirect: He asked me if (or whether) I was ill. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ମୋତେ ପଚାରିଲେ ଯେ ମୁଁ ଅସୁସ୍ଥ ଥିଲି କି।)

  2. Direct: I said to him, "Do you know me?"

    Indirect: I asked him if (or whether) he knew me. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ମୁଁ ତାଙ୍କୁ ପଚାରିଲି ଯେ ସେ ମୋତେ ଜାଣିଥିଲେ କି।)

  3. Direct: She said to the boy, "Did you go to school yesterday?"

    Indirect: She asked the boy if (or whether) he had gone to school the previous day. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ପିଲାଟିକୁ ପଚାରିଲେ ଯେ ସେ ପୂର୍ବଦିନ ସ୍କୁଲ ଯାଇଥିଲା କି।)

  4. Direct: He said to me, "What is your name?"

    Indirect: He asked me what my name was. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ମୋତେ ପଚାରିଲେ ଯେ ମୋ ନାମ କ’ଣ ଥିଲା।)

  5. Direct: The teacher said to the student, "Why are you late?"

    Indirect: The teacher asked the student why he (or she) was late. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଶିକ୍ଷକ ଛାତ୍ରଟିକୁ ପଚାରିଲେ ଯେ ସେ କାହିଁକି ଡେରିରେ ଆସିଥିଲା।)

  6. Direct: I said to the stranger, "Where do you live?"

    Indirect: I asked the stranger where he (or she) lived. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ମୁଁ ଅପରିଚିତ ଲୋକଟିକୁ ପଚାରିଲି ଯେ ସେ କେଉଁଠାରେ ରହୁଥିଲେ।)

  7. Direct: He said to her, "When will you come?"

    Indirect: He asked her when she would come. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ତାଙ୍କୁ ପଚାରିଲେ ଯେ ସେ କେତେବେଳେ ଆସିବେ।)

  8. Direct: My mother asked me, "Have you finished your breakfast?"

    Indirect: My mother asked me if (or whether) I had finished my breakfast. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ମୋ ମା' ମୋତେ ପଚାରିଲେ ଯେ ମୁଁ ମୋର ଜଳଖିଆ ସାରିଥିଲି କି।)

  9. Direct: The passenger said to the clerk, "When does the train leave?"

    Indirect: The passenger asked the clerk when the train left. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଯାତ୍ରୀ ଜଣକ କିରାଣୀଙ୍କୁ ପଚାରିଲେ ଯେ ଟ୍ରେନ୍ କେତେବେଳେ ଛାଡେ।)

  10. Direct: "How did you solve the problem?" he asked me.

    Indirect: He asked me how I had solved the problem. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ମୋତେ ପଚାରିଲେ ଯେ ମୁଁ କିପରି ସମସ୍ୟାଟି ସମାଧାନ କଲି।)

  11. Direct: "Can you lend me your pen?" she asked.

    Indirect: She asked me if I could lend her my pen. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ମୋତେ ପଚାରିଲେ ଯେ ମୁଁ ତାଙ୍କୁ ମୋ କଲମ ଧାର ଦେଇପାରିବି କି।)

  12. Direct: "Whose book is this?" said the teacher.

    Indirect: The teacher asked whose book that was. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଶିକ୍ଷକ ପଚାରିଲେ ଯେ ସେଇଟା କାହାର ବହି ଥିଲା।)

  13. Direct: He said, "Will you listen to me?"

    Indirect: He asked them (or him/her) if they (or he/she) would listen to him. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ସେମାନଙ୍କୁ ପଚାରିଲେ ଯେ ସେମାନେ ତାଙ୍କ କଥା ଶୁଣିବେ କି।)

  14. Direct: "Are you coming with us?" they asked.

    Indirect: They asked me if I was coming with them. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେମାନେ ମୋତେ ପଚାରିଲେ ଯେ ମୁଁ ସେମାନଙ୍କ ସହ ଆସୁଥିଲି କି।)

  15. Direct: The father asked his son, "Why did you fail?"

    Indirect: The father asked his son why he had failed. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ବାପା ତାଙ୍କ ପୁଅକୁ ପଚାରିଲେ ଯେ ସେ କାହିଁକି ବିଫଳ ହେଲା।)


✍️ Activity C: Transformation (Imperatives: Direct to Indirect)

Instructions (English): Change the following imperative sentences from Direct Speech to Indirect Speech. Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ଆଦେଶସୂଚକ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ପ୍ରତ୍ୟକ୍ଷ ଉକ୍ତିରୁ ପରୋକ୍ଷ ଉକ୍ତିକୁ ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ କରନ୍ତୁ।

  1. Direct: The teacher said to the boy, "Sit down."

    Indirect: The teacher ordered the boy to sit down. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଶିକ୍ଷକ ପିଲାଟିକୁ ବସିବାକୁ ଆଦେଶ ଦେଲେ।)

  2. Direct: He said to me, "Please help me."

    Indirect: He requested me to help him. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ମୋତେ ତାଙ୍କୁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରିବାକୁ ଅନୁରୋଧ କଲେ।)

  3. Direct: The doctor said to the patient, "Don't smoke."

    Indirect: The doctor advised the patient not to smoke. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଡାକ୍ତର ରୋଗୀଙ୍କୁ ଧୂମପାନ ନ କରିବାକୁ ଉପଦେଶ ଦେଲେ।)

  4. Direct: My father said to me, "Work hard."

    Indirect: My father advised me to work hard. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ମୋ ବାପା ମୋତେ କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ କରିବାକୁ ଉପଦେଶ ଦେଲେ।)

  5. Direct: The captain said to the soldiers, "March on."

    Indirect: The captain commanded the soldiers to march on. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: କ୍ୟାପଟେନ୍ ସୈନିକମାନଙ୍କୁ ଆଗକୁ ବଢିବାକୁ ଆଦେଶ ଦେଲେ।)

  6. Direct: He said, "Let's go for a picnic."

    Indirect: He suggested that they should go for a picnic. (or He suggested going for a picnic.) (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ଏକ ପିକନିକ୍ ଯିବାକୁ ପ୍ରସ୍ତାବ ଦେଲେ।)

  7. Direct: The beggar said to me, "Give me some food."

    Indirect: The beggar begged me to give him some food. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଭିକାରୀଟି ମୋତେ କିଛି ଖାଦ୍ୟ ଦେବାକୁ ମାଗିଲା।)

  8. Direct: She said to her son, "Never tell a lie."

    Indirect: She advised her son never to tell a lie. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ତାଙ୍କ ପୁଅକୁ କେବେବି ମିଛ ନ କହିବାକୁ ଉପଦେଶ ଦେଲେ।)

  9. Direct: I said to him, "Please wait here."

    Indirect: I requested him to wait there. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ମୁଁ ତାଙ୍କୁ ସେଠାରେ ଅପେକ୍ଷା କରିବାକୁ ଅନୁରୋଧ କଲି।)

  10. Direct: The notice said, "Do not touch the exhibits."

    Indirect: The notice warned (or instructed) people not to touch the exhibits. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ନୋଟିସ୍ ଲୋକମାନଙ୍କୁ ପ୍ରଦର୍ଶିତ ସାମଗ୍ରୀକୁ ନ ଛୁଇଁବାକୁ ସତର୍କ କଲା।)


✍️ Activity D: Transformation (Exclamatory: Direct to Indirect)

Instructions (English): Change the following exclamatory sentences from Direct Speech to Indirect Speech. Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବିସ୍ମୟସୂଚକ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ପ୍ରତ୍ୟକ୍ଷ ଉକ୍ତିରୁ ପରୋକ୍ଷ ଉକ୍ତିକୁ ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ କରନ୍ତୁ।

  1. Direct: He said, "What a beautiful sight!"

    Indirect: He exclaimed that it was a very beautiful sight. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ଆଶ୍ଚର୍ଯ୍ୟ ହୋଇ କହିଲେ ଯେ ଏହା ଏକ ବହୁତ ସୁନ୍ଦର ଦୃଶ୍ୟ ଥିଲା।)

  2. Direct: She said, "Alas! My cat is dead."

    Indirect: She exclaimed with sorrow that her cat was dead. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ଦୁଃଖରେ କହିଲେ ଯେ ତାଙ୍କ ବିଲେଇ ମରିଯାଇଛି।)

  3. Direct: The team said, "Hurrah! We have won the match."

    Indirect: The team exclaimed with joy that they had won the match. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଦଳଟି ଆନନ୍ଦରେ ଚିତ୍କାର କଲା ଯେ ସେମାନେ ମ୍ୟାଚ୍ ଜିତିଯାଇଛନ୍ତି।)

  4. Direct: He said to me, "How foolish I have been!"

    Indirect: He confessed to me that he had been very foolish. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ମୋ ଆଗରେ ସ୍ୱୀକାର କଲେ ଯେ ସେ ବହୁତ ମୂର୍ଖ ଥିଲେ।)

  5. Direct: She said, "What a pity!"

    Indirect: She exclaimed that it was a great pity. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ଆଶ୍ଚର୍ଯ୍ୟ ହୋଇ କହିଲେ ଯେ ଏହା ବହୁତ ଦୁଃଖର ବିଷୟ ଥିଲା।)

  6. Direct: The boy said, "What a tall building!"

    Indirect: The boy exclaimed that it was a very tall building. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ପିଲାଟି ଆଶ୍ଚର୍ଯ୍ୟ ହୋଇ କହିଲା ଯେ ଏହା ଏକ ବହୁତ ଉଚ୍ଚ କୋଠା ଥିଲା।)

  7. Direct: He said, "Good morning, my friend."
