II. Answer the following questions. ✍️

Question 1. What event does Dr. Kurien refer to when he mentions receiving the Padma Vibhushan?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ପଦ୍ମ ବିଭୂଷଣ ଗ୍ରହଣ କରିବା କଥା ଉଲ୍ଲେଖ କରିବା ବେଳେ ଡକ୍ଟର କୁରିଏନ୍ କେଉଁ ଘଟଣାକୁ ସୂଚାଉଛନ୍ତି?)

Answer: Dr. Kurien refers to the magnificent ceremony in 1999 when the President of India honored him with the Padma Vibhushan (India’s second-highest civilian award) in recognition of his monumental contributions to the White Revolution and the dairy industry. 🏅

Question 2. How did Dr. Kurien’s decision to join the cooperative in Anand affect his life?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଆଣନ୍ଦରେ ସମବାୟ ସମିତିରେ ଯୋଗଦେବାର ଡକ୍ଟର କୁରିଏନଙ୍କ ନିଷ୍ପତ୍ତି ତାଙ୍କ ଜୀବନକୁ କିପରି ପ୍ରଭାବିତ କଲା?)

Answer: Dr. Kurien’s decision to join the cooperative in Anand transformed his life completely. Initially hesitant, he chose to stay and support the poor dairy farmers. This decision led him to build Amul, spearhead the White Revolution, and dedicate his entire life to empowering rural India. 🥛🐄

Question 3. Dr. Kurien’s decision to work in Anand reveals his values and sense of duty. Explain.

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଆଣନ୍ଦରେ କାମ କରିବାର ଡକ୍ଟର କୁରିଏନଙ୍କ ନିଷ୍ପତ୍ତି ତାଙ୍କର ମୂଲ୍ୟବୋଧ ଏବଂ କର୍ତ୍ତବ୍ୟବୋଧକୁ ପ୍ରକାଶ କରେ। ବ୍ୟାଖ୍ୟା କରନ୍ତୁ।)

Answer: Dr. Kurien’s choice to stay in Anand instead of chasing a highly paid or comfortable job reveals his deep sense of responsibility, compassion, and patriotism. It shows that he valued social justice, empowering poor farmers, and serving his nation above his own personal comfort. 🇮🇳

Question 4. Why did Dr. Kurien write the letter to his grandson?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଡକ୍ଟର କୁରିଏନ୍ ତାଙ୍କ ନାତିଙ୍କୁ କାହିଁକି ଚିଠି ଲେଖିଥିଲେ?)

Answer: Dr. Kurien wrote the letter to his grandson to pass down the wisdom he gained throughout his life. He wanted to inspire the younger generation to find a meaningful purpose, embrace selfless service, not fear failure, and live a life driven by strong values. ✉️👦

Question 5. According to Dr. Kurien “life is a privilege and it is wrong to waste it”. What is your opinion on this?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଡକ୍ଟର କୁରିଏନଙ୍କ ଅନୁଯାୟୀ "ଜୀବନ ଏକ ସୌଭାଗ୍ୟ ଏବଂ ଏହାକୁ ନଷ୍ଟ କରିବା ଭୁଲ୍"। ଏହା ଉପରେ ଆପଣଙ୍କ ମତ କ'ଣ?)

Answer: I completely agree with Dr. Kurien. Human life is a rare gift, offering us the chance to make a positive impact on the world. Wasting it on selfish or meaningless pursuits is wrong because we miss the opportunity to uplift society, help others, and leave a lasting, meaningful legacy behind. 🌟

Question 6. What message does Dr. Kurien want to convey about selfless service and its long-term impact on the society?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ନିଃସ୍ୱାର୍ଥପର ସେବା ଏବଂ ସମାଜ ଉପରେ ଏହାର ଦୀର୍ଘକାଳୀନ ପ୍ରଭାବ ବିଷୟରେ ଡକ୍ଟର କୁରିଏନ୍ କେଉଁ ବାର୍ତ୍ତା ଦେବାକୁ ଚାହୁଁଛନ୍ତି?)

Answer: Dr. Kurien wants to convey that selfless service is the highest calling one can answer. Its long-term impact is profound—it has the power to permanently uplift entire communities, bring economic independence to millions of poor farmers, and build a stronger, more united nation for future generations. 🌍🤝
