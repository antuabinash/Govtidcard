✍️ Activity 5

Instructions (English): Combine each pair of the following sentences into a compound sentence using conjunctions such as: 'and', 'but', 'or', 'so', 'yet', 'for', 'otherwise'.

Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟ ଯୋଡିଗୁଡିକୁ 'and', 'but', 'or', 'so', 'yet', 'for', 'otherwise' ଭଳି ସଂଯୋଜକ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ଯୌଗିକ ବାକ୍ୟରେ (compound sentence) ପରିଣତ କରନ୍ତୁ।

Sentence Pair (ବାକ୍ୟ ଯୋଡି)

Combined Compound Sentence (ଯୌଗିକ ବାକ୍ୟ)

(i) He finished his exercises. He put away his books.

He finished his exercises and put away his books. (ସେ ତାଙ୍କର ଅଭ୍ୟାସ ସାରିଲେ ଏବଂ ତାଙ୍କ ବହିଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ରଖିଦେଲେ।)

(ii) He must not be late. He will be punished.

He must not be late, otherwise he will be punished. (ସେ ବିଳମ୍ବ କରିବା ଉଚିତ୍ ନୁହେଁ, ନଚେତ୍ ସେ ଦଣ୍ଡିତ ହେବେ।)

(iii) Make haste. You will be late.

Make haste, or you will be late. (ଶୀଘ୍ର କର, ନଚେତ୍ ତୁମର ବିଳମ୍ବ ହେବ।)

(iv) He is rich. He is not contented.

He is rich, but he is not contented. (ସେ ଧନୀ, କିନ୍ତୁ ସେ ସନ୍ତୁଷ୍ଟ ନୁହଁନ୍ତି।)

(v) He was horrified. He saw bloodstain on the floor.

He was horrified, for he saw bloodstain on the floor. (ସେ ଭୟଭୀତ ହୋଇଗଲେ, କାରଣ ସେ ଚଟାଣରେ ରକ୍ତ ଦାଗ ଦେଖିଲେ।)

(vi) He tried hard. He did not succeed.

He tried hard, yet he did not succeed. (ସେ କଠିନ ଚେଷ୍ଟା କଲେ, ତଥାପି ସେ ସଫଳ ହେଲେ ନାହିଁ।)

(vii) They took every precaution. They suffered from the disease.

They took every precaution, yet they suffered from the disease. (ସେମାନେ ସମସ୍ତ ସତର୍କତା ଅବଲମ୍ବନ କଲେ, ତଥାପି ସେମାନେ ରୋଗରେ ପୀଡିତ ହେଲେ।)

(viii) I have a lot of work. I must do it now.

I have a lot of work, so I must do it now. (ମୋର ବହୁତ କାମ ଅଛି, ତେଣୁ ମୁଁ ଏହାକୁ ଏବେ କରିବା ଉଚିତ୍।)

(ix) Be good. You will be happy.

Be good, and you will be happy. (ଭଲ ହୁଅ, ଏବଂ ତୁମେ ଖୁସି ରହିବ।)

(x) He practised daily. He became an expert player.

He practised daily, so he became an expert player. (ସେ ପ୍ରତିଦିନ ଅଭ୍ୟାସ କଲେ, ତେଣୁ ସେ ଜଣେ ଦକ୍ଷ ଖେଳାଳି ହେଲେ।)


✍️ Activity 6

Instructions (English): Combine each pair of the following sentences into a compound sentence using one of the correlatives: either ... or, neither ... nor, not only ... but also, both ... and.

Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟ ଯୋଡିଗୁଡିକୁ ଏହି correlatives: either ... or, neither ... nor, not only ... but also, both ... and ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ଯୌଗିକ ବାକ୍ୟରେ ପରିଣତ କରନ୍ତୁ।

Sentence Pair (ବାକ୍ୟ ଯୋଡି)

Combined Compound Sentence (ଯୌଗିକ ବାକ୍ୟ)

(i) Energy is necessary for success in life. Patience is also necessary for success in life.

Both energy and patience are necessary for success in life. (ଶକ୍ତି ଏବଂ ଧୈର୍ଯ୍ୟ ଉଭୟ ଜୀବନରେ ସଫଳତା ପାଇଁ ଆବଶ୍ୟକ।)

(ii) I have left my glasses on the bus. I have left my glasses at the office.

I have left my glasses either on the bus or at the office. (ମୁଁ ମୋ ଚଷମା କି' ତ ବସରେ ଛାଡିଛି କିମ୍ବା ଅଫିସରେ ଛାଡିଛି।)

(iii) He cannot speak English. He cannot speak Hindi.

He can speak neither English nor Hindi. (ସେ ଇଂରାଜୀ କିମ୍ବା ହିନ୍ଦୀ କିଛି କହିପାରନ୍ତି ନାହିଁ।)

(iv) The boy is very healthy. He is also very happy.

The boy is not only very healthy but also very happy. (ବାଳକଟି କେବଳ ବହୁତ ସୁସ୍ଥ ନୁହେଁ, ବରଂ ବହୁତ ଖୁସି ମଧ୍ୟ।)

(v) He does not drink tea. He does not drink coffee.

He drinks neither tea nor coffee. (ସେ ଚା କିମ୍ବା କଫି କିଛି ପିଅନ୍ତି ନାହିଁ।)

(vi) She draws pictures neatly. She also paints them beautifully.

She not only draws pictures neatly but also paints them beautifully. (ସେ କେବଳ ଚିତ୍ରଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ସଫା ଭାବରେ ଆଙ୍କନ୍ତି ନାହିଁ, ବରଂ ସେଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ସୁନ୍ଦର ଭାବରେ ରଙ୍ଗ ମଧ୍ୟ କରନ୍ତି।)

(vii) The artists will sing for us. They will dance for us too.

The artists will both sing and dance for us. (କଳାକାରମାନେ ଆମ ପାଇଁ ଉଭୟ ଗୀତ ଗାଇବେ ଏବଂ ନାଚିବେ।)
