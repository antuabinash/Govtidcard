My Land : Together We Can

My Land : Together We Can – Study Material Class 5 English Pallavi

🌿 Each and Every Paragraph & Stanza Explanation

➡️ Paragraph 1

📜 Original Paragraph:

 A flock of doves flew in search of food.

They saw some grains of rice on the ground under a banyan tree.

They all came down and started eating grains of rice.

 But they all were caught in net.

They saw a hunter hiding behind a tree.

All got afraid. "What to do now?", they thought.

🗣️ English-Odia (Pronunciation): ଏ ଫ୍ଲକ୍ ଅଫ୍ ଡୋଭସ୍ ଫ୍ଲିଉ ଇନ୍ ସର୍ଚ୍ଚ ଅଫ୍ ଫୁଡ୍। ଦେ ସ ସମ୍ ଗ୍ରେନ୍ସ ଅଫ୍ ରାଇସ୍ ଅନ୍ ଦ ଗ୍ରାଉଣ୍ଡ ଅଣ୍ଡର୍ ଏ ବାନିଆନ୍ ଟ୍ରି। ଦେ ଅଲ୍ କେମ୍ ଡାଉନ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ଷ୍ଟାର୍ଟେଡ୍ ଇଟିଂ ଗ୍ରେନ୍ସ ଅଫ୍ ରାଇସ୍। ବଟ୍ ଦେ ଅଲ୍ ୱେଆର୍ କଟ୍ ଇନ୍ ନେଟ୍। ଦେ ସ ଏ ହଣ୍ଟର୍ ହାଇଡିଂ ବିହାଇଣ୍ଡ ଏ ଟ୍ରି। ଅଲ୍ ଗଟ୍ ଆଫ୍ରେଡ୍। "ହ୍ୱାଟ୍ ଟୁ ଡୁ ନାଓ?", ଦେ ଥଟ୍।

📝 Odia Meaning: କପୋତ (ପାରା) ମାନଙ୍କର ଗୋଟିଏ ପଲ ଖାଦ୍ୟ ସନ୍ଧାନରେ ଉଡ଼ିଯାଉଥିଲେ। ସେମାନେ ଗୋଟିଏ ବରଗଛ ତଳେ ଭୂଇଁରେ କିଛି ଚାଉଳ ଦାନା ପଡ଼ିଥିବାର ଦେଖିଲେ। ସେମାନେ ସମସ୍ତେ ତଳକୁ ଆସିଲେ ଏବଂ ଚାଉଳ ଦାନା ଖାଇବା ଆରମ୍ଭ କଲେ। କିନ୍ତୁ ସେମାନେ ସମସ୍ତେ ଜାଲରେ ଧରାପଡ଼ିଗଲେ। ସେମାନେ ଦେଖିଲେ ଯେ ଜଣେ ଶିକାରୀ ଗଛ ପଛରେ ଲୁଚିଛି। ସମସ୍ତେ ଡରିଗଲେ। ସେମାନେ ଭାବିଲେ, "ଏବେ କ'ଣ କରିବା?"।

💡 English Meaning: A group of doves was flying around looking for something to eat. They spotted rice grains under a large banyan tree and flew down to eat them. However, it was a trap, and they were all caught in a net. When they saw a hunter hiding behind a tree, they became very scared and wondered how they could escape.

➡️ Paragraph 2

📜 Original Paragraph:

 "Together we can" said the old dove and planned to fly together picking the net.

So, together they flew carrying the net.

🗣️ English-Odia (Pronunciation): "ଟୁଗେଦର୍ ୱି କ୍ୟାନ୍" ସେଡ୍ ଦ ଓଲ୍ଡ ଡୋଭ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ପ୍ଲାନ୍‌ଡ୍ ଟୁ ଫ୍ଲାଏ ଟୁଗେଦର୍ ପିକିଂ ଦ ନେଟ୍। ସୋ, ଟୁଗେଦର୍ ଦେ ଫ୍ଲିଉ କ୍ୟାରିଂ ଦ ନେଟ୍।

📝 Odia Meaning: ବୃଦ୍ଧ କପୋତଟି କହିଲା, "ଆମେ ଏକାଠି ମିଶି କରିପାରିବା" ଏବଂ ଜାଲକୁ ଟେକି ଏକାଠି ଉଡ଼ିବାକୁ ଯୋଜନା କଲା। ତେଣୁ, ସେମାନେ ସମସ୍ତେ ଏକାଠି ଜାଲକୁ ବୋହି ଉଡ଼ିଗଲେ।

💡 English Meaning: A wise, older dove came up with a brilliant idea. He told the other doves that if they all used their strength together, they could lift the heavy net. Listening to his advice, all the birds flapped their wings at the exact same time and flew up into the sky, taking the net with them.

🔤 English ➡️ Odia Meaning (Dictation Words - Easy to Hard):

old ➡️ ବୃଦ୍ଧ

net ➡️ ଜାଲ

picking ➡️ ଟେକିବା ବା ଉଠାଇବା

planned ➡️ ଯୋଜନା କଲା

carrying ➡️ ବୋହି ନେବା

➡️ Paragraph 3

📜 Original Paragraph: They reached their friend's house, the house of Micky mouse.

 "Can you cut the long net?" asked the old dove.

"Don't worry, together we can.

" Saying so he called all his family members and cut the net.

The doves were free!

🗣️ English-Odia (Pronunciation): ଦେ ରିଚ୍‌ଡ୍ ଦେଆର୍ ଫ୍ରେଣ୍ଡସ୍ ହାଉସ୍, ଦ ହାଉସ୍ ଅଫ୍ ମିକି ମାଉସ୍। "କ୍ୟାନ୍ ୟୁ କଟ୍ ଦ ଲଙ୍ଗ୍ ନେଟ୍?" ଆସ୍କଡ୍ ଦ ଓଲ୍ଡ ଡୋଭ୍। "ଡୋଣ୍ଟ ଓରି, ଟୁଗେଦର୍ ୱି କ୍ୟାନ୍।" ସେଇଂ ସୋ ହି କଲ୍‌ଡ ଅଲ୍ ହିଜ୍ ଫ୍ୟାମିଲି ମେମ୍ବର୍ସ ଆଣ୍ଡ କଟ୍ ଦ ନେଟ୍। ଦ ଡୋଭସ୍ ୱେଆର୍ ଫ୍ରି!

📝 Odia Meaning: ସେମାନେ ତାଙ୍କ ସାଙ୍ଗ ମିକି ମାଉସ୍ (ମୂଷା) ଘରେ ପହଞ୍ଚିଲେ। ବୃଦ୍ଧ କପୋତ ପଚାରିଲା, "ତୁମେ ଏହି ଲମ୍ବା ଜାଲଟିକୁ କାଟି ପାରିବ କି?" "ବ୍ୟସ୍ତ ହୁଅ ନାହିଁ, ଏକାଠି ଆମେ ପାରିବା।" ଏହା କହି ସେ ତା'ର ସବୁ ପରିବାର ସଦସ୍ୟଙ୍କୁ ଡାକିଲା ଏବଂ ଜାଲଟିକୁ କାଟିଦେଲା। କପୋତମାନେ ମୁକ୍ତ ହୋଇଗଲେ!

💡 English Meaning: The doves flew directly to the home of their good friend, Micky the mouse. The old dove asked the mouse for help. The mouse assured them not to worry and immediately called his entire family. Working together as a team, the mice quickly bit through the ropes and set the doves completely free.

reached ➡️ ପହଞ୍ଚିଲେ

worry ➡️ ବ୍ୟସ୍ତ ହେବା

members ➡️ ସଦସ୍ୟମାନେ

 

➡️ Stanza 1

📜 Original Stanza:

Together we stand, strong and tall,

Helping each other, we never fall.

Cheering aloud, we shout and sing,

Teamwork can overcome anything.

🗣️ English-Odia (Pronunciation): ଟୁଗେଦର୍ ୱି ଷ୍ଟାଣ୍ଡ, ଷ୍ଟ୍ରଙ୍ଗ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ଟଲ୍, ହେଲ୍ପିଂ ଇଚ୍ ଅଦର୍, ୱି ନେଭର୍ ଫଲ୍। ଚିଅରିଂ ଏଲାଉଡ୍, ୱି ସାଉଟ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ସିଙ୍ଗ୍, ଟିମ୍‌ୱାର୍କ କ୍ୟାନ୍ ଓଭରକମ୍ ଏନିଥିଙ୍ଗ୍।

📝 Odia Meaning: ଆମେ ଏକାଠି ଦୃଢ଼ ଓ ଉଚ୍ଚ (ଶକ୍ତ) ହୋଇ ଛିଡ଼ା ହେଉ, ପରସ୍ପରକୁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରି, ଆମେ କେବେହେଲେ ତଳେ ପଡ଼ୁନାହିଁ (ହାରୁନାହିଁ)। ଜୋରରେ ଉଲ୍ଲାସ କରି ଆମେ ପାଟି କରୁ ଓ ଗୀତ ଗାଉ, ମିଳିତ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ (ଟିମ୍‌ୱାର୍କ) ଯେକୌଣସି ବାଧାକୁ ଅତିକ୍ରମ କରିପାରେ।

💡 English Meaning: When we stand together as a group, we become extremely strong. Because we help and support one another, we never fail. We can happily celebrate and sing because we know that when we work as a team, we can defeat any problem in the world.

🔤 English ➡️ Odia Meaning (Dictation Words - Easy to Hard):

shout ➡️ ପାଟି କରିବା

cheering ➡️ ଆନନ୍ଦ ଉଲ୍ଲାସ କରିବା

teamwork ➡️ ମିଳିତ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ

overcome ➡️ ଅତିକ୍ରମ କରିବା ବା ଜୟ କରିବା

➡️ Stanza 2

📜 Original Stanza:

Win or lose, we always share,

Our bond of trust is always there.

Team spirit fills the air with cheer.

🗣️ English-Odia (Pronunciation): ୱିନ୍ ଅର୍ ଲୁଜ୍, ୱି ଅଲୱେଜ୍ ସେୟାର୍, ଆୱାର୍ ବଣ୍ଡ ଅଫ୍ ଟ୍ରଷ୍ଟ ଇଜ୍ ଅଲୱେଜ୍ ଦେଆର୍। ୱିଥ୍ ଇଚ୍ ହ୍ୟାଣ୍ଡ ଜଏନଡ୍, ଦ ଗୋଲ୍ ଇଜ୍ ନିଅର୍, ଟିମ୍ ସ୍ପିରିଟ୍ ଫିଲ୍ସ ଦ ଏଆର୍ ୱିଥ୍ ଚିଅର୍।

📝 Odia Meaning: ଜିତିଲେ ବା ହାରିଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ ଆମେ ସବୁବେଳେ ଭାଗ କରୁ, ଆମର ବିଶ୍ୱାସର ବନ୍ଧନ ସବୁବେଳେ ରହିଥାଏ। ପରସ୍ପରର ହାତ ମିଳାଇଲେ ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟ ପାଖ ହୋଇଯାଏ, ଦଳଗତ ମନୋଭାବ ବାତାବରଣକୁ ଖୁସିରେ ଭରିଦିଏ।

💡 English Meaning: It does not matter if we win or lose; we share our results happily. Our friendship is built on strong trust. When everyone joins hands and works together, achieving success becomes very easy, and everyone feels motivated and joyful.

➡️ Stanza 3

📜 Original Stanza:

Together we shine, our hearts as one,

For in our team, the joy's begun!

🗣️ English-Odia (Pronunciation): ଟୁଗେଦର୍ ୱି ସାଇନ୍, ଆୱାର୍ ହାର୍ଟସ୍ ଆଜ୍ ୱାନ୍, ଫର୍ ଇନ୍ ଆୱାର୍ ଟିମ୍, ଦ ଜୟସ୍ ବିଗନ୍!

📝 Odia Meaning: ଆମେ ଏକାଠି ଚମକୁ, ଆମ ସମସ୍ତଙ୍କ ହୃଦୟ ଗୋଟିଏ, କାରଣ ଆମ ଦଳରେ, ଆନନ୍ଦ ଆରମ୍ଭ ହୋଇଯାଇଛି!

💡 English Meaning: Working together makes us all successful and bright. Because we share the same feelings and goals in our team, we always experience great happiness and fun.

🔤 English ➡️ Odia Meaning (Dictation Words - Easy to Hard):

joy ➡️ ଆନନ୍ଦ

📝 Summary (English Only)

The chapter "Together We Can" teaches the valuable lesson of unity and teamwork through a short story and an inspiring poem. The story describes a flock of doves looking for food. They spot grains of rice under a banyan tree, but as they fly down and start eating, they suddenly realize they are trapped in a net and notice a hunter hiding nearby. An old, wise dove tells them not to panic and advises them to fly together. Using their combined strength, the doves lift the heavy net into the air and fly away. They reach the house of their friend, Micky Mouse. The mouse gathers his family, and together they bite through the net, setting the doves free.

The story is beautifully complemented by a poem that highlights the importance of teamwork. The poem explains that when people stand together and help each other, they become unbreakable and never fail. Whether they win or lose, they share a deep bond of trust. By joining hands and keeping their hearts united as one, team spirit fills the air with joy, proving that working together can overcome any obstacle.

📝 Summary (Odia)

"ଟୁଗେଦର୍ ୱି କ୍ୟାନ୍" (ଏକାଠି ଆମେ କରିପାରିବା) ଅଧ୍ୟାୟଟି ଏକ ସୁନ୍ଦର କାହାଣୀ ଏବଂ ଏକ ପ୍ରେରଣାଦାୟକ କବିତା ମାଧ୍ୟମରେ ଏକତା ଏବଂ ଦଳଗତ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟର ମୂଲ୍ୟ ଶିଖାଏ। କାହାଣୀଟି କିଛି କପୋତଙ୍କ ବିଷୟରେ, ଯେଉଁମାନେ ଖାଦ୍ୟ ସନ୍ଧାନରେ ଯାଇ ଗୋଟିଏ ବରଗଛ ତଳେ ଥିବା ଚାଉଳ ଦାନା ଖାଇବାକୁ ବସନ୍ତି, କିନ୍ତୁ ଜାଣିବାକୁ ପାଆନ୍ତି ଯେ ସେମାନେ ଜାଲରେ ଫସିଯାଇଛନ୍ତି ଏବଂ ସେଠାରେ ଜଣେ ଶିକାରୀ ଲୁଚିଛି। ସେତେବେଳେ ଜଣେ ବୃଦ୍ଧ କପୋତ ସମସ୍ତଙ୍କୁ ଭୟଭୀତ ନହୋଇ ଏକାଠି ଉଡ଼ିବାକୁ ପରାମର୍ଶ ଦିଅନ୍ତି। ନିଜର ମିଳିତ ଶକ୍ତି ପ୍ରୟୋଗ କରି, କପୋତମାନେ ସେହି ଓଜନିଆ ଜାଲଟିକୁ ଟେକି ଉଡ଼ିଯାଆନ୍ତି। ସେମାନେ ତାଙ୍କ ସାଙ୍ଗ ମିକି ମାଉସ୍ (ମୂଷା) ଘରେ ପହଞ୍ଚନ୍ତି। ମୂଷା ତା'ର ପରିବାରକୁ ଡାକି ଜାଲକୁ କାଟିଦିଏ ଏବଂ କପୋତମାନେ ମୁକ୍ତ ହୋଇଯାଆନ୍ତି।

ଏହି କାହାଣୀ ସହିତ ଥିବା କବିତାଟି ଦଳଗତ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟର ମହତ୍ତ୍ୱ ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କରେ। କବିତାଟି ବୁଝାଏ ଯେ ଯେତେବେଳେ ଲୋକମାନେ ଏକାଠି ଛିଡ଼ା ହୁଅନ୍ତି ଏବଂ ପରସ୍ପରକୁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରନ୍ତି, ସେମାନେ ଅଦମ୍ୟ ହୋଇଯାଆନ୍ତି ଏବଂ କେବେହେଲେ ହାରନ୍ତି ନାହିଁ। ଜିତିବା ବା ହାରିବା ଯାହା ବି ହେଉ, ସେମାନେ ଏକ ଗଭୀର ବିଶ୍ୱାସର ବନ୍ଧନ ବାଣ୍ଟନ୍ତି। ହାତ ମିଳାଇ ଏବଂ ହୃଦୟକୁ ଏକାଠି କରି କାମ କଲେ ଦଳଗତ ମନୋଭାବ ଖୁସି ଆଣିଦିଏ ଏବଂ ଏକାଠି କାମ କରିବା ଦ୍ୱାରା ଯେକୌଣସି ବାଧାକୁ ଅତିକ୍ରମ କରାଯାଇପାରେ ବୋଲି ଏହା ପ୍ରମାଣ କରେ।

🎓 Suggestions for Students

🌟 Always remember the moral of this chapter: "Unity is Strength." If a homework assignment or a task feels too hard, ask your friends to team up and help you!

🌟 Read the poem out loud with a strong, confident voice. The rhyming words like tall/fall and share/there make it sound like a beautiful song.

🌟 Practice the hard dictation words like "Overcome," "Bond," and "Spirit" in your rough notebook.

👨‍🏫 Suggestions for Teachers: How to Teach, How Many Periods

🌟 Total Periods Required: 3 to 4 Periods.

🌟 Period 1 (Storytelling & Activity): Before opening the book, show the students one small stick and break it easily. Then tie a bundle of sticks together and ask a student to try breaking it. Explain that this is what "Together We Can" means. Read the prose story of the doves clearly.

🌟 Period 2 (Poem Recitation): Read the poem stanza by stanza. Encourage the class to recite it together in a chorus to actively practice "teamwork" in the classroom.

🌟 Period 3 & 4 (Comprehension & Spelling): Ask the students questions like: "Who helped the doves cut the net?" and "What happens when we help each other?". Conclude the lesson with a spelling and vocabulary dictation test based on the words provided above.
