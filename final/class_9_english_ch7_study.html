Nine Gold Medals

Nine Gold Medals – Study Material Class 9 ENGLISH

1. About the Poet/Author ✍️

Name: David Roth

Details: David Roth is an American singer, songwriter, and author known for his inspirational acoustic and folk music. He was born in 1954 in Chicago, Illinois. His songs often carry powerful social messages, focusing on themes of harmony, empathy, and the victory of the human spirit.

Family & Personal Life: He currently resides in Cape Cod, Massachusetts. He has spent his life travelling and performing his music to inspire people globally.

Extra Book/Album Names: He has released numerous musical albums and books, including Nights at the Comedy Club, Digging Through My Closet, and More Pearls. His song "Nine Gold Medals" became so famous that it was later included in various inspirational anthologies and school textbooks worldwide.

Theme of the Poem: The poem beautifully highlights human compassion, sportsmanship, and empathy. It teaches us that helping someone in need is far more valuable than winning a competition.

2. Stanza-by-Stanza Explanation 📖

Stanza 1 🌍

Original Snippet: "The athletes had come from so many countries... To run for the gold, for the silver and bronze..."

English Meaning: Athletes from various countries had gathered to participate in the Special Olympics. They had spent weeks and months undergoing rigorous training to win gold, silver, and bronze medals.

Odia Meaning: ବିଭିନ୍ନ ଦେଶରୁ କ୍ରୀଡ଼ାବିତ୍‌ମାନେ ସ୍ୱତନ୍ତ୍ର ଅଲିମ୍ପିକ୍ସରେ ଭାଗ ନେବା ପାଇଁ ଆସିଥିଲେ। ସ୍ୱର୍ଣ୍ଣ, ରୌପ୍ୟ ଏବଂ ବ୍ରୋଞ୍ଜ ପଦକ ଜିତିବା ପାଇଁ ସେମାନେ ସପ୍ତାହ ଓ ମାସ ଧରି କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ ଏବଂ ପ୍ରଶିକ୍ଷଣ ନେଇଥିଲେ।

Stanza 2 🏟️

Original Snippet: "All round the field spectators were gathered... The blocks were all lined up for those who would use them..."

English Meaning: A large crowd of spectators had gathered around the field, cheering for the young athletes. The final event of the day, the hundred-yard dash, was about to begin, and the starting blocks were perfectly lined up for the runners.

Odia Meaning: ପଡ଼ିଆ ଚାରିପଟେ ଦର୍ଶକମାନେ ଏକତ୍ରିତ ହୋଇ ଯୁବ କ୍ରୀଡ଼ାବିତ୍‌ମାନଙ୍କୁ ଉତ୍ସାହିତ କରୁଥିଲେ। ଦିନର ଶେଷ ତଥା ମୁଖ୍ୟ ପ୍ରତିଯୋଗିତା, ଶହେ ୟାର୍ଡ ଦୌଡ଼ ଆରମ୍ଭ ହେବାକୁ ଯାଉଥିଲା ଏବଂ ଦୌଡ଼ାଳିମାନଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ ଆରମ୍ଭ ବ୍ଲକ୍‌ଗୁଡ଼ିକ ଧାଡ଼ିରେ ରଖାଯାଇଥିଲା।

Stanza 3 🔫

Original Snippet: "The sign was given, the pistol exploded... But the youngest among them he stumbled and staggered..."

English Meaning: The starting signal was given, and the pistol was fired. All the runners charged forward. However, the youngest athlete lost his balance, stumbled, and fell down on the hard asphalt track.

Odia Meaning: ଆରମ୍ଭ ସଙ୍କେତ ଦିଆଗଲା ଏବଂ ପିସ୍ତଲ ଫୁଟିଲା। ସମସ୍ତ ଦୌଡ଼ାଳି ଆଗକୁ ଦୌଡ଼ିଲେ। କିନ୍ତୁ ସେମାନଙ୍କ ମଧ୍ୟରେ ଥିବା ସବୁଠାରୁ ସାନ କ୍ରୀଡ଼ାବିତ୍ ଜଣକ ଭାରସାମ୍ୟ ହରାଇ ଝୁଣ୍ଟି ପଡ଼ିଲେ ଏବଂ କଠିନ ପିଚୁ ରାସ୍ତା ଉପରେ ଖସି ପଡ଼ିଲେ।

Stanza 4 😢

Original Snippet: "He gave out a cry in frustration and anguish... But as sure as I'm standing here telling this story..."

English Meaning: The fallen boy cried out in immense pain and deep frustration because all his hard work and dreams of winning were shattered in the dirt. But then, a very strange and unexpected thing happened.

Odia Meaning: ତଳେ ପଡ଼ିଯାଇଥିବା ବାଳକଟି ତୀବ୍ର ଯନ୍ତ୍ରଣା ଏବଂ ହତାଶାରେ ଚିତ୍କାର କଲା, କାରଣ ତା'ର ସମସ୍ତ କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ ଓ ଜିତିବାର ସ୍ୱପ୍ନ ଧୂଳିସାତ୍ ହୋଇଯାଇଥିଲା। କିନ୍ତୁ ତା'ପରେ ଏକ ଆଶ୍ଚର୍ଯ୍ୟଜନକ ଏବଂ ଅପ୍ରତ୍ୟାଶିତ ଘଟଣା ଘଟିଲା।

Stanza 5 🛑

Original Snippet: "The eight other runners pulled up on their heels... And one by one they all turned around and went back..."

English Meaning: The other eight runners, who had trained so hard to win, suddenly stopped in their tracks. One by one, they turned around, went back to the fallen boy, and gently lifted him to his feet.

Odia Meaning: ଅନ୍ୟ ଆଠ ଜଣ ଦୌଡ଼ାଳି, ଯେଉଁମାନେ ଜିତିବା ପାଇଁ ଏତେ କଠିନ ତାଲିମ ନେଇଥିଲେ, ହଠାତ୍ ଅଟକି ଗଲେ। ସେମାନେ ଜଣକ ପରେ ଜଣେ ପଛକୁ ଫେରିଲେ, ତଳେ ପଡ଼ିଥିବା ବାଳକ ପାଖକୁ ଗଲେ ଏବଂ ତାକୁ ସାଦରରେ ଉଠାଇ ଠିଆ କରାଇଲେ।

Stanza 6 🤝

Original Snippet: "Then all the nine runners joined hands and continued... And a banner above that said (Special Olympics)..."

English Meaning: Instead of competing against each other, all nine athletes joined hands and walked towards the finish line together. The hundred-yard dash turned into a walk, making the "Special Olympics" banner above them truly meaningful.

Odia Meaning: ପରସ୍ପର ସହ ପ୍ରତିଯୋଗିତା କରିବା ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତେ, ସମସ୍ତ ନଅ ଜଣ କ୍ରୀଡ଼ାବିତ୍ ହାତ ମିଳାଇଲେ ଏବଂ ଏକାଠି ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟସ୍ଥଳ ଆଡ଼କୁ ଚାଲିଲେ। ଶହେ ୟାର୍ଡର ଦ୍ରୁତ ଦୌଡ଼ ଏକ ଚାଲିବାରେ ପରିଣତ ହେଲା, ଯାହା ଉପରେ ଲାଗିଥିବା "ସ୍ୱତନ୍ତ୍ର ଅଲିମ୍ପିକ୍ସ" ବ୍ୟାନରକୁ ପ୍ରକୃତରେ ସାର୍ଥକ କଲା।

Stanza 7 🏆

Original Snippet: "That's how the race ended, with nine gold medals... And nine smiling faces..."

English Meaning: The race ended with all nine athletes crossing the finish line together holding hands. They were all awarded gold medals. Their bright, smiling faces communicated a beautiful message of empathy that words could never describe.

Odia Meaning: ସମସ୍ତ ନଅ ଜଣ କ୍ରୀଡ଼ାବିତ୍ ହାତ ଧରାଧରି ହୋଇ ଏକାଠି ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟସ୍ଥଳ ଅତିକ୍ରମ କରିବା ସହ ଦୌଡ଼ ଶେଷ ହେଲା। ସମସ୍ତଙ୍କୁ ସ୍ୱର୍ଣ୍ଣ ପଦକ ପ୍ରଦାନ କରାଗଲା। ସେମାନଙ୍କର ଉଜ୍ଜ୍ୱଳ ଏବଂ ହସ ହସ ମୁହଁ ସହାନୁଭୂତିର ଏପରି ଏକ ସୁନ୍ଦର ବାର୍ତ୍ତା ଦେଉଥିଲା ଯାହା ଶବ୍ଦରେ ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କରାଯାଇପାରିବ ନାହିଁ।

4. Summary (English only) 📜

The poem "Nine Gold Medals" by David Roth tells a deeply moving story about the Special Olympics, a sports event for differently-abled athletes. Athletes from various countries had gathered after months of rigorous training, dreaming of winning gold, silver, or bronze medals. The stadium was filled with spectators cheering for the participants as the final event of the day, the hundred-yard dash, was about to begin. The athletes took their positions at the starting blocks, fully resolved to win.

As the pistol was fired to signal the start of the race, all the runners surged forward. However, tragedy struck immediately when the youngest athlete lost his balance, stumbled, and fell hard onto the asphalt track. Devastated, he cried out in pain and deep frustration because his dreams and hard work were shattered in an instant.

What happened next was extraordinarily beautiful. Instead of continuing the race to win individual medals, the other eight athletes stopped in their tracks. Ignoring their own desire to win, they turned back, went to the fallen boy, and gently helped him to his feet. Joining hands, all nine athletes walked together towards the finish line. The competitive race turned into a peaceful walk of solidarity. The event concluded with all nine athletes being awarded a gold medal for their outstanding display of empathy, compassion, and true sportsmanship, leaving everyone with a message far more powerful than words.

5. Summary (Odia) 📖

ଡେଭିଡ୍ ରୋଥ୍‌ଙ୍କ ଦ୍ୱାରା ରଚିତ "ନାଇନ୍ ଗୋଲ୍ଡ ମେଡାଲ୍ସ" (ନଅଟି ସ୍ୱର୍ଣ୍ଣ ପଦକ) କବିତାଟି ଭିନ୍ନକ୍ଷମ କ୍ରୀଡ଼ାବିତ୍‌ମାନଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ ଉଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ 'ସ୍ୱତନ୍ତ୍ର ଅଲିମ୍ପିକ୍ସ' ବିଷୟରେ ଏକ ହୃଦୟସ୍ପର୍ଶୀ କାହାଣୀ ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କରେ। ସ୍ୱର୍ଣ୍ଣ, ରୌପ୍ୟ ଏବଂ ବ୍ରୋଞ୍ଜ ପଦକ ଜିତିବାର ସ୍ୱପ୍ନ ନେଇ ମାସ ମାସ ଧରି କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ କରିବା ପରେ ବିଭିନ୍ନ ଦେଶରୁ କ୍ରୀଡ଼ାବିତ୍‌ମାନେ ଏକତ୍ରିତ ହୋଇଥିଲେ। ଦିନର ଅନ୍ତିମ ପ୍ରତିଯୋଗିତା, ଶହେ ୟାର୍ଡ ଦୌଡ଼ ଆରମ୍ଭ ହେବାକୁ ଯାଉଥିବା ବେଳେ ଷ୍ଟାଡିୟମ୍ ଦର୍ଶକମାନଙ୍କ ଉତ୍ସାହରେ ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ଥିଲା। କ୍ରୀଡ଼ାବିତ୍‌ମାନେ ଜିତିବାର ଦୃଢ଼ ନିଶ୍ଚୟ ସହ ନିଜ ନିଜ ସ୍ଥାନରେ ପ୍ରସ୍ତୁତ ହୋଇ ରହିଥିଲେ।

ଦୌଡ଼ ଆରମ୍ଭର ସଙ୍କେତ ସ୍ୱରୂପ ପିସ୍ତଲ ଫୁଟିବା ମାତ୍ରେ ସମସ୍ତ ଦୌଡ଼ାଳି ଆଗକୁ ମାଡ଼ି ଗଲେ। କିନ୍ତୁ ହଠାତ୍ ଏକ ଦୁଃଖଦ ଘଟଣା ଘଟିଲା। ସେମାନଙ୍କ ମଧ୍ୟରେ ଥିବା ସବୁଠାରୁ ସାନ କ୍ରୀଡ଼ାବିତ୍ ଜଣକ ଭାରସାମ୍ୟ ହରାଇ ଝୁଣ୍ଟି ପଡ଼ିଲେ ଏବଂ କଠିନ ପିଚୁ ରାସ୍ତା ଉପରେ ଖସି ପଡ଼ିଲେ। ମୁହୂର୍ତ୍ତକ ମଧ୍ୟରେ ତାଙ୍କର ସମସ୍ତ ସ୍ୱପ୍ନ ଓ କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ ଧୂଳିସାତ୍ ହୋଇଯିବାରୁ ସେ ହତାଶା ଏବଂ ଯନ୍ତ୍ରଣାରେ ଚିତ୍କାର କଲେ।

ଏହା ପରେ ଯାହା ଘଟିଲା ତାହା ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ଆଶ୍ଚର୍ଯ୍ୟଜନକ ଏବଂ ସୁନ୍ଦର ଥିଲା। ନିଜର ପଦକ ଜିତିବା ଆଶାକୁ ପଛରେ ପକାଇ ଅନ୍ୟ ଆଠ ଜଣ କ୍ରୀଡ଼ାବିତ୍ ଦୌଡ଼ିବା ବନ୍ଦ କରିଦେଲେ। ସେମାନେ ପଛକୁ ଫେରିଲେ, ତଳେ ପଡ଼ିଥିବା ବାଳକ ପାଖକୁ ଯାଇ ତାଙ୍କୁ ଉଠାଇଲେ। ତା'ପରେ ସମସ୍ତ ନଅ ଜଣ କ୍ରୀଡ଼ାବିତ୍ ହାତ ମିଳାଇ ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟସ୍ଥଳ ଆଡ଼କୁ ଏକାଠି ଚାଲିଲେ। ପ୍ରତିଯୋଗିତାମୂଳକ ଦୌଡ଼ ଏକ ସହାନୁଭୂତିର ଚାଲିବାରେ ପରିଣତ ହେଲା। ପ୍ରକୃତ ଖେଳୁଆଡ଼ ମନୋଭାବ ଏବଂ ଦୟାର ଅଦ୍ଭୁତ ପ୍ରଦର୍ଶନ ପାଇଁ ସମସ୍ତ ନଅ ଜଣ କ୍ରୀଡ଼ାବିତ୍‌ଙ୍କୁ ସ୍ୱର୍ଣ୍ଣ ପଦକ ପ୍ରଦାନ କରାଗଲା, ଯାହା ଶବ୍ଦଠାରୁ ଅଧିକ ଶକ୍ତିଶାଳୀ ଏକ ମାନବିକତାର ବାର୍ତ୍ତା ଦେଇଥିଲା।

6. Suggestions for teachers how to teach 🧑‍🏫

  1. Focus on Core Values: Begin the class by asking students about a time they helped someone instead of focusing on their own success. Shift the focus from "winning at all costs" to the importance of empathy, teamwork, and human compassion.

  2. Role-Play the Event: Organize a short physical activity outside the classroom where students line up for a race. Secretly instruct one student to pretend to fall, and ask the others how they would instinctively react. This practical example will help them feel the emotional weight of the poem.

  3. Discuss the "Special Olympics": Educate the students briefly about what the Special Olympics is. Explaining that these athletes overcome immense physical and mental hurdles will make the sacrifice of the other eight runners even more profound for the students to understand.
