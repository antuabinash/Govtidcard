Adverb Clauses – Book Q A Class 10 English Grammar

✍️ Activity 1

Instructions (English): Complete the following sentences with adverb clauses starting with the word(s) given in brackets.

Instructions (Odia): ବନ୍ଧନୀରେ ଦିଆଯାଇଥିବା ଶବ୍ଦ(ଗୁଡ଼ିକ)ରେ ଆରମ୍ଭ ହେଉଥିବା adverb clause ସହିତ ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

(Note: These answers are examples, as the activity is open-ended.)

(ସୂଚନା: ଏହି ଉତ୍ତରଗୁଡ଼ିକ ଉଦାହରଣ ଅଟେ, କାରଣ ଏହି କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକଳାପଟି ମୁକ୍ତ।)

  1. We reached home _____ (when)

    Answer: We reached home when the sun set.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଆମେ ଘରେ ପହଞ୍ଚିଲୁ ଯେତେବେଳେ ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟ ଅସ୍ତ ହେଲେ।)

  2. Ramesh ran _____ (so fast that)

    Answer: Ramesh ran so fast that no one could catch him.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ରମେଶ ଏତେ ଜୋରରେ ଦୌଡ଼ିଲା ଯେ ତାକୁ କେହି ଧରିପାରିଲେ ନାହିଁ।)

  3. You wouldn't succeed _____ (if)

    Answer: You wouldn't succeed if you don't work hard.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ତୁମେ ସଫଳ ହେବ ନାହିଁ ଯଦି ତୁମେ କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ ନ କର।)

  4. The sum is not as difficult _____ (as)

    Answer: The sum is not as difficult as it looks.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଅଙ୍କଟି ସେତେ କଷ୍ଟ ନୁହେଁ ଯେତିକି ଏହା ଜଣାପଡୁଛି।)

  5. The thief behaved _____ (as if)

    Answer: The thief behaved as if he were the owner of the house.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଚୋରଟି ଏପରି ବ୍ୟବହାର କଲା ସତେ ଯେପରି ସେ ଘରର ମାଲିକ।)

  6. You should rest _____ (because)

    Answer: You should rest because you are tired.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ତୁମେ ବିଶ୍ରାମ ନେବା ଉଚିତ୍ କାରଣ ତୁମେ କ୍ଳାନ୍ତ ଅଛ।)

  7. My friends will come _____ (even if)

    Answer: My friends will come even if it rains.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ମୋ ସାଙ୍ଗମାନେ ଆସିବେ ଯଦିଓ ବର୍ଷା ହୁଏ।)

  8. He speaks better _____ (than)

    Answer: He speaks better than his brother does.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସେ ତା' ଭାଇ ଅପେକ୍ଷା ଭଲ କହନ୍ତି।)

  9. The thief hid the jewellery _____ (where)

    Answer: The thief hid the jewellery where no one could find it.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଚୋରଟି ଅଳଙ୍କାରଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ଲୁଚାଇ ଦେଲା ଯେଉଁଠାରେ କେହି ଏହାକୁ ପାଇବେ ନାହିଁ।)

  10. The man is absolutely honest _____ (even though)

    Answer: The man is absolutely honest even though he is very poor.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଲୋକଟି ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ସଚ୍ଚୋଟ ଯଦିଓ ସେ ବହୁତ ଗରିବ।)

Instructions (Odia): Adverb clause ଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ଚିହ୍ନଟ କରନ୍ତୁ ଏବଂ ସେଗୁଡ଼ିକ କେଉଁ ପ୍ରକାରର ତାହା ଦର୍ଶାନ୍ତୁ।

✍️ Activity 3

Instructions (English): Match the adverb clauses with the corresponding main clauses. Then rewrite the sentences starting with the main clauses.

Instructions (Odia): Adverb clause ଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ସେମାନଙ୍କର ମୁଖ୍ୟ ଉପବାକ୍ୟ ସହିତ ମେଳ କରନ୍ତୁ। ତା'ପରେ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ମୁଖ୍ୟ ଉପବାକ୍ୟରୁ ଆରମ୍ଭ କରି ପୁନର୍ଲିଖନ କରନ୍ତୁ।

  1. It floats if you pour oil on water.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଏହା ଭାସେ ଯଦି ତୁମେ ପାଣି ଉପରେ ତେଲ ଢାଳ।)

  2. It expands if you heat iron.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଏହା ପ୍ରସାରିତ ହୁଏ ଯଦି ତୁମେ ଲୁହାକୁ ଗରମ କର।)

  3. It becomes dark if the sun sets.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଅନ୍ଧାର ହୁଏ ଯଦି ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟ ଅସ୍ତ ହୁଅନ୍ତି।)

  4. I shall not go out if it rains today.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ମୁଁ ଆଜି ବାହାରକୁ ଯିବି ନାହିଁ ଯଦି ବର୍ଷା ହୁଏ।)

  5. You can understand if you read the lesson.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ତୁମେ ବୁଝିପାରିବ ଯଦି ତୁମେ ପାଠଟି ପଢ।)

  6. We can reach there in time if we start now.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଆମେ ସେଠାରେ ସମୟինୁସାରେ ପହଞ୍ଚିପାରିବା ଯଦି ଆମେ ଏବେ ଆରମ୍ଭ କରୁ।)

  7. I would fly in the sky if I had wings.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ମୁଁ ଆକାଶରେ ଉଡନ୍ତି ଯଦି ମୋର ଡେଣା ଥାଆନ୍ତା।)

  8. I would take you to her place if I knew her address.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ମୁଁ ତୁମକୁ ତାଙ୍କ ସ୍ଥାନକୁ ନେଇଯାଆନ୍ତି ଯଦି ମୁଁ ତାଙ୍କ ଠିକଣା ଜାଣିଥାନ୍ତି।)

  9. I could have arrived there on time if you had not reminded me.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ମୁଁ ସେଠାରେ ଠିକ୍ ସମୟରେ ପହଞ୍ଚି ପାରିଥା'ନ୍ତି ଯଦି ତୁମେ ମୋତେ ମନେ ପକାଇ ନଥା'ନ୍ତ।)

  10. You would have got a first class if you had studied more sincerely.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ତୁମେ ପ୍ରଥମ ଶ୍ରେଣୀ ପାଇଥା'ନ୍ତ ଯଦି ତୁମେ ଅଧିକ ଆନ୍ତରିକତାର ସହ ପଢିଥା'ନ୍ତ।)


✍️ Activity 4

Instructions (English): Complete the following sentences using main clauses.

Instructions (Odia): ମୁଖ୍ୟ ଉପବାକ୍ୟ (Main Clause) ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

(Note: These answers are examples, as the activity is open-ended.)

(ସୂଚନା: ଏହି ଉତ୍ତରଗୁଡ଼ିକ ଉଦାହରଣ ଅଟେ, କାରଣ ଏହି କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକଳାପଟି ମୁକ୍ତ।)

  1. If you meet him, _____

    Answer: If you meet him, please give him this book.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଯଦି ତୁମେ ତାଙ୍କୁ ଭେଟ, ଦୟାକରି ତାଙ୍କୁ ଏହି ବହିଟି ଦେବ।)

  2. Had I the wings of a dove, _____

    Answer: Had I the wings of a dove, I would fly away.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଯଦି ମୋର କପୋତର ଡେଣା ଥାଆନ୍ତା, ମୁଁ ଉଡିଯାଆନ୍ତି।)

  3. If India had won this match, _____

    Answer: If India had won this match, they would have qualified for the finals.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଯଦି ଭାରତ ଏହି ମ୍ୟାଚ୍ ଜିତିଥାନ୍ତା, ତେବେ ସେମାନେ ଫାଇନାଲ୍ ପାଇଁ ଯୋଗ୍ୟତା ଅର୍ଜନ କରିଥାନ୍ତେ।)

  4. If you are not watching TV, _____

    Answer: If you are not watching TV, please turn it off.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଯଦି ତୁମେ ଟିଭି ଦେଖୁନାହଁ, ଦୟାକରି ଏହାକୁ ବନ୍ଦ କରିଦିଅ।)

  5. If you had driven more carefully, _____

    Answer: If you had driven more carefully, the accident would not have happened.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଯଦି ତୁମେ ଅଧିକ ସାବଧାନତାର ସହ ଗାଡି ଚଳାଇଥା'ନ୍ତ, ତେବେ ଦୁର୍ଘଟଣା ଘଟି ନଥା'ନ୍ତା।)

  6. If Mahatma Gandhi were alive today, _____

    Answer: If Mahatma Gandhi were alive today, he would be very sad.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଯଦି ମହାତ୍ମା ଗାନ୍ଧୀ ଆଜି ଜୀବିତ ଥାଆନ୍ତେ, ତେବେ ସେ ବହୁତ ଦୁଃଖିତ ହୋଇଥାନ୍ତେ।)

  7. If you boil water upto 100°C, _____

    Answer: If you boil water upto 100°C, it evaporates.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଯଦି ତୁମେ ପାଣିକୁ ୧୦୦°C ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ ଫୁଟାଅ, ଏହା ବାଷ୍ପୀଭୂତ ହୁଏ।)

  8. If my friend comes in time, _____

    Answer: If my friend comes in time, we will go to the market.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଯଦି ମୋ ସାଙ୍ଗ ଠିକ୍ ସମୟରେ ଆସେ, ଆମେ ବଜାର ଯିବୁ।)

  9. If I were you, _____

    Answer: If I were you, I would not do that.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ମୁଁ ଯଦି ତୁମେ ହୋଇଥାନ୍ତି, ମୁଁ ତାହା କରି ନଥା'ନ୍ତି।)

  10. If Germany had won the World War-II, _____

    Answer: If Germany had won the World War-II, the world would be a different place.

    (ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଯଦି ଜର୍ମାନୀ ଦ୍ୱିତୀୟ ବିଶ୍ୱଯୁଦ୍ଧ ଜିତିଥାନ୍ତା, ତେବେ ପୃଥିବୀ ଏକ ଭିନ୍ନ ସ୍ଥାନ ହୋଇଥାନ୍ତା।)

✍️ Activity 7

Instructions (English): Combine the following pairs of sentences first into complex sentences and then into compound ones.

Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟ ଯୋଡିଗୁଡିକୁ ପ୍ରଥମେ ଜଟିଳ ବାକ୍ୟରେ ଏବଂ ତା'ପରେ ଯୌଗିକ ବାକ୍ୟରେ ମିଶ୍ରଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

  1. Pair: Call on me tomorrow. I will give you the money then.

    • Complex: If you call on me tomorrow, I will give you the money.

    • Compound: You call on me tomorrow and I will give you the money.

  2. Pair: I can't go. I have no ticket.

    • Complex: I can't go because I have no ticket.

    • Compound: I have no ticket, so I can't go.

  3. Pair: The days are unlucky. We mustn't give up hope.

    • Complex: Although the days are unlucky, we mustn't give up hope.

    • Compound: The days are unlucky, yet we mustn't give up hope.

  4. Pair: The day was very cold. I had to take a woolen sweater.

    • Complex: As the day was very cold, I had to take a woolen sweater.

    • Compound: The day was very cold, so I had to take a woolen sweater.

  5. Pair: I broke a tooth. I had to go to see the dentist.

    • Complex: I had to go to see the dentist because I broke a tooth.

    • Compound: I broke a tooth, so I had to go to see the dentist.


✍️ Activity 8

Instructions (English): Find out the subordinate clauses in the following passage and say what type of clauses they are. Mention why you think so.

Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦରେ ଥିବା ଅଧୀନ ଉପବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ (subordinate clauses) ଖୋଜନ୍ତୁ ଏବଂ ସେଗୁଡ଼ିକ କେଉଁ ପ୍ରକାରର ତାହା କୁହନ୍ତୁ। ଆପଣ କାହିଁକି ସେପରି ଭାବୁଛନ୍ତି ତାହା ଉଲ୍ଲେଖ କରନ୍ତୁ।

Passage: Tuni asks Tuna, "Please go to the shop for me and buy a packet of milk and, if they have eggs, get six." A short time later, Tuna comes back with six packets of milk. Tuni asks him," Why did you buy so much milk?" He replies," They have eggs."

Answer:

  1. Clause (ଉପବାକ୍ୟ): if they have eggs (ଯଦି ସେମାନଙ୍କ ପାଖରେ ଅଣ୍ଡା ଅଛି)

    • Type (ପ୍ରକାର): Adverb Clause of Condition (ସର୍ତ୍ତମୂଳକ କ୍ରିୟା-ବିଶେଷଣ ଉପବାକ୍ୟ)

    • Reason (କାରଣ): This clause sets a condition (the availability of eggs) for the main imperative action ("get six").

  2. Clause (ଉପବାକ୍ୟ): Why did you buy so much milk (ତୁମେ କାହିଁକି ଏତେ କ୍ଷୀର କିଣିଲ)

    • Type (ପ୍ରକାର): Noun Clause (ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ ଉପବାକ୍ୟ)

    • Reason (କାରଣ): This clause (a direct question) functions as the direct object of the main verb "asks". (Tuni asks him what? -> "Why did you buy...")
