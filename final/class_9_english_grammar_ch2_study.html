Verbs

Verbs – Study Material Class 9 English Grammar

Step 1: Definition of Verb (କ୍ରିୟାର ସଂଜ୍ଞା)

A Verb is a word that shows action, state, feeling, or occurrence in a sentence.

Verb ହେଉଛି ସେହି ଶବ୍ଦ ଯାହା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ, ଅବସ୍ଥା, ଅନୁଭବ ବା ଘଟଣାକୁ ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

A sentence cannot be complete without a verb.

କୌଣସି ବାକ୍ୟ କ୍ରିୟା ବିନା ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ହୁଏନାହିଁ।

Examples

Sentence Verb Odia Meaning
Ram runs runs ରାମ ଦୌଡେ
She sings sings ସେ ଗାଏ
Birds fly fly ପକ୍ଷୀ ଉଡନ୍ତି
The sun rises rises ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟ ଉଦୟ ହୁଏ

😊 Easy idea
If you want to know the verb, ask the question:
"What is happening?" or "What is the subject doing?"

Step 2: Formula of Verb in a Sentence (ବାକ୍ୟରେ କ୍ରିୟାର ଗଠନ)

Sentences follow certain patterns where verbs play an important role.

Sentence Formula

Formula Example Meaning
Subject + Verb Birds fly ପକ୍ଷୀ ଉଡନ୍ତି
Subject + Verb + Object Ram plays cricket ରାମ କ୍ରିକେଟ୍ ଖେଳେ
Subject + Verb + Complement She is happy ସେ ଖୁସି ଅଛି
Subject + Auxiliary Verb + Main Verb She is reading ସେ ପଢୁଛି

📘 Short Trick
S + V = Complete basic sentence

Step 3: Types of Verbs (କ୍ରିୟାର ପ୍ରକାର)

1️⃣ Action Verb (କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ କ୍ରିୟା)

Action verbs show physical action.

Action verbs କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ ବା କାମକୁ ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

Examples

Verb Sentence Meaning
run The boy runs fast ଛୁଆ ଦୌଡୁଛି
play Children play football ଛୁଆମାନେ ଖେଳୁଛନ୍ତି
write She writes a letter ସେ ଚିଠି ଲେଖୁଛି
read He reads a book ସେ ପୁସ୍ତକ ପଢୁଛି

💡 Trick
Action verbs answer the question
What does the subject do?

2️⃣ Mental Verb (ମନସିକ କ୍ରିୟା)

Mental verbs show thinking, knowing or feeling.

ଏହି କ୍ରିୟାମାନେ ମନର କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକୁ ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

Examples

Verb Sentence Meaning
think I think it is right ମୁଁ ଭାବୁଛି
know I know the answer ମୁଁ ଉତ୍ତର ଜାଣିଛି
remember I remember the story ମୁଁ କାହାଣୀ ମନେ ରଖିଛି
believe She believes him ସେ ବିଶ୍ୱାସ କରେ

🧠 Trick
Mental verbs are mind verbs.

3️⃣ Communicative Verb (ସଂଯୋଗ କ୍ରିୟା)

Communicative verbs express communication or speaking.

ଏହି କ୍ରିୟାମାନେ କଥାବାର୍ତ୍ତା ବା ସଂଯୋଗକୁ ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

Examples

Verb Sentence Meaning
say He said the truth ସେ ସତ୍ୟ କହିଲା
ask The teacher asked a question ଶିକ୍ଷକ ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ କଲେ
tell She told a story ସେ କାହାଣୀ କହିଲା
speak He speaks English ସେ ଇଂରାଜୀ କହେ

🗣 Trick
Communicative verbs = Speaking verbs

4️⃣ Verbs of Aspect (ଅବସ୍ଥା ସୂଚକ କ୍ରିୟା)

These verbs show the start, continuation or end of an action.

ଏହି କ୍ରିୟାମାନେ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ ଆରମ୍ଭ ବା ଶେଷକୁ ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

Examples

Verb Sentence Meaning
start She started reading ସେ ପଢିବା ଆରମ୍ଭ କଲା
begin The class begins now ଶ୍ରେଣୀ ଆରମ୍ଭ ହେଉଛି
stop He stopped talking ସେ କଥା ବନ୍ଦ କଲା
continue She continues her work ସେ କାମ ଚାଲୁ ରଖିଛି

Step 4: Main Verb and Auxiliary Verb

(ମୂଳ କ୍ରିୟା ଓ ସହାୟକ କ୍ରିୟା)

Main Verb (ମୂଳ କ୍ରିୟା)

The main verb shows the main action.

ମୂଳ କ୍ରିୟା ବାକ୍ୟର ମୁଖ୍ୟ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକୁ ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

Example

Sentence Main Verb
She reads a book reads
Birds fly fly
The boy runs runs

Auxiliary Verb (ସହାୟକ କ୍ରିୟା)

Auxiliary verbs help the main verb.

ସହାୟକ କ୍ରିୟା ମୂଳ କ୍ରିୟାକୁ ସହାୟତା କରେ।

Examples

Sentence Auxiliary Verb Main Verb
She is reading is reading
They are playing are playing
He has finished work has finished

💡 Trick to remember Auxiliary Verbs

Be verbs
am, is, are, was, were

Have verbs
has, have, had

Do verbs
do, does, did

Easy memory trick

👉 BHD Rule
Be – Have – Do

Step 5: Forms of Verbs (କ୍ରିୟାର ରୂପ)

Most verbs have three forms.

Base Form Past Form Past Participle Meaning
go went gone ଯିବା
eat ate eaten ଖାଇବା
write wrote written ଲେଖିବା
sing sang sung ଗାଇବା

💡 Trick

Remember the formula

V1 → V2 → V3

Base → Past → Past Participle

Step 6: Verb Agreement (Subject–Verb Agreement)

The verb must match the subject.

କ୍ରିୟା କର୍ତ୍ତା ସହିତ ମେଳ ଖାଇବା ଆବଶ୍ୟକ।

Table

Subject Verb
I am
We are
You are
They are
He is
She is
It is

Example Sentences

I am a student.
We are playing football.
He is reading a book.

Step 7: Important Grammar Formula

Present Continuous Formula

Formula Example
Subject + am/is/are + Verb + ing She is reading

Present Perfect Formula

Formula Example
Subject + has/have + V3 She has finished

Simple Past Formula

Formula Example
Subject + V2 She wrote a letter

Verb shows action, state or occurrence.

Verb is necessary for a sentence.

Main verb shows the main action.

Auxiliary verbs help the main verbs.

Three forms of verbs are used in grammar.
