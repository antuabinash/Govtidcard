School's Goodbye

School's Goodbye – Study Material Class 10 ENGLISH

👨‍🏫 About the Author: Lord Eustace Percy

1. Original Text & Pronunciation

The essay "School's Goodbye" conveys a message to school children by Lord Eustace Percy, a former Minister of Education in Britain. You will benefit from reading it as it tells students how to become good human beings and citizens in future.

WithTeachers.in

2. Word Meanings (English → Odia)

WithTeachers.in

Conveys: ପ୍ରଦାନ କରେ କିମ୍ବା ଜଣାଏ

Minister of Education: ଶିକ୍ଷା ମନ୍ତ୍ରୀ

Human beings: ମଣିଷ

3. Odia Meaning (Translation / Explanation)

ଏହି ପ୍ରବନ୍ଧ "ସ୍କୁଲ୍ସ ଗୁଡ୍‌ବାଏ" ବ୍ରିଟେନର ପୂର୍ବତନ ଶିକ୍ଷା ମନ୍ତ୍ରୀ ଲର୍ଡ ୟୁଷ୍ଟେସ୍ ପର୍ସିଙ୍କ ଦ୍ୱାରା ସ୍କୁଲ୍ ପିଲାମାନଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ ଏକ ବାର୍ତ୍ତା ପ୍ରଦାନ କରେ। ଏହାକୁ ପଢ଼ିବା ଦ୍ୱାରା ତୁମେମାନେ ଲାଭବାନ୍ ହେବ କାରଣ ଏହା ଛାତ୍ରଛାତ୍ରୀମାନଙ୍କୁ ଭବିଷ୍ୟତରେ କିପରି ଭଲ ମଣିଷ ଏବଂ ସୁନାଗରିକ ହେବେ ସେ ବିଷୟରେ ଶିକ୍ଷା ଦିଏ। 

🏫 Paragraph 1: Best Wishes for the Future

1. Original Text & Pronunciation

You are now about to leave school and before you go we desire to send you our best wishes for your future welfare.

English-Odia Pronunciation:

ୟୁ ଆର୍ ନାଓ ଏବାଉଟ୍ ଟୁ ଲିଭ୍ ସ୍କୁଲ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ବିଫୋର୍ ୟୁ ଗୋ ଉଇ ଡିଜାୟାର୍ ଟୁ ସେଣ୍ଡ୍ ୟୁ ଆୱାର୍ ବେଷ୍ଟ ଉଇସେସ୍ ଫର୍ ୟୋର୍ ଫ୍ୟୁଚର୍ ୱେଲଫେୟାର୍।

2. Word Meanings (English → Odia)

About to: ପ୍ରାୟତଃ କିମ୍ବା ଯାଉଛ,

Leave: ଛାଡିବା,

Desire: ଇଚ୍ଛା କରିବା,

Best wishes: ଶୁଭକାମନା,

Future welfare: ଭବିଷ୍ୟତର ମଙ୍ଗଳ ବା କଲ୍ୟାଣ। 

3. Odia Meaning (Translation / Explanation)

ତୁମେମାନେ ଏବେ ସ୍କୁଲ୍ ଛାଡିବାକୁ ଯାଉଛ ଏବଂ ତୁମେ ଯିବା ପୂର୍ବରୁ, ଆମେ ତୁମର ଭବିଷ୍ୟତର ମଙ୍ଗଳ ପାଇଁ ଆମର ଶ୍ରେଷ୍ଠ ଶୁଭକାମନା ଜଣାଇବାକୁ ଇଚ୍ଛା କରୁଛୁ। 

4. English Meaning (Elaborate in Simple Language)

The author warmly addresses the students who are currently in their final year and are getting ready to graduate and leave their school. Before these students depart for the outside world, the school and the teachers want to express their deep desire to wish the students great success, health, and happiness for their upcoming future.

📚 Paragraph 2: Education Never Ends

1. Original Text & Pronunciation

Although you are parting from school in which you have spent so many years, we hope you will not forget it and think that your education is finished. In whatever trade or profession you desire to follow, you will soon find that, if you are ambitious to succeed in it, you must continue your education.

English-Odia Pronunciation:

ଅଲଦୋ ୟୁ ଆର୍ ପାର୍ଟିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଫ୍ରମ୍ ସ୍କୁଲ୍ ଇନ୍ ହୁଇଚ୍ ୟୁ ହ୍ୟାଭ୍ ସ୍ପେଣ୍ଟ୍ ସୋ ମେନି ଇୟର୍ସ, ଉଇ ହୋପ୍ ୟୁ ଉଇଲ୍ ନଟ୍ ଫରଗେଟ୍ ଇଟ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ୍ ଥିଙ୍କ୍ ଦ୍ୟାଟ୍ ୟୋର୍ ଏଜୁକେସନ୍ ଇଜ୍ ଫିନିସ୍‌ଡ୍। ଇନ୍ ହ୍ୱାଟଏଭର୍ ଟ୍ରେଡ୍ ଅର୍ ପ୍ରୋଫେସନ୍ ୟୁ ଡିଜାୟାର୍ ଟୁ ଫଲୋ, ୟୁ ଉଇଲ୍ ସୁନ୍ ଫାଇଣ୍ଡ୍ ଦ୍ୟାଟ୍, ଇଫ୍ ୟୁ ଆର୍ ଆମ୍ବିସିଅସ୍ ଟୁ ସକ୍ସିଡ୍ ଇନ୍ ଇଟ୍, ୟୁ ମଷ୍ଟ କଣ୍ଟିନ୍ୟୁ ୟୋର୍ ଏଜୁକେସନ୍।

WithTeachers.in

2. Word Meanings (English → Odia)

Although: ଯଦିଓ,

Parting from: ଅଲଗା ହେଉଛ ବା ଛାଡି ଯାଉଛ,

Spent: ଅତିବାହିତ କରିଛ,

Forget: ଭୁଲିଯିବା,

Finished: ଶେଷ ହୋଇଛି,

Trade: ବ୍ୟବସାୟ ବା ବୃତ୍ତି,

 Profession: ପେଶା ବା ଜୀବିକା,

Ambitious: ଉଚ୍ଚାଭିଳାଷୀ,

Succeed: ସଫଳ ହେବା,

Continue: ଜାରି ରଖିବା।

3. Odia Meaning (Translation / Explanation)

ଯଦିଓ ତୁମେମାନେ ସେହି ସ୍କୁଲଠାରୁ ଅଲଗା ହେଉଛ ଯେଉଁଠାରେ ତୁମେ ଏତେ ବର୍ଷ ବିତାଇଛ, ଆମେ ଆଶା କରୁଛୁ ଯେ ତୁମେମାନେ ଏହାକୁ ଭୁଲିବ ନାହିଁ ଏବଂ ଭାବିବ ନାହିଁ ଯେ ତୁମର ଶିକ୍ଷା ସମାପ୍ତ ହୋଇଯାଇଛି। ତୁମେ ଯେକୌଣସି ବ୍ୟବସାୟ କିମ୍ବା ପେଶା ଆପଣାଇବାକୁ ଇଚ୍ଛା କରୁଛ, ତୁମେ ଖୁବ୍ ଶୀଘ୍ର ଜାଣିପାରିବ ଯେ, ଯଦି ତୁମେ ସେଥିରେ ସଫଳ ହେବା ପାଇଁ ଉଚ୍ଚାଭିଳାଷୀ ଅଟ, ତେବେ ତୁମକୁ ତୁମର ଶିକ୍ଷା ନିଶ୍ଚିତ ଭାବରେ ଜାରି ରଖିବାକୁ ପଡିବ। 

4. English Meaning (Elaborate in Simple Language)

Even though the students are finally saying goodbye and leaving the familiar school where they have grown up and spent many long years, the author sincerely hopes they will always remember their beloved school. More importantly, he strongly warns them not to make the mistake of thinking that their learning strictly stops just because they are leaving school. No matter what kind of business, job, or career they choose to pursue in the future, they will quickly realize a major truth: if they truly want to be highly successful and reach the very top of their chosen field, they will have to continuously keep learning and educating themselves throughout their entire lives. 

🏆 Paragraph 3: The Secret to Success

1. Original Text & Pronunciation

Success in life is not easily secured; it only comes to those who work hard and continue learning.

English-Odia Pronunciation:

ସକ୍ସେସ୍ ଇନ୍ ଲାଇଫ୍ ଇଜ୍ ନଟ୍ ଇଜିଲି ସିକ୍ୟୋର୍ଡ୍; ଇଟ୍ ଓନଲି କମ୍ସ ଟୁ ଦୋଜ୍ ହୁ ୱାର୍କ୍ ହାର୍ଡ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ୍ କଣ୍ଟିନ୍ୟୁ ଲର୍ଣ୍ଣିଙ୍ଗ୍।

2. Word Meanings (English → Odia)

Success: ସଫଳତା,

Easily: ସହଜରେ,

Secured: ହାସଲ କରାଯାଏ,

Work hard: କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ କରିବା,

Continue learning: ଶିଖିବା ଜାରି ରଖିବା। 

3. Odia Meaning (Translation / Explanation)

ଜୀବନରେ ସଫଳତା ସହଜରେ ହାସଲ କରାଯାଇପାରିବ ନାହିଁ; ଏହା କେବଳ ସେହିମାନଙ୍କ ପାଖକୁ ଆସେ ଯେଉଁମାନେ କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ କରନ୍ତି ଏବଂ ନିରନ୍ତର ଶିଖିବା ଜାରି ରଖନ୍ତି। 

4. English Meaning (Elaborate in Simple Language)

Achieving true success in life is never a simple, quick, or easy task. Success does not come by magic, luck, or laziness; rather, it is only achieved by dedicated people who are entirely willing to put in a lot of hard effort and who never stop absorbing new knowledge and skills. 

💼 Paragraph 4: Choosing the Right Career
WithTeachers.in

1. Original Text & Pronunciation

Very soon you will require to choose a trade or profession. Choose with the greatest care and avoid as far as you can any occupation that leads nowhere. Aim rather at work that has in it the promise of an interesting and happy future; and if at first you are forced to take a job that can only last for a short time, try to get one, as soon as possible, that is to your liking. If at any time you are in doubt as to the choice you should make, do not hesitate to ask advice from one of your teachers.

English-Odia Pronunciation:

ଭେରି ସୁନ୍ ୟୁ ଉଇଲ୍ ରିକ୍ୱାୟାର୍ ଟୁ ଚୁଜ୍ ଏ ଟ୍ରେଡ୍ ଅର୍ ପ୍ରୋଫେସନ୍। ଚୁଜ୍ ଉଇଥ୍ ଦ ଗ୍ରେଟେଷ୍ଟ କେୟାର୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ଏଭଏଡ୍ ଆଜ୍ ଫାର୍ ଆଜ୍ ୟୁ କ୍ୟାନ୍ ଏନି ଅକ୍ୟୁପେସନ୍ ଦ୍ୟାଟ୍ ଲିଡସ୍ ନୋହ୍ୱେୟାର୍। ଏମ୍ ରାଦର୍ ଆଟ୍ ୱାର୍କ୍ ଦ୍ୟାଟ୍ ହ୍ୟାଜ୍ ଇନ୍ ଇଟ୍ ଦ ପ୍ରୋମିଜ୍ ଅଫ୍ ଆନ୍ ଇଣ୍ଟରେଷ୍ଟିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ହ୍ୟାପି ଫ୍ୟୁଚର୍; ଆଣ୍ଡ ଇଫ୍ ଆଟ୍ ଫାଷ୍ଟ ୟୁ ଆର୍ ଫୋର୍ସଡ୍ ଟୁ ଟେକ୍ ଏ ଜବ୍ ଦ୍ୟାଟ୍ କ୍ୟାନ୍ ଓନଲି ଲାଷ୍ଟ ଫର୍ ଏ ସର୍ଟ ଟାଇମ୍, ଟ୍ରାଏ ଟୁ ଗେଟ୍ ୱାନ୍, ଆଜ୍ ସୁନ୍ ଆଜ୍ ପସିବଲ୍, ଦ୍ୟାଟ୍ ଇଜ୍ ଟୁ ୟୋର୍ ଲାଇକିଙ୍ଗ୍। ଇଫ୍ ଆଟ୍ ଏନି ଟାଇମ୍ ୟୁ ଆର୍ ଇନ୍ ଡାଉଟ୍ ଆଜ୍ ଟୁ ଦ ଚଏସ୍ ୟୁ ସୁଡ୍ ମେକ୍, ଡୁ ନଟ୍ ହେଜିଟେଟ୍ ଟୁ ଆସ୍କ ଆଡଭାଇସ୍ ଫ୍ରମ୍ ୱାନ୍ ଅଫ୍ ୟୋର୍ ଟିଚର୍ସ।

2. Word Meanings (English → Odia)

Require: ଆବଶ୍ୟକ ହେବ,

 Choose: ବାଛିବା,

3. Odia Meaning (Translation / Explanation)

ଖୁବ୍ ଶୀଘ୍ର ତୁମକୁ ଏକ ବ୍ୟବସାୟ କିମ୍ବା ପେଶା ବାଛିବାର ଆବଶ୍ୟକତା ପଡିବ। ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ସତର୍କତାର ସହିତ ବାଛ ଏବଂ ଯେତେଦୂର ସମ୍ଭବ ଏପରି କୌଣସି ବୃତ୍ତିକୁ ଏଡାଇ ଦିଅ ଯାହାର କୌଣସି ଭବିଷ୍ୟତ ନାହିଁ। ଏହା ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତେ ଏପରି ଏକ କାମ ଉପରେ ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟ ରଖ ଯେଉଁଥିରେ ଏକ ଆକର୍ଷଣୀୟ ଏବଂ ସୁଖମୟ ଭବିଷ୍ୟତର ସମ୍ଭାବନା ଥିବ; ଏବଂ ଯଦି ପ୍ରଥମେ ତୁମେ ଏପରି ଏକ ଚାକିରି କରିବାକୁ ବାଧ୍ୟ ହେଉଛ ଯାହା କେବଳ ଅଳ୍ପ ସମୟ ପାଇଁ ରହିବ, ତେବେ ଯଥାଶୀଘ୍ର ନିଜ ପସନ୍ଦର ଏକ ସ୍ଥାୟୀ ଚାକିରି ପାଇବାକୁ ଚେଷ୍ଟା କର। ଯଦି କୌଣସି ସମୟରେ ତୁମେ କି ପେଶା ବାଛିବ ସେ ନେଇ ସନ୍ଦେହରେ ଥାଅ, ତେବେ ତୁମର କୌଣସି ଶିକ୍ଷକଙ୍କଠାରୁ ପରାମର୍ଶ ମାଗିବାକୁ କେବେବି ସଂକୋଚ କର ନାହିଁ।


4. English Meaning (Elaborate in Simple Language)

Very soon after finishing their basic schooling, students will definitely need to select a specific job or career path for their lives. The author carefully advises them to make this major life choice with the highest level of caution and to strictly stay away from dead-end jobs that do not offer any growth or future progress. Instead, they should target careers that clearly guarantee an engaging, interesting, and joyful future. Sometimes, out of sudden necessity, a person might be forced to initially take a temporary job just to survive, but they should keep continuously trying to find a permanent job that they actually love as quickly as possible. Finally, if a student ever feels highly confused or unsure about which career path to carefully take, they should never feel shy or hesitant to go back to their old school teachers and respectfully ask for their wise guidance and advice.


🏃‍♂️ Paragraph 5: The Importance of Good Health

1. Original Text & Pronunciation

Success in life depends largely on good health. Keep your body fit, and by cleanliness, fresh air, regular habits, and suitable recreations, make yourself strong to play the game, and to do it in every sense of the word. Avoid anything that will sap your health. Smoking in your youth stunts the body, and clouds the brain. Be temperate in all things, and beware of drink. 3. Odia Meaning (Translation / Explanation)

ଏକ ସୁସ୍ଥ ଶରୀରରେ ଏକ ସୁସ୍ଥ ମନ, ଉତ୍ତମ ଚରିତ୍ର, ନମ୍ର ବ୍ୟବହାର, ଏବଂ ନିଜ ଦେଶର କର୍ତ୍ତବ୍ୟ ଓ ଏହାର ଉଚ୍ଚ ଆଦର୍ଶ ପ୍ରତି ତୁମର ବିଶ୍ୱସ୍ତତା ଥିଲେ, ଭଗବାନଙ୍କ କୃପାରୁ ତୁମେ ତୁମ ପରିବାର ପାଇଁ ଏକ ଗୌରବ ସାଜିବ, ଜଣେ ସୁନାଗରିକ ହେବ, ଏବଂ ତୁମର ସମଗ୍ର ଜୀବନରେ ଏକ ପ୍ରକୃତ ସଫଳତା ହାସଲ କରିବ।
