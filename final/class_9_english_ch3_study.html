Road Safety Week – Study Material Class 9 ENGLISH

ROAD SAFETY WEEK

1. About the Author This chapter is an informative and educational essay rather than a literary work by a single author. It has been compiled and included by the Board of Editors, Board of Secondary Education, Odisha.

  • Background: The piece is designed as a public awareness text aimed at young students.

    Theme: The primary theme is to educate teenagers about the paramount importance of road safety rules, the causes of accidents, and the preventive measures necessary to save precious human lives on the road.

     

2. Paragraph Explanation

Paragraph 1

  •  

    Original Snippet: "Many people think that road safety is non-essential... demands a greater need of awareness among people about road safety."

  • English Meaning: Road safety is extremely important because it protects people from injuries and death on the roads. Everyone, including pedestrians and drivers, faces risks. The World Health Organization states that a lack of awareness is the leading cause of road accident deaths, meaning we must strictly follow traffic rules as the number of vehicles increases.

  • Odia Meaning: ସଡ଼କ ସୁରକ୍ଷା ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ଗୁରୁତ୍ୱପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ କାରଣ ଏହା ଲୋକମାନଙ୍କୁ ରାସ୍ତାଘାଟରେ ଆହତ ଏବଂ ମୃତ୍ୟୁରୁ ରକ୍ଷା କରିଥାଏ। ପଥଚାରୀ ଏବଂ ଚାଳକ ସମେତ ସମସ୍ତେ ବିପଦର ସମ୍ମୁଖୀନ ହୁଅନ୍ତି। ବିଶ୍ୱ ସ୍ୱାସ୍ଥ୍ୟ ସଂଗଠନର କହିବା ଅନୁଯାୟୀ, ସଚେତନତାର ଅଭାବ ହେଉଛି ସଡ଼କ ଦୁର୍ଘଟଣାରେ ମୃତ୍ୟୁର ପ୍ରମୁଖ କାରଣ। ଯାନବାହାନ ସଂଖ୍ୟା ବୃଦ୍ଧି ପାଉଥିବାରୁ ଆମେ ଟ୍ରାଫିକ୍ ନିୟମକୁ କଡ଼ାକଡ଼ି ପାଳନ କରିବା ଉଚିତ୍।

Paragraph 2

  • Original Snippet: "Road Safety Week is one of the awareness programmes... They are instructed not to use cell phones or listen to music while driving."

  • English Meaning: Road Safety Week is a national campaign aimed at spreading awareness about traffic rules to reduce accidents. Along with the government, various NGOs and private organizations support this event. The goal is to educate people about the dangers of drunk driving, speeding, and driving without helmets or seat-belts, as well as to discourage using phones or music while driving.

  • Odia Meaning: ସଡ଼କ ସୁରକ୍ଷା ସପ୍ତାହ ହେଉଛି ଏକ ଜାତୀୟ ଅଭିଯାନ ଯାହାର ଉଦ୍ଦେଶ୍ୟ ହେଉଛି ଦୁର୍ଘଟଣା ହ୍ରାସ କରିବା ପାଇଁ ଟ୍ରାଫିକ୍ ନିୟମ ବିଷୟରେ ସଚେତନତା ସୃଷ୍ଟି କରିବା। ସରକାରଙ୍କ ସହିତ ବିଭିନ୍ନ ବେସରକାରୀ ସଂସ୍ଥା ମଧ୍ୟ ଏହାକୁ ସମର୍ଥନ କରନ୍ତି। ମଦ୍ୟପାନ କରି ଗାଡ଼ି ଚଳାଇବା, ଅତ୍ୟଧିକ ବେଗ, ଏବଂ ବିନା ହେଲମେଟ୍ କିମ୍ବା ସିଟ୍-ବେଲ୍ଟରେ ଗାଡ଼ି ଚଳାଇବାର ବିପଦ ବିଷୟରେ ଲୋକଙ୍କୁ ଶିକ୍ଷିତ କରିବା ଏବଂ ମୋବାଇଲ୍ ବ୍ୟବହାର ନକରିବାକୁ ପରାମର୍ଶ ଦେବା ଏହାର ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟ ଅଟେ।

Paragraph 3

  •  

    Original Snippet: "Road Safety Week is organised every year in the month of January... Free medical check-up camps and driving training workshops are also organised for the drivers."

     

  • English Meaning: Organized every January by the Ministry of Road Transport, this week is widely celebrated in major Indian cities. Banners, posters, and leaflets are distributed to educate travelers. Schools and colleges participate, while local authorities strictly check for drunk driving (Zero Tolerance). Activities like painting competitions, seminars, and free medical camps for drivers are also arranged.

  • Odia Meaning: ପ୍ରତିବର୍ଷ ଜାନୁଆରୀ ମାସରେ ସଡ଼କ ପରିବହନ ମନ୍ତ୍ରଣାଳୟ ଦ୍ୱାରା ଆୟୋଜିତ ଏହି ସପ୍ତାହ ଭାରତର ପ୍ରମୁଖ ସହରଗୁଡ଼ିକରେ ପାଳନ କରାଯାଏ। ଯାତ୍ରୀମାନଙ୍କୁ ସଚେତନ କରିବା ପାଇଁ ପୋଷ୍ଟର ଏବଂ ଲିଫଲେଟ୍ ବଣ୍ଟନ କରାଯାଏ। ବିଦ୍ୟାଳୟ ଏବଂ କଲେଜଗୁଡ଼ିକ ଅଂଶଗ୍ରହଣ କରନ୍ତି ଏବଂ ସ୍ଥାନୀୟ ପ୍ରଶାସନ ମଦ୍ୟପାନ କରି ଗାଡ଼ି ଚଳାଉଥିବା ଲୋକଙ୍କୁ କଡ଼ାକଡ଼ି ଯାଞ୍ଚ କରନ୍ତି। ଏହାବ୍ୟତୀତ ଚିତ୍ରାଙ୍କନ ପ୍ରତିଯୋଗିତା ଏବଂ ଚାଳକମାନଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ ମାଗଣା ଚିକିତ୍ସା ଶିବିର ମଧ୍ୟ ଆୟୋଜିତ ହୁଏ।

Paragraph 4

  •  

    Original Snippet: "There are various important factors that may cause serious accidents... many unsafe and poorly kept commercial vehicles run on the roads risking the life of drivers as well as others."

     

  • English Meaning: There are five main causes of fatal road accidents: (1) Drunk driving and over-speeding. (2) Underage driving (legal age is 18 for private, 16 for 50cc without gear, and 20 for commercial vehicles). (3) Distracted driving (using cell phones or talking). (4) Careless habits like not wearing seatbelts or jumping signals. (5) Unsafe pedestrians and poorly maintained commercial vehicles.

  • Odia Meaning: ସାଂଘାତିକ ସଡ଼କ ଦୁର୍ଘଟଣାର ପାଞ୍ଚଟି ମୁଖ୍ୟ କାରଣ ଅଛି: (୧) ମଦ୍ୟପାନ କରି ଏବଂ ଅଧିକ ବେଗରେ ଗାଡ଼ି ଚଳାଇବା। (୨) କମ୍ ବୟସରେ ଗାଡ଼ି ଚଳାଇବା (ବେସରକାରୀ ଯାନ ପାଇଁ ୧୮ ବର୍ଷ, ୫୦ ସିସି ପାଇଁ ୧୬ ବର୍ଷ ଏବଂ ବ୍ୟବସାୟିକ ଯାନ ପାଇଁ ୨୦ ବର୍ଷ ନିୟମ)। (୩) ଅନ୍ୟମନସ୍କ ହୋଇ ଗାଡ଼ି ଚଳାଇବା (ମୋବାଇଲ୍ ବ୍ୟବହାର)। (୪) ସିଟ୍ ବେଲ୍ଟ ନପିନ୍ଧିବା ଏବଂ ସିଗନାଲ୍ ନମାନିବା ପରି ଖରାପ ଅଭ୍ୟାସ। (୫) ଅସାବଧାନ ପଥଚାରୀ ଏବଂ ଖରାପ ରକ୍ଷଣାବେକ୍ଷଣ ଥିବା ବ୍ୟବସାୟିକ ଯାନ।

People talking on phones while driving, or not wearing helmets/seat-belts, must be fined heavily. Fitness certificates for vehicles must be mandatory.

4. Long Summary (English) "Road Safety Week" is a highly informative text that underscores the paramount importance of road safety to prevent injuries and save lives. Every year, countless lives are lost due to a lack of awareness about traffic rules, as reported by the World Health Organization. To combat this, the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways in India organizes the 'Road Safety Week' campaign every January. This national event, supported by NGOs and local authorities, aims to sensitize the public against dangerous habits like drunken driving, high-speed driving, and driving without helmets or seatbelts. During this week, massive awareness drives including poster distributions, medical check-ups, school competitions, and strict police checking are conducted across major cities.

The chapter details five primary factors responsible for fatal road accidents. First is drunken and rash driving, which risks multiple lives. Second is underage driving; the legal age for a private vehicle is 18, and 20 for commercial ones, though 16-year-olds can drive 50cc non-gear vehicles with parental consent. Third is distracted driving, specifically talking or texting on mobile phones or listening to loud music while behind the wheel. Fourth is the violation of basic rules like jumping traffic signals or not wearing seatbelts. Lastly, the presence of poorly maintained commercial vehicles and careless pedestrians heavily contributes to the casualty rate.

To prevent these accidents, the text suggests strict enforcement of laws. Authorities must heavily fine those caught driving drunk, without helmets, or using phones. The process of issuing driving licenses should be made stringent, and fitness certificates for all running vehicles must be made compulsory. Ultimately, Road Safety Week serves as a crucial reminder of the preciousness of human life, urging everyone to take responsibility for their own safety and the safety of others on the road.

How can a teacher introduce the chapter effectively to grab students' attention?

What collaborative activity can teachers use to explain traffic rules?

Answer: 🛑 Teachers can use the chart of traffic symbols provided in the book and organize a classroom quiz where groups of students guess the exact meaning of symbols like 'U-Turn Prohibited' or 'Speed Limit'.

How can teachers help students understand the dangers of 'distracted driving'?

Answer: 📱 Teachers should organize a short role-play or skit in the classroom where one student pretends to text while walking or doing a task, demonstrating how attention is lost and leading to a discussion on why doing this while driving is fatal.

What method should teachers adopt to teach the 'Word Meanings' and vocabulary?
