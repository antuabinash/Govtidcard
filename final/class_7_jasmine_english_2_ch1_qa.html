Question I: You have learnt that naming words are called nouns and only proper nouns begin with a capital letter. Why has the author used a capital letter for the ‘River’ even when it is a common noun? 🌊🔠

Odia Translation: ଆପଣ ଶିଖିଛନ୍ତି ଯେ ନାମବାଚକ ଶବ୍ଦକୁ ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ କୁହାଯାଏ ଏବଂ କେବଳ ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ ନାମବାଚକ ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ ବଡ଼ ଅକ୍ଷରରୁ ଆରମ୍ଭ ହୁଏ। ଲେଖକ ଏକ ସାଧାରଣ ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ ହୋଇଥିଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ 'ନଦୀ' (River) ପାଇଁ ଏକ ବଡ଼ ଅକ୍ଷର କାହିଁକି ବ୍ୟବହାର କରିଛନ୍ତି?

Answer: The author capitalized "River" to personify it. In this story, the River acts as a mentor, a guide, and a friend to Jahnavi. By giving it a capital letter, the author treats the River as a specific, living character rather than just a regular body of water.
