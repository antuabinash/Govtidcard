📋 Additional Questions

Fables and Folk Tales-The Raven and the Fox – Additional Questions Class 6 Jasmine English

 📝 Section A:

1️⃣ Who is the author of the poem? ✍️

(Odia: କବିତାର କବି କିଏ?)

Answer: Jean de La Fontaine

2️⃣ Where was Mr. Raven sitting? 🌳

(Odia: ମିଷ୍ଟର ରାଭେନ୍ କେଉଁଠାରେ ବସିଥିଲେ?)

Answer: Upon a limb (branch)

3️⃣ What was Mr. Raven holding in his beak? 🧀

(Odia: ରାଭେନ୍ ତାଙ୍କ ଥଣ୍ଟରେ କ’ଣ ଧରିଥିଲେ?)

Answer: A morsel (piece of food)

4️⃣ Who looked up at the Raven? 🦊

(Odia: କିଏ ରାଭେନ୍ କୁ ଚାହିଁଥିଲା?)

Answer: Reynard the Fox

5️⃣ How did the Fox describe the Raven's feathers? ✨

(Odia: ଶିଆଳ ରାଭେନ୍‌ର ପରଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ କିପରି ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କଲା?)

Answer: Handsome

6️⃣ What did the Fox say the birds would call the Raven if he sang? 👑

(Odia: ଯଦି ରାଭେନ୍ ଗୀତ ଗାଏ, ପକ୍ଷୀମାନେ ତାଙ୍କୁ କ’ଣ ଡାକିବେ ବୋଲି ଶିଆଳ କହିଲା?)

Answer: King

7️⃣ What is the Raven’s real voice called? 🗣️

(Odia: ରାଭେନ୍‌ର ପ୍ରକୃତ ସ୍ୱରକୁ କ’ଣ କୁହାଯାଏ?)

Answer: A croak

8️⃣ Why did the Raven open his beak? 👄

(Odia: ରାଭେନ୍ କାହିଁକି ତାର ଥଣ୍ଟ ଖୋଲିଲା?)

Answer: To sing in foolish pride

9️⃣ What happened when the Raven opened his beak? ⬇️

(Odia: ରାଭେନ୍ ତାର ଥଣ୍ଟ ଖୋଲିବା ମାତ୍ରେ କ’ଣ ଘଟିଲା?)

Answer: The morsel fell down

🔟 How did the Fox react when the food fell? 😂

(Odia: ଖାଦ୍ୟ ତଳେ ପଡ଼ିବା ପରେ ଶିଆଳ କିପରି ପ୍ରତିକ୍ରିୟା ଦେଖାଇଲା?)

Answer: He laughed "Ha-ha!"

1️⃣1️⃣ What did the Fox tell the Raven to ignore? 👂

(Odia: ଶିଆଳ ରାଭେନ୍ କୁ କେଉଁ କଥାକୁ ଅଣଦେଖା କରିବାକୁ କହିଲା?)

Answer: Sweet words that make you glow

1️⃣2️⃣ What quality did the Fox say is "rather unwise"? 🧠

(Odia: ଶିଆଳ କେଉଁ ଗୁଣକୁ "ଅଜ୍ଞତା" ବୋଲି କହିଲା?)

Answer: Pride

1️⃣3️⃣ Which word in the poem means a branch? 🌿

(Odia: କବିତାରେ କେଉଁ ଶବ୍ଦର ଅର୍ଥ ହେଉଛି ଏକ ଡାଳ?)

Answer: Limb

1️⃣4️⃣ Who was the cunning character in the story? 🤨

(Odia: ଗପରେ କିଏ ଚତୁର ଚରିତ୍ର ଥିଲା?)

Answer: The Fox

1️⃣5️⃣ Where does the story take place? 🌲

(Odia: ଏହି ଗପଟି କେଉଁଠାରେ ଘଟିଛି?)

Answer: In the woods (forest)

✅ Section B: True or False

1️⃣6️⃣ Was the Raven a handsome singer? 🎤

(Odia: ରାଭେନ୍ କ’ଣ ଜଣେ ସୁନ୍ଦର ଗାୟକ ଥିଲେ?)

Answer: False

1️⃣7️⃣ Did the Fox want to eat the Raven? 🍴

(Odia: ଶିଆଳ କ’ଣ ରାଭେନ୍‌କୁ ଖାଇବାକୁ ଚାହୁଁଥିଲା?)

Answer: False (He wanted the morsel)

1️⃣8️⃣ Is Reynard the name of the Raven? 🐦

(Odia: ରେନାର୍ଡ କ’ଣ ରାଭେନ୍‌ର ନାମ?)

Answer: False (It is the Fox's name)

1️⃣9️⃣ Did the Raven see the joke? 🤔

(Odia: ରାଭେନ୍ କ’ଣ ପରିହାସଟିକୁ ବୁଝିପାରିଲା?)

Answer: False

2️⃣0️⃣ Was the Fox laughing at the end? 😆

(Odia: ଶେଷରେ ଶିଆଳ କ’ଣ ହସୁଥିଲା?)

Answer: True

2️⃣1️⃣ Did the morsel fall from the Fox's mouth? 🥖

(Odia: ଖାଦ୍ୟ କ’ଣ ଶିଆଳ ପାଟିରୁ ତଳେ ପଡ଼ିଗଲା?)

Answer: False (It fell from the Raven's beak)

2️⃣2️⃣ Did the Fox address the Raven as 'Sir'? 🎩

(Odia: ଶିଆଳ କ’ଣ ରାଭେନ୍‌କୁ 'ସାର୍' ବୋଲି ସମ୍ବୋଧନ କରିଥିଲା?)

Answer: True

2️⃣3️⃣ Is pride considered wise in this poem? 💡

(Odia: ଏହି କବିତାରେ ଗର୍ବକୁ କ’ଣ ବୁଦ୍ଧିମାନର କାମ ବୋଲି କୁହାଯାଇଛି?)

Answer: False

2️⃣4️⃣ Did the Fox go far to seek the morsel? 🚶

(Odia: ଶିଆଳ କ’ଣ ଖାଦ୍ୟ ଖଣ୍ଡିକ ପାଇବାକୁ ବହୁ ଦୂରକୁ ଯାଇଥାନ୍ତା?)

Answer: True

2️⃣5️⃣ Are fables stories that often have a lesson? 📚

(Odia: ଫେବୁଲ୍ (Fables) ଗୁଡ଼ିକ କ’ଣ ସେହି ଗପ ଯେଉଁଥିରେ କିଛି ଶିକ୍ଷା ଥାଏ?)

Answer: True

🔍 Section D: Very Short Answer Questions

3️⃣6️⃣ What is the name of the Fox? 🦊

(Odia: ଶିଆଳର ନାମ କ’ଣ?)

3️⃣7️⃣ Where did the morsel fall? ⬇️

(Odia: ଖାଦ୍ୟ ଖଣ୍ଡିକ କେଉଁଠି ପଡ଼ିଲା?)

3️⃣8️⃣ What sound does a Raven make? 📢

(Odia: ରାଭେନ୍ କିପରି ଶବ୍ଦ କରେ?)

Answer: Croak

3️⃣9️⃣ Is the Raven wise or foolish? 🤡

(Odia: ରାଭେନ୍ ଜ୍ଞାନୀ ନା ବୋକା?)

Answer: Foolish

4️⃣0️⃣ Who said "Sir Raven, you are a handsome bird"? 🗣️

4️⃣1️⃣ What did the Raven lose? 🧀

4️⃣2️⃣ How many characters are in the poem? 👥

Answer: Two (The Raven and the Fox)

4️⃣3️⃣ Which animal is known for being cunning? 🦊

4️⃣4️⃣ Did the Fox eat the food? 😋

(Odia: ଶିଆଳ କ’ଣ ଖାଦ୍ୟଟି ଖାଇଲା?)

Answer: Yes (He eyed it and then it fell)

4️⃣5️⃣ What is the moral of the story? 💡

(Odia: ଏହି ଗଳ୍ପର ଶିକ୍ଷା କ’ଣ?)

Answer: Pride is unwise and ignore flattery

📖 Section E: Short Answer Questions

4️⃣6️⃣ Why did the Fox praise the Raven? 🦊

(Odia: ଶିଆଳ କାହିଁକି ରାଭେନ୍‌କୁ ପ୍ରଶଂସା କଲା?)

Answer: The Fox praised the Raven because he wanted the morsel of food that the Raven was holding in his beak.

4️⃣7️⃣ How did the Raven feel after hearing the Fox’s words? 😊

(Odia: ଶିଆଳର କଥା ଶୁଣିବା ପରେ ରାଭେନ୍ କିପରି ଅନୁଭବ କଲା?)

Answer: The Raven felt very proud and "glowed" because of the sweet words and flattery.

4️⃣8️⃣ What mistake did the Raven make? ❌

(Odia: ରାଭେନ୍ କେଉଁ ଭୁଲ୍ କଲା?)

Answer: The Raven made the mistake of opening his beak to sing, which caused him to drop his food.

4️⃣9️⃣ What does the Fox mean by "Pride is rather unwise"? 🧠

(Odia: "ଗର୍ବ କରିବା ବୁଦ୍ଧିମାନର କାମ ନୁହେଁ" ବୋଲି କହି ଶିଆଳ କ’ଣ ବୁଝାଇବାକୁ ଚାହୁଁଛି?)

Answer: He means that being too proud makes you lose your common sense and allows others to trick you easily.

5️⃣0️⃣ Why is the Fox called "cunning"? 🤨

(Odia: ଶିଆଳକୁ କାହିଁକି "ଚତୁର" ବୋଲି କୁହାଯାଏ?)

Answer: He is called cunning because he used clever tricks and fake praise to get what he wanted from the Raven.

5️⃣1️⃣ How can we identify alliteration in the poem? 🔍

(Odia: ଆମେ କବିତାରେ Alliteration କୁ କିପରି ଚିହ୍ନି ପାରିବା?)

Answer: We can find it when two or more words begin with the same consonant sound, like "big beak" or "woods would".

5️⃣2️⃣ What is a "morsel"? 🥖

(Odia: "Morsel" ର ଅର୍ଥ କ’ଣ?)

Answer: A morsel is a very small piece of food.

(Odia: କବିତାରୁ 'glow' ସହିତ ସମାନ ଶବ୍ଦ ଥିବା ଏକ ଶବ୍ଦ ଖୋଜନ୍ତୁ।)

(Odia: 'Wise' ର ବିପରୀତ ଶବ୍ଦ କ’ଣ?)

Answer: Foolish or Unwise

5️⃣5️⃣ What is the past tense of "laugh"? 😂

(Odia: 'Laugh' ର ଅତୀତ କାଳ (past tense) କ’ଣ?)

Answer: Laughed

5️⃣6️⃣ Identify the alliteration in: "The song of sweet birds." 🐦

(Odia: "The song of sweet birds" ରେ Alliteration କୁ ଚିହ୍ନଟ କରନ୍ତୁ।)

Answer: The repetition of the 's' sound in 'song' and 'sweet'.
