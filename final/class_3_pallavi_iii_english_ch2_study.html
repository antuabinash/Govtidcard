Learn English Letters (Part - 1) – Study Material Class 3 Pallavi-III (Eng)

Section: Pre-reading (Rhyme)

1. Original Paragraph:
\"Oh dear children! Stand with me 
we will sing ABC 
Raise your hand and sing with me 
ABCDEFG\".

2. English-Odia Pronunciation:
\"ଓହ ଡିଅର୍ ଚିଲ୍ଡ୍ରେନ୍! ଷ୍ଟାଣ୍ଡ ୱିଥ୍ ମି
ୱି ୱିଲ୍ ସିଙ୍ଗ ଏବିସି
ରେଜ୍ ୟୋର୍ ହ୍ୟାଣ୍ଡ ଆଣ୍ଡ୍ ସିଙ୍ଗ ୱିଥ୍ ମି
ଏବିସିଡିଇଏଫ୍‌ଜି\".

3. Odia Meaning:
\"ହେ ପ୍ରିୟ ପିଲାମାନେ! ମୋ ସହିତ ଠିଆ ହୁଅ, ଆମେ ABC ଗୀତ ଗାଇବା । ନିଜ ହାତ ଉଠାଅ ଏବଂ ମୋ ସହିତ ଗାଅ ABCDEFG\" ।

4. English Meaning (Simple Explanation):
This is a fun introductory rhyme asking children to stand up, raise their hands, and join in singing the first few letters of the alphabet together.

5. Dictionary / Word Meanings (Easy to Hard):
  Sing (ସିଙ୍ଗ): ଗୀତ ଗାଇବା (To make musical sounds with the voice)
  Stand (ଷ୍ଟାଣ୍ଡ): ଠିଆ ହେବା (To be in an upright position)
  Dear (ଡିଅର୍): ପ୍ରିୟ/ସ୍ନେହର (Loved or cherished)
  Raise (ରେଜ୍): ଉଠାଇବା (To lift up)
  Children (ଚିଲ୍ଡ୍ରେନ୍): ପିଲାମାନେ (Young human beings)

 Section: While-reading (Alphabet Song)

1. Original Paragraph:
ABCDEFG 
HIJKLMNOP 
LMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ 
X Y Z, Sugar on bread 
Next day morning, come to me 
We will learn the A B C... 
Happy, happy we shall be 
When we know the A B C...

2. English-Odia Pronunciation:
ଏବିସିଡିଇଏଫ୍‌ଜି
ଏଚ୍‌ଆଇଜେକେଏଲ୍‌ଏମ୍‌ଏନ୍‌ଓପି
ଏଲ୍‌ଏମ୍‌ଏନ୍‌ଓପିକ୍ଯୁଆର୍‌ଏସ୍‌ଟିଇଉଭିଡବ୍ଲ୍ୟୁଏକ୍ସୱାଇଜେଡ୍
ଏକ୍ସ ୱାଇ ଜେଡ୍, ସୁଗାର ଅନ୍ ବ୍ରେଡ୍
ନେକ୍ସ୍ଟ ଡେ ମର୍ଣ୍ଣିଂ, କମ୍ ଟୁ ମି
ୱି ୱିଲ୍ ଲର୍ନ ଦ ଏ ବି ସି...
ହ୍ୟାପି, ହ୍ୟାପି ୱି ସାଲ୍ ବି
ହ୍ୱେନ୍ ୱି ନୋ ଦ ଏ ବି ସି...

3. Odia Meaning:
A B C D E F G H I J K L M N O P L M N O P Q R S T U V W X Y Z, ପାଉଁରୁଟି ଉପରେ ଚିନି । ପରଦିନ ସକାଳେ ମୋ ପାଖକୁ ଆସ, ଆମେ A B C ଶିଖିବା... ଯେତେବେଳେ ଆମେ A B C ଜାଣିବା, ଆମେ ବହୁତ ଖୁସି ହେବା...

4. English Meaning (Simple Explanation):
This is a popular traditional rhyme used to teach the English alphabet from A to Z. It includes a playful line about sugar on bread and encourages children to return the next morning to continue learning their ABCs, which will make them very happy.

5. Dictionary / Word Meanings (Easy to Hard):
  Next (ନେକ୍ସ୍ଟ): ପରବର୍ତ୍ତୀ (Following in time or order)
  Bread (ବ୍ରେଡ୍): ପାଉଁରୁଟି (Food made from flour and water)
  Sugar (ସୁଗାର): ଚିନି (A sweet substance used to flavor food)
  Happy (ହ୍ୟାପି): ଖୁସି (Feeling or showing pleasure)
  Learn (ଲର୍ନ): ଶିଖିବା (To gain knowledge)

Section: Capital and Small Letters Song

1. Original Paragraph:
A, B, C big and bold, 
Capital letters, stories to be told 
abc small and sweet 
helping us write, can't be beat.

2. English-Odia Pronunciation:
ଏ, ବି, ସି ବିଗ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ୍ ବୋଲ୍ଡ,
କ୍ୟାପିଟାଲ୍ ଲେଟର୍ସ, ଷ୍ଟୋରିଜ୍ ଟୁ ବି ଟୋଲ୍ଡ
ଏବିସି ସ୍ମଲ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ସ୍ୱିଟ୍
ହେଲ୍ପିଂ ଅସ୍ ରାଇଟ୍, କ୍ୟାଣ୍ଟ ବି ବିଟ୍.

3. Odia Meaning:
A, B, C ବଡ଼ ଏବଂ ସ୍ପଷ୍ଟ, କ୍ୟାପିଟାଲ୍ ଅକ୍ଷରଗୁଡ଼ିକରେ କାହାଣୀ କୁହାଯିବ । a, b, c ଛୋଟ ଏବଂ ସୁନ୍ଦର, ଯାହା ଆମକୁ ଲେଖିବାରେ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରେ ଏବଂ ସେଗୁଡ଼ିକର ତୁଳନା ନାହିଁ ।

4. English Meaning (Simple Explanation):
This short poem introduces the difference between uppercase (capital) letters, which are described as large and strong, and lowercase (small) letters, which are described as small, nice, and very useful for writing.

5. Dictionary / Word Meanings (Easy to Hard):
  Big (ବିଗ୍): ବଡ଼ (Large in size)
  Small (ସ୍ମଲ୍): ଛୋଟ (Not large in size)
  Sweet (ସ୍ୱିଟ୍): ସୁନ୍ଦର/ମିଠା (Pleasing or nice)
  Bold (ବୋଲ୍ଡ): ସ୍ପଷ୍ଟ/ମୋଟା ଲେଖା (Thick, clear letters)
  Helping (ହେଲ୍ପିଂ): ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରିବା (Making something easier to do)
