🧠 Stanza 1: The Brain in the Jar1. Original Text BOOK

Hi! I am Vicky's brilliant brain

Sitting in a formalin jar with disdain;

On display to promote knowledge gain

Watching people stare at me again and again.

English-Odia Pronunciation:

ହାଏ! ଆଇ ଆମ୍ ଭିକିଜ୍ ବ୍ରିଲିଆଣ୍ଟ ବ୍ରେନ୍

ସିଟିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଇନ୍ ଏ ଫର୍ମାଲିନ୍ ଜାର୍ ଉଇଥ୍ ଡିସଡେନ୍;

ଅନ୍ ଡିସପ୍ଲେ ଟୁ ପ୍ରୋମୋଟ୍ ନଲେଜ୍ ଗେନ୍

ୱାଚିଙ୍ଗ୍ ପିପୁଲ୍ ଷ୍ଟେଆର୍ ଆଟ୍ ମି ଏଗେନ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ଏଗେନ୍।


2. Word Meanings (English → Odia)

Brilliant: ଅତି ବୁଦ୍ଧିମାନ

 Formalin jar: ରାସାୟନିକ ତରଳ ପଦାର୍ଥ ଥିବା କାଚ ପାତ୍ର

Disdain: ଅବଜ୍ଞା କିମ୍ବା ଘୃଣା

Display: ପ୍ରଦର୍ଶନ

 Promote: ପ୍ରୋତ୍ସାହିତ କରିବା ବା ବୃଦ୍ଧି କରିବା

Knowledge gain: ଜ୍ଞାନ ଆହରଣ

Stare: ଏକଲୟରେ ଚାହିଁବା


3. Odia Meaning (Translation / Explanation)

ନମସ୍କାର! ମୁଁ ହେଉଛି ଭିକିର ଅତି ବୁଦ୍ଧିମାନ ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କ। ମୁଁ ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ ଘୃଣା ଓ ଅବଜ୍ଞାର ସହିତ ଏକ ଫର୍ମାଲିନ୍ ଜାର୍ ଭିତରେ ବସିଛି। ମୋତେ ଏଠାରେ ଲୋକମାନଙ୍କର ଜ୍ଞାନ ବୃଦ୍ଧି କରିବା ଉଦ୍ଦେଶ୍ୟରେ ପ୍ରଦର୍ଶନ ପାଇଁ ରଖାଯାଇଛି ଏବଂ ମୁଁ ଦେଖୁଛି ଯେ ଲୋକମାନେ ବାରମ୍ବାର ମୋ ଆଡକୁ ଏକଲୟରେ ଚାହିଁ ରହୁଛନ୍ତି।


4. English Meaning (Elaborate in Simple Language)

The poem begins with the voice of a human brain that once belonged to a highly intelligent young man named Vicky. The brain is currently kept preserved in a glass jar filled with a chemical called formalin, and it feels a strong sense of disrespect and unhappiness about being trapped there. It has been placed on public display in a museum specifically to help medical students and people gain knowledge, and it spends its time watching visitors constantly staring at it.

😢 Stanza 2: The Insult of Being a Specimen

1. Original Text BOOK

How insulting to be called a 'specimen'

Two years ago I was part of a living human;

I got no stroke, tumour or bugs within

Yet ended up in this jar, to my chagrin.

English-Odia Pronunciation:

ହାଓ ଇନସଲଟିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଟୁ ବି କଲଡ୍ ଏ 'ସ୍ପେସିମେନ୍'

ଟୁ ଇୟର୍ସ ଏଗୋ ଆଇ ୱାଜ୍ ପାର୍ଟ ଅଫ୍ ଏ ଲିଭିଙ୍ଗ୍ ହ୍ୟୁମାନ୍;

ଆଇ ଗଟ୍ ନୋ ଷ୍ଟ୍ରୋକ୍, ଟିଉମର୍ ଅର୍ ବଗସ୍ ଉଇଦିନ୍

ୟେଟ୍ ଏଣ୍ଡେଡ୍ ଅପ୍ ଇନ୍ ଦିସ୍ ଜାର୍, ଟୁ ମାଇ ସାଗ୍ରିନ୍।

 Bugs: ସଂକ୍ରମଣ କିମ୍ବା ରୋଗ ଜୀବାଣୁ

3. Odia Meaning (Translation / Explanation)

ଗୋଟିଏ 'ନମୁନା' ବୋଲି ଡକାଯିବା କେତେ ଅପମାନଜନକ ଅଟେ! ଠିକ୍ ଦୁଇ ବର୍ଷ ପୂର୍ବେ ମୁଁ ଜଣେ ଜୀବିତ ମଣିଷର ଅଂଶ ଥିଲି। ମୋ ଭିତରେ କୌଣସି ଷ୍ଟ୍ରୋକ୍ (ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କାଘାତ), ଟ୍ୟୁମର୍ କିମ୍ବା ରୋଗର ଜୀବାଣୁ ନଥିଲା। ତଥାପି, ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ଦୁଃଖ ଓ ନିରାଶାର ବିଷୟ ଯେ ମୋର ଶେଷ ଠିକଣା ଏହି କାଚ ଜାର୍ ଭିତରେ ହେଲା।

4. English Meaning (Elaborate in Simple Language)

The brain expresses how deeply humiliating and insulting it feels to be referred to merely as a scientific "specimen". It painfully remembers that just two years ago, it was alive and perfectly functioning inside a living human being. The brain is very sad and frustrated because it was completely healthy—it did not suffer from any brain attack (stroke), abnormal cell growth (tumour), or mild infections (bugs). Despite being entirely healthy, it tragically ended up locked inside this chemical jar. 

👦 Stanza 3: Vicky's Vibrant Life

1. Original Text BOOK

Vicky was a young boy, smart and sane

 Full of life and vigour, sun, wind or rain;

Computers and maths were his domain

 His motto in life was 'No pain, No gain'.

English-Odia Pronunciation:

ଭିକି ୱାଜ୍ ଏ ୟଙ୍ଗ୍ ବୟ, ସ୍ମାର୍ଟ ଆଣ୍ଡ ସେନ୍

ଫୁଲ୍ ଅଫ୍ ଲାଇଫ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ଭିଗର୍, ସନ୍, ଉଇଣ୍ଡ୍ ଅର୍ ରେନ୍;

କମ୍ପ୍ୟୁଟର୍ସ ଆଣ୍ଡ ମ୍ୟାଥ୍ସ ୱେୟାର୍ ହିଜ୍ ଡୋମେନ୍

ହିଜ୍ ମୋଟୋ ଇନ୍ ଲାଇଫ୍ ୱାଜ୍ 'ନୋ ପେନ୍, ନୋ ଗେନ୍'।


2. Word Meanings (English → Odia)

Young: ଯୁବକ

Smart: ଚତୁର ବା ସୁନ୍ଦର

Sane: ସୁସ୍ଥ ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କ ଥିବା ବ୍ୟକ୍ତି

 Vigour: ଶକ୍ତି ବା ଉତ୍ସାହ

Domain: ପ୍ରିୟ ବିଷୟ କିମ୍ବା ଜ୍ଞାନର କ୍ଷେତ୍ର

Motto: ଜୀବନର ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟ ବା ମୂଳମନ୍ତ୍ର

No pain, No gain: ବିନା କଷ୍ଟରେ କୃଷ୍ଣ ମିଳନ୍ତି ନାହିଁ (କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ ବିନା ସଫଳତା ମିଳେ ନାହିଁ)


3. Odia Meaning (Translation / Explanation)

ଭିକି ଜଣେ ଚତୁର ଏବଂ ସୁସ୍ଥ ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କ ଥିବା ଯୁବକ ଥିଲା। ଖରା, ପବନ ହେଉ କିମ୍ବା ବର୍ଷା, ସେ ସବୁବେଳେ ଜୀବନୀଶକ୍ତି ଏବଂ ପ୍ରବଳ ଉତ୍ସାହରେ ଭରପୂର ରହୁଥିଲା। କମ୍ପ୍ୟୁଟର ଏବଂ ଗଣିତ ତା'ର ସବୁଠାରୁ ପ୍ରିୟ ତଥା ଦକ୍ଷତାର ବିଷୟ ଥିଲା। ତା' ଜୀବନର ମୂଳମନ୍ତ୍ର ଥିଲା 'କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ ବିନା ସଫଳତା ମିଳେ ନାହିଁ'।


4. English Meaning (Elaborate in Simple Language)

The brain fondly remembers Vicky as a very handsome, intelligent, and mentally healthy young boy. No matter what the weather was outside—whether it was sunny, windy, or raining—Vicky was always completely full of positive energy and enthusiasm for life. His absolute favorite subjects, where he showed great skill, were computers and mathematics. He firmly believed in the life principle that one must work very hard and go through struggles in order to achieve true success ("No pain, No gain").

👵 Stanza 4: Vicky's Love and College Life


1. Original Text BOOK

Vicky's love for his parents was truly insane

And he adored his gran'ma, wrinkles and cane;

In college, when he stole a glance at Lorraine

 Why his heart went thud-thud, only I could explain!

4. English Meaning (Elaborate in Simple Language)

Vicky had an extraordinary and limitless amount of love for his parents. He also deeply respected and loved his old grandmother, affectionately loving everything about her, even her old wrinkled skin and the walking stick she used. The brain proudly claims that only it fully knew and understood those secret romantic feelings causing his fast heartbeats.

Additional Questions for Practice (2.5 Marks each)


1. Who is the speaker of the poem "From the Formalin Jar"? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: "ଫର୍ମାଲିନ୍ ଜାର୍ ରୁ" କବିତାର ବକ୍ତା କିଏ?)

Ans: The speaker is Vicky's brilliant brain.


2. In what is the brain preserved? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କଟି କେଉଁଥିରେ ସଂରକ୍ଷିତ ହୋଇ ରହିଛି?)

Ans: The brain is preserved in a formalin jar.


3. What were Vicky's two favourite subjects? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ଭିକିଙ୍କର ଦୁଇଟି ପ୍ରିୟ ବିଷୟ କ’ଣ ଥିଲା?) 

Ans: Vicky's favourite subjects were Computers and maths.


4. What was Vicky's motto in life? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ତାଙ୍କ ଜୀବନର ଆଦର୍ଶ କ’ଣ ଥିଲା?) 

Ans: His motto in life was 'No pain, No gain'.


5. Why does the brain feel insulted? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କ କାହିଁକି ଅପମାନିତ ଅନୁଭବ କରୁଛି?) 

Ans: The brain feels insulted to be called a 'specimen'.


6. What does the brain mean by saying "I got no stroke, tumour or bugs within"? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: "ମୋର କୌଣସି ଷ୍ଟ୍ରୋକ୍, ଟ୍ୟୁମର୍ କିମ୍ବା ବଗ୍ ନଥିଲା" କହି ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କ କ’ଣ ବୁଝାଇବାକୁ ଚାହେଁ?) 

Ans: The brain means it was perfectly healthy and did not end up in the jar due to any disease.


7. Who was Lorraine? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ଲୋରେନ୍ କିଏ?)

Ans: Lorraine was likely a college classmate whom Vicky admired.


8. What mistake did Vicky make on the day of his accident? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ଦୁର୍ଘଟଣା ଦିନ ଭିକି କି’ ଭୁଲ୍ କରିଥିଲେ?) 

Ans: He did refrain from wearing his helmet.


9. Why did Vicky ride his bike without a helmet that one time? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ସେହି ଗୋଟିଏ ଥର ଭିକି କାହିଁକି ବିନା ହେଲମେଟରେ ବାଇକ୍ ଚଳାଇଥିଲେ?) 

Ans: He was going to his friend in the next lane and probably thought it was not necessary.


10. What does the brain mean by "a stray moment inane"? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: "ଏକ ବୁଲା ଅଜ୍ଞାନ ମୁହୂର୍ତ୍ତ" ଦ୍ୱାରା ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କ କ’ଣ ବୁଝାଏ?)

Ans: It refers to a single, stupid or careless moment of not wearing the helmet.


11. What message does the brain give to the readers in the last stanza? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ଶେଷ ପଦରେ ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କ ପାଠକମାନଙ୍କୁ କି’ ବାର୍ତ୍ତା ଦେଇଛି?) 

Ans: The brain warns readers to DRIVE SAFE and not let their lives be in vain.


12. What does the poet mean by "On display to promote knowledge gain"? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: "ଜ୍ଞାନ ବୃଦ୍ଧି ପାଇଁ ପ୍ରଦର୍ଶନରେ" କହି କବି କ’ଣ ବୁଝାଇଛନ୍ତି?) 

Ans: The brain is used to educate students or the public about the brain or accident trauma.


13. What is the "chagrin" that the brain feels? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କ ଅନୁଭବ କରୁଥିବା "chagrin" (ବିରକ୍ତି) କ’ଣ?) 

Ans: The chagrin is the disappointment that a perfectly healthy brain ended up in a jar due to a preventable accident.


14. Why does the brain call risky driving a "bane"? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କ କାହିଁକି ବିପଦପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ଡ୍ରାଇଭିଂକୁ "ଅଭିଶାପ" (bane) ବୋଲି କହିଛି?) Ans: It is called a bane because it is a cause of great misery and death.

Ans: The irony is that the message about living life safely comes from a brain that is preserved after life has been lost.

Additional Questions for Practice (5 Marks each)

1. Describe Vicky as a person, based on the brain's recollection. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କର ସ୍ମୃତି ଆଧାରରେ, ଭିକିଙ୍କୁ ଜଣେ ବ୍ୟକ୍ତି ଭାବରେ ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କରନ୍ତୁ ।)

Ans: Vicky was a young, smart, and sane boy, full of life and vigour. He was a brilliant student whose domain was computers and maths. He was a hard worker, believing in 'No pain, No gain'. He was also a very loving son who adored his parents and grandmother. During his college days, he also experienced the normal feelings of a young man, like his heart going "thud-thud" for a girl named Lorraine.


2. Narrate the events of the day of Vicky's accident. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ଭିକିଙ୍କ ଦୁର୍ଘଟଣା ଦିନର ଘଟଣାଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କରନ୍ତୁ ।)

Ans: It was a wet monsoon day. Vicky had to go to his friend's house, which was just in the next lane, for an ordinary or "mundane" task. Because the distance was so short, for once, he did refrain from wearing his helmet. While riding, he met with a "gory and inhumane" accident. This single careless moment cost him his life and left his family "crushed with pain".


3. Explain the feelings of the brain as it sits in the formalin jar. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ଫର୍ମାଲିନ୍ ଜାର୍‌ରେ ବସିଥିବା ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କର ଭାବନାକୁ ବ୍ୟାଖ୍ୟା କରନ୍ତୁ ।)

Ans: The brain feels a strong sense of disdain (unworthiness) and insult. It hates being stared at by people and being called a 'specimen'. Its strongest feeling is chagrin (annoyance and disappointment) because it was a perfectly healthy brain, with no "stroke, tumour or bugs". It ended up preserved in a jar not because of any natural cause, but because of a single, preventable, and silly mistake made by its owner, Vicky.


4. Why does the poet use the word "insane" to describe Vicky's love for his parents? What other details show his caring nature? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: ଭିକିଙ୍କର ପିତାମାତାଙ୍କ ପ୍ରତି ଥିବା ଭଲପାଇବାକୁ ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କରିବା ପାଇଁ କବି କାହିଁକି "insane" (ଅତ୍ୟଧିକ) ଶବ୍ଦ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରିଛନ୍ତି? ତାଙ୍କର ଯତ୍ନଶୀଳ ସ୍ୱଭାବକୁ ଅନ୍ୟ କେଉଁ ବିବରଣୀ ଦର୍ଶାଏ?)

Ans: The poet uses "insane" to mean his love was excessive and unconditional, not that he was mentally ill. It emphasizes the depth of his family bond. This caring nature is further shown by the fact that he also "adored his gran'ma, wrinkles and cane". This detail shows he loved his grandmother completely, including her signs of old age. This deep love for his family makes his death even more tragic, as it highlights the pain his family must have felt.


5. What is the central theme or message of the poem? How is it conveyed through the speaker? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: କବିତାର କେନ୍ଦ୍ରୀୟ ବିଷୟବସ୍ତୁ ବା ବାର୍ତ୍ତା କ’ଣ? ବକ୍ତାଙ୍କ ମାଧ୍ୟମରେ ଏହା କିପରି ପ୍ରକାଶ ପାଇଛି?)

Ans: The central theme is the critical importance of road safety and not taking life for granted. The poem is a powerful warning against the devastating consequences of a single moment of carelessness, like not wearing a helmet. This message is conveyed in a unique and heart-breaking way by using Vicky's brain as the speaker. By having the "brilliant brain" reflect on its own useless end in a jar, the poem highlights the tragic waste of a promising young life. The speaker, a "specimen", directly addresses the reader, begging them to "DRIVE SAFE" so they don't meet the same fate.


6."His motto in life was 'No pain, No gain'." How is this motto both true and tragically ironic in the context of the poem? (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ: "ତାଙ୍କ ଜୀବନର ଆଦର୍ଶ ଥିଲା 'କଷ୍ଟ ନକଲେ କୃଷ୍ଣ ମିଳେନା'।" କବିତାର ପ୍ରସଙ୍ଗରେ ଏହି ଆଦର୍ଶ କିପରି ସତ୍ୟ ଏବଂ ଦୁଃଖଦ ଭାବରେ ବିଡମ୍ବନାପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ଅଟେ?)

 Ans: The motto "No pain, No gain" was true for Vicky's life as a student; he was smart in computers and maths, implying he worked hard to achieve his goals. The tragic irony is that his death was caused by the avoidance of a minor "pain" or inconvenience: the simple act of wearing a helmet. By avoiding this small, temporary discomfort, he did not "gain" anything. Instead, he lost everything. Furthermore, his avoidance of this small pain resulted in the ultimate "pain" for his family, who were "crushed with pain" by his loss.


7. Do you think preserving Vicky's brain as a 'specimen' to 'promote knowledge gain' is a fitting end? Justify your answer. As the poem states, the brain is "On display to promote knowledge gain". This "knowledge" is not just about anatomy, but about the real-world consequence of a head injury from an accident. It acts as a stark, physical warning, ensuring that Vicky's final "task" is to educate others and potentially save their lives.
