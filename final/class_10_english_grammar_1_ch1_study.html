Tense Revisited – Study Material Class 10 English Grammar

1. Introduction to Tense (କାଳର ପରିଚୟ)

Tense vs. Time (କାଳ ବନାମ ସମୟ)

·         Time (ସମୟ): This is a universal concept of a continuous flow, measured in seconds, minutes, hours, or years. It includes the Past, Present, and Future.

o    (Odia: ସମୟ ଏକ ସର୍ବଜନୀନ ଧାରଣା ଏବଂ ଏକ ନିରନ୍ତର ପ୍ରବାହ (ଅତୀତ, ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ, ଭବିଷ୍ୟତ), ଯାହାକୁ ସେକେଣ୍ଡ, ମିନିଟ୍, ଘଣ୍ଟା ବା ବର୍ଷରେ ମପାଯାଏ ।)

·         Tense (କାଳ): This is a grammatical concept. It is the form a verb takes to show the time of an action. Tense indicates both the time (ସମୟ) and the state (ସ୍ଥିତି) of an action.

o    (Odia: କାଳ ହେଉଛି ଏକ ବ୍ୟାକରଣଗତ ଧାରଣା । ଏହା ହେଉଛି କ୍ରିୟାର ସେହି ରୂପ ଯାହା ଏକ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟର ସମୟକୁ ଦର୍ଶାଏ । ଏହା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟର ସମୟ ଏବଂ ସ୍ଥିତି ଉଭୟକୁ ସୂଚିତ କରେ ।)

In English, there are only two tenses:

1.      Present Tense (ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ କାଳ)

2.      Past Tense (ଅତୀତ କାଳ)

We use these two tenses to express all three phases of time (Past, Present, and Future).

Four Forms (Aspects) of Tense (କାଳର ଚାରୋଟି ରୂପ ବା ସ୍ଥିତି)

The "state" of an action is shown using four forms, also called aspects:

·         Simple / Indefinite (ସାଧାରଣ)

·         Progressive / Continuous (ଚାଲୁ)

·         Perfect (ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ)

·         Perfect Progressive / Perfect Continuous (ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ଚାଲୁ)

2. Present and Past: Simple & Progressive Forms

Tense Form (କାଳର ରୂପ)

Rule (ନିୟମ)

Uses (ବ୍ୟବହାର)

Examples (ଉଦାହରଣ)

Present Simple (ସାଧାରଣ ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ)

S + V1 (s/es)

1. Habitual action (ଅଭ୍ୟାସଗତ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)



2. General, Scientific, or Universal Truths (ସାଧାରଣ, ବୈଜ୍ଞାନିକ ବା ଚିରନ୍ତନ ସତ୍ୟ)



3. Permanent state/fact (ସ୍ଥାୟୀ ସତ୍ୟ ବା ଘଟଣା)

1. I go to school everyday.



2. Water boils at 100°C.



3. The sun rises in the east.



4. Tigers live in forests.

Present Progressive (ଚାଲୁ ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ)

S + is/am/are + V1ing

1. Action in progress now (ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ ଚାଲୁଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)



2. Temporary action (ଅସ୍ଥାୟୀ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)



3. Something happening over a period of time (କିଛି ସମୟ ଧରି ଚାଲୁଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)



4. A definite arrangement in the near future (ନିକଟ ଭବିଷ୍ୟତର ସୁନିଶ୍ଚିତ ଯୋଜନା)

1. It is raining now.



2. Today he is going there on foot.



3. My brother is searching for a job.



4. The Prime Minister is coming tomorrow.

Past Simple (ସାଧାରଣ ଅତୀତ)

S + V2

1. A single act completed in the past (ଅତୀତରେ ସମାପ୍ତ ହୋଇଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)



2. Past habit (ଅତୀତର ଅଭ୍ୟାସ)



3. Past action over a period of time (ଅତୀତରେ କିଛି ସମୟ ଧରି ଚାଲିଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)

1. India became free in 1947.



2. When I was a child, I used to listen to stories.



3. The British ruled over India for many years.

Past Progressive (ଚାଲୁ ଅତୀତ)

S + was/were + V1ing

1. An action that was in progress in the past (ଅତୀତରେ ଚାଲୁଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)



2. A temporary action in the past (ଅତୀତର ଅସ୍ଥାୟୀ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)

1. I was cooking when Sita came.



2. It was raining yesterday.

3. Perfect & Perfect Progressive Forms

Tense Form (କାଳର ରୂପ)

Rule (ନିୟମ)

Uses (ବ୍ୟବହାର)

Examples (ଉଦାହରଣ)

Present Perfect (ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ)

S + has/have + V3

1. A past action with present relevance (ଅତୀତ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ ଯାହାର ଫଳ ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ ଅଛି)



2. Used to denote a period of time with 'for'.

1. I have (already) done my homework.



2. I have lived in Delhi for a week.

Past Perfect (ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ଅତୀତ)

S + had + V3

1. An "older action" in the past (ଅତୀତର ଦୁଇଟି କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ ମଧ୍ୟରୁ ପୁରୁଣା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)



2. Used for the action that happened before another past simple action.

1. When we reached the station, the train had left.



2. We had visited Puri last year.

Present Perfect



Progressive (ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ଚାଲୁ ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ)

S + has/have + been + V1ing

1. An action that started in the past and is still continuing (ଅତୀତରୁ ଆରମ୍ଭ ହୋଇ ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ ଚାଲୁଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)



2. Used with 'since' (point of time) or 'for' (period of time).

1. It has been raining since last night.



2. I have been living here for five years.

Past Perfect



Progressive (ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ଚାଲୁ ଅତୀତ)

S + had + been + V1ing

1. A past action in progress for some time when another action took place (ଅତୀତରେ ଏକ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ କିଛି ସମୟ ଧରି ଚାଲୁଥିଲା ଯେତେବେଳେ ଅନ୍ୟ ଏକ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ ଘଟିଲା).

1. They had been driving for two hours when their car broke down.

4. Ways of Expressing Future Time (ଭବିଷ୍ୟତ ସମୟକୁ ପ୍ରକାଶ କରିବା)

English uses several present tense forms and modals to express future time.

Method (Form) (ପଦ୍ଧତି)

Use (ବ୍ୟବହାର)

Example (ଉଦାହରଣ)

Modals (will + V1)

1. Simple, unplanned future action (ସାଧାରଣ, ଅଣ-ଯୋଜନାବଦ୍ଧ ଭବିଷ୍ୟତ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ).

1. Wait a minute, I will come with you.

Be going to + V1

1. Future fulfillment of a present cause/indication (ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନର କାରଣ ବା ସୂଚନା).



2. Present intention or decision (ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନର ଉଦ୍ଦେଶ୍ୟ ବା ନିଷ୍ପତ୍ତି).

1. There are dark clouds. It is going to rain.



2. We are going to leave for Puri tomorrow.

Present Progressive (is/am/are + V1ing)

1. A planned or arranged future action (ଯୋଜନାବଦ୍ଧ ଭବିଷ୍ୟତ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ).

1. The Chief Minister is meeting the Governor tomorrow.



2. We are going to Puri on Monday.

Present Simple (V1 s/es)

1. An official programme or fact (ସରକାରୀ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକ୍ରମ ବା ସତ୍ୟ).

1. The match begins at 4.30 pm.



2. Tomorrow is holiday.

Be about to + V1

1. Something imminent (ନିକଟ ଭବିଷ୍ୟତରେ ଘଟିବାକୁ ଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ).

1. We are about to leave now.

Future Progressive (will be + V1ing)

1. An action to be in progress in the future (ଭବିଷ୍ୟତରେ ଚାଲୁ ରହିବାକୁ ଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ).

1. I will be writing the letter tomorrow.

Future Perfect (will have + V3)

1. An action to be completed in the future (ଭବିଷ୍ୟତରେ ସମାପ୍ତ ହୋଇସାରିଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ).

1. She will have completed her task by tomorrow.

⏳ 1. Time vs. Tense: The Foundation (ସମୟ ଏବଂ କାଳ)

To master grammar, we must first understand the difference between time and tense.

  • Time is a continuous flow measured in seconds, minutes, hours, days, or years. (ସମୟ ହେଉଛି ଏକ ନିରନ୍ତର ପ୍ରବାହ ଯାହାକୁ ଆମେ ସେକେଣ୍ଡ, ମିନିଟ୍, ଘଣ୍ଟା, ଦିନ କିମ୍ବା ବର୍ଷରେ ମାପିଥାଉ।)

  • Tense is a grammatical concept specific to a particular language. (କାଳ ବା Tense ହେଉଛି ଏକ ବ୍ୟାକରଣଗତ ଧାରଣା ଯାହା କେବଳ ଏକ ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ ଭାଷା ପାଇଁ ସୀମିତ।)

  • The tense system in English is not similar to that of any other language such as Odia or Sanskrit. (ଇଂରାଜୀ ଭାଷାର କାଳ ନିୟମ ଓଡ଼ିଆ କିମ୍ବା ସଂସ୍କୃତ ଭଳି ଅନ୍ୟ କୌଣସି ଭାଷା ସହିତ ସମାନ ନୁହେଁ।)

  • English language has only two defined tenses of verbs: present and past. (ଇଂରାଜୀ ଭାଷାରେ କ୍ରିୟାର କେବଳ ଦୁଇଟି କାଳ ଅଛି: ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ ଏବଂ ଅତୀତ।)

  • With the help of these two tenses we express three phases of time: past, present and future. (ଏହି ଦୁଇଟି କାଳ ସାହାଯ୍ୟରେ ଆମେ ସମୟର ତିନୋଟି ପର୍ଯ୍ୟାୟ ପ୍ରକାଶ କରୁ: ଅତୀତ, ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ ଏବଂ ଭବିଷ୍ୟତ।)

💡 Master Trick: Never say "Future Tense" in English! Always remember that "Future" is a time, not a verb tense. We use present tense verbs or helper verbs (like will/shall) to talk about future time.

🟢 2. Ways of Expressing the Present Time (ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ ସମୟକୁ ପ୍ରକାଶ କରିବାର ଉପାୟ)

We use specific tense forms to talk about things happening in the present time.

📌 A. Present Simple

Formula: Subject + Base Verb (V1) or Verb+s/es (V5) + Object

  • We use the present simple for habitual action.

    • Example: I go to school everyday. (ମୁଁ ପ୍ରତିଦିନ ସ୍କୁଲକୁ ଯାଏ।)

  • We use it for laws of nature.

    • Example: The sun rises in the east. (ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟ ପୂର୍ବ ଦିଗରେ ଉଦିତ ହୁଅନ୍ତି।)

  • We use it for general or scientific truths.

    • Example 1: Tigers live in forests. (ବାଘମାନେ ଜଙ୍ଗଲରେ ବାସ କରନ୍ତି।)

    • Example 2: Water boils at 100°C. (ଜଳ ୧୦୦ ଡିଗ୍ରୀ ସେଲସିୟସରେ ଫୁଟେ।)

  • We use it for a present state or present fact.

    • Example 1: I feel hungry. (ମୋତେ ଭୋକ ଲାଗୁଛି।)

    • Example 2: I study at this school. (ମୁଁ ଏହି ବିଦ୍ୟାଳୟରେ ପଢେ।)

  • We use it for a permanent state or permanent fact.

    • Example 1: Permanent snow covers the Antarctica. (ଚିରସ୍ଥାୟୀ ବରଫ ଆଣ୍ଟାର୍କଟିକାକୁ ଆବୃତ କରିଥାଏ।)

    • Example 2: Bhubaneswar is the capital of Odisha. (ଭୁବନେଶ୍ୱର ଓଡ଼ିଶାର ରାଜଧାନୀ ଅଟେ।)

🧠 Ninja Trick for Present Simple: If a sentence has words like everyday, always, usually, generally, it is almost always a habitual action and takes the Present Simple form!

📌 B. Present Progressive (Continuous)

Formula: Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing + Object

  • We use the present progressive for something happening now.

    • Example: It is raining (now). (ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ ବର୍ଷା ହେଉଛି।)

  • We use it for something happening over a period of time.

    • Example: My brother is searching for a job. (ମୋ ଭାଇ ଏକ ଚାକିରି ଖୋଜୁଛନ୍ତି।)

  • We use it for a temporary action.

    • Example: He usually goes to school on his bicycle, but today he is going there on foot. (ସେ ସାଧାରଣତଃ ନିଜ ସାଇକେଲରେ ସ୍କୁଲକୁ ଯାଏ, କିନ୍ତୁ ଆଜି ସେ ଚାଲି ଚାଲି ଯାଉଛି।)

🔴 3. Ways of Expressing the Past Time (ଅତୀତ ସମୟକୁ ପ୍ରକାଶ କରିବାର ଉପାୟ)

How do we talk about actions that have already finished? We have six distinct ways!

📌 A. Present Perfect

Formula: Subject + has/have + Past Participle (V3) + Object

  • We use the present perfect for a past action with present relevance. (ଅତୀତରେ ସରିଥିବା କାମ ଯାହାର ପ୍ରଭାବ ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ ଉପରେ ଅଛି।)

    • Example: I have (already) done my homework. (ମୁଁ ପୂର୍ବରୁ ମୋର ଘର କାମ ସାରିଦେଇଛି।)

📌 B. Present Perfect Progressive

Formula: Subject + has/have + been + Verb-ing + Object

  • We use this for something that started in the past, continuing at present and likely to continue in the future.

    • Example: I am out of breath because I have been running round the playground. (ମୁଁ ଧଇଁସଇଁ ହୋଇଯାଇଛି କାରଣ ମୁଁ ପଡ଼ିଆ ଚାରିପଟେ ଦୌଡୁଥିଲି।)

📌 C. Past Simple

Formula: Subject + Past Verb (V2) + Object

  • We use the past simple for a past action that happened in a moment, or a past action that happened over a period of time.

    • Example 1: India became free in 1947. (୧୯୪୭ ମସିହାରେ ଭାରତ ସ୍ୱାଧୀନ ହେଲା।)

    • Example 2: The British ruled over India for many years. (ଇଂରେଜମାନେ ବହୁ ବର୍ଷ ଧରି ଭାରତ ଶାସନ କରିଥିଲେ।)

📌 D. Past Progressive

Formula: Subject + was/were + Verb-ing + Object

  • We use the past progressive for an action in progress in the past.

    • Example: I was studying in class IX last year. (ଗତ ବର୍ଷ ମୁଁ ନବମ ଶ୍ରେଣୀରେ ପଢୁଥିଲି।)

📌 E. Past Perfect

Formula: Subject + had + Past Participle (V3) + Object

  • We use the past perfect for an older action in the past.

    • Example Part 1: When we reached the station, the train had left. (ଆମେ ଷ୍ଟେସନରେ ପହଞ୍ଚିବା ବେଳକୁ ଟ୍ରେନ୍ ଛାଡି ଦେଇଥିଲା।)

    • Example Part 2: So, we went to our village by bus. (ତେଣୁ, ଆମେ ବସରେ ଆମ ଗାଁକୁ ଗଲୁ।)

🧠 Ninja Trick for Past Perfect: If two things happened in the past, the older thing (first action) takes Past Perfect, and the newer thing (second action) takes Past Simple!

📌 F. Past Perfect Progressive

Formula: Subject + had + been + Verb-ing + Object

  • We use this for a past action in progress for some time when another action took place.

    • Example: They had been driving for two hours when their car broke down. (ଗାଡ଼ି ଖରାପ ହେବା ବେଳକୁ ସେମାନେ ଦୁଇ ଘଣ୍ଟା ଧରି ଗାଡ଼ି ଚଲାଉଥିଲେ।)

🔵 4. Ways of Expressing the Future Time (ଭବିଷ୍ୟତ ସମୟକୁ ପ୍ରକାଶ କରିବାର ଉପାୟ)

Future actions have no specific "tense" verb, so we combine different present tense rules to talk about tomorrow!

📌 A. With the Help of Modals (will/shall)

Formula: Subject + will/shall + Base Verb (V1) + Object

  • We use modals for simple future of an unplanned action. (ହଠାତ୍ ନିଆଯାଇଥିବା ନିଷ୍ପତ୍ତି ପାଇଁ।)

    • Example: Wait a minute. I will come with you. (ଟିକେ ଅପେକ୍ଷା କର। ମୁଁ ତୁମ ସହିତ ଆସିବି।)

📌 B. Be going to + Verb

Formula: Subject + am/is/are + going to + Base Verb (V1) + Object

  • We use this for future fulfilment of present cause or indication.

    • Example: There are dark clouds in the sky. It is going to rain. (ଆକାଶରେ କଳା ବାଦଲ ଅଛି। ବର୍ଷା ହେବାକୁ ଯାଉଛି।)

  • We use this for a future action with present intention or decision.

    • Example: We are going to leave for Puri tomorrow morning. (ଆମେ ଆସନ୍ତାକାଲି ସକାଳେ ପୁରୀ ବାହାରିବାକୁ ଯାଉଛୁ।)

📌 C. Present Progressive for Future Time

Formula: Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing + Object

  • We use this for a planned or arranged programme of a future action. (ପୂର୍ବରୁ ଯୋଜନା କରାଯାଇଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକ୍ରମ।)

    • Example: The Chief Minister is meeting the Governor tomorrow morning. (ମୁଖ୍ୟମନ୍ତ୍ରୀ ଆସନ୍ତାକାଲି ସକାଳେ ରାଜ୍ୟପାଳଙ୍କୁ ଭେଟୁଛନ୍ତି।)

📌 D. Present Simple for Future Time

Formula: Subject + Base Verb (V1) / Verb+s/es (V5) + Object

  • We use the present simple for an official programme (where future is seen as a fact). (ସରକାରୀ ବା ନିର୍ଦ୍ଧାରିତ ସମୟସୂଚୀ ପାଇଁ।)

    • Example 1: Tomorrow is Sunday. (ଆସନ୍ତାକାଲି ରବିବାର ଅଟେ।)

    • Example 2: The match begins at 4.30 pm. (ମ୍ୟାଚ୍ ଅପରାହ୍ନ ୪.୩୦ ରେ ଆରମ୍ଭ ହେବ।)

📌 E. Other Advanced Future Forms

  • Be about to: Used for something imminent. (ଖୁବ୍ ଶୀଘ୍ର ଘଟିବାକୁ ଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ।)

  • Will be V-ing (Future Progressive): Used for an action to be in progress in the future. (ଭବିଷ୍ୟତରେ ଚାଲୁ ରହିବାକୁ ଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ।)

  • Will have V-en (Future Perfect): Used for an action to be completed in the future. (ଭବିଷ୍ୟତରେ ଶେଷ ହୋଇସାରିଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ।)
