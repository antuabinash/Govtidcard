38.

ତୁମେ ଯାହା କହୁଛ ମୁଁ ତାହା ବିଶ୍ୱାସ କରେ।

41.

ଯେଉଁମାନେ ନିଜକୁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରନ୍ତି, ଭଗବାନ ସେମାନଙ୍କୁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରନ୍ତି।

Complex

42.

He must run fast to catch the train.

ଟ୍ରେନ୍ ଧରିବା ପାଇଁ ତାଙ୍କୁ ନିଶ୍ଚୟ ଜୋରରେ ଦୌଡିବାକୁ ପଡିବ।

43.

ସେ ଅସୁସ୍ଥ ଥିଲେ, ତେଣୁ ସେ ଡାକ୍ତରଙ୍କ ପାଖକୁ ଗଲେ।

F. Sentence Combination (Compound)

Instructions (English): Combine the following pairs of simple sentences into a single Compound sentence.

Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ସରଳ ବାକ୍ୟ ଯୋଡିଗୁଡିକୁ ଏକକ ଯୌଗିକ ବାକ୍ୟରେ ପରିଣତ କରନ୍ତୁ।

Simple Sentences (ସରଳ ବାକ୍ୟ)

Combined Compound Sentence (ଯୌଗିକ ବାକ୍ୟ)

76. He is poor. He is honest. (ସେ ଗରିବ। ସେ ସଚ୍ଚୋଟ।)

He is poor, but he is honest.

77. Make haste. You will miss the train. (ଶୀଘ୍ର କର। ତୁମେ ଟ୍ରେନ୍ ପାଇବ ନାହିଁ।)

Make haste, or you will miss the train.

78. He was ill. He could not attend the meeting. (ସେ ଅସୁସ୍ଥ ଥିଲେ। ସେ ସଭାରେ ଯୋଗ ଦେଇପାରିଲେ ନାହିଁ।)

He was ill, so he could not attend the meeting.

79. He is rich. He is not happy. (ସେ ଧନୀ। ସେ ଖୁସି ନୁହଁନ୍ତି।)

He is rich, yet he is not happy.

80. He took the book. He read it. (ସେ ବହିଟି ନେଲେ। ସେ ଏହାକୁ ପଢିଲେ।)

He took the book and read it.

81. He must confess his fault. He will be punished. (ସେ ତାଙ୍କ ଦୋଷ ସ୍ୱୀକାର କରିବା ଉଚିତ୍। ସେ ଦଣ୍ଡିତ ହେବେ।)

He must confess his fault, otherwise he will be punished.

82. He is not a fool. He is not a knave. (ସେ ମୂର୍ଖ ନୁହଁନ୍ତି। ସେ ଠକ ନୁହଁନ୍ତି।)

He is neither a fool nor a knave.

83. He is intelligent. He is hardworking. (ସେ ବୁଦ୍ଧିମାନ। ସେ ପରିଶ୍ରମୀ।)

He is not only intelligent but also hardworking.

84. You can take this book. You can leave it. (ତୁମେ ଏ ବହିଟି ନେଇପାର। ତୁମେ ଏହାକୁ ଛାଡିଦେଇପାର।)

You can either take this book or leave it.

85. He was horrified. He saw blood. (ସେ ଭୟଭୀତ ହୋଇଗଲେ। ସେ ରକ୍ତ ଦେଖିଲେ।)

He saw blood, so he was horrified. (or) He was horrified, for he saw blood.


G. Sentence Combination (Complex)

Instructions (English): Combine the following pairs of simple sentences into a single Complex sentence.

Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ସରଳ ବାକ୍ୟ ଯୋଡିଗୁଡିକୁ ଏକକ ଜଟିଳ ବାକ୍ୟରେ ପରିଣତ କରନ୍ତୁ।

Simple Sentences (ସରଳ ବାକ୍ୟ)

Combined Complex Sentence (ଜଟିଳ ବାକ୍ୟ)

86. He said something. I did not hear it. (ସେ କିଛି କହିଲେ। ମୁଁ ତାହା ଶୁଣିପାରିଲି ନାହିଁ।)

I did not hear what he said.

87. I met a man. He was blind. (ମୁଁ ଜଣେ ଲୋକକୁ ଭେଟିଲି। ସେ ଅନ୍ଧ ଥିଲେ।)

I met a man who was blind.

88. He worked hard. He wanted to win the prize. (ସେ କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ କଲେ। ସେ ପୁରସ୍କାର ଜିତିବାକୁ ଚାହୁଁଥିଲେ।)

He worked hard so that he could win the prize.

89. He is very weak. He cannot walk. (ସେ ବହୁତ ଦୁର୍ବଳ। ସେ ଚାଲିପାରିବେ ନାହିଁ।)

He is so weak that he cannot walk.

90. The rain stopped. We went out. (ବର୍ଷା ଛାଡିଗଲା। ଆମେ ବାହାରକୁ ଗଲୁ।)

When the rain stopped, we went out.

91. I know the boy. He stood first. (ମୁଁ ପିଲାଟିକୁ ଜାଣେ। ସେ ପ୍ରଥମ ହୋଇଛି।)

I know the boy who stood first.

92. He is rich. He is not happy. (ସେ ଧନୀ। ସେ ଖୁସି ନୁହଁନ୍ତି।)

Although he is rich, he is not happy.

93. This is the place. I was born here. (ଏହା ସେହି ସ୍ଥାନ। ମୁଁ ଏଠାରେ ଜନ୍ମ ହୋଇଥିଲି।)

This is the place where I was born.

94. Do not eat too much. You will be ill. (ଅଧିକ ଖାଅ ନାହିଁ। ତୁମେ ଅସୁସ୍ଥ ହୋଇପଡିବ।)

If you eat too much, you will be ill.

95. I have a pen. Its colour is blue. (ମୋର ଏକ କଲମ ଅଛି। ଏହାର ରଙ୍ଗ ନୀଳ।)

I have a pen whose colour is blue.

Main Clause (M.C.)

I. Fill in the Blanks

Instructions (English): Fill in the blanks with a suitable coordinator (and, but, or, so, yet, for) or subordinator (who, which, that, when, where, if, though, because, as).

Instructions (Odia): ଉପଯୁକ୍ତ coordinator (and, but, or, so, yet, for) କିମ୍ବା subordinator (who, which, that, when, where, if, though, because, as) ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ଶୂନ୍ୟସ୍ଥାନ ପୂରଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

  1. He is poor, _____ he is honest.

Answer: but

  1. I don't know _____ he will come.

Answer: if / when

  1. This is the place _____ the accident happened.

Answer: where

  1. He worked hard, _____ he failed.

Answer: yet / but

  1. Work hard, _____ you will fail.

Answer: or

  1. I was ill, _____ I could not go.

Answer: so

  1. He is the man _____ stole my watch.

Answer: who

  1. _____ he is rich, he is a miser.

Answer: Though

  1. I missed the train _____ I was late.

Answer: because

  1. He bought the book _____ was on the table.

Answer
