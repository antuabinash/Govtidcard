'ଏ ବୋଟଲ୍ ଅଫ୍ ଡ୍ୟୁ' (A Bottle of Dew) କାହାଣୀ ଆଧାରରେ ସଂଳାପ ସହିତ ଏକ ସ୍କ୍ରିପ୍ଟ ପ୍ରସ୍ତୁତ କର ଏବଂ ଏହାକୁ ଅଭିନୟ କର।

Rama Natha: Pranam, Gurudev.

Mahipati: Pranam, what brings you here?

Rama Natha: I want to know about the magic potion that turns objects into gold. Can you tell me?

Mahipati: Yes, I know how to make it. Sit down.

Rama Natha: Thank you! Please tell me the secret.

Mahipati: You must plant a banana plantation, water it regularly with your own hands, and collect five liters of morning dew in winter. Bring it to me, and I will chant the magic words over it. 🎭
