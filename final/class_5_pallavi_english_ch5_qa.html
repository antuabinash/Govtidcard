💡 Book Q & A

My Colourful World : The Rainbow – Book Q A Class 5 English Pallavi

🌈 Let us Discuss

⛵ 1. What sail on rivers? (ନଦୀରେ କ'ଣ ଭାସେ?)

Answer: Boats sail on the rivers.

🚢 2. What sail on the seas? (ସମୁଦ୍ରରେ କ'ଣ ଭାସେ?)

Answer: Ships sail on the seas.

☁️ 3. What sail across the sky? (ଆକାଶରେ କ'ଣ ଭାସିବୁଲେ?)

Answer: Clouds sail across the sky.

⛴️ 4. What are the boats and ships compared to?

(ଡଙ୍ଗା ଏବଂ ଜାହାଜକୁ କାହା ସହିତ ତୁଳନା କରାଯାଇଛି?) 

Answer: Boats and ships are compared to the clouds sailing across the sky.

🌊 5. What are the rivers and seas compared to?

(ନଦୀ ଏବଂ ସମୁଦ୍ରକୁ କାହା ସହିତ ତୁଳନା କରାଯାଇଛି?)

 Answer: The rivers and seas are compared to the sky.

⛅ 6. Which are prettier – the boats sailing on rivers or the clouds sailing across the sky?

(କେଉଁଟି ଅଧିକ ସୁନ୍ଦର - ନଦୀରେ ଭାସୁଥିବା ଡଙ୍ଗା ନା ଆକାଶରେ ଭାସୁଥିବା ବାଦଲ?) 

Answer: The clouds sailing across the sky are prettier.

🌉 7. Where are the bridges? Do they look pretty?

 (ପୋଲଗୁଡ଼ିକ କେଉଁଠାରେ ଅଛି? ସେଗୁଡ଼ିକ ସୁନ୍ଦର ଦେଖାଯାଏ କି?)

Answer: The bridges are on the rivers. Yes, they look pretty.

🏹 8. What does 'the bow' in the poem refer to?

(କବିତାରେ 'the bow' ବା ଧନୁ କାହାକୁ ବୁଝାଉଛି?) 

Answer: 'The bow' in the poem refers to the rainbow.

🛣️ 9. What builds a road from earth to sky?

(ପୃଥିବୀରୁ ଆକାଶକୁ କିଏ ରାସ୍ତା ତିଆରି କରେ?) 

Answer: The rainbow (the bow) builds a road from earth to sky.

🌈 10. What is the rainbow compared to? (ଇନ୍ଦ୍ରଧନୁକୁ କାହା ସହିତ ତୁଳନା କରାଯାଇଛି?)

Answer: The rainbow is compared to a bridge spanning heaven and a road from earth to sky.

✨ 11. Which is prettier – the bridge on a river or the rainbow across the sky?

(କେଉଁଟି ଅଧିକ ସୁନ୍ଦର - ନଦୀ ଉପରେ ଥିବା ପୋଲ ନା ଆକାଶରେ ଥିବା ଇନ୍ଦ୍ରଧନୁ?) 

Answer: The rainbow across the sky is far prettier.

🌳 12. Which things in the poem are made by nature?

(କବିତାରେ ଥିବା କେଉଁ ଜିନିଷଗୁଡ଼ିକ ପ୍ରକୃତି ଦ୍ୱାରା ତିଆରି?)

 Answer: Rivers, seas, sky, clouds, trees, earth, heaven, and the rainbow are made by nature.

🏗️ 13. Which things in the poem are man-made?

(କବିତାରେ ଥିବା କେଉଁ ଜିନିଷଗୁଡ଼ିକ ମଣିଷ ଦ୍ୱାରା ତିଆରି?) 

Answer: Boats, ships, and bridges are man-made.

🌍 14. Which things are more beautiful – the things made by man or the things made by nature?

 (କେଉଁ ଜିନିଷଗୁଡ଼ିକ ଅଧିକ ସୁନ୍ଦର - ମଣିଷ ତିଆରି କରିଥିବା ଜିନିଷ ନା ପ୍ରକୃତି ତିଆରି କରିଥିବା ଜିନିଷ?)

 Answer: The things made by nature are far more beautiful.

Answer: False

⛵ 2️⃣ (b) Boats sail on the sea.

(ଡଙ୍ଗାଗୁଡ଼ିକ ସମୁଦ୍ରରେ ଯାତ୍ରା କରନ୍ତି |)

Answer: False

🌉 3️⃣ (c) Bridges are built on rivers.

(ନଦୀଗୁଡ଼ିକ ଉପରେ ପୋଲ ନିର୍ମାଣ କରାଯାଇଥାଏ |)

Answer: True

🛣️ 4️⃣ (d) A road is built from earth to sky.

(ପୃଥିବୀରୁ ଆକାଶକୁ ଏକ ରାସ୍ତା ନିର୍ମାଣ କରାଯାଇଥାଏ |)

Answer: True
