👩‍🏫 About the Author: Enid Blyton

✍️ Short Biography: Enid Mary Blyton (1897–1968) was a highly successful English children's writer. Her books have been among the world's best-sellers since the 1930s. She wrote on many topics including nature, mystery, and magic, aiming to entertain children while teaching them valuable life lessons.

📚 Famous Works: She is famous for writing hugely popular book series like "The Famous Five", "The Secret Seven", and "Noddy".

🖋️ Writing Style: Enid Blyton's writing style is extremely simple, highly rhythmic, and very appealing to young readers. Her poems and stories always focus on everyday childhood experiences and teach strong moral values like kindness, honesty, and the joy of sharing.

📜 Original Stanza: She had some sweets that she wouldn't share. She had a book that she wouldn't lend. She wouldn't let anyone play with the doll. She's nobody's friend!

📝 Odia Meaning (All Sentences): ତା' ପାଖରେ କିଛି ମିଠା ଥିଲା ଯାହା ସେ କାହା ସହ ବାଣ୍ଟିବାକୁ ଚାହୁଁନଥିଲା। ତା' ପାଖରେ ଗୋଟିଏ ବହି ଥିଲା ଯାହା ସେ କାହାକୁ ପଢ଼ିବାକୁ ଦେଉନଥିଲା। ସେ ନିଜ କଣ୍ଢେଇ ସହ କାହାକୁ ଖେଳିବାକୁ ଦେଉନଥିଲା। ସେ କାହାର ବି ସାଙ୍ଗ ନୁହେଁ!

💡 English Meaning: There was a little girl who had some sweets, a book, and a beautiful doll, but she refused to share them with anyone. Because she was selfish and didn't want to play together, she had absolutely no friends.

📝 Summary (English Only)

The poem "Nobody's Friend" by Enid Blyton beautifully highlights the importance of sharing and kindness. In the first stanza, the poet talks about a little girl who possesses sweets, a book, and a doll, but she stubbornly refuses to share any of them with others. Because of her highly selfish nature, she ends up having no friends at all. In the second stanza, the poet describes a young boy who eats all his tasty toffees completely by himself. He strictly does not let anyone ride his tricycle or play with his toy train. Just like the selfish girl, his unwillingness to share leaves him completely lonely as nobody's friend.

In the final stanza, the poet introduces a wonderful and completely different attitude. This new child is generous and happily offers to share their sweets, ball, books, and games with any child who wants to play. They are so kind that they even offer half of their own apple and half of their cake. By cheerfully sharing their belongings, this child becomes a beloved friend to everyone around them. The core message of the poem is that sharing is caring, and we must always share our things with others if we wish to make true friends.

📝 Summary (Odia)

"ନୋବଡିଜ୍ ଫ୍ରେଣ୍ଡ୍" (କାହାର ବି ସାଙ୍ଗ ନୁହେଁ) କବିତାଟି ଅନ୍ୟମାନଙ୍କ ସହ ଜିନିଷ ବାଣ୍ଟିବାର ଗୁରୁତ୍ୱ ବିଷୟରେ ଏକ ସୁନ୍ଦର ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କରେ। ପ୍ରଥମ ପଦରେ କବି ଜଣେ ବାଳିକା ବିଷୟରେ କହିଛନ୍ତି ଯାହା ପାଖରେ ମିଠା, ବହି ଏବଂ କଣ୍ଢେଇ ଥାଏ, କିନ୍ତୁ ସେ କାହାକୁ ଦିଏ ନାହିଁ। ତା'ର ସ୍ୱାର୍ଥପର ସ୍ୱଭାବ ଯୋଗୁଁ ତା'ର କୌଣସି ସାଙ୍ଗ ନଥାନ୍ତି ଏବଂ ସେ ଏକୁଟିଆ ରହିଯାଏ। ଦ୍ୱିତୀୟ ପଦରେ ଜଣେ ବାଳକ ବିଷୟରେ କୁହାଯାଇଛି ଯିଏ ନିଜର ସବୁ ଟଫି ଏକୁଟିଆ ଖାଇଯାଏ ଏବଂ ନିଜର ତିନି ଚକିଆ ସାଇକେଲ୍ ଓ ଖେଳନା ଟ୍ରେନ୍ ସହ କାହାକୁ ଖେଳିବାକୁ ଦିଏ ନାହିଁ। ତେଣୁ ସେ ମଧ୍ୟ କାହାର ସାଙ୍ଗ ହୋଇପାରେ ନାହିଁ।

ଶେଷ ପଦରେ, କବି ଏକ ଭିନ୍ନ ସ୍ୱଭାବର ପିଲା ବିଷୟରେ କହିଛନ୍ତି। ଏହି ପିଲାଟି ବହୁତ ଉଦାର ଏବଂ ନିଜର ମିଠା, ବଲ୍, ବହି ଓ ଖେଳନା ଅନ୍ୟମାନଙ୍କ ସହ ବାଣ୍ଟିବାକୁ ଖୁସିରେ ରାଜି ଥାଏ। ଏପରିକି ସେ ନିଜ ସେଓ ଓ କେକ୍ ରୁ ଅଧା ଦେବାକୁ ମଧ୍ୟ ପ୍ରସ୍ତୁତ ଥାଏ। ନିଜ ଜିନିଷ ଅନ୍ୟମାନଙ୍କ ସହ ଖୁସିରେ ବାଣ୍ଟୁଥିବାରୁ, ସେ ସମସ୍ତଙ୍କର ପ୍ରିୟ ସାଙ୍ଗ ହୋଇପାରିଛି। କବିତାର ମୁଖ୍ୟ ଶିକ୍ଷା ହେଉଛି ଯେ ଆମେ ନିଜ ଜିନିଷ ଅନ୍ୟମାନଙ୍କ ସହ ବାଣ୍ଟିଲେ ହିଁ ପ୍ରକୃତ ସାଙ୍ଗ ପାଇପାରିବା। ସହଭାଗିତା ହିଁ ପ୍ରକୃତ ବନ୍ଧୁତାର ମୂଳଦୁଆ।

💡 Suggestion for Teachers: How to Teach This Chapter

✨ Interactive Role-Play: Select three students to act out the three different children in the poem. Give them physical props (a book, a toy train, an apple). This visual dramatization helps young learners perfectly understand the stark difference between a selfish child and a generous child.

✨ Classroom "Sharing Day" Activity: Organize a special day where students are encouraged to bring an extra pencil, eraser, or a small snack to share with their desk partner. Ask them to express how happy it made them feel to share.

✨ Phonics & Vocabulary Practice: Use the carefully graded dictation words (Easy to Hard) provided above to conduct a fun spelling bee. Ask students to pronounce the words out loud using the Odia phonetic script to ensure correct English pronunciation.

✨ Group Discussion on Values: Draw a T-chart on the blackboard. Label one side "Selfish Actions" and the other "Friendly Actions". Ask the class to categorize the behaviors mentioned in the poem to reinforce the moral values seamlessly.
