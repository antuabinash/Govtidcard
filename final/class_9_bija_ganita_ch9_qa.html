✅ ଉତ୍ତର:

∴\therefore∴ ଗ.ସା.ଗୁ. =(x+1)= (x + 1)=(x+1)

(ii)

∴\therefore∴ ଗ.ସା.ଗୁ. =(a−b)= (a - b)=(a−b)
