✍️ Activity 2

Instructions (English): Break the following sentences into two simple sentences each.

Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ଦୁଇଟି ଲେଖାଏଁ ସରଳ ବାକ୍ୟରେ ବିଭକ୍ତ କରନ୍ତୁ।

(a) I want my friend to get the medal.

Answer: I want (something). My friend will get the medal.

(b) She helped me do the exercise.

Answer: She helped me. I did the exercise.

(c) I hate telling lies.

Answer: I hate (something). I tell lies.

(d) I expect to write a story by tomorrow.

Answer: I expect (something). I will write a story by tomorrow.

(e) I expect you to write a story by tomorrow.

Answer: I expect (something). You will write a story by tomorrow.

(f) Our teacher made us write the story.

Answer: Our teacher made us (do something). We wrote the story.

✍️ Activity 4

Instructions (English): Complete the following sentences by adding a non-finite clause using the words given in the brackets.

Instructions (Odia): ବନ୍ଧନୀରେ ଦିଆଯାଇଥିବା ଶବ୍ଦଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ଏକ non-finite clause ଯୋଗ କରି ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

(a) They made ___________ (I/do/it)

Answer: They made me do it.

(b) The gardener won't let ___________ (they/touch/the flowers)

Answer: The gardener won't let them touch the flowers.

(c) We heard ___________ (the beggar/curse/the rude boys)

Answer: We heard the beggar curse the rude boys.

(d) The clerk doesn't allow ___________ (anyone/see/the files)

Answer: The clerk doesn't allow anyone to see the files.

(e) Please remind ___________ (he/write/the letter)

Answer: Please remind him to write the letter.


✍️ Activity 5

Instructions (English): Complete the following sentences using the non-finite verbs given in brackets. Some of these non-finite verbs may go with a noun phrase of your choice.

Instructions (Odia): ବନ୍ଧନୀରେ ଦିଆଯାଇଥିବା non-finite verbs ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ କରନ୍ତୁ। ଆବଶ୍ୟକ ସ୍ଥଳେ ଆପଣ ଏକ noun phrase ବ୍ୟବହାର କରିପାରନ୍ତି।

(a) The teacher doesn't let ___________ (make) a noise in the class.

Answer: The teacher doesn't let us make a noise in the class.

(b) I would like ___________ (meet) my uncle.

Answer: I would like to meet my uncle.

(c) Hot weather makes ___________ (feel) uncomfortable.

Answer: Hot weather makes me feel uncomfortable.

(d) Please remind ___________ (phone) Ashok tomorrow.

Answer: Please remind me to phone Ashok tomorrow.

(e) The film was very sad. It made ___________ (cry).

Answer: It made us cry.

(f) Lata's parents have always encouraged ___________ (study).

Answer: Lata's parents have always encouraged her to study.

(g) He would like ___________ (come) to the party.

Answer: He would like to come to the party.


✍️ Activity 6

Instructions (English): Fill in the blanks with to + verb, ∅ + verb (bare infinitive), or verb-ing.

Instructions (Odia): to + verb, ∅ + verb (bare infinitive), କିମ୍ବା verb-ing ଦ୍ୱାରା ଶୂନ୍ୟସ୍ଥାନ ପୂରଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

(a) I enjoy ________ (play) on the river bank.

Answer: playing

(b) Where do you want ________ (go)?

Answer: to go

(c) I learnt ________ (swim) when I was eight years old.

Answer: to swim

(d) I am trying ________ (study). Please stop ________ (talk).

Answer: to study ... talking

(e) I don't mind ________ (travel) by bus but I prefer ________ (travel) by train.

Answer: travelling ... to travel

(f) She would not let me ________ (read) the letter.

Answer: read

(g) Good bye. I hope ________ (see) you soon.

Answer: to see

Instructions (Odia): [climb, do, go, cry, clean, come, walk, laugh, sleep, hear] ତାଲିକାରୁ ଉପଯୁକ୍ତ କ୍ରିୟା ବାଛି to + verb କିମ୍ବା ∅ + verb (bare infinitive) ଦ୍ୱାରା ଶୂନ୍ୟସ୍ଥାନ ପୂରଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

✍️ Activity 8

Instructions (English): Read the following sentences and the hints are given in the brackets. Write another sentence with a related meaning using the hints given.

Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ଏବଂ ବନ୍ଧନୀରେ ଥିବା ସୂଚନାକୁ ପଢନ୍ତୁ। ସୂଚନା ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ସମାନ ଅର୍ଥବୋଧକ ଅନ୍ୟ ଏକ ବାକ୍ୟ ଲେଖନ୍ତୁ।

(a) (Example) Please don't tell anyone that I have been punished. (I/not/want/anyone/know)

Answer: I don't want anyone to know about it.

(b) I was sure that Ashok would help me. (I/had/asked/him/help me)

Answer: I had asked him to help me.

(c) "Don't touch that wire", the man said to me. (man/told/not/touch)

Answer: The man told me not to touch that wire.

(d) My father said that I could use his watch. (Father/allow/use/his watch)

Answer: Father allowed me to use his watch.

(e) He told me that it would be good if I told the police about this accident. (He/advise/report/to the police)

Answer: He advised me to report this accident to the police.

(f) There is a football match between Mohan Bagan and East Bengal. (You/want/East Bengal/win?)

Answer: Do you want East Bengal to win?

Instructions (English): Put the verb given in brackets in the proper form, by using to + verb, ∅ + verb (bare infinitive) or verb-ing, whichever is appropriate.

Instructions (Odia): ବନ୍ଧନୀରେ ଥିବା କ୍ରିୟାକୁ to + verb, ∅ + verb (bare infinitive) କିମ୍ବା verb-ing ର ସଠିକ୍ ରୂପରେ ଲେଖନ୍ତୁ।

(d) I prefer ______ (remain) silent in this case.

Answer: to remain

(e) I hate ________ (tell) lies.

Answer: telling

(f) Don't forget ________ (post) the letter.

Answer: to post
