Wit and Wisdom: A Concrete Example

Wit and Wisdom: A Concrete Example – Study Material Class 8 Jasmine English

🖋️ Biography Details

👤 Reginald Arkell (1881–1959) was a well-known British scriptwriter, comic novelist, and poet.

📚 He is best remembered for his humorous and light-hearted observations of everyday life, especially his writings on nature, gardening, and village life.

🎭 His writing style is simple, witty, and highly entertaining, which makes his poems very easy to read and enjoy for students.

🌿 His most famous works include the Green Fingers series of garden poetry, which perfectly captures the funny side of gardening, just like the poem we are reading.

📖 1. Stanza-wise Explanation

🌿 Stanza 1

Original Paragraph:

My next-door neighbour, Mrs. Jones, 

has got a garden full of stones:

A crazy path, a lily pond,

a rockery and, just beyond

A sundial with a strange device,

which Mrs. Jones thinks rather nice.

English Pronunciation in Odia:

ମାଇ ନେକ୍ସଟ୍-ଡୋର୍ ନେଇବର୍, ମିସେସ୍. ଜୋନ୍ସ,

ହାଜ୍ ଗଟ୍ ଏ ଗାର୍ଡେନ୍ ଫୁଲ୍ ଅଫ୍ ଷ୍ଟୋନ୍ସ:

ଏ କ୍ରେଜି ପାଥ୍, ଏ ଲିଲି ପଣ୍ଡ୍,

ଏ ରକେରୀ ଆଣ୍ଡ୍, ଜଷ୍ଟ ବିୟଣ୍ଡ

ଏ ସନ୍‌ଡାଏଲ୍ ୱିଥ୍ ଏ ଷ୍ଟ୍ରେଞ୍ଜ୍ ଡିଭାଇସ୍,

ହ୍ୱିଚ୍ ମିସେସ୍. ଜୋନ୍ସ ଥିଙ୍କସ୍ ରାଦର୍ ନାଇସ୍.

English → Odia Meaning 

🔹 Full → ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ (Sampurna)

🔹 Pond → ପୋଖରୀ (Pokhari)

🔹 Garden → ବଗିଚା (Bagicha)

🔹 Stones → ପଥର (Pathara)

🔹 Beyond → ଆଗକୁ / ସାମ୍ନାରେ (Agaku / Samnare)

🔹 Neighbour → ପଡ଼ୋଶୀ (Padosi)

🔹 Crazy path → ଅଙ୍କାବଙ୍କା ରାସ୍ତା (Ankabanka rasta)

🔹 Rockery → ପଥର ସଜା (Pathara saja)

🔹 Sundial → ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟଘଡ଼ି (Surjyaghadi)

🔹 Strange device → ଅଦ୍ଭୁତ ଯନ୍ତ୍ର (Adbhuta jantra)

Odia Meaning (Translation/Explanation):

ମୋର ପଡ଼ୋଶୀ ଶ୍ରୀମତୀ ଜୋନ୍ସଙ୍କର ଗୋଟିଏ ବଗିଚା ଅଛି ଯାହା ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ପଥରରେ ଭର୍ତ୍ତି । ସେଠାରେ ଏକ ଅଙ୍କାବଙ୍କା ରାସ୍ତା, ଗୋଟିଏ ପଦ୍ମ ପୋଖରୀ, ଏକ ପଥରର ସାଜସଜ୍ଜା ଏବଂ ତା' ସାମ୍ନାରେ ଏକ ଅଦ୍ଭୁତ ଯନ୍ତ୍ର ଥିବା ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟଘଡ଼ି ଅଛି ଯାହାକୁ ଶ୍ରୀମତୀ ଜୋନ୍ସ ବହୁତ ପସନ୍ଦ କରନ୍ତି ।

English Meaning (Simple Explanation): The poet introduces his neighbor, Mrs. Jones. She has a very unique garden that is completely filled with stones instead of soft grass and big plants. It features a winding path, a pond for lilies, a rock decoration, and a strange-looking sundial that she really loves.

 

🌿 Stanza 2

Original Paragraph:

My next-door neighbour, Mrs. Jones,

puts little plants between the stones

They are so delicate and small,

 they don't mean anything at all.

 I can't think how she gets them in,

unless she plants them with a pin.

English Pronunciation in Odia:

ମାଇ ନେକ୍ସଟ୍-ଡୋର୍ ନେଇବର୍, ମିସେସ୍. ଜୋନ୍ସ,

ପୁଟସ୍ ଲିଟିଲ୍ ପ୍ଲାଣ୍ଟସ୍ ବିଟୁଇନ୍ ଦି ଷ୍ଟୋନ୍ସ

ଦେ ଆର୍ ସୋ ଡେଲିକେଟ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ସ୍ମଲ୍,

ଦେ ଡୋଣ୍ଟ ମିନ୍ ଏନିଥିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଆଟ୍ ଅଲ୍.

ଆଇ କାଣ୍ଟ ଥିଙ୍କ୍ ହାଓ ସି ଗେଟସ୍ ଦେମ୍ ଇନ୍,

ଅନଲେସ୍ ସି ପ୍ଲାଣ୍ଟସ୍ ଦେମ୍ ୱିଥ୍ ଏ ପିନ୍.

English → Odia Meaning 

🔹 Pin → ପିନ୍ / କଣ୍ଟା (Pin / Kanta)

🔹 Small → ଛୋଟ (Chhota)

🔹 Plants → ଗଛ (Gacha)

🔹 Between → ମଝିରେ (Majhire)

🔹 Delicate → କୋମଳ ବା ନାଜୁକ (Komala / Najuka)

🔹 Unless → ଯଦି ନୁହେଁ (Jadi nuhen)

Odia Meaning (Translation):

ମୋର ପଡ଼ୋଶୀ ଶ୍ରୀମତୀ ଜୋନ୍ସ ପଥର ମଝିରେ ଛୋଟ ଛୋଟ ଗଛ ଲଗାନ୍ତି । ସେଗୁଡିକ ଏତେ କୋମଳ ଏବଂ ଛୋଟ ଯେ, ସେଗୁଡିକର କୌଣସି ଅସ୍ତିତ୍ୱ ଥିବା ପରି ଲାଗେ ନାହିଁ । କବି ଭାବି ପାରନ୍ତି ନାହିଁ ଯେ ସେ ଏତେ ଛୋଟ ଗଛକୁ ମାଟି ଭିତରେ କିପରି ଲଗାନ୍ତି, ବୋଧହୁଏ ସେ ଏକ ଛୋଟ ପିନ୍ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରନ୍ତି ।

English Meaning (Simple Explanation): Mrs. Jones carefully places incredibly tiny plants in the small gaps between her garden stones. These plants are so fragile and invisibly small that the poet jokingly wonders how she even manages to get them into the ground. He guesses she might have to use a tiny pin to plant them.

 🌿 Stanza 3

Original Paragraph:

My next-door neighbour, Mrs. Jones,

once asked me round to see her stones.

We stood and talked about a flower

for quite a quarter of an hour.

 "Where is this lovely thing?" I cried.

"You're standing on it," she replied.

English Pronunciation in Odia:

ମାଇ ନେକ୍ସଟ୍-ଡୋର୍ ନେଇବର୍, ମିସେସ୍. ଜୋନ୍ସ,

ୱାନ୍ସ ଆସ୍କଡ୍ ମି ରାଉଣ୍ଡ ଟୁ ସି ହର୍ ଷ୍ଟୋନ୍ସ.

ଉଇ ଷ୍ଟୁଡ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ଟକ୍‌ଡ୍ ଆବାଉଟ୍ ଏ ଫ୍ଲାୱାର୍

ଫର୍ କ୍ୱାଇଟ୍ ଏ କ୍ୱାର୍ଟର୍ ଅଫ୍ ଆନ୍ ଆୱାର୍.

"ହ୍ୱେୟାର ଇଜ୍ ଦିସ୍ ଲଭଲି ଥିଙ୍ଗ୍?" ଆଇ କ୍ରାଏଡ୍.

"ୟୁ ଆର୍ ଷ୍ଟାଣ୍ଡିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଅନ୍ ଇଟ୍," ସି ରିପ୍ଲାଏଡ୍.

English → Odia Meaning 

🔹 Stood → ଠିଆ ହେଲେ (Thia hele)

🔹 Flower → ଫୁଲ (Phula)

🔹 Talked → କଥା ହେଲେ (Katha hele)

🔹 Lovely → ସୁନ୍ଦର (Sundara)

🔹 Cried → ଚିତ୍କାର କଲେ / ଆଶ୍ଚର୍ଯ୍ୟରେ ପଚାରିଲେ (Chitkara kale / Ascharjyare pacharile)

🔹 Replied → ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଲେ (Uttara dele)

🔹 Quarter of an hour → ପନ୍ଦର ମିନିଟ୍ (Pandara minit)

Odia Meaning :

ଦିନେ ମୋର ପଡ଼ୋଶୀ ଶ୍ରୀମତୀ ଜୋନ୍ସ ମୋତେ ତାଙ୍କ ପଥର ବଗିଚା ଦେଖିବାକୁ ଡାକିଲେ । ଆମେ ପ୍ରାୟ ପନ୍ଦର ମିନିଟ୍ ଧରି ଗୋଟିଏ ଫୁଲ ବିଷୟରେ କଥା ହେଲୁ । ମୁଁ ଆଶ୍ଚର୍ଯ୍ୟ ହୋଇ ପଚାରିଲି, "ସେହି ସୁନ୍ଦର ଜିନିଷଟି କେଉଁଠି ଅଛି?" ସେ ଉତ୍ତର ଦେଲେ, "ତୁମେ ତାହା ଉପରେ ଠିଆ ହୋଇଛ।"

English Meaning (Simple Explanation): One day, Mrs. Jones invited the poet over to admire her stone garden. They stood there talking about a specific, beautiful flower for a whole fifteen minutes. When the poet finally became excited and asked where this wonderful flower was, Mrs. Jones bluntly told him that he was accidentally stepping right on it. 

📖 2. Summary (English Only)

The poem "A Concrete Example" by Reginald Arkell describes Mrs. Jones, the speaker's next-door neighbor, who has a highly unusual garden. Instead of lush greenery, her garden is entirely filled with stones, featuring a crazy path, a lily pond, a rockery, and a peculiar sundial that she finds rather nice. Among these stones, Mrs. Jones plants extremely delicate and tiny plants. These plants are so microscopic that the speaker playfully wonders how she manages to get them into the ground, joking that she must plant them using a pin. The humor peaks when Mrs. Jones invites the speaker to admire her garden. They spend a quarter of an hour discussing a beautiful flower, but when the speaker excitedly asks to see it, Mrs. Jones bluntly reveals that the speaker is already standing on it.

📖 3. Summary (Odia)

କବି ରେଜିନାଲ୍ଡ ଆର୍କେଲଙ୍କ ରଚିତ "ଏ କଂକ୍ରିଟ୍ ଏକ୍ସାମ୍ପଲ୍" କବିତାଟି ଶ୍ରୀମତୀ ଜୋନ୍ସ ନାମକ ଜଣେ ପଡ଼ୋଶୀଙ୍କ ବିଷୟରେ ଅଟେ, ଯାହାଙ୍କର ଏକ ଅସାଧାରଣ ବଗିଚା ଅଛି । ସାଧାରଣ ସବୁଜ ଗଛଲତା ବଦଳରେ ତାଙ୍କ ବଗିଚାଟି ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ରୂପେ ପଥରରେ ଭର୍ତ୍ତି । ସେଥିରେ ଏକ ଅଙ୍କାବଙ୍କା ରାସ୍ତା, ପଦ୍ମ ପୋଖରୀ, ପଥରର ସାଜସଜ୍ଜା ଏବଂ ଏକ ଅଦ୍ଭୁତ ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟଘଡ଼ି ରହିଛି ଯାହାକୁ ସେ ବହୁତ ପସନ୍ଦ କରନ୍ତି ।

ଏହି ପଥରଗୁଡ଼ିକ ମଝିରେ ଶ୍ରୀମତୀ ଜୋନ୍ସ ବହୁତ ଛୋଟ ଏବଂ କୋମଳ ଗଛ ଲଗାନ୍ତି । ଏହି ଗଛଗୁଡ଼ିକ ଏତେ ଛୋଟ ଯେ କବି ମଜାରେ କୁହନ୍ତି ବୋଧହୁଏ ସେଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ଲଗାଇବା ପାଇଁ ସେ ଏକ ଛୋଟ ପିନ୍ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରୁଥିବେ । କବିତାର ଶେଷ ଭାଗରେ ଏକ ମଜାଦାର ଘଟଣା ଘଟେ ଯେତେବେଳେ ଶ୍ରୀମତୀ ଜୋନ୍ସ କବିଙ୍କୁ ନିଜ ବଗିଚା ଦେଖାଇବାକୁ ଡାକନ୍ତି । ସେମାନେ ପନ୍ଦର ମିନିଟ୍ ଧରି ଏକ ସୁନ୍ଦର ଫୁଲ ବିଷୟରେ କଥା ହୁଅନ୍ତି, କିନ୍ତୁ କବି ସେହି ଫୁଲଟିକୁ ନ ଦେଖିପାରି ପଚାରନ୍ତି ତାହା କେଉଁଠି ଅଛି । ସେତେବେଳେ ଶ୍ରୀମତୀ ଜୋନ୍ସ ଉତ୍ତର ଦିଅନ୍ତି ଯେ କବି ନିଜେ ଅଜାଣତରେ ସେହି ଫୁଲଟି ଉପରେ ଠିଆ ହୋଇଛନ୍ତି!

💡 4 & 5. Suggestions for Students

📘 To fully enjoy this poem, practice reading it aloud to understand the musical rhythm and the rhyming words at the end of each line, like 'Jones' and 'stones'.

🗣️ Pair up with a classmate and act out the funny conversation at the end of the poem to clearly express the surprise.

✍️ Focus on learning the correct spellings and meanings of new English words such as 'delicate', 'sundial', and 'rockery' to improve your vocabulary.

🌳 Try to imagine what a garden made entirely of stones looks like and compare it to the green parks or gardens you see in your own neighborhood.

👩‍🏫 6. Suggestions for Teachers & Teaching Plan

⏳ The total time required to teach this poem effectively is 4 periods.

🎤 In the first period, introduce the topic by asking students about the items they usually find in a normal garden to create a contrast with a stone garden. Recite the poem aloud with proper voice modulation to bring out the light-hearted tone.

📖 During the second period, explain each stanza clearly, focusing on the Odia translation and the meanings of new vocabulary words like 'device' and 'crazy path'.

🤔 Use the third period to discuss the comprehension questions in the 'Let us think and reflect' section, making sure students understand the symbolic meaning behind the title and Mrs. Jones's unique gardening taste.

🎭 In the fourth period, conduct the speaking activities where students practice formal and informal apologies in various situations. Finally, guide them step-by-step through drafting the formal notice for the Herb Garden inauguration as a writing exercise.
