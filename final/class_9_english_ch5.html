1. About the Author ✍️

This famous story is written by the legendary Indian English writer, R.K. Narayan.

Full Name: Rasipuram Krishnaswami Iyer Narayanaswami (1906–2001).

Family Details: He was born in Madras (now Chennai). His younger brother, R.K. Laxman, was a world-famous cartoonist who created "The Common Man" for The Times of India.

Extra Details & Famous Books: R.K. Narayan is best known for creating the fictional South Indian town of "Malgudi". His most famous works include Malgudi Days, Swami and Friends, The Bachelor of Arts, and The Guide. He won the prestigious Sahitya Akademi Award for The Guide, which was also adapted into a blockbuster Hindi movie.

Theme of the Story: The story humorously highlights child psychology, the fear of the dark, and how an ordinary, timid boy becomes an accidental hero overnight.

Part 1: The Newspaper Report 📰

Original Text: "For Swami events took an unexpected turn... flight up a tree..."

English Meaning: Swami's father was reading the newspaper and shared a news story about a brave village boy. The boy was returning home by the jungle path when he suddenly faced a tiger. The boy fought the tiger and climbed a tree, staying there for half a day until some people rescued him and killed the tiger.

English Meaning: Swami disagreed with his father. He felt that only a strong, grown-up man could fight a tiger, not a boy. His father argued that true courage is the most important thing. Even if a person is thin and weak, if he has courage, he can achieve anything.

Part 3: The Terrible Challenge 😨

Original Text: "Leave alone strength, can you prove you have courage?... Let me see if you can sleep alone tonight in my office room."

English Meaning: To test Swami's courage, his father challenged him to sleep alone in the dark office room that night. Swami was terrified because he always slept beside his grandmother (Granny) in the passage. Sleeping alone in the dark was a frightful proposition for him.

4. Long Summary (English) 📜

The story "A Hero" revolves around a young, timid boy named Swami. One day, his father reads a newspaper report about a brave village boy who fought a tiger. Swami argues that the boy must have been a strong, grown-up man. However, his father firmly believes that courage is everything, while physical strength and age are secondary. To prove his point and test his son's courage, the father challenges Swami to sleep completely alone in the dark office room that night.

Swami is a cowardly boy who has always slept beside his grandmother. He desperately tries to make excuses, changes the subject, and even pretends to be deeply asleep. But his strict father forces him out of bed and makes him sleep in the isolated, dark office room. Left alone in the terrifying darkness, Swami is haunted by memories of scary ghost stories. He spends the night sweating in fear, hiding under a bench, imagining that devils are waiting to catch him.

In the middle of the night, Swami suddenly sees a dark shadow creeping into the room. Driven by absolute desperation and thinking it is a devil coming to kill him, Swami crawls out, hugs the intruder's leg, and bites it hard with his teeth. The intruder screams in agony and falls down. Hearing the noise, Swami’s father and the servant rush in and capture the man. It turns out that the man was actually a notorious housebreaker wanted by the police. The next day, the police, teachers, and friends shower Swami with praise, calling him a true hero. However, despite his accidental heroism, Swami goes back to sleeping securely next to his grandmother the very next night.

ସ୍ୱାମୀ ଜଣେ ଡରୁଆ ପିଲା ଥିଲା ଯିଏ ସବୁବେଳେ ନିଜ ଜେଜେମାଙ୍କ ପାଖରେ ଶୋଉଥିଲା। ସେ ଏହି ଆହ୍ୱାନରୁ ରକ୍ଷା ପାଇବା ପାଇଁ ବହୁତ ବାହାନା କଲା, କଥା ବଦଳାଇଲା ଏବଂ ଜଲଦି ଶୋଇପଡ଼ିବାର ଅଭିନୟ ମଧ୍ୟ କଲା। କିନ୍ତୁ ତା'ର କଠୋର ବାପା ତାକୁ ବାଧ୍ୟ କରି ନେଇ ଅଫିସ୍ ରୁମରେ ଶୋଇବାକୁ ଛାଡ଼ିଦେଲେ। ଭୟଙ୍କର ଅନ୍ଧାରରେ ଏକୁଟିଆ ରହି ସ୍ୱାମୀକୁ ଭୂତପ୍ରେତଙ୍କ କାହାଣୀ ମନେ ପଡ଼ିଗଲା। ସେ ଏକ ବେଞ୍ଚ ତଳେ ଲୁଚି ରହିଲା ଏବଂ ଭୂତ ତାକୁ ମାରିଦେବ ଭାବି ଭୟରେ ଝାଳନାଳ ହୋଇଗଲା।

ରାତି ଅଧରେ ସ୍ୱାମୀ ଅନ୍ଧାରରେ ଗୋଟିଏ ଛାଇ ଘୁଞ୍ଚି ଘୁଞ୍ଚି ଆସୁଥିବାର ଦେଖିଲା। ତାହାକୁ ଏକ ରାକ୍ଷସ ଭାବି ନିଜ ଜୀବନ ବଞ୍ଚାଇବା ଉଦ୍ଦେଶ୍ୟରେ, ସେ ପ୍ରାଣବିକଳରେ ଆଗକୁ ଯାଇ ସେହି ବ୍ୟକ୍ତିର ଗୋଡ଼କୁ ଜାବୁଡ଼ି ଧରିଲା ଏବଂ ଜୋରରେ କାମୁଡ଼ି ଦେଲା। ବ୍ୟକ୍ତି ଜଣକ ଯନ୍ତ୍ରଣାରେ ଚିତ୍କାର କରି ତଳେ ପଡ଼ିଗଲା। ପାଟି ଶୁଣି ବାପା ଓ ଚାକର ଦୌଡ଼ି ଆସିଲେ ଏବଂ ତାକୁ ଧରି ପକାଇଲେ। ଜଣାପଡ଼ିଲା ଯେ ସେହି ବ୍ୟକ୍ତି ଜଣକ ପୋଲିସ୍ ଖୋଜୁଥିବା ଜଣେ କୁଖ୍ୟାତ ସିନ୍ଧିଚୋର ଥିଲା। ପରଦିନ ପୋଲିସ, ଶିକ୍ଷକ ଏବଂ ସାଙ୍ଗମାନେ ସ୍ୱାମୀକୁ ପ୍ରଶଂସାରେ ପୋତି ପକାଇଲେ ଏବଂ ତାକୁ ଏକ ହିରୋର ମାନ୍ୟତା ଦେଲେ। କିନ୍ତୁ ଏହି ଆକସ୍ମିକ ସାହସିକତା ସତ୍ତ୍ୱେ, ତା' ପରଦିନ ରାତିରେ ସ୍ୱାମୀ ପୁଣିଥରେ ନିଜ ଜେଜେମାଙ୍କ ପାଖକୁ ଫେରିଯାଇ ଶୋଇପଡ଼ିଲା।

6. Suggestions for Teachers: How to Teach This Chapter 🧑‍🏫

  1. Debate on Courage vs. Strength: Start the class by writing "Courage vs. Strength" on the board. Ask the students whether they agree with Swami or his father. This builds critical thinking and perfectly sets the theme of the story.

  2. Relate to Personal Experiences: Ask the students: "How many of you are afraid of sleeping alone in the dark?" This helps them connect emotionally with Swami's psychological fear and makes the lesson highly relatable.

  3. Role-Play Activity: Divide the class into pairs (Father and Swami). Ask them to enact the dialogue from the textbook where the father forces Swami to sleep in the office. This improves English speaking skills, pronunciation, and dialogue delivery.

  4. Vocabulary Games: Since this chapter has excellent descriptive words (scowled, desperation, apparition), conduct a quick 'Word Match' game on the blackboard matching English words to their Odia meanings.

  5. Discussing 'Accidental Heroism': Conclude the chapter by discussing a profound question: "Was Swami really a brave hero, or was he just fighting out of desperate fear?" This teaches students how circumstances sometimes force us to face our fears.
