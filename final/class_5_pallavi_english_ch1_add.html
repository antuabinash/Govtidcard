Let's Have Fun: Grandpa's Glasses – Additional Questions Class 5 English Pallavi

🌟 Here are 60 extra practice questions (with Odia translations and answers) carefully created from the chapter "Let's Have Fun: Grandpa's Glasses".

🎭 Section A: The Role-Play Activity

🌟 1. Question: Who is looking for the pen?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: କିଏ କଲମ ଖୋଜୁଛନ୍ତି?)

✅ Answer: The Teacher is looking for the pen.

🌟 2. Question: What was the teacher looking for at first?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଶିକ୍ଷକ ପ୍ରଥମେ କ'ଣ ଖୋଜୁଥିଲେ?)

✅ Answer: His pen.

🌟 3. Question: Did Mili find the pen?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ମିଲି କଲମ ପାଇଲା କି?)

✅ Answer: No, Mili did not find the pen.

🌟 4. Question: Who asked if the pen was in the pocket?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: କଲମଟି ପକେଟରେ ଅଛି କି ବୋଲି କିଏ ପଚାରିଲା?)

✅ Answer: Safina asked if it was in the pocket.

🌟 5. Question: Where was the teacher's pen finally found?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଶିକ୍ଷକଙ୍କ କଲମ ଶେଷରେ କେଉଁଠାରେ ମିଳିଲା?)

✅ Answer: In his own pocket.

🌟 6. Question: Who pointed out that the pen is in the pocket?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: କଲମଟି ପକେଟରେ ଅଛି ବୋଲି କିଏ ଦେଖାଇଲା?)

✅ Answer: David pointed it out.

🌟 7. Question: Did the children laugh at the end?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ପିଲାମାନେ ଶେଷରେ ହସିଲେ କି?)

✅ Answer: Yes, all the children laughed.

🌟 8. Question: Was the teacher looking for his glasses?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଶିକ୍ଷକ ତାଙ୍କ ଚଷମା ଖୋଜୁଥିଲେ କି?)

✅ Answer: No, he was looking for his pen.

🌟 9. Question: Who asked "Sir, what're you looking for?"

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: କିଏ ପଚାରିଲା, "ସାର୍, ଆପଣ କ'ଣ ଖୋଜୁଛନ୍ତି?")

✅ Answer: Amit asked this question.

🌟 10. Question: Name the four students in the role-play.

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ନାଟକରେ ଥିବା ଚାରିଜଣ ଛାତ୍ରଙ୍କ ନାମ କୁହ।)

✅ Answer: Amit, Mili, Safina, and David.

👓 Section B: The Poem "Grandpa's Glasses"

🌟 11. Question: Who is the main person in the poem?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: କବିତାରେ ମୁଖ୍ୟ ବ୍ୟକ୍ତି କିଏ?)

✅ Answer: Grandpa.

🌟 12. Question: How many pairs of glasses did Grandpa have?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଜେଜେବାପାଙ୍କର କେତେ ହଳ ଚଷମା ଥିଲା?)

✅ Answer: Only one pair.

🌟 13. Question: Can Grandpa read the newspaper without glasses?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଜେଜେବାପା ଚଷମା ବିନା ଖବରକାଗଜ ପଢ଼ିପାରିବେ କି?)

✅ Answer: No, he cannot.

🌟 14. Question: Name one thing Grandpa cannot cut without his glasses.

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଜେଜେବାପା ଚଷମା ବିନା କାଟି ପାରୁନଥିବା ଗୋଟିଏ ଜିନିଷର ନାମ କୁହ।)

✅ Answer: Vegetables.

🌟 15. Question: Did Grandpa check under the bed?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଜେଜେବାପା ଖଟ ତଳେ ଖୋଜିଥିଲେ କି?)

✅ Answer: No, he checked under his chair.

🌟 16. Question: Where did he glance?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ସେ କେଉଁଠି ଦୃଷ୍ଟି ପକାଇଲେ?)

✅ Answer: Under his chair.

🌟 17. Question: Who said "Stop Grandpa!"?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: କିଏ କହିଲା "ରୁହନ୍ତୁ ଜେଜେବାପା!"?)

✅ Answer: The grandchild (the speaker of the poem).

🌟 18. Question: Were the glasses in Grandpa's pocket?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଚଷମା ଜେଜେବାପାଙ୍କ ପକେଟରେ ଥିଲା କି?)

✅ Answer: No, they were not.

🌟 19. Question: Did Grandpa search near the door?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଜେଜେବାପା କବାଟ ପାଖରେ ଖୋଜିଥିଲେ କି?)

✅ Answer: No, he searched near the window.

🌟 20. Question: Where exactly were the glasses hiding?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଚଷମା ଠିକ୍ କେଉଁଠାରେ ଲୁଚି ରହିଥିଲା?)

✅ Answer: Right there on his head.

🌟 21. Question: Does Grandpa watch TV?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଜେଜେବାପା ଟିଭି ଦେଖନ୍ତି କି?)

✅ Answer: Yes, but he cannot watch it without his glasses.

🌟 22. Question: Is Grandpa a forgetful person?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଜେଜେବାପା ଜଣେ ଭୁଲାଖଣ୍ଡି ମଣିଷ କି?)

✅ Answer: Yes, he forgot his glasses were on his head.

🌟 23. Question: Why was Grandpa searching everywhere?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଜେଜେବାପା କାହିଁକି ସବୁଆଡେ ଖୋଜୁଥିଲେ?)

✅ Answer: Because he lost his glasses.

🌟 24. Question: What did the grandchild do before talking to Grandpa?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଜେଜେବାପାଙ୍କ ସହ କଥା ହେବା ପୂର୍ବରୁ ନାତି/ନାତୁଣୀ କ'ଣ କଲେ?)

✅ Answer: Went up close to him.

🌟 25. Question: What is the Odia meaning of 'Everywhere'?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: 'Everywhere' ର ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅର୍ଥ କ'ଣ?)

✅ Answer: ସବୁଆଡେ (Sabuade).

🍎 Section D: Sir Isaac Newton & General Questions

🌟 46. Question: Who was Sir Isaac Newton?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ସାର୍ ଆଇଜାକ୍ ନ୍ୟୁଟନ୍ କିଏ ଥିଲେ?)

✅ Answer: He was a great scientist.

🌟 47. Question: What did Newton want to have for supper?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ନ୍ୟୁଟନ୍ ରାତ୍ରିଭୋଜନରେ କ'ଣ ଖାଇବାକୁ ଚାହୁଁଥିଲେ?)

✅ Answer: He wanted to have eggs.

🌟 48. Question: Where did Newton drop his watch?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ନ୍ୟୁଟନ୍ ତାଙ୍କ ଘଣ୍ଟା କେଉଁଠାରେ ପକାଇ ଦେଇଥିଲେ?)

✅ Answer: Into the boiling water.

🌟 49. Question: Did Newton boil the egg?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ନ୍ୟୁଟନ୍ ଅଣ୍ଡା ସିଝାଇଥିଲେ କି?)

✅ Answer: No, he boiled his watch by mistake.

🌟 50. Question: Where was the egg actually?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ପ୍ରକୃତରେ ଅଣ୍ଡାଟି କେଉଁଠାରେ ଥିଲା?)

✅ Answer: It was in his hand.

🌟 51. Question: Are great thinkers often forgetful?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ମହାନ ବିଚାରକମାନେ ପ୍ରାୟତଃ ଭୁଲାଖଣ୍ଡି ହୋଇଥାନ୍ତି କି?)

✅ Answer: Yes, they are often forgetful.

🌟 52. Question: Preposition: My glasses are ___ the table. (on/in)

🌟 53. Question: Preposition: I looked ___ the house. (around/above)

🌟 54. Question: Preposition: He searched ___ two hours. (for/at)

🌟 55. Question: Preposition: He sat down ___ the bed. (in/above)

🌟 56. Question: Riddle: I go to the market, bring you home, and you cry when you cut me. What am I?

🌟 57. Question: In the chapter puzzle, which alphabet is hidden in the box of 'b's?
