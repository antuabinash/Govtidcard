Time and Tense

Time and Tense – Study Material Class 9 English Grammar

Step 1: Definition of Time (ସମୟର ସଂଜ୍ଞା)

Time is the concept that tells when an action happens.

Time ବୋଲି କୁହିଲେ କୌଣସି କାମ କେବେ ଘଟିଛି, ଘଟୁଛି କିମ୍ବା ଘଟିବ ତାହାକୁ ବୁଝାଯାଏ।

Time is divided into three parts.

Type of Time

Meaning

Odia Meaning

Past

Time that has already passed

ଅତୀତ ସମୟ

Present

Time happening now

ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ ସମୟ

Future

Time yet to come

ଭବିଷ୍ୟତ ସମୟ

Example

Sentence

Time

I played yesterday

Past

I play today

Present

I will play tomorrow

Future

😊 Easy Trick
Yesterday → Past
Today → Present
Tomorrow → Future

Step 2: Definition of Tense (କାଳର ସଂଜ୍ଞା)

Tense is the form of a verb that shows the time of an action.

Tense ହେଉଛି କ୍ରିୟାର ଏମିତି ରୂପ ଯାହା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ କେବେ ଘଟିଛି ତାହାକୁ ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

In English grammar, tense helps us understand when an action takes place.

Example

Verb

Step 3: Definition of Present Tense (ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ କାଳର ସଂଜ୍ଞା)

Present Tense shows an action that happens now, regularly, or always.

Present tense ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ ସମୟରେ ଘଟୁଥିବା ବା ନିୟମିତ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକୁ ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

Examples

Sentence

Meaning

She reads a book

ସେ ପୁସ୍ତକ ପଢେ

The sun rises in the east

ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟ ପୂର୍ବରେ ଉଦୟ ହୁଏ

Formula

Structure

Example

Subject + V1

I play

Subject + V1 + s/es

He plays

💡 Trick
If subject = He / She / It
Add s / es to the verb.

Step 4: Definition of Present Continuous Tense

Present Continuous Tense shows an action that is happening right now.

Present continuous tense ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ ଚାଲୁଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକୁ ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

Formula

Structure

Example

Subject + am/is/are + Verb + ing

She is reading

Examples

Sentence

Meaning

I am writing a letter

ମୁଁ ଚିଠି ଲେଖୁଛି

They are playing football

ସେମାନେ ଖେଳୁଛନ୍ତି

💡 Trick
Look for am / is / are + ing

Step 5: Definition of Past Tense (ଅତୀତ କାଳର ସଂଜ୍ଞା)

Past Tense shows an action that happened in the past.

Past tense ଅତୀତ ସମୟରେ ଘଟିଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକୁ ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

Formula

Structure

Example

Subject + V2

She wrote a letter

Examples

Sentence

Meaning

Ram played football

ରାମ ଫୁଟବଲ ଖେଳିଲା

She sang a song

ସେ ଗୀତ ଗାଇଲା

💡 Trick
Past tense often uses V2 form

Example
go → went
eat → ate
write → wrote

Step 6: Definition of Future Tense (ଭବିଷ୍ୟତ କାଳର ସଂଜ୍ଞା)

Future Tense shows an action that will happen later.

Future tense ଆଗାମୀ ସମୟରେ ଘଟିବାକୁ ଥିବା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକୁ ଦର୍ଶାଏ।

Formula

Structure

Example

Subject + will + V1

She will write

Examples

Sentence

Meaning

I will go tomorrow

ମୁଁ କାଲି ଯିବି

They will play football

ସେମାନେ ଖେଳିବେ

💡 Trick
Future tense often uses will + verb

Step 7: Difference Between Time and Tense

Time

Tense

Time tells when something happens

Tense shows verb form

Time is a general idea

Tense is a grammar rule

Past, Present, Future

Present tense, Past tense

📘 Simple Formula

Time → Concept of time
Tense → Form of verb

Step 11: Quick Revision (ସଂକ୍ଷିପ୍ତ ପୁନରାବୃତ୍ତି)

Time tells when an action happens.

Tense tells the form of the verb.

English grammar mainly has three tenses.

Present tense shows current actions.

Past tense shows completed actions.

Future tense shows upcoming actions.
