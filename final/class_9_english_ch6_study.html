Home and Love – Study Material Class 9 ENGLISH

CHAPTER 6: HOME AND LOVE 🏡❤️

1. About the Poet / Author ✍️

This beautiful and touching poem is penned by the famous poet Robert William Service.

🧑‍🏫 Full Name: Robert William Service (1874–1958).

🌍 Background: He was a British-Canadian poet and writer. Because of his famous poems about the Canadian North, he was popularly known as the "Bard of the Yukon".

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Details: He was born in Lancashire, England. His father was a Scottish bank clerk. Later in life, he married a French woman named Germaine Bourgoin and settled down in France.

📚 Extra Details & Famous Books: Before becoming a full-time writer, he worked in a bank and traveled extensively. His most famous poetry collections include Songs of a Sourdough, The Spell of the Yukon, and Rhymes of a Red Cross Man.

🎯 Theme of the Poem: The poem beautifully highlights that material wealth is useless without the emotional warmth of "Home" and "Love". These two things are inseparable and bring ultimate divine happiness to human life.

 

2. Stanza/Paragraph Explanation 📖

Stanza 1: The Sweetest Words 🕊️

📜 Original Snippet: "Just Home and Love! the words are small... And yet you will not find in all..."

🧠 English Meaning: The words "Home" and "Love" are very small, consisting of only four letters each. However, the poet says that in the entire vast vocabulary of human language, there are no two words that are more complete, tender, and deeply meaningful. Even the angels in heaven cannot find any words sweeter than these two.

🌐 Odia Meaning: 'ଘର' ଏବଂ 'ଭଲପାଇବା' (Home and Love) ଶବ୍ଦ ଦୁଇଟି ବହୁତ ଛୋଟ ଏବଂ ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକରେ କେବଳ ଚାରୋଟି ଲେଖାଏଁ ଅକ୍ଷର ଅଛି। କିନ୍ତୁ କବି କହିଛନ୍ତି ଯେ ସମଗ୍ର ମାନବ ଭାଷାରେ ଏହାଠାରୁ ଅଧିକ ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ, କୋମଳ ଏବଂ ଅର୍ଥପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ଶବ୍ଦ ନାହିଁ। ସ୍ୱର୍ଗରେ ଦେବଦୂତମାନେ ମଧ୍ୟ ଏହି ଦୁଇଟି ଠାରୁ ଅଧିକ ମଧୁର ଶବ୍ଦ ପାଇପାରିବେ ନାହିଁ।

 

Stanza 2: The Inseparable Pair 🤝

📜 Original Snippet: "Just Home and Love! it's hard to guess... Which of the two were best to gain..."

🧠 English Meaning: The poet states that it is extremely difficult to choose which of the two is more important. If you have a Home but no Love, your home becomes a place of bitterness and pain. Similarly, if you have Love but no Home, it brings sorrow and aching tears. They are deeply connected and incomplete without each other. They must always go together perfectly, just like a hand and a glove.

🌐 Odia Meaning: କବି କହିଛନ୍ତି ଯେ ଏହି ଦୁଇଟି ମଧ୍ୟରୁ କେଉଁଟି ଅଧିକ ଗୁରୁତ୍ୱପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ତାହା ବାଛିବା କଷ୍ଟକର। ଯଦି ତୁମ ପାଖରେ କେବଳ ଘର ଅଛି କିନ୍ତୁ ଭଲପାଇବା ନାହିଁ, ତେବେ ତାହା ଯନ୍ତ୍ରଣା ଏବଂ ତିକ୍ତତା ଆଣିଥାଏ। ସେହିପରି, ଘର ବିନା କେବଳ ଭଲପାଇବା ଥିଲେ ତାହା ଦୁଃଖ ଓ ଲୁହ ଦେଇଥାଏ। ସେମାନେ ଗୋଟିଏ ଯୋଡ଼ା ଗ୍ଲୋଭସ୍ (ହାତମୋଜା) ପରି ସର୍ବଦା ଏକାଠି ରହିବା ଉଚିତ୍, କାରଣ ଗୋଟିଏ ବିନା ଅନ୍ୟଟି ଅସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ।

Stanza 3: The Ultimate Joy 🎶

📜 Original Snippet: "And if you've both, well then I'm sure... You ought to sing the whole day long..."

🧠 English Meaning: The poet concludes with a beautiful message. He says that if a person is lucky enough to possess both a home and love, they should be extremely grateful and sing with joy all day long. It does not matter if the person is poor; having both "Home" and "Love" makes their life completely fulfilled and their song divine.

🌐 Odia Meaning: କବି ଏକ ସୁନ୍ଦର ବାର୍ତ୍ତା ସହ କବିତା ଶେଷ କରିଛନ୍ତି। ସେ କହିଛନ୍ତି ଯେ, ଯଦି ଜଣେ ବ୍ୟକ୍ତି ପାଖରେ ଉଭୟ ଘର ଏବଂ ଭଲପାଇବା ଅଛି, ତେବେ ସେ ବହୁତ ଭାଗ୍ୟବାନ ଏବଂ ଖୁସିରେ ଦିନସାରା ଗୀତ ଗାଇବା ଉଚିତ୍। ଜଣେ ଗରିବ ହୋଇଥିଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ କିଛି ଫରକ ପଡ଼େନାହିଁ, ଏହି ଦୁଇଟି ଥିଲେ ତା'ର ଜୀବନ ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ସଫଳ ଏବଂ ସ୍ୱର୍ଗୀୟ ହୋଇଯାଏ।

4. Long Summary (English) 📜

The poem "Home and Love" by Robert William Service is a profound reflection on the true essence of human happiness. The poet begins by highlighting the simplicity of the words "Home" and "Love." Although they are small, four-letter words, they hold the deepest and most tender meanings in the entire human vocabulary. The poet playfully suggests that even the angels in heaven would not be able to find any words sweeter or more complete than these two. They represent the ultimate sanctuary and emotional warmth that every human being desperately seeks.

As the poem progresses, the poet explains the deep, unbreakable interdependence between Home and Love. It is impossible to choose which one is better because one is practically meaningless without the other. Having a home without love turns it into a place of emptiness, bitterness, and pain. Conversely, having love without a safe home to shelter it leads to sorrow and aching tears. Therefore, the poet compares them to a hand and a glove, insisting that they must always travel together to provide true comfort and meaning.

In the final stanza, the poet delivers an inspiring and uplifting message. He asserts that anyone who is blessed to have both a home and love in their life should be overwhelmingly happy. Such a person should sing with joy throughout the day, expressing their gratitude. The poet emphasizes that material wealth is entirely secondary; even if a person is financially poor, possessing both home and love makes their life incredibly rich, and their existence becomes truly divine and heavenly.

5. Long Summary (Odia) 📖

ରବର୍ଟ ୱିଲିୟମ୍ ସର୍ଭିସ୍‌ଙ୍କ ଦ୍ୱାରା ରଚିତ "ହୋମ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ଲଭ୍" (ଘର ଏବଂ ଭଲପାଇବା) କବିତାଟି ମାନବ ଜୀବନର ପ୍ରକୃତ ସୁଖ ଉପରେ ଏକ ସୁନ୍ଦର ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା ଅଟେ। କବି ପ୍ରଥମେ "ଘର" ଏବଂ "ଭଲପାଇବା" ଶବ୍ଦର ସରଳତା ଉପରେ ଆଲୋକପାତ କରିଛନ୍ତି। ଯଦିଓ ଏଗୁଡ଼ିକ ମାତ୍ର ଚାରୋଟି ଅକ୍ଷର ବିଶିଷ୍ଟ ଛୋଟ ଶବ୍ଦ, ତଥାପି ସମଗ୍ର ମାନବ ଭାଷାରେ ଏହି ଦୁଇଟି ଶବ୍ଦ ସବୁଠାରୁ ଗଭୀର ଏବଂ କୋମଳ ଅର୍ଥ ବହନ କରନ୍ତି। କବି କହିଛନ୍ତି ଯେ ସ୍ୱର୍ଗର ଦେବଦୂତମାନେ ମଧ୍ୟ ଏହାଠାରୁ ଅଧିକ ମଧୁର କିମ୍ବା ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ଶବ୍ଦ ଖୋଜି ପାଇବେ ନାହିଁ। ଏହା ମଣିଷର ଆବେଗ ଏବଂ ସୁରକ୍ଷାର ସର୍ବଶ୍ରେଷ୍ଠ ପ୍ରତୀକ ଅଟେ।

କବିତା ଆଗକୁ ବଢ଼ିବା ସହ, କବି ଘର ଏବଂ ଭଲପାଇବା ମଧ୍ୟରେ ଥିବା ଅତୁଟ ସମ୍ପର୍କକୁ ବୁଝାଇଛନ୍ତି। ଏହି ଦୁଇଟି ମଧ୍ୟରୁ କେଉଁଟି ଶ୍ରେଷ୍ଠ ତାହା ବାଛିବା ଅସମ୍ଭବ, କାରଣ ଗୋଟିଏ ବିନା ଅନ୍ୟଟି ମୂଲ୍ୟହୀନ। ଭଲପାଇବା ନଥିବା ଏକ ଘର କେବଳ ଯନ୍ତ୍ରଣା ଏବଂ ତିକ୍ତତାର ସ୍ଥାନ ପାଲଟିଯାଏ। ଅନ୍ୟପକ୍ଷରେ, ଘର ବିନା କେବଳ ଭଲପାଇବା ଥିଲେ ତାହା ଦୁଃଖ ଏବଂ ଲୁହର କାରଣ ହୋଇଥାଏ। ତେଣୁ, କବି ଏହାକୁ ହାତ ଏବଂ ମୋଜା (ଗ୍ଲୋଭସ୍) ସହିତ ତୁଳନା କରି କହିଛନ୍ତି ଯେ, ଜୀବନକୁ ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ କରିବା ପାଇଁ ଏହି ଦୁଇଟି ସର୍ବଦା ଏକାଠି ରହିବା ଆବଶ୍ୟକ।

ଶେଷ ପଦରେ କବି ଏକ ପ୍ରେରଣାଦାୟକ ବାର୍ତ୍ତା ଦେଇଛନ୍ତି। ସେ କହିଛନ୍ତି ଯେ ଯଦି କୌଣସି ବ୍ୟକ୍ତି ଜୀବନରେ ଉଭୟ ଘର ଏବଂ ଭଲପାଇବା ପାଇବାର ସୌଭାଗ୍ୟ ଲାଭ କରିଛି, ତେବେ ସେ ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ଖୁସି ହେବା ଉଚିତ୍। ସେହି ବ୍ୟକ୍ତି ଆନନ୍ଦରେ ଦିନସାରା ଗୀତ ଗାଇବା ଉଚିତ୍। ଭୌତିକ ସମ୍ପତ୍ତି ଏଠାରେ ଗୁରୁତ୍ୱପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ନୁହେଁ; ଯଦିଓ ଜଣେ ଆର୍ଥିକ ଦୃଷ୍ଟିରୁ ଗରିବ ହୋଇଥାଏ, ଘର ଏବଂ ଭଲପାଇବା ଥିଲେ ତା'ର ଜୀବନ ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ସମୃଦ୍ଧ ହୋଇଯାଏ ଏବଂ ତା'ର ଜୀବନ ଶୈଳୀ ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ସ୍ୱର୍ଗୀୟ ହୋଇଉଠେ।

6. Suggestions for Teachers: How to Teach This Chapter 🧑‍🏫

🗣️ Recitation & Rhythm: Start the class by reciting the poem aloud with proper intonation. Let the students repeat after you. This poem has a very musical and rhythmic quality, and reading it aloud helps students appreciate its simplicity.

🤔 Personal Connection: Ask the students simple questions before explaining the poem. For example, "What is the difference between a 'House' (a physical building made of bricks) and a 'Home' (a place filled with love)?" This clears the core concept of the poem immediately.

🎭 Visual Metaphor: Bring a pair of gloves to the class. Show them one glove and ask, "Is this useful without the other hand?" Use this prop to perfectly explain the line "They travel like a hand and glove."

📝 Word Web Activity: Write the words "HOME" and "LOVE" on the blackboard and ask students to come up and write associated feelings (e.g., safe, happy, warm, family).

💡 Moral Value Focus: Conclude the lesson by discussing why the poet says poor people can also sing all day long. Teach the students that money does not buy happiness; loving relationships and a safe family environment are our true wealth.
