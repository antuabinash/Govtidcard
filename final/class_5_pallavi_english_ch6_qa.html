My Colourful World : The Wise Parrot – Book Q A Class 5 English Pallavi

🗣️ Let us Discuss

🏡 1️⃣ Where did the merchant live ?

(ବଣିକ ଜଣକ କେଉଁଠାରେ ବାସ କରୁଥିଲେ ?) 

Answer: The merchant lived in a quiet village.

🦜 2️⃣ What did he keep as a pet ?

(ସେ ପୋଷା ପକ୍ଷୀ ଭାବରେ କ’ଣ ରଖିଥିଲେ ?)

 Answer: He kept a bright and talkative parrot as a pet.

🎶 3️⃣ The parrot pleased the merchant with.............................

(A) food (B) gold (C) stories (D) songs

Tick (✓) correct words. (ଶୁଆଟି ବଣିକଙ୍କୁ କେଉଁଥିରେ ଖୁସି କରୁଥିଲା...)

Answer: The parrot pleased the merchant with (C) stories and (D) songs.

🪙 4️⃣ Was the parrot happy in the gold cage ?

(ସୁନା ପଞ୍ଜୁରୀ ଭିତରେ ଶୁଆଟି ଖୁସି ଥିଲା କି ?) 

Answer: No, the parrot was not happy in the gold cage; deep inside, it longed for the open skies and green trees.

☁️ 5️⃣ What did it really want ? (ଏହା ପ୍ରକୃତରେ କ’ଣ ଚାହୁଁଥିଲା ?)

 Answer: It really wanted to taste the joy of flying in the open sky.

💼 6️⃣ The merchant had to travel far away. What for ? (ବଣିକଙ୍କୁ ବହୁତ ଦୂରକୁ ଯାତ୍ରା କରିବାକୁ ପଡ଼ିଥିଲା | କାହିଁକି ?)

Answer: The merchant had to travel far away for business.

🌶️ 7️⃣ The parrot was fond of .......................and .......................

(ଶୁଆଟି ................... ଏବଂ ................... ଖାଇବାକୁ ଭଲ ପାଉଥିଲା |) 

Answer: The parrot was fond of tender fruits and chillies.

cages 8️⃣ Did the merchant let the parrot go away ? Why ?

(ବଣିକ ଜଣକ ଶୁଆକୁ ଛାଡିଦେଲେ କି ? କାହିଁକି ?) 

Answer: No, the merchant did not let the parrot go away because the bird was very dear to him, and he could not bear to let it go.

👋 9️⃣ Who sent greetings ? To whom ?

(କିଏ ଶୁଭେଚ୍ଛା ପଠାଇଥିଲା ? କାହାକୁ ?)

 Answer: The caged parrot sent greetings to its friends (a flight of parrots) in the jungle.

🤔 1️⃣0️⃣ If you were the merchant, would you let the parrot fly away ?

(ଯଦି ତୁମେ ବଣିକ ହୋଇଥାଆନ୍ତ, ତେବେ କ’ଣ ତୁମେ ଶୁଆଟିକୁ ଉଡ଼ିଯିବାକୁ ଦେଇଥାଆନ୍ତ ?)

Answer: Yes, if I were the merchant, I would let the parrot fly away because freedom is precious for every living being, and birds belong in the open sky.

🧠 1️⃣1️⃣ Which parrot in the story was wise ?

(କାହାଣୀରେ କେଉଁ ଶୁଆଟି ଜ୍ଞାନୀ (ଚତୁର) ଥିଲା ?)

(A) the parrot in the cage (B) both of the parrots (C) the parrot in the forest

(D) none of the parrots Tick (✓) correct words. 

Answer: ☑️ (B) both of the parrots (The forest parrot was wise enough to demonstrate how to escape by faking its death, and the caged parrot was wise enough to understand the hidden message and act on it.)

📖 3️⃣ Read the following rules:

  • Birds fly.

  • Nikhil flew his kites.

  • The parrot has flown away.

The underlined words are different forms of 'fly'. 'Fly' is a verb or action word. We use it in 3 forms. Form 1 is fly, form 2 is flew, form 3 is flown. In other words form 1 is verb 1 (V1), form 2 is verb 2 (V2) and form 3 is verb 3 (V3). Every English verb has 3 forms – V1, V2, V3 In sentence 3, V3 of 'fly' is used after 'has'. V3 is usually used after has/have/had.
