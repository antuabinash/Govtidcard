Let us do these activities before we read. (Pages 139-140)

  1. 🌟 How would you choose to spend your weekends?

    (i) Trying new activities

    (ii) Reading a book

    (iii) Spending time with friends

    (iv) Learning something new

    Answer: (iv) Learning something new. (Note: You can choose any option that suits you!)

  2. 🗺️ Which of these locations would you love to discover?

    (i) Dense forests and clear streams

    (ii) Magical lands with fairies

    (iii) Busy cities with interesting people

    (iv) Quiet places to think and create

    Answer: (iv) Quiet places to think and create.

  3. 🎨 Which of these activities sounds the most appealing to you?

    (i) Climbing a mountain or exploring a jungle

    (ii) Finding a hidden treasure or discovering a secret world

    (iii) Solving puzzles or guessing riddles with friends

    (iv) Making something creative or building a model

    Answer: (iv) Making something creative or building a model.

  4. 🤝 What is your take on working in a team?

    (i) I love leading the team.

    (ii) I enjoy working with others.

    (iii) I prefer sharing ideas with friends.

    (iv) I like working alone.

    Answer: (i) I love leading the team.

  5. 🧠 Based on your selections, which personality type do you think fits you best? (Bold Explorer, Imaginative Dreamer, Energetic Planner, Thoughtful Creator). Discuss with the class.

    Answer: I believe I am a combination of a Bold Explorer, an Energetic Planner, and a Thoughtful Creator. I love taking on new challenges with a curious mind, organizing fun ideas, and expressing my creativity. This mixture helps me enjoy new adventures while building meaningful things along the way!

Let us discuss (Pages 144-145)

  1. 🐉 Suraj likens the train to a magical beast. Select the phrases from the text that show this comparison.

    Answer: The phrases showcasing this magical comparison are:

  • (a) The railway lines were two straight black serpents disappearing into the tunnel...

  • (b) The steam engine shot out of the tunnel, snorting and puffing like some green, black and gold dragon.

  • (c) It charged straight at the tunnel, and into it thundering past Suraj like the beautiful dragon of his dreams.

  1. 🚂 Suraj realized the train was approaching because ___________.

    Answer: He heard the loud, sharp whistle of the train engine echoing from afar.

  2. 🦇 What did Suraj notice inside the dark tunnel?

    Answer: The interior was completely pitch-black. He noticed that the walls were damp and sticky, a bat swooped past him, and a small lizard hurried across the railway tracks.

  3. 🏛️ Choose the correct option: What does Sunder Singh say about the tunnel?

    He says that ___________ (it is damp and sticky inside / he has lent it to the Government).

    Answer: he has lent it to the Government.

  4. 🔄 The watchman uses a specific system to warn the train driver of danger. Complete the flow chart based on the text.

    Answer:

  • Step 1: Before the train arrives, the watchman walks the entire length of the tunnel.

  • Step 2: If everything is clear and safe, he goes back to his hut for a nap.

  • Step 3: If he spots any danger, he walks up the track and waves a bright red flag.

  • Step 4: The engine driver sees the flag and slows the train down to avoid an accident.

Let us discuss (Page 149)

  1. 🐾 Sunder Singh was aware of the leopard's presence in the tunnel because ___________.

    Answer: he could hear its soft, padded footsteps and the distinct rustling of dry leaves inside.

  2. 🤫 Why do you suppose Suraj couldn't shout inside the tunnel initially?

    (i) He was afraid of the leopard.

    (ii) He didn’t want to disturb the leopard.

    (iii) He was trying to listen for the leopard.

    (iv) He didn’t really believe Sunder Singh.

    Answer: (iii) He was trying to listen for the leopard.

  3. 🐆 What caused the leopard to vanish into the pitch-black tunnel?

    Answer: The loud, echoing shouts of both Suraj and Sunder Singh frightened the animal. Hearing the magnified noises, the leopard assumed a large group of humans was chasing it and ran away in fear.

  4. 🪔 How did Suraj know his friend Sunder Singh was at the tunnel?

    Answer: While riding a train to Delhi with his father, Suraj spotted a small glowing lamp outside in the dark. Even without seeing a face, he instantly knew it was his good friend Sunder Singh standing guard.

  5. 💼 Why did Suraj’s father bring him along on the trip to Delhi?

    Answer: His father believed it was the right time for Suraj to start learning the ropes of their family business.

Let us think and reflect (Pages 149-151)

Extract 1: "Suraj had to turn and look back at the opening to reassure himself..."

  1. ☀️ True or False: Suraj glanced back at the tunnel entrance to see if a train was arriving.

    Answer: False. (He looked back simply to confirm that there was still daylight outside).

  2. 🚶‍♂️ Choose the correct option: Suraj didn't pause inside the tunnel because ________.

    A. there was nothing to see in the tunnel

    B. the tunnel was damp and sticky

    C. he was in a hurry to get out of the tunnel

    D. it was time for another train to come

    Answer: A. there was nothing to see in the tunnel.

  3. 🌳 What is the meaning of the phrase 'Till then, the cutting belonged to the jungle again'?

    Answer: It implies that until the next train passes through, the man-made track area becomes a quiet, undisturbed part of the wild forest once more.

  4. 🦁 Suraj wished to explore the tunnel's interior. What does this reveal about his personality?

    Answer: This shows that Suraj is highly curious, fearless, and adventure-loving.

Extract 2: "The train will soon be here," said Suraj...

  1. 😟 What was the watchman worrying about?

    Answer: He heard the leopard inside the tunnel and was worried that if they didn't chase it out, the approaching train would tragically run it over.

  2. 🐐 Complete the sentence: Sunder Singh was confident the leopard wouldn't attack them because ________.

    Answer: He was very familiar with this specific leopard and knew its weakness was hunting goats and stray dogs, not humans.

  3. ✅ Pick the true statements from the list below:

    A. Suraj wants to see leopards in the jungle.

    B. Suraj is concerned about Sunder Singh’s safety.

    C. The leopard in the tunnel attacks goats and stray dogs.

    D. Sunder Singh wants to protect Suraj.

    E. Sunder Singh knows all about the animals in the jungle.

    Answer: Statements B, C, D, and E are true.

  4. 🪓 Choose the correct option: Sunder Singh chooses to carry his axe. What does this indicate about him?

    A. violent

    B. cautious

    C. bold

    D. afraid

    Answer: B. cautious.

General Questions:

  1. 🗣️ What is Sunder Singh implying when he claims he has 'lent' the tunnel to the Government?

    Answer: Because the tunnel is situated in an isolated jungle and government officials never visit it, Sunder Singh is the only one caring for it. While he legally knows it belongs to the government, his daily dedication makes him feel a strong sense of personal ownership over the tunnel.

  2. 🏙️ "It is safer in the forest than in the town." What example does Sunder give to support this, and what does it reveal about him?

    Answer: Sunder explains that he was nearly hit by a speeding bus during a recent trip to town. This reveals that he values the peaceful, predictable life of the jungle much more than the chaotic, dangerous environment of the city.

  3. 🌿 Prove that Sunder Singh is deeply connected to jungle life using a valid reason.

    Answer: Sunder Singh's deep bond with the jungle is proven by his perfect understanding of animal behavior. He isn't blindly terrified of the leopard because he knows its specific diet and habits. However, he still carries an axe to ensure safety, showing a brilliant mix of respect and knowledge of the wild.

  4. 🤝 What brings Sunder Singh and Suraj together in an unusual friendship?

    Answer: Their mutual fascination with nature and the forest unites them. Suraj, an adventurous city boy, loves exploring the mysteries of the tunnel, while Sunder Singh appreciates the calm of the jungle. Their shared respect for the wild bridges the gap between their completely different backgrounds and ages.

  5. ✨ Why is Sunder Singh compared to a 'firefly'?

    Answer: Just like a tiny firefly lights up the dark, Sunder Singh stands in the pitch-black jungle with his glowing lamp. He acts as a guiding light for the massive trains, offering comfort and a feeling of safety to travelers passing through the lonely night.

  6. 🚵‍♂️ Do you consider Suraj to be an adventurous boy? Provide reasons.

    Answer: Absolutely! Suraj cycled far from the city, walked deep into a dense jungle alone, and boldly explored a dark tunnel. He stood close to a roaring train, felt the cinders flying, and bravely helped the watchman chase a wild leopard out of the darkness. All these actions clearly prove his deep love for adventure.

Let us learn (Pages 151-153)

  1. 🚂 Complete the sentences using the correct phrases: (drifting smoke, flickering lights, sawing sound, approaching engine)

    Answer:

    1. We could hear the approaching engine of the train long before it came into view at the station.

    1. The street was completely dark but the little boy walked to his house guided by the flickering lights from the houses.

    1. The sawing sound from the carpenter’s workshop grew louder every hour.

    1. From the balcony, she watched the drifting smoke from her neighbour’s chimney float across the backyard.

  1. 🔊 Fill in the blanks with the correct form of the sound words: (puffing, hissing, creaking, rustling, chattering)

    Answer:

    1. The steam engine reached the station puffing black smoke into the sky.

    1. Suddenly the snake raised its hood and made a hissing noise.

    1. When the wind blew, I heard the creaking of the wooden roof.

    1. He heard a rustling of leaves behind him when he was walking in the woods.

    1. I get disturbed by the continuous chattering of the monkeys in the neighbourhood.

  1. 🕰️ Grammar: 'Would' and 'Used to'

    Question 1: Why is the word 'would' used in the text about the watchman?

    (i) to make a polite request | (ii) to express a desire | (iii) to talk about a habit in the past

    Answer: (iii) to talk about a habit in the past.

Question 2: Complete the paragraph with the correct forms of used to or would.

Answer:

When I was a child we used to live in a village near a forest. Back then, we (i) used to grow vegetables in our back garden. Every day we (ii) would get up early and go for a walk. The weather in summer (iii) used to be really nice. There was a lake near the house and we (iv) would go there every Sunday for a picnic. Winters (v) used to be rather cold. In the evenings, my father (vi) would tell us incredible stories and we (vii) would listen with admiration.

  1. 📝 Punctuate the following paragraph using colons (:) and semicolons (;).

    Answer:

    We explored the busy by-lanes of Charminar, the heart of Hyderabad; it was an exciting day. We wanted to do a few things: buy bangles, taste biryani, and purchase pearl earrings, which are special to this area. As we walked through the lanes, we saw stalls selling spices, clothes, and jewellery; the market was full of colours. We visited some famous spots: Charminar, Golconda Fort, Salar Jung Museum, and Chowmahalla Palace; each place was beautiful.

Let us speak (Pages 154-155)

  1. 🗣️ Work in pairs and roleplay the conversation between Anju and the Tour Manager to find the trekking site on the map.

    Answer:

    Anju: Hello! We just arrived at the railway station. Could you guide us to the trekking site?

    Tour Manager: Of course, Anju! Let's get you there. Start from the railway station, take a left, and then take the first right.

    Anju: Got it. I can see the Lassi Corner and a school on this road.

    Tour Manager: Perfect! Keep going, turn left at the crossing, and walk straight until you hit the roundabout.

    Anju: Okay, I am at the roundabout now. Which way?

    Tour Manager: Take the third exit from the roundabout and continue straight. You will see the Trekking Site on your right, directly opposite the Lake and Bird Sanctuary!

    Anju: I found it! Thank you so much for the directions.

Let us write (Page 155)

  1. ✍️ Write a descriptive paragraph about your experience on a train journey. Highlight the sights, sounds, and your feelings.

    Answer:

    A Magical Train Ride

    My adventure began the moment I stepped onto the bustling railway platform, clutching my ticket tightly. After finding my comfortable window seat, I watched the chaotic yet fascinating station scene—vendors shouting, passengers rushing, and luggage carts rolling by. Suddenly, a loud, piercing whistle signaled our departure, and the train lurched forward with a rhythmic "clack-clack" on the metal tracks. As we left the crowded city behind, the view transformed into a gorgeous painting of endless green fields, sparkling rivers, and quaint little villages. Inside the compartment, the atmosphere was lively; families shared homemade snacks, and children played games. I felt incredibly relaxed and mesmerized, completely lost in the beautiful sights and soothing sounds of my unforgettable train journey.
