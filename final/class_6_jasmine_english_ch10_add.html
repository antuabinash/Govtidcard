Section K: Paragraph Writing (5 Marks)

Answer 93. The Importance of Sports and Wellness:

🏃‍♂️ Sports and wellness are absolutely essential for the holistic development of a student. In today’s digital age, where children spend hours sitting in front of screens, participating in physical sports is the key to maintaining a healthy body and preventing lifestyle diseases. Regular exercise keeps our muscles strong and our hearts healthy. Furthermore, wellness ensures that our minds remain sharp, reducing academic stress and anxiety. When a student eats a balanced diet and plays outdoors, their concentration in class naturally improves. Ultimately, a healthy lifestyle builds a strong foundation for a happy, energetic, and successful future.

Answer 94. How Team Sports Teach Us Important Life Skills:

🤝 Playing team sports is like attending a miniature school of life. On the field, players learn the immense value of teamwork, realizing that collective effort is far more powerful than individual talent. Sports teach discipline, as students must follow strict rules and practice daily. Moreover, it teaches the crucial skill of communication, as players must coordinate strategies under pressure. Most importantly, sports teach resilience and sportsmanship—how to accept crushing defeats with grace and celebrate victories with deep humility. These lessons shape students into patient, cooperative, and strong-willed adults.

Answer 95. My Favorite Outdoor Sport and Why I Love Playing It:
