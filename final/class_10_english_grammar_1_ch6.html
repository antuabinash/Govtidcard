1. Introduction to Clauses (ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶର ପରିଚୟ)

What is a Clause? (ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶ କ’ଣ?)

A clause is a group of words that forms part of a longer sentence. It must contain a subject and a predicate (which includes a finite verb).

(Odia: ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶ (Clause) ହେଉଛି ଏକ ଶବ୍ଦ ସମୂହ ଯେଉଁଥିରେ ଏକ କର୍ତ୍ତା (subject) ଏବଂ ଏକ ବିଧେୟ (predicate) ଥାଏ । ଏହା ଏକ ବଡ଼ ବାକ୍ୟର ଅଂଶ ହୋଇପାରେ ।)

Types of Clauses (ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶର ପ୍ରକାର)

  1. Independent Clause (ସ୍ୱାଧୀନ ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶ):
    • It can stand alone as a complete sentence. It is also called a Main Clause.
    • A sentence with only one independent clause is a Simple Sentence.
    • A sentence with two or more independent clauses (joined by and, but, or, etc.) is a Compound Sentence.
  2. Dependent Clause (ପରାଧୀନ ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶ):
    • It cannot stand alone as a complete sentence. It depends on the main clause. It is also called a Subordinate Clause.
    • A sentence with one main clause and one or more subordinate clauses is a Complex Sentence.

Types of Subordinate Clauses (ପରାଧୀନ ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶର ପ୍ରକାର)

There are three types of subordinate clauses:

  1. Noun Clause (ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶ)
  2. Relative Clause / Adjective Clause (ବିଶେଷଣ ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶ)
  3. Adverb Clause (କ୍ରିୟା ବିଶେଷଣ ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶ)

This chapter focuses on the first two types.

2. Noun Clauses (ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶ)

A Noun Clause is a dependent clause that does the work of a noun. It can be a subject, object, or complement, just like a single-word noun.

(Odia: ଏକ Noun Clause ହେଉଛି ଏକ dependent clause ଯାହା ବାକ୍ୟରେ ଏକ ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ (noun) ପରି କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ କରେ । ଏହା କର୍ତ୍ତା, କର୍ମ ବା ପୂରକ ଭାବରେ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ କରିପାରେ ।)

Markers of Noun Clauses (ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶର ସୂଚକ)

Noun clauses are introduced by specific words:

  • that (for statements)
  • if / whether (for Yes/No questions)
  • Wh-words: what, who, whom, whose, which, where, when, why, how
  • Wh-ever words: whatever, whoever, whenever, etc.


Functions of Noun Clauses (ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶର କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)

A noun clause can function in many ways in a sentence.

Example (ଉଦାହରଣ)

1. Subject of a verb

କ୍ରିୟାର କର୍ତ୍ତା

The clause acts as the subject of the main verb.

What he says is correct.



That his father has come is true.

2. Object of a transitive verb

କ୍ରିୟାର କର୍ମ

The clause acts as the direct object of the main verb. (Answers "what?")

Radha said (that) she was happy.



I asked Radha how she felt.

3. Subject Complement

କର୍ତ୍ତାର ପୂରକ

The clause follows a linking verb (like is, was, seems) and renames or describes the subject.

The problem is that we do not have any money.



Life is what we make it.

4. Object of a preposition

ଅବ୍ୟୟର କର୍ମ

The clause follows a preposition (like to, in, on, about).

You should pay attention to what I say.



There is no meaning in what you say.

5. In Apposition to a Noun/Pronoun

ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ/ସର୍ବନାମର ସମାନାଧିକାର

The clause is "placed near" (apposition) a noun or pronoun to explain it.

(Noun): The news that his father has come is true. (The clause explains "The news".)



(Pronoun): It is true that his father has come. (The clause explains "It".)

6. Adjective Complement

ବିଶେଷଣର ପୂରକ

The clause completes the meaning of an adjective.

He is happy that he is learning English.

7. Object of an Infinitive

Infinitive ର କର୍ମ

The clause acts as the object of a to-infinitive.

He went to fix what he had done.

8. Object of a Gerund

Gerund ର କର୍ମ

The clause acts as the object of a gerund (-ing form).

Believing what your eyes see sometimes leads to misunderstanding.


3. Relative Clauses (ସମ୍ବନ୍ଧସୂଚକ ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶ)

A Relative Clause is also called an Adjective Clause (ବିଶେଷଣ ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶ). It is a dependent clause that works like an adjective. It modifies (describes or identifies) a noun or pronoun that comes before it. This noun is called the antecedent (ପୂର୍ବବର୍ତ୍ତୀ ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ).

(Odia: Relative Clause ଏକ ବିଶେଷଣ (adjective) ପରି କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ କରେ । ଏହା ତା' ପୂର୍ବରୁ ଥିବା ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ ବା ସର୍ବନାମ (antecedent) କୁ ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା ବା ଚିହ୍ନଟ କରେ ।)

Markers of Relative Clauses (Relative Pronouns)

  • who / whom (for people)
  • whose (for possession)
  • which (for things and animals)
  • that (for people, things, and animals)
  • where (for places)

Combining Sentences (ବାକ୍ୟ ସଂଯୋଗ)

Relative clauses are used to combine two sentences that share a common noun.

  • Sentences: The boy is our monitor. + I have borrowed his book.
  • Combined: The boy whose book I have borrowed is our monitor.
  • Sentences: I write with the pen. + My father bought the pen for me.
  • Combined: I write with the pen which my father bought for me.

Dropping the Relative Pronoun (ସର୍ବନାମ ଛାଡିବା)

The relative pronoun (who, which, that) can be omitted (dropped) only if it is the object in the relative clause.

  • Can be dropped: I have read the book (which) you gave me. (Here, "which" is the object of "gave".)
  • Cannot be dropped: The boy who is playing is my friend. (Here, "who" is the subject of "is playing".)

4. Defining vs. Non-Defining Relative Clauses

This is the most important concept for understanding relative clauses.

Feature (ବୈଶିଷ୍ଟ୍ୟ)

Defining (Identifying) Clause

Non-Defining (Non-Identifying) Clause

Odia Name

ସଂଜ୍ଞାବାଚକ (ଅପରିହାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)

ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନାମୂଳକ (ଅନାବଶ୍ୟକ)

Function (କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)

Identifies the noun. It is essential to the meaning. (ବିଶେଷ୍ୟକୁ ଚିହ୍ନଟ କରେ । ଅର୍ଥ ପାଇଁ ଏହା ଜରୁରୀ ।)

Gives extra information about the noun, which is already identified. Not essential. (ଅତିରିକ୍ତ ସୂଚନା ଦିଏ । ଏହା ଅର୍ଥ ପାଇଁ ଜରୁରୀ ନୁହେଁ ।)

Punctuation (ବିରାମ ଚିହ୍ନ)

No commas (କମା (,) ଲାଗେ ନାହିଁ)

Uses commas (,) to separate the clause. (କମା (,) ଦ୍ୱାରା ଅଲଗା ହୁଏ)

Pronoun 'that' ('that'ର ବ୍ୟବହାର)

Can use that (for people or things). (ବ୍ୟବହାର କରାଯାଇପାରେ)

Cannot use that. (ବ୍ୟବହାର କରାଯାଇପାରିବ ନାହିଁ)

Dropping Pronoun (ସର୍ବନାମ ଛାଡିବା)

Can be dropped (if it is the object).

Cannot be dropped. (କଦାପି ଛଡାଯାଇପାରିବ ନାହିଁ)

Example (ଉଦାହରଣ)

The travellers who knew about the floods took another road.



(Meaning: Only those specific travellers who knew about the floods took another road.)

My uncle, who is a judge, is coming here tomorrow.



(Meaning: I have an uncle (he is already identified), and as extra information, he is a judge.)

Implication (ଅର୍ଥ)

"My uncle who is a judge is coming."



(Implies: I have more than one uncle, and I am talking about the one who is a judge.)

"My uncle, who is a judge, is coming."



(Implies: I have only one uncle.)

Special Use of 'Which' (Non-Defining)

A non-defining clause using which can refer to the entire main clause before it, not just a single noun.

  • Example: Satish scored a goal in the last minute, which was fortunate.
    • (Here, "which" = the entire fact that "Satish scored a goal in the last minute".)

🔗 Activity 1 (Relative Clauses)

Instructions (English): Combine the following pairs of sentences using one of them as a relative clause.

Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟ ଯୋଡିଗୁଡିକୁ ଗୋଟିଏ relative clause ଭାବେ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ମିଶ୍ରଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

1.      The birds are chirping. + The birds live in the neighbourhood.

Answer: The birds that (or which) live in the neighbourhood are chirping.

2.      I write with the pen. + My father bought the pen for me.

Answer: I write with the pen that (or which) my father bought for me.

3.      The sick were delighted. + Florence Nightingale nursed the sick.

Answer: The sick whom Florence Nightingale nursed were delighted.

4.      I like the man. + The man has a smiling face.

Answer: I like the man who has a smiling face.

5.      The thief escaped in the dark. + The thief had robbed me.

Answer: The thief who had robbed me escaped in the dark.

6.      The boy is our monitor. + I have borrowed his book.

Answer: The boy whose book I have borrowed is our monitor.

7.      The chair has a cracked leg. + He is sitting in the chair.

Answer: The chair in which he is sitting has a cracked leg. (or The chair (that) he is sitting in has a cracked leg.)

ସୂଚନା: ଏହା ଏକ ଶ୍ରେଣୀଗୃହ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକଳାପ ଏବଂ ଏହାର କୌଣସି ଗୋଟିଏ "ସଠିକ୍" ଉତ୍ତର ନାହିଁ। Relative clause ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ସଂଜ୍ଞା ତିଆରି କରିବା ହେଉଛି ଏହାର ଉଦ୍ଦେଶ୍ୟ।

Student A: "It is an animal which lives in the jungle and is called the king." (ଏହା ଏକ ପଶୁ ଯିଏ ଜଙ୍ଗଲରେ ରହେ ଏବଂ ରାଜା କୁହାଯାଏ।)

  1. I bought a pen. It is not working.

Answer: The pen which (that) I bought is not working.

  1. I remember the day. We won the match on that day.

Answer: I remember the day when we won the match.

  1. He is the artist. I was talking about him.

Answer: He is the artist about whom I was talking. (or He is the artist whom I was talking about.)


🔗 Activity F: Sentence Combination (Noun Clauses)

Instructions (English): Combine the following pairs of sentences into one complex sentence using a Noun Clause.

Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟ ଯୋଡିଗୁଡିକୁ ଏକ Noun Clause ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ଗୋଟିଏ ଜଟିଳ ବାକ୍ୟରେ ମିଶ୍ରଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

  1. He will pass. I am sure of it.

Answer: I am sure that he will pass.

  1. He is a liar. It is known to all.

Answer: That he is a liar is known to all.

  1. Where does he live? I do not know.

Answer: I do not know where he lives.

  1. He said something. I agree to it.

Answer: I agree to what he said.

  1. He may be innocent. We don't know.

Answer: We don't know if (whether) he is innocent.

  1. What is his problem? His problem is this. He has no money.

Answer: His problem is that he has no money.

  1. He is wrong. I believe it.

Answer: I believe that he is wrong.

  1. Why is he angry? Tell me the reason.

Answer: Tell me why he is angry.

  1. He will come. That is my hope.

Answer: My hope is that he will come.

  1. You are a fool. The fact is obvious.

Answer: The fact that you are a fool is obvious.

Instructions (English): Rewrite the sentences by changing the underlined phrase into a subordinate clause (Noun or Relative).

Instructions (Odia): ରେଖାଙ୍କିତ ବାକ୍ୟାଂଶକୁ (phrase) ଏକ ଅଧୀନ ଉପବାକ୍ୟ (Noun କିମ୍ବା Relative) ରେ ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ କରି ବାକ୍ୟଟିକୁ ପୁନର୍ଲିଖନ କରନ୍ତୁ।

✏️ Activity H: Error Correction

Instructions (English): Find the error (related to clauses) in each sentence and rewrite it correctly.

Instructions (Odia): ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ ବାକ୍ୟରେ ଥିବା (clause ସମ୍ବନ୍ଧୀୟ) ଭୁଲକୁ ଖୋଜନ୍ତୁ ଏବଂ ଏହାକୁ ସଠିକ୍ କରି ପୁନର୍ଲିଖନ କରନ୍ତୁ।

Incorrect Sentence (ଭୁଲ ବାକ୍ୟ)

Correct Sentence (ସଠିକ୍ ବାକ୍ୟ)

1.

The man, that is standing there, is my father.

The man, who is standing there, is my father.

2.

Tell me what is his name?

Tell me what his name is.

3.

The boy who I lent my book him has not returned it.

The boy whom I lent my book has not returned it.

4.

I asked him that what he wanted.

I asked him what he wanted.

5.

This is the book, that I lost.

This is the book that I lost. (No comma)

6.

He told me what was the matter.

He told me what the matter was.

7.

My uncle who is a rich man lives in Mumbai.

My uncle, who is a rich man, lives in Mumbai.

8.

I believe what does he say.

I believe what he says.

9.

The book what I bought is costly.

The book which (or that) I bought is costly.

10.

It is clear that is he honest.

It is clear that he is honest.
