My Land : Together We Can – Book Q A Class 5 English Pallavi

Let us Discuss

1️⃣ The poem is about ..........................of people.

(କବିତାଟି ଲୋକମାନଙ୍କର ....... ବିଷୟରେ ଅଟେ।)

Options: fight, failure, unity, quarrel

Answer: unity

2️⃣ What happens when we help each other ? (stanza1)

(ଯେତେବେଳେ ଆମେ ପରସ୍ପରକୁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରୁ, ସେତେବେଳେ କ’ଣ ହୁଏ?)

Answer: When we help each other, we never fall.

3️⃣ What helps to face any difficulty ? (stanza 2)

(କୌଣସି ଅସୁବିଧାର ସମ୍ମୁଖୀନ ହେବାକୁ କ’ଣ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରେ?)

Answer: Teamwork can overcome anything.

4️⃣ What should we share ? (stanza 3)

(ଆମେ କ’ଣ ବାଣ୍ଟିବା ଉଚିତ୍?)

Answer: We should always share whether we win or lose.

5️⃣ We can reach our goal when.............................

(ଆମେ ଆମର ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟରେ ପହଞ୍ଚିପାରିବା ଯେତେବେଳେ...)

Options:

(A) we join our hands.

(B) we are divided.

(C) we sing and dance.

(D) we do not work together.

Answer: (A) we join our hands.

Let us Read

1️⃣ Read the following passage silently and answer the questions that follow.

(ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦକୁ ନୀରବରେ ପଢ଼ ଏବଂ ଏହା ପରେ ଥିବା ପ୍ରଶ୍ନଗୁଡ଼ିକର ଉତ୍ତର ଦିଅ |)

Passage:

An ant lived in a kitchen with its family and friends. One day he saw a big 'laddu' on the rack. He wanted to bring the laddu for his family and friends. But he could not, as the laddu was too big. He thought of a plan. He called all his friends and family members to take the laddu. All together they slowly brought the laddu to their home and ate it happily.

Questions :

1️⃣ (i) What did the ant see on the rack

(ପିମ୍ପୁଡ଼ିଟି ର୍ୟାକ୍ ଉପରେ କ’ଣ ଦେଖିଲା?)

Answer: The ant saw a big 'laddu' on the rack.

2️⃣ (ii) Could the ant bring the Laddu ?

(ପିମ୍ପୁଡ଼ିଟି ଲଡୁ ଆଣିପାରିଲା କି?)

Answer: No, the ant could not bring the laddu alone because it was too big.

3️⃣ (iii) How did he bring the laddu ?

(ସେ ଲଡୁକୁ କିପରି ଆଣିଲା?)

Answer: He called all his friends and family members, and all together they slowly brought the laddu to their home.

4️⃣ (iv) What do you learn from the story ?

(ତୁମେ ଏହି କାହାଣୀରୁ କ’ଣ ଶିଖିଲ?)

Answer: We learn from the story that when we work together as a team, we can easily complete difficult tasks. (Teamwork makes things possible).

Answer (Completed Story):

A boy lived in a village. One rainy morning he was going to school. The road was muddy. He fell down. He got up and reached school in his dirty and wet clothes.

The teacher praised him. Holidays came. He called his friends. They all worked hard and filled the pit with stones and sand. The road was made. It was safe for the people. Everyone thanked the boy and the team. Do you know the boy? He was no other than Madhusudan Das.

An ant lived in a kitchen with its family and friends. One day he saw a big 'laddu' on the rack. He wanted to bring the laddu for his family and friends. But he could not, as the laddu was too big. He thought of a plan. He called all his friends and family members to take the laddu. All together they slowly brought the laddu to their home and ate it happily.
