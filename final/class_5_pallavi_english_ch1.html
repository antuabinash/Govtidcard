📜 Original Stanza:

 Today our Grandpa

Is searching everywhere,

He has lost his glasses,

It was his only pair !

🗣️ English-Odia .:

 ଟୁଡେ ଆୱାର୍ ଗ୍ରାଣ୍ଡପା,

ଇଜ୍ ସର୍ଚ୍ଚିଂ ଏଭ୍ରିହ୍ୱେଆର୍,

ଇଟ୍ ୱାଜ୍ ହିଜ୍ ଓନଲି ପେୟାର୍ !

📝 Odia Meaning: ଆଜି ଆମ ଜେଜେବାପା ସବୁଆଡେ ଖୋଜୁଛନ୍ତି, ସେ ତାଙ୍କ ଚଷମା ହରାଇ ଦେଇଛନ୍ତି, ତାହା ତାଙ୍କର ଏକମାତ୍ର ଚଷମା ଥିଲା !

➡️ Stanza 4

📜 Original Stanza:

 I told him, "Stop Grandpa !"

I went up close and said !

"Grandpa, your glasses

Are right there on your head!"

🗣️ English-Odia .: ଆଇ ଟୋଲ୍ଡ ହିମ୍, "ଷ୍ଟପ୍ ଗ୍ରାଣ୍ଡପା !" ଆଇ ୱେଣ୍ଟ ଅପ୍ କ୍ଲୋଜ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ସେଡ୍ ! "ଗ୍ରାଣ୍ଡପା, ୟୋର୍ ଗ୍ଲାସେସ୍, ଆର୍ ରାଇଟ୍ ଦେଆର୍ ଅନ୍ ୟୋର୍ ହେଡ୍!"

📝 Odia Meaning: ମୁଁ ତାଙ୍କୁ କହିଲି, "ଅଟକି ଯାଅ ଜେଜେବାପା !" ମୁଁ ପାଖକୁ ଗଲି ଏବଂ କହିଲି ! "ଜେଜେବାପା, ତୁମ ଚଷମା ଠିକ୍ ତୁମ ମୁଣ୍ଡ ଉପରେ ଅଛି!"

💡 English Meaning: Finally, the grandchild stopped him from searching. The child went near Grandpa and laughingly told him that his missing glasses were actually pushed up and resting right on top of his own head the entire time!

🔤 Word Meanings :

Told ➡️ କହିଲି

Right there ➡️ ଠିକ୍ ସେଠାରେ

Close ➡️ ପାଖକୁ

📝 Summary (English Only)

"Grandpa's Glasses" is a highly entertaining and funny poem about forgetfulness. The poem begins by describing a grandfather who is searching everywhere in the house because he has lost his spectacles. He is very worried because it is his only pair. Without his glasses, he is completely helpless. He is unable to read his daily newspaper, watch television, or even cut vegetables.

To find his missing glasses, Grandpa searches in every possible corner. He checks inside his pockets, takes a quick look under his chair, and even searches near the window. Just when he is looking everywhere in frustration, his grandchild comes to the rescue. The grandchild stops Grandpa, walks very close to him, and points out a hilarious mistake. The glasses were never lost; Grandpa had simply pushed them up, and they were resting right on top of his own head all along!

📝 Summary (Odia)

"ଗ୍ରାଣ୍ଡପାଜ୍ ଗ୍ଲାସେସ୍" (ଜେଜେବାପାଙ୍କ ଚଷମା) ହେଉଛି ଭୁଲିଯିବା ସ୍ୱଭାବ ଉପରେ ଆଧାରିତ ଏକ ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ମଜାଦାର କବିତା। କବିତାଟି ଜଣେ ଜେଜେବାପାଙ୍କୁ ନେଇ ଆରମ୍ଭ ହୁଏ ଯିଏ ତାଙ୍କର ଏକମାତ୍ର ଚଷମା ହରାଇ ଦେଇ ଘରର ସବୁଆଡେ ବ୍ୟସ୍ତ ହୋଇ ଖୋଜୁଛନ୍ତି। ଚଷମା ବିନା ସେ କୌଣସି କାମ କରିପାରୁନାହାନ୍ତି। ସେ ଖବରକାଗଜ ପଢିପାରୁନାହାନ୍ତି, ଟିଭି ଦେଖିପାରୁନାହାନ୍ତି କିମ୍ବା ପନିପରିବା ମଧ୍ୟ କାଟିପାରୁନାହାନ୍ତି।

ନିଜର ହଜିଯାଇଥିବା ଚଷମା ପାଇବା ପାଇଁ ସେ ସବୁ ଜାଗାରେ ଖୋଜନ୍ତି। ସେ ନିଜ ପକେଟ୍ ଭିତରେ ଯାଞ୍ଚ କରନ୍ତି, ଚୌକି ତଳେ ଖୋଜନ୍ତି ଏବଂ ଝରକା ପାଖରେ ମଧ୍ୟ ଦେଖନ୍ତି। ଯେତେବେଳେ ସେ ସବୁଆଡେ ଖୋଜି ନିରାଶ ହୋଇଯାଆନ୍ତି, ସେତେବେଳେ ତାଙ୍କ ନାତି/ନାତୁଣୀ ତାଙ୍କୁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରେ। ପିଲାଟି ତାଙ୍କୁ ଅଟକାଇ ପାଖକୁ ଯାଏ ଏବଂ ଏକ ମଜାଦାର କଥା କହେ। ଚଷମା ଆଦୌ ହଜି ନଥିଲା; ଜେଜେବାପା ସେଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ଟେକି ଦେଇଥିଲେ ଏବଂ ତାହା ଠିକ୍ ତାଙ୍କ ମୁଣ୍ଡ ଉପରେ ହିଁ ଥିଲା!

🎓 Suggestions for Students

🌟 Have fun reciting this poem! Try to act like a confused grandpa while reciting the first three stanzas, and act like a laughing child in the last stanza.

🌟 Notice the rhyming words at the end of the lines, like 'everywhere' and 'chair', or 'said' and 'head'. This makes the poem sound like a song.

🌟 Practice the dictation words in your notebook to improve your spelling skills.

👨‍🏫 Suggestions for Teachers: How to Teach

🌟 Total Periods Required: 2 Periods.

🌟 Period 1 (Recitation & Enjoyment): Start the class with a fun question: "Have you ever seen your grandparents looking for something that they are already holding?" Read the poem aloud with clear voice modulation and actions. Encourage students to sing the poem together as a chorus.

🌟 Period 2 (Vocabulary & Role-Play): Conduct a small classroom role-play. Let one student play 'Grandpa' with imaginary glasses on their head, pretending to look under chairs and tables, while another student plays the grandchild who finds it. Explain the new words like 'glanced' and 'pair' using simple examples. Conduct a quick spelling test at the end.

✍️ Section E: Grammar & Language Skills

🌟 61. Question: What is the plural form of the words 'pocket' and 'glass'?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: 'pocket' ଏବଂ 'glass' ଶବ୍ଦର ବହୁବଚନ ରୂପ କ'ଣ?)

🌟 62. Question: Choose the correct preposition: Grandpa looked (in / under) his chair.

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ସଠିକ୍ ପ୍ରିପୋଜିସନ୍ ବାଛ: ଜେଜେବାପା ତାଙ୍କ ଚୌକି (in / under) ଖୋଜିଲେ।)

🌟 63. Question: Write the past tense of the verbs 'search' and 'find'.
