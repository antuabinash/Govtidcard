Friendship-The Chair – Book Q A Class 6 Jasmine English

📖 Book Page 56: Let Us Do These Activities Before We Read

  1. 💭 Write two words that come to your mind when you think of friendship.
  • Answer: Trust, Support. (You can also write Love, Care, or Joy).
  1. 🔠 Fill in the blanks to complete the words, which mean the same as 'friend' with the help of the meanings:
  • (a) A person who you do things together with: P _ n _ r
    • Answer: Partner.
  • (b) A student who is in your class: Cla _ m _ t _
    • Answer: Classmate.

🗣️ Book Page 58: Let Us Discuss (Part I)

  1. 👦 Papu was very proud of having so many friends. State whether True or False.
  • Answer: True.
  1. 🪑 What did the grandfather take from the attic?
  • Answer: The grandfather brought an invisible, special magic chair from the attic.
  1. 🤔 Will Papu face any challenges?
  • Answer: Yes, Papu will face a big challenge because the chair is invisible, making it very tricky and difficult to sit on without falling.
  1. ✨ Will Papu be able to use the magic?
  • Answer: Yes, he will finally experience the magic and use it to discover who his real friends are.

🗣️ Book Page 60: Let Us Discuss (Part II)

  1. 🪑 Papu successfully sat on the invisible chair on his first attempt. State whether True or False.
  • Answer: False.
  1. 🍎 Fill in the blanks with a suitable word. Papu's grandfather challenged him to a bet of ________.
  • Answer: fruit chaat.
  1. 🤝 Match the characters with their action:
  • Characters: Rony, Amrit, Prateek / Other classmates
  • Answer: * Rony, Amrit, and Prateek ➡️ a. held Papu up, so he wouldn't fall.
    • Other classmates ➡️ b. made fun of Papu's attempts.

🤔 Book Page 60: Let Us Think and Reflect

  1. 📖 Read the following lines and fill in the blanks. "Take it. It's a very special chair. As it's invisible, it's rather tricky to sit on."
  • (a) 🪑 The chair is ________ and ________ (two words that describe the chair).
    • Answer: special, invisible.
  • (b) 🤹 It is tricky to sit on the chair because ________.
    • Answer: it is invisible and cannot be seen.
  • (c) 🔄 Write the word opposite in meaning of:
    • (i) special - Answer: ordinary (or common).
    • (ii) invisible - Answer: visible.
  1. ✍️ Answer the following questions.
  • (a) 🗣️ What was the bet about?
    • Answer: The bet was about proving how many true friends Papu actually had. Grandpa challenged him, saying most of them were just companions, not real friends.
  • (b) 🤕 What happened when Papu sat on the chair for the first time?
    • Answer: Because he could not see the chair, he missed the seat and fell straight onto his backside, and his classmates laughed at him.
  • (c) 🫂 Who are Papu's real friends? How was he able to find his real friends?
    • Answer: Rony, Amrit, and Prateek are his true friends. He found this out because when he was falling, they held him up in mid-air to support him, while the fake friends just enjoyed his bad luck and laughed.
  • (d) 💭 Do you like the magic chair test? Why?
    • Answer: Yes, the magic chair test is brilliant. It practically proves that true friends are those who care for us and support us when we fall, instead of making fun of our mistakes.
  • (e) 💡 Mention any other way that would help you find out who are your real friends?
    • Answer: Another way to find genuine friends is to share a personal problem with them. A true friend will try their best to comfort and help you, while a fake friend might make excuses and ignore you.

✍️ Book Pages 64-65: Let Us Write

  1. ✉️ Here is a letter from Papu to his friend Amrit. Read the letter and fill in the blanks using the words given in the box. (Words: request, planned, incomplete, fruit chaat, hope, fine, friends, know, grandfather).
  • Answer:

Dear Amrit,

I hope you are fine. I am well too. You know that my grandfather has planned for a fruit chaat party. I request you to attend this party. All our friends are coming, without you; this party is really incomplete. I hope that you may come. Please convey my regards to Uncle and Aunt.

Yours lovingly,

Papu
