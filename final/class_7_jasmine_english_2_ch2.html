✍️ Writer Biography: Eliza Cook

Eliza Cook (1818–1889) was a renowned English poet born in London. She started writing poetry at a very young age and became highly popular for her simple language and inspiring themes. Her poems often carried strong moral messages, focusing on hard work, perseverance, and everyday struggles. "Try Again" is one of her most famous narrative poems, beloved for the powerful lesson it teaches about never giving up in the face of failure.

📖 Stanza 1 Explanation

📜 Original Text:

 King Bruce of Scotland flung himself down

In a lonely mood to think;

'Tis true he was monarch, and wore a crown,

But his heart was beginning to sink.

🗣️ English Pronunciation in Odia Script:

କିଙ୍ଗ ବ୍ରୁସ୍ ଅଫ୍ ସ୍କଟଲ୍ୟାଣ୍ଡ ଫ୍ଲଙ୍ଗ୍ ହିମସେଲ୍ଫ୍ ଡାଉନ୍

ଇନ୍ ଏ ଲୋନଲି ମୁଡ୍ ଟୁ ଥିଙ୍କ୍;

ଟିସ୍ ଟ୍ରୁ ହି ୱାଜ୍ ମୋନାର୍କ, ଆଣ୍ଡ୍ ୱୋର୍ ଏ କ୍ରାଉନ୍,

ବଟ୍ ହିଜ୍ ହାର୍ଟ ୱାଜ୍ ବିଗିନିଂ ଟୁ ସିଙ୍କ୍.

🌟 Mood ➡️ ମନୋଭାବ

🌟 Crown ➡️ ମୁକୁଟ

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ (Odia Meaning):

💡 English Meaning:

King Bruce of Scotland sat down alone in a very sad mood to think. Even though he was a powerful king and wore a royal crown, he was losing hope and feeling deeply discouraged.

📖 Stanza 2 Explanation

📜 Original Text:

To make his people glad;

He had tried and tried, but couldn't succeed

And so he became quite sad.

ଆଣ୍ଡ୍ ସୋ ହି ବିକେମ୍ କ୍ୱାଇଟ୍ ସ୍ୟାଡ୍.

💡 English Meaning:

📖 Stanza 3 Explanation

😔 Despair ➡️ ନିରାଶା

🌟 Give up ➡️ ତ୍ୟାଗ କରିବା / ହାର ମାନିବା

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ (Odia Meaning):

ସେ ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ନିରାଶ ହୋଇ ଏକ ଗଭୀର ଦୁଃଖରେ ବସିପଡିଲେ। କିଛି ସମୟ ଗଭୀର ଚିନ୍ତା କରିବା ପରେ ସେ ନିଜକୁ କହିଲେ, "ମୁଁ ଏବେ ସବୁ ଚେଷ୍ଟା ଛାଡ଼ିଦେବି।"

💡 English Meaning:

Overwhelmed by extreme sadness and hopelessness, he sat down feeling completely heartbroken. After thinking deeply for a little while, he decided to quit and give up entirely.

📖 Stanza 4 Explanation

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ (Odia Meaning):

💡 English Meaning:

Right at that exact moment, a spider fell down from above, hanging by its thin, silk-like thread. The King paused his sad thoughts to watch what the spider was going to do next.

📝 All Summary (English Only)

King Bruce of Scotland felt completely defeated and heartbroken after failing multiple times to achieve something great for his people. Overwhelmed by deep despair, he sat alone and decided to completely abandon his goals and give up. Right at that exact moment of hopelessness, he noticed a tiny spider falling from its web on the high ceiling, hanging by a delicate, thin thread.

The spider began an incredibly difficult upward climb. It slipped, fell clumsily, and grew dizzy, but absolutely refused to surrender. Despite tumbling down nine separate times, the little creature courageously continued its upward struggle without a single complaint. Slowly and steadily, with one final bold push at the very end, the spider successfully secured itself in its native cobweb.

Deeply inspired by this fantastic display of perseverance, King Bruce cheered for the spider. He realized that if a tiny insect could conquer its despair, he could do the exact same thing. Bracing his mind and gathering his courage, the King made another attempt at his goal, and this time, he finally achieved the success he had been striving for. The poem beautifully highlights that failures are merely stepping stones to success.
