Let's Have Fun: Grandpa's Glasses

Let's Have Fun: Grandpa's Glasses – Study Material Class 5 English Pallavi

Pre-reading

Role-Play :

Amit: Sir, What're you looking for ?

Mili: Sir, are you looking for your glasses ?

Teacher: No, no, Mili.

Mili: Sir, are you looking for something else ?

Teacher: Yes, my pen. I can't find it now.

Safina: Is that in your pocket ?

David: It is there, Sir.

Teacher: Oh my God ! Thank you children !

(All laughed and laughed.........)

2. Line-by-Line Translation & Explanation

Here is the dialogue broken down sentence by sentence with the Odia pronunciation, Odia meaning, and a simple English explanation.

Amit : Sir, What're you looking for ?

  • English Pronunciation in Odia: ଅମିତ: ସାର୍, ହ୍ଵାଟ୍ ଆର୍ ୟୁ ଲୁକିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଫର୍?

  • Odia Meaning: ଅମିତ: ଆଜ୍ଞା / ସାର୍, ଆପଣ କଣ ଖୋଜୁଛନ୍ତି?

  • Simple English Meaning: Amit asks the teacher what he is trying to find.

Mili : Sir, are you looking for your glasses ?

  • English Pronunciation in Odia: ମିଲି: ସାର୍, ଆର୍ ୟୁ ଲୁକିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଫର୍ ୟୋର୍ ଗ୍ଲାସେସ୍?

  • Odia Meaning: ମିଲି: ସାର୍, ଆପଣ ନିଜ ଚଷମା ଖୋଜୁଛନ୍ତି କି?

  • Simple English Meaning: Mili asks if the teacher is looking for his spectacles (reading glasses).

Teacher : No, no, Mili.

  • English Pronunciation in Odia: ଟିଚର୍: ନୋ, ନୋ, ମିଲି.

  • Odia Meaning: ଶିକ୍ଷକ: ନା, ନା, ମିଲି।

  • Simple English Meaning: The teacher replies that he is not looking for his glasses.

Mili : Sir, are you looking for something else ?

  • English Pronunciation in Odia: ମିଲି: ସାର୍, ଆର୍ ୟୁ ଲୁକିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଫର୍ ସମ୍‌ଥିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଏଲ୍ସ?

  • Odia Meaning: ମିଲି: ସାର୍, ଆପଣ ଅନ୍ୟ କିଛି ଖୋଜୁଛନ୍ତି କି?

  • Simple English Meaning: Mili then asks if he is searching for another item instead.

Teacher : Yes, my pen. I can't find it now.

  • English Pronunciation in Odia: ଟିଚର୍: ୟେସ୍, ମାଇଁ ପେନ୍. ଆଇ କାଣ୍ଟ ଫାଇଣ୍ଡ୍ ଇଟ୍ ନାଓ.

  • Odia Meaning: ଶିକ୍ଷକ: ହଁ, ମୋ କଲମ। ମୁଁ ଏବେ ଏହାକୁ ପାଉନାହିଁ (ଖୋଜି ପାରୁନାହିଁ)।

  • Simple English Meaning: The teacher confirms he is looking for his pen because he has lost it.

Safina : Is that in your pocket ?

  • English Pronunciation in Odia: ସଫିନା: ଇଜ୍ ଦ୍ୟାଟ୍ ଇନ୍ ୟୋର୍ ପକେଟ୍?

  • Odia Meaning: ସଫିନା: ତାହା ଆପଣଙ୍କ ପକେଟରେ (ଜାମା ପକେଟରେ) ଅଛି କି?

  • Simple English Meaning: Safina suggests checking his shirt pocket to see if the pen is there.

David : It is there, Sir.

  • English Pronunciation in Odia: ଡେଭିଡ୍: ଇଟ୍ ଇଜ୍ ଦେୟାର୍, ସାର୍.

  • Odia Meaning: ଡେଭିଡ୍: ଏହା ସେଠାରେ ଅଛି, ସାର୍।

  • Simple English Meaning: David points out and confirms that the pen is indeed right there in the teacher's pocket.

Teacher : Oh my God ! Thank you children !

  • English Pronunciation in Odia: ଟିଚର୍: ଓଃ ମାଇଁ ଗଡ୍! ଥ୍ୟାଙ୍କ୍ ୟୁ ଚିଲଡ୍ରେନ୍!

  • Odia Meaning: ଶିକ୍ଷକ: ହେ ଭଗବାନ! ତୁମମାନଙ୍କୁ ଧନ୍ୟବାଦ ପିଲାମାନେ!

  • Simple English Meaning: The teacher is surprised at his own forgetfulness and happily thanks the students for helping him.

(All laughed and laughed.........)

  • English Pronunciation in Odia: (ଅଲ୍ ଲାଫଡ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ୍ ଲାଫଡ୍.........)

  • Odia Meaning: (ସମସ୍ତେ ବହୁତ ହସିଲେ.........)

  • Simple English Meaning: Everyone found the situation funny and started laughing together.

3. Vocabulary / Dictation Words (Easiest to Hardest)

Here are the important words from the text for dictation and practice, ordered from easy to hard, along with their Odia meanings:

  1. Sir (ସାର୍) - ଶିକ୍ଷକ / ମହାଶୟ (Teacher/Respected person)

  2. Pen (ପେନ୍) - କଲମ (Writing tool)

  3. Now (ନାଓ) - ଏବେ / ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ (At this moment)

  4. Find (ଫାଇଣ୍ଡ୍) - ଖୋଜିବା / ପାଇବା (To discover or locate something)

  5. Pocket (ପକେଟ୍) - ପକେଟ୍ (A small pouch in clothing)

  6. Children (ଚିଲଡ୍ରେନ୍) - ପିଲାମାନେ (Kids/Students)

  7. Looking for (ଲୁକିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଫର୍) - ଖୋଜୁଛନ୍ତି (Searching for something)

  8. Glasses (ଗ୍ଲାସେସ୍) - ଚଷମା (Spectacles worn on the eyes)

  9. Laughed (ଲାଫଡ୍) - ହସିଲେ (Past tense of laugh)

  10. Something else (ସମ୍‌ଥିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଏଲ୍ସ) - ଅନ୍ୟ କିଛି (Another different thing)

🌿 Each and Every Stanza Explanation

➡️ Stanza 1

📜 Original Stanza:

 Today our Grandpa

Is searching everywhere,

He has lost his glasses,

It was his only pair !

🗣️ English-Odia .:

 ଟୁଡେ ଆୱାର୍ ଗ୍ରାଣ୍ଡପା,

ଇଜ୍ ସର୍ଚ୍ଚିଂ ଏଭ୍ରିହ୍ୱେଆର୍,

ହି ହ୍ୟାଜ୍ ଲଷ୍ଟ୍ ହିଜ୍ ଗ୍ଲାସେସ୍,

ଇଟ୍ ୱାଜ୍ ହିଜ୍ ଓନଲି ପେୟାର୍ !

📝 Odia Meaning: ଆଜି ଆମ ଜେଜେବାପା ସବୁଆଡେ ଖୋଜୁଛନ୍ତି, ସେ ତାଙ୍କ ଚଷମା ହରାଇ ଦେଇଛନ୍ତି, ତାହା ତାଙ୍କର ଏକମାତ୍ର ଚଷମା ଥିଲା !

💡 English Meaning: Today, the poet's grandfather is looking all around the house for his spectacles. He is very worried because he has lost them, and he does not have another extra pair to wear.

🔤 Word Meanings :

Lost ➡️ ହରାଇଛନ୍ତି

Only ➡️ ଏକମାତ୍ର

Pair ➡️ ହଳ (ଯୋଡ଼ା)

Searching ➡️ ଖୋଜୁଛନ୍ତି

Everywhere ➡️ ସବୁଆଡେ

➡️ Stanza 2

📜 Original Stanza:

 He cannot read the newspaper,

He cannot watch TV,

He cannot cut the vegetables,

Oh! Where can it be?

🗣️ English-Odia .: ହି କ୍ୟାନଟ୍ ରିଡ୍ ଦ ନ୍ୟୁଜପେପର୍, ହି କ୍ୟାନଟ୍ ୱାଚ୍ ଟିଭି, ହି କ୍ୟାନଟ୍ କଟ୍ ଦ ଭେଜିଟେବୁଲ୍ସ, ଓଃ! ହ୍ୱେଆର୍ କ୍ୟାନ୍ ଇଟ୍ ବି?

📝 Odia Meaning: ସେ ଖବରକାଗଜ ପଢିପାରୁନାହାନ୍ତି, ସେ ଟିଭି ଦେଖିପାରୁନାହାନ୍ତି, ସେ ପନିପରିବା କାଟିପାରୁନାହାନ୍ତି, ଓଃ! ଏହା କେଉଁଠି ଥାଇପାରେ?

💡 English Meaning: Because Grandpa cannot see clearly without his glasses, he is unable to do his daily activities. He cannot read the morning news, enjoy television, or chop vegetables. He wonders where his glasses could possibly be hiding.

🔤 Word Meanings :

📜 Original Stanza:

He glanced under his chair,

🗣️ English-Odia .:

 ହି ଗ୍ଲାନ୍ସଡ୍ ଅଣ୍ଡର୍ ହିଜ୍ ଚେୟାର୍,

📝 Odia Meaning: ସେ ନିଜ ପକେଟ୍ ଭିତରେ ଯାଞ୍ଚ କଲେ, ସେ ତାଙ୍କ ଚୌକି ତଳେ ଆଖି ବୁଲାଇଲେ, ସେ ଝରକା ପାଖରେ ଖୋଜିଲେ, ସେ ସବୁଆଡେ ଦେଖିଲେ।

💡 English Meaning: Grandpa frantically looked for his spectacles in all possible places. He checked his clothes, took a quick look under his sitting chair, and searched near the window, but he still could not find them anywhere.

🔤 Word Meanings :

➡️ Stanza 4

📜 Original Stanza:

 I told him, "Stop Grandpa !"

I went up close and said !

"Grandpa, your glasses

Are right there on your head!"

🗣️ English-Odia .: ଆଇ ଟୋଲ୍ଡ ହିମ୍, "ଷ୍ଟପ୍ ଗ୍ରାଣ୍ଡପା !" ଆଇ ୱେଣ୍ଟ ଅପ୍ କ୍ଲୋଜ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ସେଡ୍ ! "ଗ୍ରାଣ୍ଡପା, ୟୋର୍ ଗ୍ଲାସେସ୍, ଆର୍ ରାଇଟ୍ ଦେଆର୍ ଅନ୍ ୟୋର୍ ହେଡ୍!"

📝 Odia Meaning: ମୁଁ ତାଙ୍କୁ କହିଲି, "ଅଟକି ଯାଅ ଜେଜେବାପା !" ମୁଁ ପାଖକୁ ଗଲି ଏବଂ କହିଲି ! "ଜେଜେବାପା, ତୁମ ଚଷମା ଠିକ୍ ତୁମ ମୁଣ୍ଡ ଉପରେ ଅଛି!"

💡 English Meaning: Finally, the grandchild stopped him from searching. The child went near Grandpa and laughingly told him that his missing glasses were actually pushed up and resting right on top of his own head the entire time!

🔤 Word Meanings :

Head ➡️ ମୁଣ୍ଡ

Stop ➡️ ଅଟକିବା

Told ➡️ କହିଲି

Right there ➡️ ଠିକ୍ ସେଠାରେ

Close ➡️ ପାଖକୁ

📝 Summary (English Only)

"Grandpa's Glasses" is a highly entertaining and funny poem about forgetfulness. The poem begins by describing a grandfather who is searching everywhere in the house because he has lost his spectacles. He is very worried because it is his only pair. Without his glasses, he is completely helpless. He is unable to read his daily newspaper, watch television, or even cut vegetables.

To find his missing glasses, Grandpa searches in every possible corner. He checks inside his pockets, takes a quick look under his chair, and even searches near the window. Just when he is looking everywhere in frustration, his grandchild comes to the rescue. The grandchild stops Grandpa, walks very close to him, and points out a hilarious mistake. The glasses were never lost; Grandpa had simply pushed them up, and they were resting right on top of his own head all along!

📝 Summary (Odia)

"ଗ୍ରାଣ୍ଡପାଜ୍ ଗ୍ଲାସେସ୍" (ଜେଜେବାପାଙ୍କ ଚଷମା) ହେଉଛି ଭୁଲିଯିବା ସ୍ୱଭାବ ଉପରେ ଆଧାରିତ ଏକ ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ମଜାଦାର କବିତା। କବିତାଟି ଜଣେ ଜେଜେବାପାଙ୍କୁ ନେଇ ଆରମ୍ଭ ହୁଏ ଯିଏ ତାଙ୍କର ଏକମାତ୍ର ଚଷମା ହରାଇ ଦେଇ ଘରର ସବୁଆଡେ ବ୍ୟସ୍ତ ହୋଇ ଖୋଜୁଛନ୍ତି। ଚଷମା ବିନା ସେ କୌଣସି କାମ କରିପାରୁନାହାନ୍ତି। ସେ ଖବରକାଗଜ ପଢିପାରୁନାହାନ୍ତି, ଟିଭି ଦେଖିପାରୁନାହାନ୍ତି କିମ୍ବା ପନିପରିବା ମଧ୍ୟ କାଟିପାରୁନାହାନ୍ତି।

ନିଜର ହଜିଯାଇଥିବା ଚଷମା ପାଇବା ପାଇଁ ସେ ସବୁ ଜାଗାରେ ଖୋଜନ୍ତି। ସେ ନିଜ ପକେଟ୍ ଭିତରେ ଯାଞ୍ଚ କରନ୍ତି, ଚୌକି ତଳେ ଖୋଜନ୍ତି ଏବଂ ଝରକା ପାଖରେ ମଧ୍ୟ ଦେଖନ୍ତି। ଯେତେବେଳେ ସେ ସବୁଆଡେ ଖୋଜି ନିରାଶ ହୋଇଯାଆନ୍ତି, ସେତେବେଳେ ତାଙ୍କ ନାତି/ନାତୁଣୀ ତାଙ୍କୁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରେ। ପିଲାଟି ତାଙ୍କୁ ଅଟକାଇ ପାଖକୁ ଯାଏ ଏବଂ ଏକ ମଜାଦାର କଥା କହେ। ଚଷମା ଆଦୌ ହଜି ନଥିଲା; ଜେଜେବାପା ସେଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ଟେକି ଦେଇଥିଲେ ଏବଂ ତାହା ଠିକ୍ ତାଙ୍କ ମୁଣ୍ଡ ଉପରେ ହିଁ ଥିଲା!

🎓 Suggestions for Students

🌟 Have fun reciting this poem! Try to act like a confused grandpa while reciting the first three stanzas, and act like a laughing child in the last stanza.

🌟 Notice the rhyming words at the end of the lines, like 'everywhere' and 'chair', or 'said' and 'head'. This makes the poem sound like a song.

🌟 Practice the dictation words in your notebook to improve your spelling skills.

👨‍🏫 Suggestions for Teachers: How to Teach

🌟 Total Periods Required: 2 Periods.

🌟 Period 1 (Recitation & Enjoyment): Start the class with a fun question: "Have you ever seen your grandparents looking for something that they are already holding?" Read the poem aloud with clear voice modulation and actions. Encourage students to sing the poem together as a chorus.

🌟 Period 2 (Vocabulary & Role-Play): Conduct a small classroom role-play. Let one student play 'Grandpa' with imaginary glasses on their head, pretending to look under chairs and tables, while another student plays the grandchild who finds it. Explain the new words like 'glanced' and 'pair' using simple examples. Conduct a quick spelling test at the end.
