III. Read the lines. 📖

Select the suitable meaning of ‘ragged’ as used in these lines. Discuss with your classmates and teacher. 💬

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଧାଡ଼ିଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ପଢ଼ନ୍ତୁ। ଏହି ଧାଡ଼ିଗୁଡ଼ିକରେ ବ୍ୟବହୃତ 'ragged' ର ଉପଯୁକ୍ତ ଅର୍ଥ ବାଛନ୍ତୁ।)

1.(of clothes) old and torn; synonym: shabby

(a ragged jacket)

2.Having an outline, an edge, or a surface that is not straight or even

(a ragged coastline)

3.Not smooth or regular; not showing control or careful preparation

(ragged breathing)

4.(Informal) very tired, especially after physical effort

(ragged after climbing.

Answer: 1. (of clothes) old and torn; synonym: shabby 🧥

  1. Explanation: In the poem, “ragged” refers to the old and worn-out appearance of the woman’s clothing.
