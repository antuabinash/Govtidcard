Instructions: Fill in the blanks with the correct form (tense) of the verb given in brackets.

2. Error Correction

Instructions: Find the error in each sentence and rewrite it correctly.

1.      I have seen him yesterday. Answer: I saw him yesterday.

2.      She is living here since 2010. Answer: She has been living here since 2010.

3.      When I arrived, he already left. Answer: When I arrived, he had already left.

4.      Look! It rains. Answer: Look! It is raining.

5.      He is usually getting up at 6 AM. Answer: He usually gets up at 6 AM.

6.      The train will leave at 5 PM tomorrow according to the schedule. Answer: The train leaves at 5 PM tomorrow according to the schedule.

7.      I am not knowing the answer. Answer: I do not know the answer.

8.      By next week, I will finish the project. Answer: By next week, I will have finished the project.

9.      He told me he is ill. Answer: He told me he was ill.

10.  We are visiting Puri last summer. Answer: We visited Puri last summer.

11.  He is having a large house. Answer: He has a large house. (State verb for possession)

12.  I am tired because I am working all day. Answer: I am tired because I have been working all day.

13.  Where you did go yesterday? Answer: Where did you go yesterday?

14.  As soon as he finished, he goes home. Answer: As soon as he finished, he went home.

15.  My brother is working in a bank for ten years. Answer: My brother has been working in a bank for ten years.

16.  Look at those clouds! It will rain. Answer: Look at those clouds! It is going to rain.

17.  I didn't see him since Monday. Answer: I haven't seen him since Monday.

18.  She was watching TV when the phone was ringing. Answer: She was watching TV when the phone rang.

19.  By the time the doctor arrived, the patient died. Answer: By the time the doctor arrived, the patient had died.

20.  I am learning English for five years. Answer: I have been learning English for five years.

21.  He said he has finished his work. Answer: He said he had finished his work.

22.  The postman didn't come yet. Answer: The postman hasn't come yet.

23.  If I will go to Delhi, I will see the Red Fort. Answer: If I go to Delhi, I will see the Red Fort.

24.  He acts as if he is the king. Answer: He acts as if he were the king.

25.  My father is preferring coffee to tea. Answer: My father prefers coffee to tea.

26.  He is tired. He works since morning. Answer: He is tired. He has been working since morning.

27.  Shakespeare has written many plays. Answer: Shakespeare wrote many plays.

28.  I am not understanding this poem. Answer: I do not understand this poem.

29.  He played chess during the vacation. Answer: He was playing chess during the vacation.

30.  I will see him yesterday. Answer: I saw him yesterday.

Column A

Column B

4. Contextual Dialogue Completion

Instructions: Complete the following dialogues with the correct form of the verbs.

Dialogue 1 Seema: Hello Lopa, what (1. you / do) ___ these days? Lopa: Nothing much. I (2. learn) ___ how to dance. I (3. join) ___ a dancing school last month. Seema: That's great! I (4. always / want) ___ to learn, but I (5. never / get) ___ the time. Answers:

1.      are you doing

2.      am learning

3.      joined

4.      have always wanted

5.      have never got/gotten


Dialogue 2 Ashok: (6. you / see) ___ Jagu last night? Bakul: No, but I (7. just / come) ___ from his house. He’s in bed. Ashok: Oh dear. What (8. happen) ___? Bakul: He (9. fall) ___ off his bike yesterday and (10. hurt) ___ his leg. Answers: 6. Did you see 7. have just come 8. happened 9. fell 10. hurt
