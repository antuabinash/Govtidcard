II & III. Modal Verbs Identification 🔍

Identify the modal verbs and their functions in these sentences:

  1. You can learn a lot from this experience.

    Function: Ability 💪

  2. They might arrive late due to traffic.

    Function: Possibility 🚦

  3. She must finish her homework before dinner.

    Function: Necessity 📚

  4. They ought to apologise for their mistake.

    Function: Moral obligation 🙏

  5. He would like to have some lassi, please.

    Function: Polite request 🥤

IV. Modal Verbs Explanation ✍️

(Based on the textbook exercise filling in appropriate modal verbs)

Answers & Explanations:

  1. May: Used to ask for or grant permission. ✅

  2. Must: Shows a strict rule or strong obligation. 🛑

  3. Should: Used to give a good recommendation or piece of advice. 💡

  4. May / Might: Indicates a degree of possibility of something happening. 🌧️

  5. Would: Used for making a polite and courteous request. ☕

  6. Ought to: Expresses a strong moral duty or advice. 🤝

  7. Can: Shows that someone has the skill or ability to do a task. 🏃‍♂️

II. International Day of Persons with Disabilities (IDPD): ♿

Find out when and how it is observed in India.

Answer: The IDPD is celebrated globally every year on December 3rd to raise awareness and support the rights of people with disabilities. In India, this day is highlighted by initiatives like the Accessible India Campaign, which works to make public areas and transport easier to navigate. There are also programs like the Deendayal Divyangjan Rehabilitation Scheme that focus on creating jobs and providing rehabilitation. The day emphasizes equality, inclusivity, and celebrating the amazing achievements of differently-abled individuals. 🎉
