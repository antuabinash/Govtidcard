Noun Clauses and Relative Clauses – Book Q A Class 10 English Grammar

🔗 Activity 1 (Relative Clauses)

Instructions (English): Combine the following pairs of sentences using one of them as a relative clause.

Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟ ଯୋଡିଗୁଡିକୁ ଗୋଟିଏ relative clause ଭାବେ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ମିଶ୍ରଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

1.      The birds are chirping. + The birds live in the neighbourhood.

Answer: The birds that (or which) live in the neighbourhood are chirping.

2.      I write with the pen. + My father bought the pen for me.

Answer: I write with the pen that (or which) my father bought for me.

3.      The sick were delighted. + Florence Nightingale nursed the sick.

Answer: The sick whom Florence Nightingale nursed were delighted.

4.      I like the man. + The man has a smiling face.

Answer: I like the man who has a smiling face.

5.      The thief escaped in the dark. + The thief had robbed me.

Answer: The thief who had robbed me escaped in the dark.

6.      The boy is our monitor. + I have borrowed his book.

Answer: The boy whose book I have borrowed is our monitor.

7.      The chair has a cracked leg. + He is sitting in the chair.

Answer: The chair in which he is sitting has a cracked leg. (or The chair (that) he is sitting in has a cracked leg.)


✂️ Activity 2 (Relative Clauses)

Instructions (English): In some of the following sentences the relative pronoun can be dropped. Rewrite those sentences without the relative pronouns.

Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ କେତେକ ବାକ୍ୟରେ relative pronoun କୁ ବାଦ୍ ଦିଆଯାଇପାରେ। ସେହି ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ relative pronoun ବିନା ପୁନର୍ଲିଖନ କରନ୍ତୁ।

1.      The children who are playing cricket live in this locality.

Answer: (Cannot be dropped - 'who' is the subject) No change.

2.      I have read the story book that my father bought yesterday.

Answer: I have read the story book my father bought yesterday.

3.      The shopkeeper who I was working for is a good person.

Answer: The shopkeeper I was working for is a good person.

4.      It is the best possible explanation that we can give about Socrates.

Answer: It is the best possible explanation we can give about Socrates.

5.      The only question that drew everybody's attention is worth discussing.

Answer: (Cannot be dropped - 'that' is the subject) No change.

6.      Our school, which works for the young people of our area, is highly popular.

Answer: (Cannot be dropped - Non-defining) No change.

7.      Pramod, who is my classmate, is very sincere.

Answer: (Cannot be dropped - Non-defining) No change.

8.      The travellers who knew about the floods took another road.

Answer: (Cannot be dropped - 'who' is the subject) No change.

9.      The travellers, who knew about the floods, took another road.

Answer: (Cannot be dropped - Non-defining) No change.

10.  The man that Mohan was talking to is his uncle.

Answer: The man Mohan was talking to is his uncle.

11.  The music which we were listening to was a 16th century devotional song.

Answer: The music we were listening to was a 16th century devotional song.

12.  I write with the pen which my father bought.

Answer: I write with the pen my father bought.


💬 Activity 3 (Relative Clauses)

Instructions (English): Define the terms as given in the example.

Instructions (Odia): ଉଦାହରଣରେ ଦିଆଯାଇଥିବା ପରି ସଂଜ୍ଞା ନିରୂପଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

1.      A driver is a person who...

Answer: ...drives a vehicle. (ଯିଏ ଏକ ଗାଡି ଚଲାଏ।)

2.      An actor is a person who...

Answer: ...acts in plays or films. (ଯିଏ ନାଟକ କିମ୍ବା ଚଳଚ୍ଚିତ୍ରରେ ଅଭିନୟ କରେ।)

3.      A journalist is a person who...

Answer: ...writes for newspapers or magazines. (ଯିଏ ଖବରକାଗଜ କିମ୍ବା ପତ୍ରିକା ପାଇଁ ଲେଖେ।)

4.      A farmer is a person who...

Answer: ...cultivates land. (ଯିଏ ଜମି ଚାଷ କରେ।)

5.      A patient is a person who...

Answer: ...is receiving medical treatment. (ଯିଏ ଡାକ୍ତରୀ ଚିକିତ୍ସା ଗ୍ରହଣ କରୁଛି।)

6.      A newspaper is a document in which...

Answer: ...news is printed. (ଯେଉଁଥିରେ ଖବର ଛପାଯାଏ।)

7.      A conductor is a person who...

Answer: ...collects fares on a bus. (ଯିଏ ବସ୍‌ରେ ଭଡା ସଂଗ୍ରହ କରେ।)

8.      A teacher is a person who...

Answer: ...teaches students. (ଯିଏ ଛାତ୍ରମାନଙ୍କୁ ପଢାଏ।)

9.      A plumber is a person who...

Answer: ...fits and repairs water pipes. (ଯିଏ ପାଣି ପାଇପ୍ ଲଗାଏ ଓ ମରାମତି କରେ।)

10.  A doctor is a person who...

Answer: ...treats sick people. (ଯିଏ ଅସୁସ୍ଥ ଲୋକଙ୍କର ଚିକିତ୍ସା କରେ।)

11.  A hospital is a place where...

Answer: ...sick people are treated. (ଯେଉଁଠାରେ ଅସୁସ୍ଥ ଲୋକଙ୍କର ଚିକିତ୍ସା କରାଯାଏ।)

12.  A transitive verb is a verb which...

Answer: ...requires an object. (ଯାହା ଏକ କର୍ମ (object) ଆବଶ୍ୟକ କରେ।)

🧐 Activity 5 (Relative Clauses)

Instructions (English): Underline the relative clauses in the following sentences. Write D for defining and N for non-defining relative clauses.

Instructions (Odia): ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକରେ relative clause ଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ରେଖାଙ୍କିତ କରନ୍ତୁ। Defining (ସୀମିତକାରୀ) ପାଇଁ D ଏବଂ Non-defining (ଅଣ-ସୀମିତକାରୀ) ପାଇଁ N ଲେଖନ୍ତୁ।

1.      A green grocer is a person who sells fruits and vegetables.

Answer: D

2.      Bhola, who is a green grocer, goes to the fields every morning.

Answer: N

3.      What is the name of your friend who often comes to our house?

Answer: D

4.      Where does Amit, who often comes to our house, live?

Answer: N

5.      My uncle who is a judge is coming here tomorrow.

Answer: D

6.      My uncle, who is a judge, is coming here tomorrow.

Answer: N

7.      My father, who is a teacher, loves to talk.

Answer: N

8.      Uneasy lies the head that wears a crown.

Answer: D

9.      We love the man who thinks of our welfare.

Answer: D

10.  We call Uncle Bhola, who thinks of our welfare, as the Gandhi of our village.

Answer: N


✏️ Activity 6 (Relative Clauses)

Instructions (English): Put commas in the following sentences, if they are necessary.

Instructions (Odia): ଯଦି ଆବଶ୍ୟକ ହୁଏ, ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକରେ କମା (,) ଚିହ୍ନ ଦିଅନ୍ତୁ।

1.      The man whom the police arrested is not known to us.

Answer: (No commas needed)

2.      The strike at the factory which lasted ten days is over now.

Answer: (No commas needed)

3.      My father who is working at Koraput is a doctor.

Answer: My father ,  who is working at Koraput ,  is a doctor.

4.      I have found the book which I was looking for.

Answer: (No commas needed)

5.      Mira's grandmother who is sick is in hospital now.

Answer: Mira's grandmother ,  who is sick ,  is in hospital now.

6.      Einstein who discovered the theory of relativity once failed in an examination.

Answer: Einstein ,  who discovered the theory of relativity ,  once failed in an examination.

7.      Engineer Choudhury who got this bridge built is on a visit to our village.

Answer: Engineer Choudhury ,  who got this bridge built ,  is on a visit to our village.

8.      Mother Teresa who faithfully served the poor is called a saint now.

Answer: Mother Teresa ,  who faithfully served the poor ,  is called a saint now.

9.      He has stopped attending classes which is unfortunate.

Answer: He has stopped attending classes ,  which is unfortunate.

10.  The chairman of the committee who is impartial solves all problems cleverly.

Answer: The chairman of the committee ,  who is impartial ,  solves all problems cleverly.


🖊️ Activity 7 (Relative Clauses)

Instructions (English): Fill in the blanks with relative pronouns such as who, which, where, whose, that, etc.

Instructions (Odia): who, which, where, whose, that ଇତ୍ୟାଦି relative pronoun ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ଶୂନ୍ୟସ୍ଥାନ ପୂରଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

1.      The house in _____ I lived as a child has been pulled down now.

Answer: which

2.      My grandmother, _____ was an extraordinary woman, lived to the age of one hundred.

Answer: who

3.      Stop him! He's the man _____ stole my purse.

Answer: who / that

4.      They have invented a television set _____ is as small as a watch.

Answer: which / that

5.      There are many people _____ lives have been spoilt by this factory.

Answer: whose

6.      Is that the button _____ you pressed?

Answer: that / which

7.      Could everybody _____ answer sheets are with me raise their hands?

Answer: whose

8.      That scientist has invented a kind of ink _____ is visible in darkness.

Answer: which / that


🤝 Activity 8 (Relative Clauses)

Instructions (English): Work in pairs. Think of a puzzle whose answer you know. The clue you give must have a relative clause.

Instructions (Odia): ଯୋଡିରେ କାମ କରନ୍ତୁ। ଏକ ପହେଳି ଭାବନ୍ତୁ ଯାହାର ଉତ୍ତର ଆପଣ ଜାଣିଛନ୍ତି। ଆପଣ ଦେଉଥିବା ସୂତ୍ରରେ ଏକ relative clause ଥିବା ଆବଶ୍ୟକ।

Note: This is a classroom activity and does not have a single "correct" answer. The goal is to practice creating definitions using relative clauses.

ସୂଚନା: ଏହା ଏକ ଶ୍ରେଣୀଗୃହ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକଳାପ ଏବଂ ଏହାର କୌଣସି ଗୋଟିଏ "ସଠିକ୍" ଉତ୍ତର ନାହିଁ। Relative clause ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ସଂଜ୍ଞା ତିଆରି କରିବା ହେଉଛି ଏହାର ଉଦ୍ଦେଶ୍ୟ।

Example 1:

Student A: "It is an animal which lives in the jungle and is called the king." (ଏହା ଏକ ପଶୁ ଯିଏ ଜଙ୍ଗଲରେ ରହେ ଏବଂ ରାଜା କୁହାଯାଏ।)

Student B: "Lion!"

Example 2:

Student A: "It is a thing that we use to write on a blackboard." (ଏହା ଏକ ଜିନିଷ ଯାହାକୁ ଆମେ କଳାପଟାରେ ଲେଖିବା ପାଇଁ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରୁ।)

Student B: "Chalk!"
