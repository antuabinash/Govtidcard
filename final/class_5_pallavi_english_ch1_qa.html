Let's Have Fun: Grandpa's Glasses – Book Q A Class 5 English Pallavi

🗣️ Let us Discuss (Page 3)
🌟 1. Question: What is Grandpa searching for?
(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଜେଜେବାପା କ'ଣ ଖୋଜୁଛନ୍ତି?)
✅ Answer: Grandpa is searching for his glasses.
🌟 2. Question: What are the possible places where he can find them?
(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ସେଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ସେ କେଉଁ ସମ୍ଭାବ୍ୟ ସ୍ଥାନରେ ପାଇପାରିବେ?)
✅ Answer: He can find them inside his pockets, under his chair, near the window, or on his head.
🌟 3. Question: What can't he do without glasses?
(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଚଷମା ବିନା ସେ କ'ଣ କରିପାରିବେ ନାହିଁ?)
✅ Answer: He cannot read the newspaper, he cannot watch TV, and he cannot cut the vegetables.
🌟 4. Question: Where does Grandpa find his glasses at last?
(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଜେଜେବାପା ଶେଷରେ ତାଙ୍କ ଚଷମା କେଉଁଠାରେ ପାଇଲେ?)
✅ Answer: Grandpa finds his glasses right there on his head.
🌟 5. Question: What makes Grandpa happy?
(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: କେଉଁ ଜିନିଷ ଜେଜେବାପାଙ୍କୁ ଖୁସି କରେ?)
✅ Answer: Finding his lost glasses makes him happy.

 (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ତୁମ ଘରେ ସାଧାରଣତଃ ହଜିଯାଉଥିବା ଜିନିଷଗୁଡ଼ିକର ଏକ ତାଲିକା ପ୍ରସ୍ତୁତ କର ଏବଂ ସେଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ଖୋଜିବାରେ କିଏ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରେ ତାହା ଲେଖ।)
✅ Answer: 
Things generally misplaced at my home Who helps in finding these misplaced things?
Keys Mother
Remote control Siblings
Mobile phone Other family members
Wallet Siblings
Pen Siblings

🌟 2. Question: Complete the story using the correct prepositions. (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ସଠିକ୍ ପ୍ରିପୋଜିସନ୍ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ଗଳ୍ପଟିକୁ ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।)

(around, after, for, in, above, on, among, at)

My grandfather has a habit of keeping his glasses __________ different places. Last week, he could not find his glasses and looked __________ the house. We checked the big box that reached __________ our waists. My grand-father looked for the glasses __________ the newspapers kept __________ the table. He continued searching __________ two hours. He sat down __________ the bed __________ giving one final look to the room.

✅ Answer: My grandfather has a habit of keeping his glasses at different places. Last week, he could not find his glasses and looked around the house. We checked the big box that reached above our waists. My grand-father looked for the glasses among the newspapers kept on the table. He continued searching for two hours. He sat down in the bed after giving one final look to the room.
