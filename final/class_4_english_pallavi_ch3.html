Going Places: Between Home And School

Going Places: Between Home And School – Study Material Class 4 English Pallavi

✨ Starting Pre-Reading Step-Wise ✨

Pre-Reading Explanation:

Before starting the lesson, the teacher should ask the students about the things they do when they are near a pool or a pond. Children naturally love playing near water, like touching the cool water, paddling with their feet, splashing hands, or making paper boats. This pre-reading activity connects the students' own playful habits with the story. The lesson "Between Home and School" is about two friends, Ravi and his friend, who walk to school and have a lot of similar fun on their way.

📖 Paragraph-Wise Explanation & Translation 📖

📝 Paragraph 1

Original Paragraph: Hello, I am Ravi. I study in Class 4. My friend and I go to school on foot. We pass through the paddy fields, the mango grove and then reach the main road. That is the shortest route we take to school. What fun we have every day!

🗣️ English-Odia (Pronunciation):

ହ୍ୟାଲୋ, ଆଇ ଆମ୍ ରବି। ଆଇ ଷ୍ଟଡି ଇନ୍ କ୍ଲାସ୍ ଫୋର୍। ମାଇ ଫ୍ରେଣ୍ଡ ଆଣ୍ଡ ଆଇ ଗୋ ଟୁ ସ୍କୁଲ୍ ଅନ୍ ଫୁଟ୍। ୱି ପାସ୍ ଥ୍ରୁ ଦ ପ୍ୟାଡି ଫିଲ୍ଡସ୍, ଦ ମ୍ୟାଙ୍ଗୋ ଗ୍ରୋଭ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ଦେନ୍ ରିଚ୍ ଦ ମେନ୍ ରୋଡ୍। ଦ୍ୟାଟ୍ ଇଜ୍ ଦ ସର୍ଟେଷ୍ଟ ରୁଟ୍ ୱି ଟେକ୍ ଟୁ ସ୍କୁଲ୍। ହ୍ୱାଟ୍ ଫନ୍ ୱି ହ୍ୟାଭ୍ ଏଭ୍ରି ଡେ!

📝 Odia Meaning: ହ୍ୟାଲୋ, ମୁଁ ରବି। ମୁଁ ଚତୁର୍ଥ ଶ୍ରେଣୀରେ ପଢେ। ମୋ ସାଙ୍ଗ ଏବଂ ମୁଁ ଚାଲି ଚାଲି ସ୍କୁଲକୁ ଯାଉ। ଆମେ ଧାନ ବିଲ, ଆମ୍ବ ତୋଟା ଦେଇ ଯାଉ ଏବଂ ତା'ପରେ ମୁଖ୍ୟ ରାସ୍ତାରେ ପହଞ୍ଚୁ। ତାହା ଆମର ସ୍କୁଲ ଯିବା ପାଇଁ ସବୁଠାରୁ ଛୋଟ ରାସ୍ତା। ଆମେ ପ୍ରତିଦିନ କେତେ ମଜା କରୁ!

💡 English Meaning (Simple Explanation): The boy introduces himself as Ravi, a class 4 student. He and his friend walk to school. They walk through rice fields and a mango orchard to reach the main road. It is their shortest path to school, and they have a lot of fun every day.

✍️ Dictation Words (Easy to Hard):

Route ➡️ ରାସ୍ତା (Rasta)

Shortest ➡️ ସବୁଠାରୁ ଛୋଟ (Sabutharu chhota)

Paddy fields ➡️ ଧାନ ବିଲ (Dhana bila)

Mango grove ➡️ ଆମ୍ବ ତୋଟା (Amba tota)

📝 Paragraph 2

Original Paragraph: -jumping over the canals! climbing the trees! swinging from the branches! watching garden lizards! Yet we reach school on time.

🗣️ English-Odia (Pronunciation):

-ଜମ୍ପିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଓଭର୍ ଦ କେନାଲ୍ସ! କ୍ଲାଇମ୍ବିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଦ ଟ୍ରିଜ୍! ସ୍ୱିଙ୍ଗିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଫ୍ରମ୍ ଦ ବ୍ରାଞ୍ଚେସ୍! ୱାଚିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଗାର୍ଡେନ୍ ଲିଜାର୍ଡ୍ସ! ୟେଟ୍ ୱି ରିଚ୍ ସ୍କୁଲ୍ ଅନ୍ ଟାଇମ୍।

📝 Odia Meaning: - କେନାଲ ଉପରେ ଡେଇଁବା! ଗଛ ଚଢ଼ିବା! ଡାଳରେ ଦୋଳି ଖେଳିବା! ବଗିଚାର ଏଣ୍ଡୁଅମାନଙ୍କୁ ଦେଖିବା! ତଥାପି ଆମେ ଠିକ୍ ସମୟରେ ସ୍କୁଲରେ ପହଞ୍ଚୁ।

💡 English Meaning (Simple Explanation): On their way, they do fun things like jumping across small water channels, climbing trees, swinging on tree branches, and looking at lizards in the garden. Even after playing so much, they never get late and always reach school on time.

✍️ Dictation Words (Easy to Hard):

Canals ➡️ କେନାଲ (Canal)

Lizards ➡️ ଏଣ୍ଡୁଅ (Endua)

Climbing ➡️ ଚଢ଼ିବା (Chadhiba)

Swinging ➡️ ଦୋଳି ଖେଳିବା (Doli kheliba)

📝 Summary (English Only)

Ravi is a fourth-grade student who walks to school every day with his friend. To reach their school quickly, they take the shortest route which goes directly through the green paddy fields and a mango grove before connecting to the main road.

During their daily walk, the two friends have a lot of fun. They deeply enjoy jumping over the water canals, climbing trees, swinging playfully from the branches, and observing garden lizards. Despite spending time playing on the way, they always manage to reach their school exactly on time.

〰️〰️〰️〰️〰️〰️〰️〰️〰️〰️

📝 Summary (Odia)

ରବି ଚତୁର୍ଥ ଶ୍ରେଣୀର ଜଣେ ଛାତ୍ର, ଯିଏ ନିଜ ସାଙ୍ଗ ସହିତ ପ୍ରତିଦିନ ଚାଲି ଚାଲି ସ୍କୁଲକୁ ଯାଏ। ସ୍କୁଲରେ ଶୀଘ୍ର ପହଞ୍ଚିବା ପାଇଁ, ସେମାନେ ସବୁଠାରୁ ଛୋଟ ରାସ୍ତାରେ ଯାଆନ୍ତି ଯାହା ଧାନ ବିଲ ଏବଂ ଆମ୍ବ ତୋଟା ଦେଇ ମୁଖ୍ୟ ରାସ୍ତା ସହିତ ଯୋଡ଼ି ହୋଇଥାଏ।

ସ୍କୁଲ ଯିବା ବାଟରେ ଦୁଇ ସାଙ୍ଗ ବହୁତ ମଜା କରନ୍ତି। ସେମାନେ ପାଣି କେନାଲ ଉପରେ ଡେଇଁବା, ଗଛ ଚଢ଼ିବା, ଡାଳରେ ଝୁଲିବା ଏବଂ ବଗିଚାରେ ଏଣ୍ଡୁଅମାନଙ୍କୁ ଦେଖିବା ଉପଭୋଗ କରନ୍ତି। ବାଟରେ ଏତେ ସବୁ ଖେଳିବା ସତ୍ତ୍ୱେ ମଧ୍ୟ, ସେମାନେ ସବୁବେଳେ ଠିକ୍ ସମୟରେ ସ୍କୁଲରେ ପହଞ୍ଚିଯାଆନ୍ତି।

💡 Suggestion for Students (How to Study)

🌟 Try reading the Odia pronunciation (ହ୍ୟାଲୋ, ଆଇ ଆମ୍ ରବି...) smoothly, and then immediately read the English spelling to connect the sounds with the alphabet.

🌟 Read the English paragraphs aloud to improve your reading speed.

🌟 Practice the English-to-Odia dictation words daily by writing them in your rough notebook.

🌟 Try telling the summary of the story to your parents in Odia, and then try a few lines in English.

👨‍🏫 Suggestion for Teachers (How to Teach & Periods)

⏳ Total Periods Required: 3 Periods

🏫 Period 1: Pre-Reading and Introduction

Begin by asking students to describe their own journey to school and what they observe on the road. Do the pre-reading activity about playing near ponds. Introduce Ravi and read the first paragraph. Use the Odia pronunciation script on the blackboard for weak readers so they gain confidence in reading English aloud.

🏫 Period 2: Reading, Pronunciation, and Comprehension

Read the second paragraph focusing on action words (jumping, climbing, swinging, watching). Ask students to enact these words. Ask simple comprehension questions like "What fields do they pass through?" or "Do they reach school late?" to keep them engaged.

🏫 Period 3: Vocabulary, Dictation, and Summary

Focus on the difficult words from the chapter (Grove, Route, Canals, Swinging). Use the Dictation Words list provided above to conduct a short spelling test. Finally, discuss the full summary of the chapter and ask the students to write down 3 interesting things they see between their home and school.
