The Missile Man of India – Additional Questions Class 9 ENGLISH

Section A: Short Answer Type Questions (2.5 Marks Each) - 30 Nos

1. Who is widely referred to as the "People's President" of India? (ଭାରତର "ଜନତାଙ୍କ ରାଷ୍ଟ୍ରପତି" ଭାବରେ କାହାକୁ ବ୍ୟାପକ ଭାବରେ ସମ୍ବୋଧିତ କରାଯାଏ?)

Answer: Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam is widely referred to as the "People's President" of India due to his immense popularity among the citizens.

2. Why was Dr. Kalam known as "The Missile Man of India"? (ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମଙ୍କୁ "ଭାରତର କ୍ଷେପଣାସ୍ତ୍ର ମାନବ" ବୋଲି କାହିଁକି କୁହାଯାଉଥିଲା?)

Answer: He was known as "The Missile Man of India" because he was intimately involved in the development of India's military missiles and civilian space programs.

3. When and where was Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam born? (ଡକ୍ଟର ଏ.ପି.ଜେ. ଅବଦୁଲ କଲାମ କେବେ ଏବଂ କେଉଁଠାରେ ଜନ୍ମଗ୍ରହଣ କରିଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He was born on 15th October 1931 in a Tamil Muslim family at Rameswaram in the state of Tamil Nadu.

4. What were the professions of Kalam's parents? (କଲାମଙ୍କ ପିତାମାତାଙ୍କ ପେଶା କ'ଣ ଥିଲା?)

Answer: His father, Jainulabdeen, was a boat owner and the Imam of a local mosque, while his mother, Ashiamma, was a housewife.

5. How did the young Kalam supplement his family's income? (ଯୁବକ କଲାମ ତାଙ୍କ ପରିବାରର ଆୟରେ କିପରି ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରୁଥିଲେ?)

Answer: To help his poverty-stricken family, young Kalam used to sell newspapers to supplement their income.

6. Who was Sivasubramania Iyer and what was his role in Kalam's life? (ଶିବସୁବ୍ରମଣ୍ୟ ଆୟର କିଏ ଥିଲେ ଏବଂ କଲାମଙ୍କ ଜୀବନରେ ତାଙ୍କର ଭୂମିକା କ'ଣ ଥିଲା?)

Answer: Sivasubramania Iyer was Kalam's affectionate science teacher at his elementary school who inspired him to develop his skills and compete with highly educated people.

7. Why did the science teacher's wife initially refuse to serve food to Kalam? (ବିଜ୍ଞାନ ଶିକ୍ଷକଙ୍କ ପତ୍ନୀ ପ୍ରଥମେ କଲାମଙ୍କୁ ଖାଦ୍ୟ ପରଷିବାକୁ କାହିଁକି ମନା କରିଦେଇଥିଲେ?)

Answer: She was an orthodox woman and disliked the idea of serving a Muslim boy in her kitchen, hence she refused.

8. What changed the attitude of the science teacher's wife towards Kalam? (କଲାମଙ୍କ ପ୍ରତି ବିଜ୍ଞାନ ଶିକ୍ଷକଙ୍କ ପତ୍ନୀଙ୍କ ମନୋଭାବ କେଉଁ କଥା ବଦଳାଇଲା?)

Answer: Kalam's polite behavior, the neat way he took his meal, and how he cleaned the floor changed her conservative attitude.

9. Where did Kalam complete his secondary education? (କଲାମ ତାଙ୍କର ମାଧ୍ୟମିକ ଶିକ୍ଷା କେଉଁଠାରେ ଶେଷ କରିଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He completed his secondary education at the Schwartz Higher Secondary School.

10. What was Kalam's academic performance during his school years? (ବିଦ୍ୟାଳୟ ସମୟରେ କଲାମଙ୍କର ଶୈକ୍ଷିକ ପ୍ରଦର୍ଶନ କିପରି ଥିଲା?)

Answer: He had average grades but was considered a bright and hardworking student who possessed a strong desire to learn, especially mathematics.

11. From which institution did Kalam graduate in Physics, and in which year? (କଲାମ କେଉଁ ଅନୁଷ୍ଠାନରୁ ପଦାର୍ଥ ବିଜ୍ଞାନରେ ସ୍ନାତକ ହାସଲ କରିଥିଲେ ଏବଂ କେଉଁ ମସିହାରେ?)

Answer: He graduated in Physics from Saint Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli, in the year 1954.

12. What was Kalam's ultimate dream in life during his youth? (ଯୁବକ ଅବସ୍ଥାରେ କଲାମଙ୍କ ଜୀବନର ଚରମ ସ୍ୱପ୍ନ କ'ଣ ଥିଲା?)

Answer: His ultimate dream in life was to become a pilot.

13. Why was getting admission into the Madras Institute of Technology a challenge for Kalam? (ମାଡ୍ରାସ ଇନଷ୍ଟିଚ୍ୟୁଟ୍ ଅଫ୍ ଟେକ୍ନୋଲୋଜିରେ ନାମ ଲେଖାଇବା କଲାମଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ କାହିଁକି ଏକ ଆହ୍ୱାନ ଥିଲା?)

Answer: It was a highly prestigious institution and an expensive affair requiring around a thousand rupees, which his poor family could not easily afford.

14. How did Asim Zohra help Kalam with his higher education? (ଆସିମ୍ ଜୋହରା କଲାମଙ୍କୁ ତାଙ୍କ ଉଚ୍ଚଶିକ୍ଷାରେ କିପରି ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କଲେ?)

Answer: His compassionate elder sister, Asim Zohra, mortgaged her own gold ornaments to arrange the admission fee for his engineering course.

15. Where did Kalam begin his professional career? (କଲାମ ତାଙ୍କର ବୃତ୍ତିଗତ କ୍ୟାରିୟର କେଉଁଠାରେ ଆରମ୍ଭ କରିଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He began his career as a trainee at the Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) in Bangalore.

16. What crucial role did Kalam play in ISRO in 1969? (୧୯୬୯ ମସିହାରେ ଇସ୍ରୋରେ କଲାମ କେଉଁ ଗୁରୁତ୍ୱପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ଭୂମିକା ଗ୍ରହଣ କରିଥିଲେ?)

Answer: In 1969, he was transferred to ISRO where he worked as the Project Director of India's first Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III).

17. What major achievement occurred under Kalam's direct guidance in 1980? (୧୯୮୦ ମସିହାରେ କଲାମଙ୍କ ପ୍ରତ୍ୟକ୍ଷ ମାର୍ଗଦର୍ଶନରେ କେଉଁ ପ୍ରମୁଖ ସଫଳତା ମିଳିଥିଲା?)

Answer: Under his direct supervision, India successfully launched its first satellite, Rohini, into orbit in 1980.

18. What does IGMDP stand for, and who headed it? (IGMDP ର ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ରୂପ କ'ଣ, ଏବଂ ଏହାର ନେତୃତ୍ୱ କିଏ ନେଇଥିଲେ?)

Answer: IGMDP stands for Integrated Missile Development Programme, and it was headed by Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam.

19. Name the four major missiles developed under the IGMDP. (IGMDP ଅଧୀନରେ ବିକଶିତ ହୋଇଥିବା ଚାରୋଟି ପ୍ରମୁଖ କ୍ଷେପଣାସ୍ତ୍ରର ନାମ ଲେଖ |)

Answer: The four major missiles developed under this programme were Prithvi, Trishul, Akash, and Nag.

20. What event did Dr. Kalam supervise in the Thar Desert in 1998? (୧୯୯୮ ମସିହାରେ ଥର ମରୁଭୂମିରେ ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମ କେଉଁ ଘଟଣାର ତଦାରଖ କରିଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He supervised the highly successful Pokhran-II nuclear tests in the Thar Desert in 1998.

21. How did Dr. Kalam describe the success of the Pokhran-II tests? (ପୋଖରାନ-୨ ପରୀକ୍ଷଣର ସଫଳତାକୁ ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମ କିପରି ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କରିଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He described the successful nuclear tests as a "defining moment" in the history of the country that marked the emergence of India's nuclear era.

22. What important political advisory role did Kalam hold from 1992 to 1999? (୧୯୯୨ ରୁ ୧୯୯୯ ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ କଲାମ କେଉଁ ଗୁରୁତ୍ୱପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ରାଜନୈତିକ ଉପଦେଷ୍ଟା ପଦବୀରେ ଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He served as the Chief Scientific Adviser to the Prime Minister of India and the Secretary of DRDO.

23. Name the three highest civilian honors awarded to Dr. Kalam by the Government of India. (ଭାରତ ସରକାରଙ୍କ ଦ୍ୱାରା ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମଙ୍କୁ ପ୍ରଦାନ କରାଯାଇଥିବା ତିନୋଟି ସର୍ବୋଚ୍ଚ ବେସାମରିକ ସମ୍ମାନର ନାମ ଲେଖ |)

Answer: He was honored with the Padma Bhushan (1981), the Padma Vibhushan (1990), and the Bharat Ratna (1997).

24. When did Dr. Kalam pass away and what was he doing at that time? (ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମଙ୍କର କେବେ ଦେହାନ୍ତ ହେଲା ଏବଂ ସେହି ସମୟରେ ସେ କ'ଣ କରୁଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He passed away on 27th July 2015 due to a massive cardiac arrest while delivering a lecture to students in Shillong.

25. How can we apply Kalam's childhood struggles to inspire modern students? (ଆମେ ଆଧୁନିକ ଛାତ୍ରଛାତ୍ରୀମାନଙ୍କୁ ପ୍ରେରଣା ଦେବା ପାଇଁ କଲାମଙ୍କ ପିଲାଦିନର ସଂଘର୍ଷକୁ କିପରି ପ୍ରୟୋଗ କରିପାରିବା?)

Answer: His struggles teach us that poverty and hardships are not barriers to success; hard work and an intense desire to learn can help anyone achieve greatness.

26. What does the incident with the science teacher's wife teach us about society? (ବିଜ୍ଞାନ ଶିକ୍ଷକଙ୍କ ପତ୍ନୀଙ୍କ ସହ ଘଟିଥିବା ଘଟଣା ଆମକୁ ସମାଜ ବିଷୟରେ କ'ଣ ଶିକ୍ଷା ଦିଏ?)

Answer: It teaches us that deeply rooted social and religious prejudices can be overcome through patience, good behavior, and equal treatment.

27. Why did Dr. Kalam's family lose their ancestral properties? (ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମଙ୍କ ପରିବାର କାହିଁକି ସେମାନଙ୍କର ପୈତୃକ ସମ୍ପତ୍ତି ହରାଇଲେ?)

Answer: Though his ancestors were wealthy traders, over the course of time, the family fell on hard times and gradually lost their wealth and properties.

28. How does Dr. Kalam's work at ISRO demonstrate his leadership skills? (ଇସ୍ରୋରେ ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମଙ୍କ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ ତାଙ୍କର ନେତୃତ୍ୱ କୌଶଳକୁ କିପରି ପ୍ରଦର୍ଶନ କରେ?)

Answer: As Project Director of SLV-III, he successfully guided and supervised a complex team to launch India's first satellite, proving his exceptional technical and managerial leadership.

29. What was the significance of Dr. Kalam camping in the extreme heat of the Thar Desert? (ଥର ମରୁଭୂମିର ଅତ୍ୟଧିକ ଗରମରେ ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମ ଶିବିର ସ୍ଥାପନ କରିବାର ମହତ୍ତ୍ୱ କ'ଣ ଥିଲା?)

Answer: It showed his extreme physical endurance and total devotion to the nation's security by personally overseeing the nuclear tests in 53-degree Celsius heat.

30. How will the nation remember Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam? (ଆମ ଦେଶ ଡକ୍ଟର ଏ.ପି.ଜେ. ଅବଦୁଲ କଲାମଙ୍କୁ କିପରି ମନେ ରଖିବ?)

Answer: The nation will forever remember him for his selfless service, immense dedication to science, and his legacy as "The Missile Man of India."

Section B: Long Answer Type Questions (5 Marks Each) - 7 Nos

31. Describe the early life, family background, and the struggles young Kalam faced during his childhood. (ଯୁବକ କଲାମଙ୍କ ପ୍ରାରମ୍ଭିକ ଜୀବନ, ପାରିବାରିକ ପୃଷ୍ଠଭୂମି ଏବଂ ତାଙ୍କ ପିଲାଦିନର ସଂଘର୍ଷ ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କର |)

Answer: Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam was born on October 15, 1931, in a Tamil Muslim family in Rameswaram. His father, Jainulabdeen, was a modest boat owner who ferried Hindu pilgrims, and his mother, Ashiamma, was a housewife. He was the youngest of five siblings. Although his ancestors had once been wealthy traders and landowners, the family fell on hard times and lost everything by the time Kalam was born. Experiencing severe poverty during his early childhood, Kalam had to work hard. He took up the job of selling newspapers early in the morning just to supplement his family's meager income, displaying a strong sense of responsibility from a very young age.

32. Narrate the incident involving Kalam, his science teacher Sivasubramania Iyer, and the teacher's wife. What is the moral of this event? (କଲାମ, ତାଙ୍କ ବିଜ୍ଞାନ ଶିକ୍ଷକ ଶିବସୁବ୍ରମଣ୍ୟ ଆୟର ଏବଂ ଶିକ୍ଷକଙ୍କ ପତ୍ନୀଙ୍କ ସହ ଜଡିତ ଘଟଣା ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କର | ଏହି ଘଟଣାର ନୈତିକ ଶିକ୍ଷା କ'ଣ?)

Answer: While at his elementary school, Kalam had an affectionate science teacher named Sivasubramania Iyer who treated him like his own. Once, the teacher invited Kalam to his home for a meal. However, the teacher's orthodox wife refused to serve food to a Muslim child in her kitchen. Unbothered, the teacher served Kalam himself. During the meal, the wife observed Kalam's polite behavior and cleanliness. This observation entirely changed her conservative mindset. When Kalam was invited a second time, she warmly welcomed him and served him with her own hands. The moral of the story is that human goodness, good manners, and patience can slowly eradicate the deepest walls of religious prejudice and social discrimination.

33. Explain the difficulties Kalam faced in pursuing his higher education and how his sister proved to be a savior. (ଉଚ୍ଚଶିକ୍ଷା ହାସଲ କରିବାରେ କଲାମ ସମ୍ମୁଖୀନ ହୋଇଥିବା ଅସୁବିଧା ଏବଂ ତାଙ୍କ ଭଉଣୀ କିପରି ତ୍ରାଣକର୍ତ୍ତା ପ୍ରମାଣିତ ହେଲେ ତାହା ବୁଝାଅ |)

Answer: After graduating in Physics from Saint Joseph's College in 1954, Kalam wanted to fulfill his lifelong dream of becoming a pilot. He applied for aerospace engineering at the prestigious Madras Institute of Technology. Although he was selected based on merit, the admission required a fee of about a thousand rupees. For his poverty-stricken family, this was an enormous amount and an incredibly expensive affair. Seeing his desperate desire to learn, his compassionate elder sister, Asim Zohra, stepped in as a savior. She sacrificed and mortgaged her own gold ornaments to arrange the required money. Thanks to her profound love and financial support, Kalam successfully completed his engineering in 1960.

34. Trace Dr. Kalam's scientific career progression from HAL to his achievements at ISRO. (HAL ରୁ ଇସ୍ରୋରେ ତାଙ୍କର ସଫଳତା ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମଙ୍କ ବୈଜ୍ଞାନିକ ବୃତ୍ତିଗତ ପ୍ରଗତିର ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କର |)

Answer: Dr. Kalam began his illustrious career humbly as a trainee at the Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) in Bangalore. Eager to contribute to national defense, he soon joined the Defence Research Development Organisation (DRDO) as a scientist. His true genius found a broader platform when he was transferred to the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) in 1969. At ISRO, he was appointed as the Project Director for India's first Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III). This was a highly complex technological challenge. Under his direct, meticulous guidance and supervision, the team achieved a historic milestone when the first Rohini satellite was successfully launched into Earth's orbit in 1980.

35. Discuss Dr. Kalam's role in the Integrated Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) and the Pokhran-II tests. Why is he called "The Missile Man"? (ସମନ୍ୱିତ କ୍ଷେପଣାସ୍ତ୍ର ବିକାଶ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକ୍ରମ (IGMDP) ଏବଂ ପୋଖରାନ-୨ ପରୀକ୍ଷଣରେ ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମଙ୍କ ଭୂମିକା ଆଲୋଚନା କର | ତାଙ୍କୁ କାହିଁକି "କ୍ଷେପଣାସ୍ତ୍ର ମାନବ" କୁହାଯାଏ?)

Answer: After 19 years at ISRO, Dr. Kalam returned to DRDO to head the Integrated Missile Development Programme (IGMDP). Under his visionary leadership, India successfully developed four strategic missiles: Prithvi, Trishul, Akash, and Nag. Because of this massive contribution to India's military missile technology, he earned the title "The Missile Man of India." Furthermore, in 1998, as the chief of DRDO, he supervised the highly secretive and successful Pokhran-II nuclear tests. He camped in the scorching 53-degree heat of the Thar Desert for a fortnight to ensure success, an event he called a "defining moment" that marked India's emergence as a global nuclear power.

36. Summarize the honors, awards, and the ultimate political position achieved by Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam during his lifetime. (ଡକ୍ଟର ଏ.ପି.ଜେ. ଅବଦୁଲ କଲାମଙ୍କ ଜୀବନକାଳ ମଧ୍ୟରେ ହାସଲ କରିଥିବା ସମ୍ମାନ, ପୁରସ୍କାର ଏବଂ ଚରମ ରାଜନୈତିକ ପଦବୀର ସାରାଂଶ ଲେଖ |)

Answer: Dr. Kalam's immense contributions to science, space, and defense did not go unnoticed. He served as the Chief Scientific Adviser to the Prime Minister from 1992 to 1999. The Government of India recognized his exceptional service by awarding him the Padma Bhushan in 1981, the Padma Vibhushan in 1990, and finally, the nation's highest civilian honor, the Bharat Ratna, in 1997. The climax of his public life came in 2002 when he was elected as the 11th President of India. Because of his humility, simple lifestyle, and deep connection with students and the common masses, he was widely and affectionately referred to as the "People's President."
