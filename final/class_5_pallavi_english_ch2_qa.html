💡 Book Q & A

Let's Have Fun: Gone with the Scooter – Book Q A Class 5 English Pallavi

📖 Pre-reading

🌟 1. Match the pictures in 'A' with their names in 'B'.

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: 'A' ରେ ଥିବା ଚିତ୍ରଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ 'B' ରେ ଥିବା ସେମାନଙ୍କ ନାମ ସହିତ ମେଳ କର।)

✅ Answer:

  • Picture 1 -> Volleyball

  • Picture 2 -> Cricket

  • Picture 3 -> Football

  • Picture 4 -> Hockey

  • Picture 5 -> Basketball

🌟 2. Suppose, you are playing cricket. When you strike the ball, where does it go?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଧରିନିଅ, ତୁମେ କ୍ରିକେଟ୍ ଖେଳୁଛ। ଯେତେବେଳେ ତୁମେ ବଲ୍‌କୁ ବାଡ଼ାଅ, ଏହା କେଉଁଠାକୁ ଯାଏ?)

✅ Answer: When we strike the ball, it goes far away across the field or over the boundary.

🌟 3. How will you find if it is lost? Search in the neighbourhood?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଯଦି ଏହା ହଜିଯାଏ, ତେବେ ତୁମେ ଏହାକୁ କିପରି ଖୋଜିବ? ପାଖଆଖରେ ଖୋଜିବ କି?)

✅ Answer: Yes, we search for it in the nearby bushes, fields, or neighbourhood.

🗣️ Let us Discuss

🌟 1. Who are there in the story?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଗଳ୍ପରେ କିଏ କିଏ ଅଛନ୍ତି?)

✅ Answer: The characters in the story are Gopi, his mother, Manoj, Ramani, Deepak, Jay, and a scooterist.

🌟 2. Where was Gopi sitting?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଗୋପୀ କେଉଁଠାରେ ବସିଥିଲା?)

✅ Answer: Gopi was sitting in the veranda reading a book.

🌟 3. What sound did Gopi hear before he went to the garden?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ବଗିଚାକୁ ଯିବା ପୂର୍ବରୁ ଗୋପୀ କେଉଁ ଶବ୍ଦ ଶୁଣିଲା?)

✅ Answer: Gopi heard something rustling past and falling with a thud.

🌟 4. Gopi ran into the garden to a) play b) work c) to see what fell d) meet his friends.

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଗୋପୀ ବଗିଚାକୁ ଦୌଡ଼ିଗଲା କାହିଁକି a) ଖେଳିବାକୁ b) କାମ କରିବାକୁ c) କ'ଣ ପଡ଼ିଲା ଦେଖିବାକୁ d) ସାଙ୍ଗମାନଙ୍କୁ ଭେଟିବାକୁ।)

✅ Answer: c) to see what fell.

🌟 5. Where did Gopi search for the ball?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଗୋପୀ ବଲ୍‌ଟିକୁ କେଉଁଠାରେ ଖୋଜିଲା?)

✅ Answer: Gopi searched for the ball in between the thick grass near the garden fence, among the bushes, and on a heap of dry leaves.

🌟 6. Do you think Gopi will keep the ball with him or try to return it to its owner?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ତୁମେ କ'ଣ ଭାବୁଛ, ଗୋପୀ ବଲ୍‌ଟିକୁ ନିଜ ପାଖରେ ରଖିବ ନା ଏହାର ମାଲିକକୁ ଫେରାଇବାକୁ ଚେଷ୍ଟା କରିବ?)

✅ Answer: Gopi tried to return it to its owner by asking his friends.

🌟 7. Who took away the ball from Gopi?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଗୋପୀଠାରୁ ବଲ୍‌ଟିକୁ କିଏ ନେଇଗଲା?)

✅ Answer: Manoj took the ball away from Gopi.

🌟 8. Where did the ball fall when Jay hit it?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଜୟ ବାଡ଼େଇବା ପରେ ବଲ୍‌ଟି କେଉଁଠାରେ ପଡ଼ିଲା?)

✅ Answer: The ball fell into the basket of a passing scooter.

🌟 9. Was the scooterist aware of it? Which word supports your answer?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ସ୍କୁଟର ଚାଳକ ଏହା ଜାଣିଥିଲେ କି? କେଉଁ ଶବ୍ଦ ତୁମର ଉତ୍ତରକୁ ସମର୍ଥନ କରୁଛି?)

✅ Answer: No, the scooterist was not aware of it. The word "unaware" supports the answer.

🌟 10. Why did everyone laugh at the end of the story?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଗଳ୍ପ ଶେଷରେ ସମସ୍ତେ କାହିଁକି ହସିଲେ?)

✅ Answer: Everyone burst into laughter because the ball was suddenly gone with the scooter!

🌟 11. Imagine what happens to the hockey ball after it is taken away by the scooter? Where does it go? Who finds it?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: କଳ୍ପନା କର, ସ୍କୁଟର ଦ୍ୱାରା ନିଆଯିବା ପରେ ହକି ବଲ୍‌ର କ'ଣ ହେବ? ଏହା କେଉଁଠାକୁ ଯାଏ? ଏହାକୁ କିଏ ପାଏ?)

✅ Answer: The scooterist goes home. Later, his own children might find the ball in the basket and start playing with it!

🌟 12. Notice in the picture that the scooterist is wearing a helmet. Why is it important to wear a helmet? Should the bike riders also wear helmets?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଚିତ୍ରରେ ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟ କର ଯେ ସ୍କୁଟର ଚାଳକ ଏକ ହେଲମେଟ୍ ପିନ୍ଧିଛନ୍ତି। ହେଲମେଟ୍ ପିନ୍ଧିବା କାହିଁକି ଜରୁରୀ? ବାଇକ୍ ଚାଳକମାନେ ମଧ୍ୟ ହେଲମେଟ୍ ପିନ୍ଧିବା ଉଚିତ୍ କି?)

✅ Answer: It is important to wear a helmet to protect the head from injuries. Yes, all bike riders must wear helmets.

🌟 1. A 'CAR' changes into a 'BUS'! Let's see how it is possible. Change one letter at a step and go on making new words. Can you change CUP to TEA in this way?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଗୋଟିଏ 'CAR' 'BUS' ରେ ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ ହୁଏ! ଆସ ଦେଖିବା ଏହା କିପରି ସମ୍ଭବ। ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ ଥର ଗୋଟିଏ ଅକ୍ଷର ବଦଳାଅ ଏବଂ ନୂଆ ଶବ୍ଦ ଗଠନ କର। ତୁମେ ଏହି ଉପାୟରେ CUP କୁ TEA ରେ ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ କରିପାରିବ କି?)

✅ Answer: Yes! Here is how we change CUP to TEA step by step:

  • CUP

  • COP (We changed U and put O and got the new word COP)

  • TOP (We changed C and put T and got the new word TOP)

  • TOE (We changed P and put E and got the new word TOE)

  • TEE (We changed O and put E and got the new word TEE)

  • TEA (We changed E and put A and got the new word TEA!)
