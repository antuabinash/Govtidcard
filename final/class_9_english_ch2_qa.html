The Swimmer Who Does Not Need Her Legs! – Book Q A Class 9 ENGLISH

Section D: Let's understand the text 📖

1. When did Janaki get the idea of swimming the English Channel ? (ଜାନକୀ ଇଂଲିଶ୍ ଚ୍ୟାନେଲ୍ ପହଁରିବା ପାଇଁ କେବେ ଧାରଣା ପାଇଲେ?)

Janaki got the idea of trying to swim the English Channel when she was a child.

2. What did she plan to do then? (ତା'ପରେ ସେ କ'ଣ କରିବାକୁ ଯୋଜନା କଲେ?)

She planned to convey her idea to the English Channel Swimming Association for details and began training very hard to fulfill the conditions they set.

3. Who said, 'I was able to swim 10 hours at a stretch in the swimming pool'? a. Mr. Gopal b. Mr. Dinesh Suvarna c. Janaki. ('ମୁଁ ସନ୍ତରଣାଗାରରେ ଲଗାତାର ୧୦ ଘଣ୍ଟା ପହଁରି ପାରିଲି' ଏକଥା କିଏ କହିଥିଲେ? କ. ମି. ଗୋପାଳ ଖ. ମି. ଦିନେଶ ସୁବର୍ଣ୍ଣା ଗ. ଜାନକୀ)

c. Janaki

4. Where did she go with the help of Thimaiah Academy of Adventure? What did she do there? (ଥିମାୟା ଏକାଡେମୀ ଅଫ୍ ଆଡଭେଞ୍ଚର୍ ସାହାଯ୍ୟରେ ସେ କେଉଁଠିକି ଗଲେ? ସେଠାରେ ସେ କ'ଣ କଲେ?)

She went to the coastal town of Malpe. There, she spent three weeks with a coach called Dinesh Suvarna and began training in the Arabian Sea.

5. Who contributed the money she needed for swimming the English channel? (ଇଂଲିଶ୍ ଚ୍ୟାନେଲ୍ ପହଁରିବା ପାଇଁ ଆବଶ୍ୟକ ଅର୍ଥ କିଏ ଯୋଗାଇ ଦେଇଥିଲେ?)

The government, a few private agencies, and several friends contributed the money she needed for the attempt.

6. Who was Stella Streeter ? (ଷ୍ଟେଲା ଷ୍ଟ୍ରିଟର୍ କିଏ ଥିଲେ?)

Stella Streeter was the famous Channel coach under whom Janaki trained for about three weeks.

7. What does 'Janaki's Maritime Express' mean? ('ଜାନକୀସ୍ ମେରିଟାଇମ୍ ଏକ୍ସପ୍ରେସ୍' ର ଅର୍ଥ କ'ଣ?)

It was the name given to the relay team by the other American swimmers to honor Janaki's incredible effort and maritime spirit.

Section E: Let's understand the text better 🧠

1. What were the conditions set by the English Channel Swimming Authority? (ଇଂଲିଶ୍ ଚ୍ୟାନେଲ୍ ସୁଇମିଂ ଅଥରିଟି କେଉଁ ସର୍ତ୍ତଗୁଡ଼ିକ ରଖିଥିଲେ?)

The conditions were that the swimmer should be able to spend at least ten hours continuously in water and should be able to do marathon swimming in the high seas for long hours.

2. Why were the officials of English Channel Swimming Association shocked? (ଇଂଲିଶ୍ ଚ୍ୟାନେଲ୍ ସୁଇମିଂ ଆସୋସିଏସନ୍‌ର ଅଧିକାରୀମାନେ କାହିଁକି ଆଶ୍ଚର୍ଯ୍ୟ ହୋଇଗଲେ?)

They were shocked because it was the first time in their experience that a physically handicapped person was trying to swim the English Channel.

3. Why was it decided that Janaki would be part of a relay team? (ଜାନକୀ ଏକ ରିଲେ ଦଳର ଅଂଶ ହେବେ ବୋଲି କାହିଁକି ନିଷ୍ପତ୍ତି ନିଆଗଲା?)

It was decided because they did not have a special boat to accompany her for a solo attempt in case she needed to be lifted out of the water.

4. What were the channel waters like? (ଚ୍ୟାନେଲର ଜଳ କିପରି ଥିଲା?)

The Channel waters were incredibly cold and very salty, filled with seaweeds and jellyfish.

5. What were the problems that Janaki faced while swimming the channel in a relay team? (ରିଲେ ଦଳରେ ଚ୍ୟାନେଲ୍ ପହଁରିବା ସମୟରେ ଜାନକୀ କେଉଁସବୁ ସମସ୍ୟାର ସମ୍ମୁଖୀନ ହୋଇଥିଲେ?)

She faced the freezing cold water, the inability to control her paralyzed legs moving left and right with the waves, salty water entering her mouth making her sick, and seaweeds and jellyfish sticking to her body.

6. What were the feelings of Janaki's parents when she was crossing the channel waters? (ଜାନକୀ ଯେତେବେଳେ ଚ୍ୟାନେଲ୍ ଜଳ ଅତିକ୍ରମ କରୁଥିଲେ ସେତେବେଳେ ତାଙ୍କ ପିତାମାତାଙ୍କ ଅନୁଭବ କ'ଣ ଥିଲା?)

Her parents were extremely worried and anxious. It was an agonizing experience for them, and they could not even eat a single morsel out of fear.

7. What does the expression paraplegic Olympics mean? (ପାରାପ୍ଲେଜିକ୍ ଅଲିମ୍ପିକ୍ସ ଅଭିବ୍ୟକ୍ତିର ଅର୍ଥ କ'ଣ?)

Paraplegic Olympics refers to an international multi-sport event (now known as Paralympics) for athletes with severe physical disabilities, particularly lower-body paralysis.

8. What is it you like most about Janaki? (Is it her determination, her courage, her hardwork....?) (ଜାନକୀଙ୍କ ବିଷୟରେ ତୁମକୁ ସବୁଠାରୁ ଅଧିକ କ'ଣ ଭଲ ଲାଗେ? ଏହା ତାଙ୍କର ଦୃଢ଼ ନିଶ୍ଚୟ, ସାହସ, କିମ୍ବା କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ କି...?)

I admire her indomitable courage and sheer determination the most. Despite her physical limitations, she transformed her impossible dream into a reality through extraordinary hard work.

Section I: Lets's learn language ✍️

1. Complete the following sentences with could, was/were able to / couldn't. (ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକରେ could, was/were able to / couldn't ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ଶୂନ୍ୟସ୍ଥାନ ପୂରଣ କର।)

(i) The fire spread quickly but everyone _____ escape.

The fire spread quickly but everyone was able to escape.

(ii) He _____ drive when he was sixteen, but he _____ because he didn't have a licence.

He could drive when he was sixteen, but he couldn't because he didn't have a licence.

(iii) They didn't have any tomatoes in the first shop I went to, but I _____ get some in the next shop.

They didn't have any tomatoes in the first shop I went to, but I was able to get some in the next shop.

(iv) The boy fell into the river but fortunately we _____ rescue him.

The boy fell into the river but fortunately we were able to rescue him.

(v) He had hurt his leg, so he _____ walk very slowly.

He had hurt his leg, so he could walk very slowly.

(vi) I looked everywhere for the book but I _____ find it.

I looked everywhere for the book but I couldn't find it.
