Let us do these activities before we read

🧘‍♂️ 1. Question: What do you know about yoga? Discuss in pairs and share with the whole class.

✅ 1. Answer: Yoga is an ancient practice that originated in India. It involves physical postures, breathing exercises, and meditation to promote physical, mental, and spiritual well-being. Yoga helps in increasing flexibility, strength, and relaxation.

🌍 2. Question: What is 21st June known for? What do we do on this day?

✅ 2. Answer: 21st June is known as International Yoga Day. On this day, people all over the world participate in yoga sessions to celebrate and promote the amazing benefits of yoga for health and well-being.

📝 3. Question: List as many words you know related to yoga. Share them with your classmates.

✅ 3. Answer: Some common words related to yoga are Asana, Pranayama, Meditation, Flexibility, Balance, Mindfulness, Om, Relaxation, Posture, and Wellness.

📖 Page 117 - 118

Let us think and reflect

 

🎯 5. Question: What is the aim of yoga?

✅ 5. Answer: The aim of yoga is to create a balance between the mind and body, achieve harmony between humans and nature, and promote overall health and well-being by quieting the mind and freeing it from distractions.

 

🤝 6. Question: Explain the idea of unity in yoga.

✅ 6. Answer: The word 'yoga' is derived from the Sanskrit root 'yuj', meaning 'to unite'. The idea of unity in yoga involves the integration of mind and body, thought and action, and achieving harmony between the individual and the environment.

📱 7. Question: What does the launch of the WHO mYoga app tell us about the importance of yoga?

✅ 7. Answer: The launch of the WHO mYoga app highlights the global recognition of the benefits of yoga. It indicates that yoga is highly valued internationally for its ability to improve physical and mental health.

⏳ 8. Question: Describe one long-term effect that the practice of yoga can have on a person's qualities.

✅ 8. Answer: One long-term effect of practicing yoga is increased mindfulness and concentration. Regular practice leads to better focus, emotional regulation, lower levels of stress, and a greater sense of inner peace.

✍️ 13. Question: Write a short paragraph on 'The Benefits of Yoga'. You may use the following sentence starters along with your own ideas to create the paragraph.

✅ 13. Answer: Yoga is important because it offers a holistic approach to improving both physical and mental well-being. One benefit of yoga that interests me is its ability to improve flexibility and joint health. Practicing yoga can help people handle stress better and find inner calm. A popular yoga pose is Surya Namaskar because it energizes the entire body. Yoga is not just physical exercise; it also connects the mind, body, and spirit. Finally, yoga can be a great way to adopt a positive, healthy, and balanced lifestyle.
