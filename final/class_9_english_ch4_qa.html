The Missile Man of India – Book Q A Class 9 ENGLISH

Section D: Let's understand the text (Q 1 - 48) 📖

Question 1: Who was Dr APJ Abdul Kalam? (ଡକ୍ଟର ଏ.ପି.ଜେ. ଅବଦୁଲ କଲାମ କିଏ ଥିଲେ?)

Answer: Dr APJ Abdul Kalam was one of the most distinguished scientists of India and the 11th President of India.

Question 2: Why is Dr Kalam known as "The Missile Man of India"? (ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମଙ୍କୁ "ଭାରତର କ୍ଷେପଣାସ୍ତ୍ର ମାନବ" ବୋଲି କାହିଁକି କୁହାଯାଏ?)

Answer: He is known as "The Missile Man of India" because he was intimately involved in India's civilian space programme and military missile development efforts.

Question 3: Where was he born? When? (ସେ କେଉଁଠାରେ ଏବଂ କେବେ ଜନ୍ମଗ୍ରହଣ କରିଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He was born at Rameswaram in the state of Tamil Nadu on 15th October 1931.

Question 4: Who were his family members? (ତାଙ୍କ ପରିବାର ସଦସ୍ୟ କିଏ କିଏ ଥିଲେ?)

Answer: His family members were his father, his mother, three elder brothers, and one elder sister.

Question 5: Who were his father and mother? (ତାଙ୍କ ବାପା ଏବଂ ମାଆ କିଏ ଥିଲେ?)

Answer: His father was Jainulabdeen and his mother was Ashiamma.

Question 6: What was his mother? (ତାଙ୍କ ମାଆ କ'ଣ କରୁଥିଲେ?)

Answer: His mother was a housewife.

Question 7: How did his father earn his living? (ତାଙ୍କ ବାପା କିପରି ଜୀବିକା ନିର୍ବାହ କରୁଥିଲେ?)

Answer: His father earned his living by renting his boat to Hindu pilgrims and working as the Imam of a local mosque.

Question 8: Were Kalam's ancestors rich or poor? How do you know it? (କଲାମଙ୍କ ପୂର୍ବପୁରୁଷ ଧନୀ ଥିଲେ ନା ଗରିବ? ତୁମେ ଏହା କିପରି ଜାଣିଲ?)

Answer: His ancestors were rich. We know it because the text states they had been wealthy traders and land owners before losing their wealth.

Question 9: How did he add to his family? (ସେ ତାଙ୍କ ପରିବାରର ଆୟରେ କିପରି ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରୁଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He added to his family's income by selling newspapers in his childhood.

Question 10: What is the third paragraph about? (ତୃତୀୟ ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦ କେଉଁ ବିଷୟରେ ଅଟେ?)

Answer: The third paragraph is about Kalam's elementary education and the inspiring influence of his loving science teacher.

Question 11: Where did he get his primary education? (ସେ ତାଙ୍କର ପ୍ରାଥମିକ ଶିକ୍ଷା କେଉଁଠାରେ ପାଇଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He got his primary education at Ramanathpuram.

Question 12: Who was his science teacher? (ତାଙ୍କର ବିଜ୍ଞାନ ଶିକ୍ଷକ କିଏ ଥିଲେ?)

Answer: His science teacher was Sivasubramania Iyer.

Question 13: What was he like? (ସେ କିପରି ଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He was very loving, affectionate, and constantly inspired Kalam to develop his skills.

Question 14: What did he invite Kalam for? (ସେ କଲାମଙ୍କୁ କାହିଁକି ନିମନ୍ତ୍ରଣ କରିଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He invited Kalam to his home for a meal.

Question 15: How did his wife react to it? (ତାଙ୍କ ପତ୍ନୀ ଏଥିପ୍ରତି କିପରି ପ୍ରତିକ୍ରିୟା ଦେଖାଇଲେ?)

Answer: His wife disliked it and strictly refused to serve food to a Muslim child.

Question 16: What changed her attitude? (କେଉଁ କଥା ତାଙ୍କର ମନୋଭାବ ବଦଳାଇଲା?)

Answer: Kalam's polite behavior, his way of taking the meal, and his cleaning of the floor changed her attitude.

Question 17: How did she behave him later? (ପରେ ସେ ତାଙ୍କ ସହ କିପରି ବ୍ୟବହାର କଲେ?)

Answer: When he was invited for the second time, she warmly welcomed him and served him food herself.

Question 18: Who does 'He' in the third sentence of para-3 refer to? (ତୃତୀୟ ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦର ତୃତୀୟ ବାକ୍ୟରେ 'He' କାହାକୁ ବୁଝାଉଛି?)

Answer: 'He' refers to Kalam's science teacher, Sivasubramania Iyer.

Question 19: The fourth paragraph is about his... (ଚତୁର୍ଥ ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦ ତାଙ୍କର ... ବିଷୟରେ ଅଟେ |)

Answer: B. higher education and C. secondary education.

Question 20: Was he good at studies at the high school? Read out the sentence in support of your answer. (ହାଇସ୍କୁଲରେ ସେ ପାଠପଢାରେ ଭଲ ଥିଲେ କି? ସମର୍ଥନରେ ବାକ୍ୟଟି ପଢ଼।)

Answer: Yes, he was hardworking. The sentence is: "In his school years, he had average grades but was considered a bright and hardworking student, who had a strong desire to learn."

Question 21: What was Kalam's dream in life? (ଜୀବନରେ କଲାମଙ୍କର ସ୍ୱପ୍ନ କ'ଣ ଥିଲା?)

Answer: His dream in life was to become a pilot.

Question 22: What did he do to fulfil his dream in life? (ନିଜ ସ୍ୱପ୍ନ ପୂରଣ କରିବା ପାଇଁ ସେ କ'ଣ କଲେ?)

Answer: He applied to study aerospace engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology.

Question 23: Do you have any dream? How can you fulfil your dream in your life? (ତୁମର କୌଣସି ସ୍ୱପ୍ନ ଅଛି କି? ତୁମେ ଜୀବନରେ ତୁମର ସ୍ୱପ୍ନ କିପରି ପୂରଣ କରିପାରିବ?)

Answer: Yes, I dream of becoming a successful teacher. I can fulfil it by studying hard and staying dedicated to my goals.

Question 24: What problem did he face for his admission into engineering? (ଇଞ୍ଜିନିୟରିଂରେ ନାମ ଲେଖାଇବା ପାଇଁ ସେ କେଉଁ ସମସ୍ୟାର ସମ୍ମୁଖୀନ ହୋଇଥିଲେ?)

Answer: The engineering course was an expensive affair requiring a thousand rupees, which his poor family could not afford.

Question 25: Who came to his help? How? (ତାଙ୍କୁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରିବାକୁ କିଏ ଆସିଲେ? କିପରି?)

Answer: His compassionate elder sister, Asim Zohra, helped him by mortgaging her gold ornaments to pay his admission fee.

Question 26: When did he complete his aerospace engineering? (ସେ କେବେ ତାଙ୍କର ଏରୋସ୍ପେସ୍ ଇଞ୍ଜିନିୟରିଂ ଶେଷ କଲେ?)

Answer: He completed his aerospace engineering in 1960.

Question 27: Name the institution where he got - (ସେହି ଅନୁଷ୍ଠାନର ନାମ ଲେଖ ଯେଉଁଠାରୁ ସେ -)

Answer: (a) Secondary Education: Schwartz Higher Secondary School

(b) Graduation in Physics: Saint Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli

(c) Aerospace Engineering: Madras Institute of Technology

Question 28: The paragraphs-5 and 6 describe Dr. Kalam's career as... (ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦ ୫ ଏବଂ ୬ ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମଙ୍କର କ୍ୟାରିୟରକୁ ଏକ ... ଭାବରେ ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କରେ |)

Answer: an aerospace scientist.

Question 29: How did he begin his career? (ସେ ନିଜର କ୍ୟାରିୟର କିପରି ଆରମ୍ଭ କଲେ?)

Answer: He began his career as a trainee at the Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) in Bangalore.

Question 30: Where was he appointed as a scientist first? (ପ୍ରଥମେ ସେ କେଉଁଠାରେ ବୈଜ୍ଞାନିକ ଭାବରେ ନିଯୁକ୍ତ ହୋଇଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He was first appointed as a scientist at the Defence Research Development Organisation (DRDO).

Question 31: When did he start his work in the Indian Space Research Organisation? (ଭାରତୀୟ ମହାକାଶ ଗବେଷଣା ସଂସ୍ଥାରେ ସେ କେବେ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ ଆରମ୍ଭ କଲେ?)

Answer: He started his work in the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) in 1969.

Question 32: What was the purpose of the satellite? (ଉପଗ୍ରହର ଉଦ୍ଦେଶ୍ୟ କ'ଣ ଥିଲା?)

Answer: The purpose of the SLV was to develop the technology to launch satellites into space.

Question 33: When was the first Rohini satellite launched into the orbit? (ପ୍ରଥମ ରୋହିଣୀ ଉପଗ୍ରହ କେବେ କକ୍ଷପଥରେ ଉତକ୍ଷେପଣ କରାଯାଇଥିଲା?)

Answer: The first Rohini satellite was successfully launched into orbit in 1980.

Question 34: How many years did Kalam spend in the ISRO? (ଇସ୍ରୋରେ କଲାମ କେତେ ବର୍ଷ ବିତାଇଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He spent nineteen fruitful years in the ISRO.

Question 35: Where did he take the leading role in the development of India's missiles and nuclear weapons programme? (ଭାରତର କ୍ଷେପଣାସ୍ତ୍ର ଏବଂ ପରମାଣୁ ଅସ୍ତ୍ର କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକ୍ରମର ବିକାଶରେ ସେ କେଉଁଠାରେ ପ୍ରମୁଖ ଭୂମିକା ଗ୍ରହଣ କରିଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He took the leading role at DRDO under the Integrated Missile Development Programme (IGMDP).

Question 36: What made him extremely popular in the country? (ଦେଶରେ ତାଙ୍କୁ କେଉଁ କଥା ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ଲୋକପ୍ରିୟ କରାଇଲା?)

Answer: The successful development of four major missiles made him extremely popular in the country.

Question 37: What was he called after the success of the four missiles - Prithvi, Trishul, Akash and Nag? (ଚାରୋଟି କ୍ଷେପଣାସ୍ତ୍ରର ସଫଳତା ପରେ ତାଙ୍କୁ କ'ଣ କୁହାଗଲା?)

Answer: He was popularly called "The Missile Man of India."

Question 38: Why did he camp in Rajastan's Thar Desert? (ରାଜସ୍ଥାନର ଥର ମରୁଭୂମିରେ ସେ କାହିଁକି ଶିବିର କରି ରହିଥିଲେ?)

Answer: He camped there to supervise the Pokhran-II nuclear tests.

Question 39: What, according to Dr Kalam, was the "defining moment" in the history of our country"? (ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମଙ୍କ ମତରେ ଆମ ଦେଶର ଇତିହାସରେ "ନିର୍ଣ୍ଣାୟକ ମୁହୂର୍ତ୍ତ" କ'ଣ ଥିଲା?)

Answer: According to him, the successful Pokhran-II nuclear test was the defining moment that established India's nuclear era.

Question 40: Why was it very hard to camp in Rajstan's Thar Desert? (ରାଜସ୍ଥାନର ଥର ମରୁଭୂମିରେ ଶିବିର କରିବା କାହିଁକି ବହୁତ କଷ୍ଟକର ଥିଲା?)

Answer: It was very hard because it was extremely hot, with temperatures reaching up to 53°C.

Question 41: What are Kalam's contributions for India's defence programme? (ଭାରତର ପ୍ରତିରକ୍ଷା କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକ୍ରମ ପାଇଁ କଲାମଙ୍କ ଅବଦାନଗୁଡ଼ିକ କ'ଣ?)

Answer: His contributions include heading the Integrated Missile Development Programme and supervising the Pokhran-II nuclear tests.

Question 42: Dr. Kalam was the Chief Scientific Advisor to... (ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମ ... ଙ୍କ ମୁଖ୍ୟ ବୈଜ୍ଞାନିକ ଉପଦେଷ୍ଟା ଥିଲେ |)

Answer: ii. the Prime Minister

Question 43: What awards and honours was Kalam honoured with? (କଲାମଙ୍କୁ କେଉଁ ପୁରସ୍କାର ଓ ସମ୍ମାନରେ ସମ୍ମାନିତ କରାଯାଇଥିଲା?)

Answer: He was honoured with the Padma Bhushan, the Padma Vibhushan, and the Bharat Ratna.

Question 44: What is the highest civilian award of our country? (ଆମ ଦେଶର ସର୍ବୋଚ୍ଚ ବେସାମରିକ ପୁରସ୍କାର କ'ଣ?)

Answer: The Bharat Ratna is the highest civilian award of our country.

Question 45: How did Kalam pass away? (କଲାମଙ୍କର ଦେହାନ୍ତ କିପରି ହେଲା?)

Answer: He passed away due to a massive cardiac arrest while delivering a lecture in Shillong on 27th July 2015.

Question 46: Why will people of our country remember Kalam forever? (ଆମ ଦେଶର ଲୋକମାନେ କଲାମଙ୍କୁ ସବୁଦିନ ପାଇଁ କାହିଁକି ମନେ ରଖିବେ?)

Answer: People will remember him forever for his sheer dedication, devotion, and selfless service to the nation.

Question 47: What qualities of Dr Kalam do you like very much? (ଡକ୍ଟର କଲାମଙ୍କର କେଉଁ ଗୁଣ ତୁମକୁ ବହୁତ ଭଲ ଲାଗେ?)

Answer: I highly admire his humility, hard work, inspiring nature, and deep patriotism.

Question 48: Can you say the names of some eminent scientists who have contributed a lot to our country? (ତୁମେ ଆମ ଦେଶ ପାଇଁ ବହୁତ ଅବଦାନ ଦେଇଥିବା କିଛି ବିଶିଷ୍ଟ ବୈଜ୍ଞାନିକଙ୍କ ନାମ କହିପାରିବ କି?)

Answer: Yes, C.V. Raman, Homi J. Bhabha, Vikram Sarabhai, and Jagadish Chandra Bose are some eminent scientists.

Answer: Chief Scientific Advisor
