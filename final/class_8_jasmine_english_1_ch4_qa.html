Question 1 : What do you think about the Republic Day Parade on 26 January?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ୨୬ ଜାନୁଆରୀ ସାଧାରଣତନ୍ତ୍ର ଦିବସ ପରେଡ୍ ବିଷୟରେ ଆପଣ କ'ଣ ଭାବୁଛନ୍ତି?)

Answer: India became a Republic on 26 January 1950. To commemorate this day, Republic Day is celebrated every year on 26 January. A parade is held at Rajpath, New Delhi, to celebrate this event. The President of India hoists the National Flag.

Question 2 ✨: What are the highlights of this parade?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଏହି ପରେଡର ମୁଖ୍ୟ ଆକର୍ଷଣ ଗୁଡ଼ିକ କ'ଣ?)

Answer: A foreign dignitary is invited to be a guest during the Republic Day parade. A march past is done by the armed forces. Many states display their floats during the parade.

Question 3 🎖️: What bravery awards are given on this day, and who receives them?

(ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଅନୁବାଦ: ଏହି ଦିନ କେଉଁ ବୀରତ୍ୱ ପୁରସ୍କାର ଦିଆଯାଏ ଏବଂ କିଏ ଏହାକୁ ଗ୍ରହଣ କରନ୍ତି?)

Answer: The awards given on Republic Day are: Param Vir Chakra, Mahavir Chakra, Vir Chakra, Ashoka Chakra, Kirti Chakra and Shaurya Chakra. The personnel of armed forces receive them.

Key Roles of Women in the Indian Army:

1.Officer Ranks: Women can serve as commissioned officers in multiple branches through SSC and PC.

2.Combat Support and Services: They serve in the Army Medical Corps, Education Corps, JAG (military law), Signals, Engineering units, Intelligence, and Military Police.

3.Corps of Military Police (CMP): Recruiting women soldiers for policing army establishments, controlling female offenders, etc.

4.Peacekeeping and UN Missions: Indian women officers have represented the country in UN Peacekeeping Missions.

5.Leadership Roles: Women officers have held command roles (e.g., Lt. Gen. Punita Arora).

Conclusion: Women in the Indian Army have proven their bravery, discipline, and dedication in every role assigned to them. Their growing presence is a symbol of gender equality and national pride.
