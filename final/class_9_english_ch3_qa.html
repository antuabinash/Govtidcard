Road Safety Week – Book Q A Class 9 ENGLISH

Section D: Let's understand the text 📖

Question 1: What is the first paragraph about? (ପ୍ରଥମ ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦଟି କେଉଁ ବିଷୟରେ ଅଟେ?)

Answer: The first paragraph is about the paramount importance of road safety, what it involves, and the need for greater awareness among people to prevent roadside injuries and deaths.

Question 2: What is road safety? What does it involve? (ସଡ଼କ ସୁରକ୍ଷା କ'ଣ? ଏଥିରେ କ'ଣ ସାମିଲ ଥାଏ?)

Answer: Road safety is the safety of people from roadside injuries and deaths. It involves various methods and measures which must be followed by road users to prevent these serious injuries and deaths.

Question 3: What is the finding of the World Health Organisation? (ବିଶ୍ୱ ସ୍ୱାସ୍ଥ୍ୟ ସଂଗଠନର ଅନୁସନ୍ଧାନ କ'ଣ?)

Answer: The World Health Organisation has found that most of the hospitalisation cases and leading causes of death are due to a lack of awareness about road safety.

Question 4: Why is there a greater need of awareness among people about road safety? (ସଡ଼କ ସୁରକ୍ଷା ବିଷୟରେ ଲୋକଙ୍କ ମଧ୍ୟରେ ସଚେତନତାର ଅଧିକ ଆବଶ୍ୟକତା କାହିଁକି ଅଛି?)

Answer: There is a greater need for awareness because there is a continuous rise in road accidents due to the increasing number of vehicle users.

Question 5: What does the second paragraph talk about? (ଦ୍ୱିତୀୟ ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦଟି କେଉଁ ବିଷୟରେ କହୁଛି?)

Answer: The second paragraph talks about the Road Safety Week Campaign, its aim to raise public awareness about traffic rules, and the various organisations that support this campaign.

Question 6: Who observes the Road Safety Week Campaign? (କିଏ ସଡ଼କ ସୁରକ୍ଷା ସପ୍ତାହ ଅଭିଯାନ ପାଳନ କରନ୍ତି?)

Answer: The government, along with many charitable organisations, non-government agencies, and private firms across the country, observes the Road Safety Week Campaign.

Question 7: When is it observed? (ଏହା କେବେ ପାଳନ କରାଯାଏ?)

Answer: It is observed every year in the month of January.

Question 8: What is the main aim of Road Safety Week Campaign? (ସଡ଼କ ସୁରକ୍ଷା ସପ୍ତାହ ଅଭିଯାନର ମୁଖ୍ୟ ଉଦ୍ଦେଶ୍ୟ କ'ଣ?)

Answer: The main aim is to sensitise people about the outcome of drunken driving, high-speed driving, and the importance of wearing a helmet for bikers and a seat-belt for four-wheel drivers.

Question 9: What things should people know about road safety by this campaign? (ଏହି ଅଭିଯାନ ମାଧ୍ୟମରେ ସଡ଼କ ସୁରକ୍ଷା ବିଷୟରେ ଲୋକମାନେ କେଉଁସବୁ କଥା ଜାଣିବା ଉଚିତ୍?)

Answer: People should know the dangers of drunken and high-speed driving, the importance of wearing helmets and seat-belts, and that they must not use cell phones or listen to music while driving.

Question 10: What does the author say in the third paragraph? (ତୃତୀୟ ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦରେ ଲେଖକ କ'ଣ କହିଛନ୍ତି?)

Answer: In the third paragraph, the author describes how the Road Safety Week is organised and celebrated in major cities with various activities, distribution of safety materials, and free medical check-ups.

Question 11: When is the Road Safety Week organised? (ସଡ଼କ ସୁରକ୍ଷା ସପ୍ତାହ କେବେ ଆୟୋଜିତ ହୁଏ?)

Answer: Road Safety Week is organised every year in the month of January by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways of India.

Question 12: Where do people celebrate this week? Who voluntarily take part in the campaign? (ଲୋକମାନେ ଏହି ସପ୍ତାହ କେଉଁଠାରେ ପାଳନ କରନ୍ତି? କିଏ ସ୍ୱେଚ୍ଛାକୃତ ଭାବରେ ଏହି ଅଭିଯାନରେ ଅଂଶଗ୍ରହଣ କରନ୍ତି?)

Answer: People celebrate this week in major cities like Delhi, Bangalore, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bhubaneswar, etc. Many schools and colleges voluntarily take part in the campaign.

 

Question 13: What do the local authorities do during this campaign? (ଏହି ଅଭିଯାନ ସମୟରେ ସ୍ଥାନୀୟ କର୍ତ୍ତୃପକ୍ଷ କ'ଣ କରନ୍ତି?)

Answer: The local authorities organise a thorough check of drivers and motorcyclists to send a strong message of "Zero Tolerance against Drunken Driving" and other safety violations.

Question 14: How do drivers get benefit out of this programme? (ଏହି କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକ୍ରମରୁ ଚାଳକମାନେ କିପରି ଲାଭବାନ ହୁଅନ୍ତି?)

Answer: Drivers benefit from free medical check-up camps and driving training workshops that are specially organised for them.

Question 15: What activities are organised for school and college children? (ସ୍କୁଲ୍ ଏବଂ କଲେଜ୍ ପିଲାମାନଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ କେଉଁ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକଳାପ ଆୟୋଜିତ ହୁଏ?)

Answer: Various painting and drawing competitions, road safety announcements, exhibitions, tests of road rules, debates, workshops, and seminars are organised for them.

Question 16: Why is the fourth paragraph important for us? (ଚତୁର୍ଥ ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦଟି ଆମ ପାଇଁ କାହିଁକି ଗୁରୁତ୍ୱପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ?)

Answer: It is highly important because it highlights the various crucial factors that cause serious road accidents resulting in fatalities, making us aware of what dangerous habits to avoid.

Question 17: How many important causes of accident have been highlighted in this paragraph? Can you add some more causes to this list? (ଏହି ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦରେ ଦୁର୍ଘଟଣାର କେତୋଟି ଗୁରୁତ୍ୱପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ କାରଣ ଦର୍ଶାଯାଇଛି? ତୁମେ ଏହି ତାଲିକାରେ ଆଉ କିଛି କାରଣ ଯୋଡ଼ିପାରିବ କି?)

Answer: Five important causes have been highlighted: drunken/rash driving, underage driving, distracted driving, careless habits (jumping signals/no seat-belts), and unsafe pedestrians/vehicles. Yes, bad road conditions and lack of proper streetlights can be added to the list.

Question 18: Who are eligible to get driving licence from the authorities? (କର୍ତ୍ତୃପକ୍ଷଙ୍କଠାରୁ ଡ୍ରାଇଭିଂ ଲାଇସେନ୍ସ ପାଇବାକୁ କିଏ ଯୋଗ୍ୟ?)

Answer: A person who is 18 years old is eligible for a private vehicle licence. A 16-year-old can get a learner's licence for a 50cc gearless vehicle with parental consent, and one must be 20 years old for a commercial vehicle licence.

Question 19: What does 'distracted driving' mean? ('ଅନ୍ୟମନସ୍କ ଚାଳନା' ର ଅର୍ଥ କ'ଣ?)

Answer: 'Distracted driving' includes speaking on cell phones, texting while driving, talking to other passengers, and listening to loud music.

Question 20: Find out the word, in the fourth paragraph, which means 'traveller on foot'? (ଚତୁର୍ଥ ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦରୁ ଏପରି ଏକ ଶବ୍ଦ ବାହାର କର ଯାହାର ଅର୍ଥ 'ପାଦରେ ଚାଲୁଥିବା ଯାତ୍ରୀ'?)

Answer: The word is 'pedestrians'.

Question 21: What can you learn from the fifth paragraph? (ପଞ୍ଚମ ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦରୁ ତୁମେ କ'ଣ ଶିଖିପାରିବ?)

Answer: We learn about the strict preventive measures that should be taken, such as checking for drunken driving, strict issuance of licences, heavy fines for rule violations, and making fitness certificates compulsory.

Question 22: How can the drunken driving be checked? (ମଦ୍ୟପାନ କରି ଗାଡ଼ି ଚଳାଇବାକୁ କିପରି ରୋକାଯାଇପାରିବ?)

Answer: Drunken driving can be checked by local authorities conducting thorough checks on drivers of all vehicles and motorcycles, and enforcing zero tolerance.

Question 23: What should the government do for issuing driving licence? (ଡ୍ରାଇଭିଂ ଲାଇସେନ୍ସ ପ୍ରଦାନ ପାଇଁ ସରକାର କ'ଣ କରିବା ଉଚିତ୍?)

Answer: Strict government orders must be issued to the licence-issuing authorities to take strong steps while issuing licences, ensuring only qualified people get them.

Question 24: What can be done for old and unused vehicles? (ପୁରୁଣା ଏବଂ ଅବ୍ୟବହୃତ ଯାନଗୁଡ଼ିକ ପାଇଁ କ'ଣ କରାଯାଇପାରିବ?)

Answer: The fitness certificate should be made compulsory for every vehicle running on the road to ensure unsafe vehicles are not used.

Question 25: Who does 'us' refer to in the last paragraph? (ଶେଷ ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦରେ 'ଆମକୁ' କାହାକୁ ବୁଝାଉଛି?)

Answer: 'Us' refers to all the people, including the readers and the general public.

Question 26: Can you suggest some more ways to prevent road accidents? (ସଡ଼କ ଦୁର୍ଘଟଣା ରୋକିବା ପାଇଁ ତୁମେ ଆଉ କିଛି ଉପାୟ ପରାମର୍ଶ ଦେଇପାରିବ କି?)

Answer: Yes, repairing damaged roads, installing proper street lighting, making speed breakers near schools and hospitals, and educating children on traffic signs can help prevent accidents.

By Ashok Kumar, The Indian Express

Cuttack, 21 November 2018: A tragic and devastating bus accident took place on 20 November 2018 on a bridge near Jagatpur, on the outskirts of Cuttack city. A private bus travelling from Talcher to Cuttack met with this terrible disaster. The accident occurred when the driver tried to save a buffalo that had suddenly strayed onto the bridge.
