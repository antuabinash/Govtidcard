🖊️ Activity B: Fill in the Blanks (Conjunctions)

Instructions (English): Fill in the blanks with a suitable subordinating conjunction (e.g., if, though, because, as, when, while, so that, as if, than, unless).

Instructions (Odia): ଉପଯୁକ୍ତ subordinating conjunction (ଯେପରିକି if, though, because, as, when, while, so that, as if, than, unless) ଦ୍ୱାରା ଶୂନ୍ୟସ୍ଥାନ ପୂରଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

  1. He is honest, _____ he is poor.

Answer: though / although

  1. I will not go _____ you come with me.

Answer: unless

  1. He worked hard _____ he might succeed.

Answer: so that / in order that

  1. He failed _____ he was lazy.

Answer: because

  1. Wait here _____ I return.

Answer: until

  1. He is taller _____ I am.

Answer: than

  1. He behaves _____ he were rich.

Answer: as if / as though

  1. I will help you _____ you need it.

Answer: if / when

  1. He was so tired _____ he could not speak.

Answer: that

  1. _____ you are ready, we can start.

Answer: If / As

  1. He arrived _____ the meeting had started.

Answer: after

  1. You must leave at once, _____ you will be late.

Answer: or / otherwise (Note: 'or' makes it a compound sentence, but it's often used in conditional contexts)

  1. He is not as intelligent _____ his brother.

Answer: as

  1. Strike the iron _____ it is hot.

Answer: while

  1. He went to the hospital _____ he was sick.

Answer: because / as

  1. I shall go _____ you may not like it.

Answer: even if / although

  1. I will wait _____ you finish your lunch.

Answer: until

  1. He ran fast _____ he could win the race.

Answer: so that

  1. _____ you do not obey, you will be punished.

Answer: If

  1. I found my book _____ I had left it.

Answer: where


🔗 Activity C: Sentence Combination

Instructions (English): Combine the following pairs of sentences into one complex sentence using the adverb clause type mentioned in brackets.

Instructions (Odia): ବନ୍ଧନୀରେ ଦିଆଯାଇଥିବା ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦେଶ ଅନୁଯାୟୀ adverb clause ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ବାକ୍ୟ ଯୋଡିଗୁଡିକୁ ଗୋଟିଏ ଜଟିଳ ବାକ୍ୟରେ ମିଶ୍ରଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

  1. He is poor. He is honest. (Use Concession)

Answer: Though he is poor, he is honest.

  1. Work hard. You will succeed. (Use Condition)

Answer: If you work hard, you will succeed.

  1. I was ill. I could not go to school. (Use Reason)

Answer: I could not go to school because I was ill.

  1. He ran fast. He wanted to win the race. (Use Purpose)

Answer: He ran fast so that he could win the race.

  1. The rain stopped. We went out to play. (Use Time)

Answer: When the rain stopped, we went out to play.

  1. He is tall. His brother is taller. (Use Comparison)

Answer: His brother is taller than he is.

  1. He is rich. He is not happy. (Use Concession)

Answer: Although he is rich, he is not happy.

  1. Do not go. Wait for me. (Use Time)

Answer: Do not go until I come.

  1. You must tell the truth. You will be punished. (Use Condition with 'unless')

Answer: Unless you tell the truth, you will be punished.

  1. He was very weak. He could not walk. (Use Result)

Answer: He was so weak that he could not walk.

  1. He was walking. A dog bit him. (Use Time with 'while')

Answer: While he was walking, a dog bit him.

  1. I will go. It doesn't matter where you go. (Use Place)

Answer: I will go wherever you go.

  1. He talks like a master. He is not the master. (Use Manner)

Answer: He talks as if he were the master.

  1. The sun rose. The fog disappeared. (Use Time with 'as soon as')

Answer: As soon as the sun rose, the fog disappeared.

  1. He is intelligent. His sister is equally intelligent. (Use Comparison with 'as...as')

Answer: He is as intelligent as his sister.

  1. He must be mad. He talks like that. (Use Manner)

Answer: He talks as if he were mad.

  1. I will not help him. He must apologize first. (Use Condition)

Answer: I will not help him unless he apologizes first.

  1. I am telling you this. I want you to know the truth. (Use Purpose)

Answer: I am telling you this so that you may know the truth.

  1. It was raining. We stayed indoors. (Use Reason)

Answer: We stayed indoors as it was raining.

  1. He reached the station. The train left immediately. (Use Time with 'no sooner...than')

Answer: No sooner had he reached the station than the train left.


✏️ Activity D: Error Correction (Conditionals & Tense)

Instructions (English): Find the error in the adverb clause (especially in conditionals) and rewrite the sentence correctly.

Instructions (Odia): Adverb clause ରେ ଥିବା ଭୁଲକୁ (ବିଶେଷକରି ସର୍ତ୍ତମୂଳକ ବାକ୍ୟରେ) ଖୋଜନ୍ତୁ ଏବଂ ବାକ୍ୟଟିକୁ ସଠିକ୍ ଭାବରେ ପୁନର୍ଲିଖନ କରନ୍ତୁ।

  1. Incorrect: If I will go to Delhi, I will see him.

Correct: If I go to Delhi, I will see him.

  1. Incorrect: If I am you, I would not do it.

Correct: If I were you, I would not do it.

  1. Incorrect: If he would have worked hard, he would have passed.

Correct: If he had worked hard, he would have passed.

  1. Incorrect: He worked hard so that he will pass.

Correct: He worked hard so that he might/could pass.

  1. Incorrect: Unless you do not work hard, you will fail. (Double negative)

Correct: Unless you work hard, you will fail.

  1. Incorrect: He is so weak as he cannot walk.

Correct: He is so weak that he cannot walk.

  1. Incorrect: He ran fast lest he will miss the train.

Correct: He ran fast lest he should miss the train.

  1. Incorrect: Wait here if I return.

Correct: Wait here until I return.

  1. Incorrect: He will not succeed if he works hard.

Correct: He will not succeed unless he works hard.

  1. Incorrect: When I will reach home, I will call you.

Correct: When I reach home, I will call you.

  1. Incorrect: He speaks like he is a leader.

Correct: He speaks as if he were a leader.

  1. Incorrect: Had I known this, I will not have come.

Correct: Had I known this, I would not have come.

  1. Incorrect: I am taller as my brother.

Correct: I am taller than my brother.

  1. Incorrect: He is very ill that he cannot eat.

Correct: He is so ill that he cannot eat.

  1. Incorrect: I shall help you if you will help me.

Correct: I shall help you if you help me.


🔄 Activity E: Transformation (Phrase to Clause)

Instructions (English): Rewrite the sentences by changing the underlined adverbial phrase into an Adverb Clause.

Instructions (Odia): ରେଖାଙ୍କିତ adverbial phrase କୁ ଏକ Adverb Clause ରେ ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ କରି ବାକ୍ୟଟିକୁ ପୁନର୍ଲିଖନ କରନ୍ତୁ।

  1. He failed  on account of his illness .

Answer: He failed because he was ill.

  1. He works hard  to earn money .

Answer: He works hard so that he can earn money.

  1.  In spite of his poverty , he is honest.

Answer: Though he is poor, he is honest.

  1.  At the time of his arrival , I was busy.

Answer: When he arrived, I was busy.

  1. He is too weak  to walk .

Answer: He is so weak that he cannot walk.

  1.  On seeing the police , the thief ran away.

Answer: As soon as he saw the police, the thief ran away.

  1. He is tall  like his father .

Answer: He is as tall as his father is.

  1. He was punished  for his misbehavior .

Answer: He was punished because he misbehaved.

  1. He went to Kolkata  to see the Victoria Memorial .

Answer: He went to Kolkata so that he could see the Victoria Memorial.

  1. The match was cancelled  owing to bad weather .

Answer: The match was cancelled because the weather was bad.

  1.  After finishing his work , he went to bed.

Answer: After he had finished his work, he went to bed.

  1. He talks  like a madman .

Answer: He talks as if he were mad.

  1. You must work hard  to get a first class .

Answer: You must work hard if you want to get a first class.

  1.  During my stay in Puri , I visited the temple daily.

Answer: While I was staying in Puri, I visited the temple daily.

  1.  In the event of his failure , he will be sad.

Answer: If he fails, he will be sad.


✍️ Activity F: Sentence Completion

Instructions (English): Complete the following sentences by adding a suitable Main Clause or Adverb Clause.

Instructions (Odia): ଏକ ଉପଯୁକ୍ତ Main Clause କିମ୍ବା Adverb Clause ଯୋଗ କରି ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ବାକ୍ୟଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ କରନ୍ତୁ।

(Note: These answers are examples, as the activity is open-ended.)

(ସୂଚନା: ଏହି ଉତ୍ତରଗୁଡ଼ିକ ଉଦାହରଣ ଅଟେ, କାରଣ ଏହି କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକଳାପଟି ମୁକ୍ତ)

  1. Although he worked hard, _____.

Answer: Although he worked hard, he did not succeed. (ଯଦିଓ ସେ କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ କଲେ, ସେ ସଫଳ ହେଲେ ନାହିଁ।)

  1. I will not forgive him unless _____.

Answer: I will not forgive him unless he apologizes. (ମୁଁ ତାଙ୍କୁ କ୍ଷମା ଦେବି ନାହିଁ ଯଦି ସେ କ୍ଷମା ନ ମାଗନ୍ତି।)

  1. He is honest, even if _____.

Answer: He is honest, even if he is poor. (ସେ ସଚ୍ଚୋଟ, ଯଦିଓ ସେ ଗରିବ।)

  1. _____ so that he could pass.

Answer: He studied hard so that he could pass. (ସେ କଠିନ ପଢାପଢି କଲେ ଯାହାଫଳରେ ସେ ପାସ୍ କରିପାରିବେ।)

  1. _____ because he was lazy.

Answer: He did not succeed because he was lazy. (ସେ ଅଳସୁଆ ଥିବାରୁ ସଫଳ ହେଲେ ନାହିଁ।)

  1. If I were a bird, _____.

Answer: If I were a bird, I would fly in the sky. (ଯଦି ମୁଁ ପକ୍ଷୀ ହୋଇଥାନ୍ତି, ମୁଁ ଆକାଶରେ ଉଡନ୍ତି।)

  1. _____ as soon as the teacher entered.

Answer: The students stood up as soon as the teacher entered. (ଶିକ୍ଷକ ପ୍ରବେଶ କରିବା ମାତ୍ରେ ଛାତ୍ରମାନେ ଠିଆ ହୋଇଗଲେ।)

  1. He behaves as if _____.

Answer: He behaves as if he knew everything. (ସେ ଏପରି ବ୍ୟବହାର କରନ୍ତି ସତେ ଯେପରି ସେ ସବୁକିଛି ଜାଣିଛନ୍ତି।)

  1. Had I known you were coming, _____.

Answer: Had I known you were coming, I would have cooked dinner. (ଯଦି ମୁଁ ଜାଣିଥାନ୍ତି ତୁମେ ଆସୁଛ, ମୁଁ ରାତ୍ରୀ ଭୋଜନ ରାନ୍ଧିଥାନ୍ତି।)

  1. I will not go out if _____.

Answer: I will not go out if it rains. (ମୁଁ ବାହାରକୁ ଯିବି ନାହିଁ ଯଦି ବର୍ଷା ହୁଏ।)
