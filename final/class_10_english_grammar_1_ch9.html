1. What is a Preposition?

A preposition is a word or expression that shows the relationship between a noun (or pronoun) and another word in the sentence. This "other word" can be a noun, pronoun, adjective, verb, or adverb.

(Odia: Preposition (ଅବ୍ୟୟ) ହେଉଛି ଏକ ଶବ୍ଦ ଯାହା ଏକ ବାକ୍ୟରେ ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ (noun) ବା ସର୍ବନାମ (pronoun) ଏବଂ ଅନ୍ୟ ଏକ ଶବ୍ଦ (ବିଶେଷ୍ୟ, ବିଶେଷଣ, କ୍ରିୟା ଇତ୍ୟାଦି) ମଧ୍ୟରେ ଥିବା ସମ୍ପର୍କକୁ ଦର୍ଶାଏ ।)

  • Example: "The book is on the table." (Shows the relationship between "book" and "table".)
  • Example: "He is good at mathematics." (Connects the adjective "good" to the noun "mathematics".)
  • Example: "You must listen to your teacher." (Connects the verb "listen" to the noun "teacher".)


2. Position of Prepositions (Preposition ର ସ୍ଥାନ)

  1. Usually before a Noun/Pronoun: This is the most common position.
    • Example: "The pen is in my pocket."
  2. At the end of a sentence: This is also possible, especially in specific structures:
    • Wh-questions: "What are you looking at?"
    • Relative Clauses: "This is the book I have been looking for."
    • Passives: "I don't speak until I am spoken to."
    • Infinitives: "There was no chair to sit on."
    • Exclamations: "What a difficult situation he is in!"

4. Functions of Prepositions (Preposition ର କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ)

Prepositions are grouped by their function. The main categories are:

  1. Prepositions of Place (ସ୍ଥାନବାଚକ ଅବ୍ୟୟ)
  2. Prepositions of Time (କାଳବାଚକ ଅବ୍ୟୟ)
  3. Other Prepositions (ଅନ୍ୟାନ୍ୟ ଅବ୍ୟୟ)


5. Prepositions of Place (ସ୍ଥାନବାଚକ ଅବ୍ୟୟ)

These prepositions describe a position or location.

At / In / On (Main Uses)

Preposition

Use (ବ୍ୟବହାର)

Examples (ଉଦାହରଣ)

At

-ରେ / -ଠାରେ

1. Exact point or specific location (ଏକ ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ ସ୍ଥାନ ବା ବିନ୍ଦୁ)



2. Specific addresses (ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ ଠିକଣା)



3. Public places/events (ସର୍ବସାଧାରଣ ସ୍ଥାନ)

2. He lives at 95, Bapuji Nagar.



3. They met at the club / at the party / at work.

In

-ରେ / -ଭିତରେ

1. Inside a larger area (ଏକ ବଡ଼ ଅଞ୍ଚଳ ମଧ୍ୟରେ)



2. Cities and countries (ଶହର ଓ ଦେଶ)



3. Inside a building (କୋଠା ବା ଘର ଭିତରେ)



4. Workplace (if it's a building) (କାର୍ଯ୍ୟସ୍ଥଳ)

1. in the country, in a village



2. He lives in London / in China.



3. He is sitting in the corner of the room.



4. My brother works in a bank / in an office.

On

-ଉପରେ

1. Touching a surface (ପୃଷ୍ଠକୁ ସ୍ପର୍ଶ କରି)



2. Workplace (if not a building) (କାର୍ଯ୍ୟସ୍ଥଳ ଯଦି କୋଠା ନୁହେଁ)



3. Proximity (close to a river, coast) (ନିକଟବର୍ତ୍ତୀ)



4. Islands (ଦ୍ଵୀପ)

1. The book is on the table.



2. He is working on a farm.



3. Cuttack is on the Mahanadi.



4. He lives on an island.

ଉପରେ (ଲମ୍ବ ଭାବରେ)

ଉପରେ (ଉଚ୍ଚରେ)

1. Directly below. (ସିଧା ତଳେ)



2. Less than (figurative). (କମ୍ ବୟସ/ସଂଖ୍ୟା)

1. The cat is sitting under the table.



2. Children under the age of five...

Below

ତଳେ (ନିମ୍ନରେ)

Lower than (not necessarily directly under). (ନିମ୍ନ ସ୍ତରରେ)

Your work is below the average.

Beneath

ତଳେ / ଅଧୀନରେ

1. Directly under.



2. Lower in rank or dignity (figurative).

1. The dolphins disappeared beneath the waves.



2. She has married beneath her.

Through

ମଧ୍ୟ ଦେଇ

Entering at one side and coming out at the other. (ଭିତର ଦେଇ)

The road goes through the forest.

Across

ଆରପାରିରେ / ଏପଟୁ ସେପଟ

1. From one side to the other. (ଏକ ପାର୍ଶ୍ୱରୁ ଅନ୍ୟ ପାର୍ଶ୍ୱକୁ)



2. On the other side of. (ଅନ୍ୟ ପାର୍ଶ୍ୱରେ)

1. Draw a line across the page.



2. The house is just across the street.

Along

କୂଳେ କୂଳେ / ସିଧାରେ

In a line from one end to the other. (ଏକ ଧାଡ଼ିରେ)

Trees grow along the bank of the river.

Behind

ପଛରେ

At or towards the back of something.

The boy was hiding behind a tree.

Before / In front of

ସାମ୍ନାରେ / ଆଗରେ

Facing something.

He is standing in front of the class.

Between

ମଧ୍ୟରେ (ଦୁଇଟି)

Used when speaking of two persons or things.

Divide the cake between the two boys.

Among

ମଧ୍ୟରେ (ଦୁଇରୁ ଅଧିକ)

Used when speaking of three or more people or things.

The sweets were distributed among the children.

Against

ବିପକ୍ଷରେ / ଆଉଜି ହୋଇ

1. Touching an upright surface. (କାନ୍ଥକୁ ଆଉଜି)



2. In the opposite direction. (ବିପରୀତ ଦିଗରେ)

1. There was a ladder against the wall.



2. He is sailing against the wind.

Off

-ରୁ (ଅଲଗା ହେବା)

From the surface of; down from. (କୌଣସି ସ୍ଥାନରୁ ତଳକୁ ବା ଅଲଗା)

He fell off the horse.

Out of

-ଭିତରୁ (ବାହାରକୁ)

From the interior. (ଭିତରୁ ବାହାରକୁ)

He took the handkerchief out of his pocket.

Towards / To

ଆଡକୁ / -କୁ

In the direction of. (ଦିଗରେ)

He ran towards a safe place.

For

ପାଇଁ / ଉଦ୍ଦେଶ୍ୟରେ

Intended to reach (destination). (ଗନ୍ତବ୍ୟ ସ୍ଥଳ ଉଦ୍ଦେଶ୍ୟରେ)

6. Prepositions of Time (କାଳବାଚକ ଅବ୍ୟୟ)

These prepositions describe when an action happens.

Preposition

Odia Meaning

Use (ବ୍ୟବହାର)

Examples (ଉଦାହରଣ)

At

-ରେ / -କୁ

1. Exact point of time. (ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ ସମୟ)



2. Festivals. (ପର୍ବପର୍ବାଣି)



3. Specific times of day. (ଦିନର ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ ଭାଗ)

1. at three o'clock, at 10:30 am



2. at Holi, at Diwali, at Christmas



3. at midday, at midnight, at night, at dawn

In

-ରେ / -ମଧ୍ୟରେ

1. Longer periods: Seasons, Years, Months. (ଋତୁ, ବର୍ଷ, ମାସ)



2. Parts of the day. (ଦିନର ଭାଗ)



3. Future time (at the end of). (ଭବିଷ୍ୟତରେ ଏକ ଅବଧି ପରେ)

1. in winter, in 2005, in January



2. in the morning, in the evening



3. He will arrive in ten minutes.

On

-ରେ

1. Specific days. (ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ ଦିନ)



2. Specific dates. (ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ ତାରିଖ)



3. Specific parts of a day. (ଏକ ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ ଦିନର ଭାଗ)

1. on Thursday, on her birthday



2. on 15th August, on March 15, 2005



3. on a Sunday morning, on a winter evening

By

ସୁଦ୍ଧା / ପୂର୍ବରୁ

Latest time at which an action will be finished. (ଶେଷ ସମୟସୀମା)

Applications must reach the office by 20th June.

For

ଧରି / ଯାବତ୍

Used with a period of time. (ସମୟର ଅବଧି)

He has lived here for one year.

Since

-ଠାରୁ / -ରୁ

Used with a point of time in the past (with Perfect Tenses). (ଅତୀତର ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ ସମୟ ବିନ୍ଦୁ)

It has been raining since 6 o'clock.

From... to/till/until

-ଠାରୁ... ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ

Shows the start and end of a period.

The road will be closed from Friday to Monday.

During

ସମୟରେ / ବେଳେ

Used with an action that took place within a specific period.

The fire broke out during the night.

On time

ଠିକ୍ ସମୟରେ

At the exact or correct time (as per schedule). (ନିର୍ଦ୍ଧାରିତ ସମୟରେ)

The train arrived on time.

In time

ସମୟ ପୂର୍ବରୁ

Earlier than the exact time. (ସମୟ ଥାଉ ଥାଉ)

He reached the airport in time to see his friend off.

Exception (ବ୍ୟତିକ୍ରମ): Prepositions 'on' and 'in' are usually not used with words like next, last, today, tomorrow, yesterday, this, and every.

  • Example: "He will visit next Friday." (NOT "on next Friday")

9. Idiomatic Uses of Prepositions (ରୂଢିଗତ ପ୍ରୟୋଗ)

Many phrases with prepositions have special, idiomatic meanings.

  • at heart (ମୂଳତଃ / ହୃଦୟରୁ): He is very kind at heart.
  • by chance (ହଠାତ୍ / ସଂଯୋଗବଶତଃ): I met him by chance.
  • in a hurry (ବ୍ୟସ୍ତରେ): He left in a hurry.
  • in need (ଆବଶ୍ୟକତାରେ): A friend in need is a friend indeed.
  • on fire (ନିଆଁ ଲାଗି): The house is on fire.
  • on holiday (ଛୁଟିରେ): Rabi has been on holiday for two weeks.
  • on foot (ଚାଲି ଚାଲି): He goes to college on foot.
  • for sale (ବିକ୍ରି ପାଇଁ): The house is for sale.
  • out of date (ପୁରୁଣା କାଳିଆ): This fashion is out of date.
  • out of control (ନିୟନ୍ତ୍ରଣ ବାହାରେ): The bus went out of control.

  2. He is in debt. (ସେ ଋଣରେ ବୁଡିଛନ୍ତି।)

  3. I met him by chance. (ମୁଁ ତାଙ୍କୁ ହଠାତ୍ ଭେଟିଲି।)

  4. He is in a hurry. (ସେ ବ୍ୟସ୍ତ ଅଛନ୍ତି।)

  5. The shop is for sale. (ଦୋକାନଟି ବିକ୍ରି ପାଇଁ ଅଛି।)

  6. Please write this in brief. (ଦୟାକରି ଏହାକୁ ସଂକ୍ଷେପରେ ଲେଖନ୍ତୁ।)

  7. He is on duty now. (ସେ ବର୍ତ୍ତମାନ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟରତ ଅଛନ୍ତି।)

  8. The child is in danger. (ପିଲାଟି ବିପଦରେ ଅଛି।)

  9. I am, on the whole, satisfied. (ମୋଟାମୋଟି ଭାବେ, ମୁଁ ସନ୍ତୁଷ୍ଟ।)

  10. He did it on purpose. (ସେ ଏହା ଜାଣିଶୁଣି କଲେ।)
